FAQs

How does functional skills platform ensure higher pass rates?

The Pass platform is built with high-quality, in-house content, ensuring complete control over updates and improvements. It provides everything from initial assessments to full mock exams, supporting learners in developing the skills and confidence needed to achieve their Functional Skills qualifications at Further Education Colleges.

What kind of support is available to learners?

The Pass support team is available six days a week via phone, email, and live chat. Functional Skills learners at Further Education Colleges receive assistance from trained professionals, ensuring they have the support needed to achieve their qualifications as efficiently as possible.

What support is there for SEN learners?

The Pass platform offers a range of accessibility features to support SEN learners, ensuring an inclusive and effective learning experience. The SEN tools ensure all learners can access high-quality Functional Skills and GCSE support with the necessary accommodations for success.

What does the learner platform include?

The Pass platform offers a comprehensive range of resources, including video tutorials by specialist Functional Skills tutors covering every topic. Each topic includes practice questions at easy, medium, and hard levels to help build confidence. When learners feel prepared, they can attempt exam-style questions, with the system providing multiple mock exams that closely resemble the actual assessment.

How does the Pass reporting platform work for tutors?

The Pass reporting platform provides tutors with real-time insights into learner progress, allowing them to track performance, identify knowledge gaps, and tailor support effectively. Features include live progress tracking, detailed performance reports, Ofsted-compliant data exports, and customisable dashboards for easy data analysis.

How does the Pass reporting platform work for organisational managers, department heads and College principles?

The Pass reporting suite provides organisational managers, department heads, and college principals with real-time oversight of tutor and student performance. Accessible through a centralised dashboard, it offers detailed insights into learner progress and pass rates. Customisable reports highlight engagement, assessment results, and overall course performance, enabling data-driven decisions to improve outcomes and ensure compliance.
