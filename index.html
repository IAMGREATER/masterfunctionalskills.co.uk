Frequently Asked Questions About Master Functional Skills

Everything you need to know about our courses, online exam sittings, and certification.

What is Functional Skills Level 2 equivalent to?

Functional Skills Level 2 is officially recognized across the UK as a direct equivalent to a GCSE Grade 4 or Grade C pass. It meets the university entry, NHS nursing, and apprenticeship gateway requirements.

How quickly can I sit my exam and get results?

You can book an exam slot with as little as 3 working days notice. Once completed, official results and verified e-certificates are issued in approximately 3 to 5 working days.

Are exams taken online from home?

Yes. All examinations are administered online from the comfort of your home using secure remote proctoring software with live invigilation.

Do you provide 1-to-1 specialist tuition support?

Yes. In addition to our self-paced courses and topic directories, we provide 1-to-1 live tuition delivered by accredited Pearson Edexcel and City & Guilds specialist tutors.
