5. Being Safe and Responsible Online • Standard Code: L1.5.4
Style 1: Solutions
Task 1 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What is the name of the licensing system that allows creators to permit public reuse of their images or articles with attribution?

A. Creative Commons (CC) (CORRECT)
B. Recycle Bin
C. Operating System
D. Task Manager
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Creative Commons licences provide standardised legal permissions for sharing and adapting creative works.

Task 2 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

Under UK Display Screen Equipment (DSE) regulations, what must an employer provide to employees who work on screens daily?

A. Workstation ergonomic risk assessments, adjustable seating, and regular eye tests (CORRECT)
B. Free video game consoles
C. Free coffee every twenty minutes
D. A new smartphone every month
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Health and Safety (DSE) regulations mandate ergonomic assessments, adjustable chairs, and eye examinations.

Task 3 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

Why must old computers, batteries, and monitors be disposed of via certified WEEE recycling facilities rather than ordinary household rubbish bins?

A. Electronics contain toxic chemicals and heavy metals (lead, mercury) that contaminate landfills and groundwater (CORRECT)
B. Rubbish bins are too small to hold computers
C. Old computers explode if thrown in a wheelie bin
D. Bin lorries only collect paper
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

WEEE regulations ensure hazardous substances are safely handled and valuable materials are recycled.
