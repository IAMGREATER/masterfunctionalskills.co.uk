Number Patterns & Linear Sequences Pearson Edexcel: Pearson Edexcel L1 & L2 Mathematics
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Procedural Fluency Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Term-to-Term & nth Term Linear Sequences) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A clinic installs modular waiting-room seating bays. The number of seats follows the linear sequence:
6, 10, 14, 18, ...
State the term-to-term rule and calculate how many seats are in a row of 12 bays (the 12th term).
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Identify constant difference (+4 seats per bay); nth term formula = 4n + 2 [M1]
2. Evaluate 12th term: (4 × 12) + 2 = 48 + 2 = 50 seats [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Add 4 each time; 12th term = 50 seats
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Multiplying the 1st term (6) by 12 = 72 instead of applying the common difference +4.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 6 + (11 × 4) = 6 + 44 = 50 seats.
Question 2 (Level 1 Foundation — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Find the next two numbers in this decimal sequence: 0.4, 0.7, 1.0, 1.3, ___, ___
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Step rule = 0.7 − 0.4 = +0.3. [M1]
2. Next terms: 1.3 + 0.3 = 1.6, and 1.6 + 0.3 = 1.9. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1.6 and 1.9
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Spotting the gap of 14 on 85, 71, 57, 43 but adding 14 to 43 (= 57) instead of subtracting 14 (= 29).
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Think of the sequence in tenths: 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19 tenths.
Section B: Workplace & Functional Scenarios Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Pharmacy Automated Medication Dispenser Schedule (Entry 3 / Level 1 Foundation)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A pharmacy stock counter records the remaining boxes of bandages at the end of each day:
Monday: 85 • Tuesday: 71 • Wednesday: 57 • Thursday: 43
If usage continues at the same daily rate, how many boxes will remain at the end of Friday and Saturday?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Find the daily step rule: 85 − 71 = 14 boxes used per day (Rule: subtract 14). [M1]
2. Apply rule for Friday and Saturday: Friday = 43 − 14 = 29 boxes. | Saturday = 29 − 14 = 15 boxes. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Friday: 29 boxes | Saturday: 15 boxes
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Always write a plus (+) or minus (−) sign in front of the gap number.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Check the difference between at least two pairs (85 − 71 = 14 and 71 − 57 = 14) before continuing.
Question 4 (Workplace Application — 2 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Continue this decreasing temperature sequence for the next two terms: 7°C, 3°C, −1°C, ___°C, ___°C
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Step rule = subtract 4°C each time. [M1]
2. −1 − 4 = −5°C. [P1]
3. −5 − 4 = −9°C. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: −5°C and −9°C
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: For 4 tables = 18 seats, doubling 18 to get 36 seats for 8 tables is wrong because it double-counts the 2 end seats (actual answer is 34)!
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Going colder from −1°C by 4°C moves further below zero to −5°C.
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Problem Solving & Justification Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Modular Conference Table Seating Pattern Problem (Level 1/2 Multi-Step)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A training centre arranges rectangular tables end-to-end in a single long row:
• 1 table seats 6 delegates (2 on each side plus 1 at each end).
• 2 tables joined end-to-end seat 10 delegates.
• 3 tables joined end-to-end seat 14 delegates.
(a) How many delegates can sit at a row of 8 joined tables?
(b) How many tables must be joined in a single row to seat 50 delegates?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Identify the sequence rule (+4 delegates for each extra table): Sequence: 6, 10, 14, 18... Each additional table adds 4 seats (Rule: 4n + 2). [M1]
2. Calculate seats for 8 tables: 1st table = 6, plus 7 extra tables × 4 = 6 + 28 = 34 delegates (or 4 × 8 + 2 = 34). [A1]
3. Work backwards from 50 delegates to find number of tables: Subtract the 2 end seats: 50 − 2 = 48 side seats. | Divide by 4 seats per table: 48 ÷ 4 = 12 tables. [M1 / A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) 34 delegates | (b) 12 tables
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Count the number of JUMPS (+4 per table) or use the 4n + 2 rule.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Notice that the 2 end seats stay constant at 2, while every table contributes 4 side seats (4n + 2 = 50 → 4n = 48 → n = 12).
Question 6 (Level 2 Multi-Step Problem Solving — 3 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A saver opens a Christmas club account with £45 in Week 1 and adds £15 every week after that (Week 2 = £60, Week 3 = £75). How much money will be in the account in Week 12?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. From Week 1 to Week 12 is 11 weekly jumps of £15. [M1]
2. Added amount = 11 × £15 = £165. [P1]
3. Total in Week 12 = £45 + £165 = £210. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £210
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Stating a conclusion without showing the full numerical comparison values.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: From Week 1 to Week 12 is 11 jumps (12 − 1 = 11), so £45 + (11 × £15) = £210.
