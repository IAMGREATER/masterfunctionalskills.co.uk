Step-by-Step Practical Guide

1
1. Slides Panel, Adding Slides & Reordering

Notice how presentation apps split into two zones: a small Slides Strip down the left and your large active slide in the middle:

  • Add a New Slide: Click New Slide (Ctrl + M in PowerPoint; Slide → New Slide in Impress).
  • Change Layout (Title / Content / Two Columns): Right-Click inside slide background → Layout.
  • Reorder Slides: Drag a slide thumbnail up or down in the left-hand strip until the orange/blue line sits where you want it!
Tutor Tip: Click the slide thumbnail on the left strip BEFORE pressing Delete if you want to remove an unwanted slide.
2
2. Making Slides Readable (Keep Text Short + High Contrast)

A presentation slide is a visual summary for people sitting across a room — NOT a printed essay page:

  • Titles: 28pt–36pt Bold cleanly inside the top title box.
  • Body Bullets: Minimum 18pt–24pt, maximum 4–5 short lines per slide (remove long paragraphs!).
  • Colour Contrast: Light background with dark ink (Navy/Black), OR Dark background (`#0f172a`) with crisp White/Sky text. Never place pale yellow or grey text on white!
Tutor Tip: Keep the exact same font family and title size across every slide so the whole deck looks consistent and professional.
3
3. Adding Photos, Speaker Notes & Starting Full-Screen Slideshow
  • Insert Image: Insert → Picture / Image, tick Keep/Lock Aspect Ratio, and drag a corner to place neatly beside bullets (never covering text!).
  • Slide Background / Theme: PowerPoint Design tab or Impress right-hand Properties / Master Slides panel.
  • Start Slideshow Full-Screen: Press F5 to start from Slide 1 (or Shift + F5 from current slide). Use Right/Left Arrow keys to move forward/back and Esc to exit back to editing.
Tutor Tip: Save finished presentations in .pptx AND optionally File > Export as PDF for printable handouts.

Three Common Beginner Traps

Avoid Trap: Copy-pasting a huge 200-word essay onto one slide

Tiny overcrowded slides fail visual communication tasks — cut down to 3–5 short bullet phrases.

Avoid Trap: Dropping an image directly on top of your bullet points

Use a Two Content layout (text left, photo right) or resize from a corner handle cleanly.

Avoid Trap: Stuck in full-screen presentation mode?

Press the top-left Esc key on your keyboard anytime to return smoothly to edit view.
