Video Calling and Digital Messaging

Participate confidently in workplace video meetings (such as Microsoft Teams or Zoom): join meetings via links, mute/unmute your microphone, turn your camera on/off, and use chat functions respectfully.

Step-by-Step Teaching & Key Concepts

Core Knowledge
1
Joining a Video Meeting

Online video calls are essential for team discussions, remote training, and client check-ins:

  1. Open your meeting invite (in your email or calendar).
  2. Click the link that says 'Click here to join the meeting'.
  3. A preview window will open. Check your appearance, select your audio (computer audio), and click Join Now.
Video Call Control Toolbar
2
Essential Call Controls: Mic and Camera

During a call, you must control your audio and visual presence:

  • Mute / Unmute (): Keep your microphone muted when you are listening. This blocks out background noises like dogs barking, coughing, or keyboard clicks. Unmute when it is your turn to speak.
  • Camera On / Off (): Toggles your webcam. Ensure your lighting is in front of you (not behind you), and use background blur if you are working in a shared room.
Tutor Abdul's Golden Etiquette Rule: The golden rule of video meetings: Stay on mute until you are invited to speak! Use the 'Raise Hand' button if you have a question so you don't interrupt the presenter.
3
Using the In-Meeting Chat

Click the Chat bubble icon to open the sidebar. You can type questions, share links, or post comments without interrupting the speaker. Keep all chat professional, polite, and relevant to the meeting topic.

Authentic Pearson Edexcel Exam Model

Accredited Model

Here is how this skill is tested in the real Pearson Edexcel onscreen digital assessment, broken down with official examiner mark codes ([B1] knowledge, [M1] process, [A1] accuracy).

Exam Scenario Prompt: A student is attending an online group revision session with 15 classmates. The tutor is explaining a difficult topic. Give two pieces of advice on how the student should manage their microphone and camera to ensure a smooth session for everyone.
Step 1: Microphone advice [B1]
Keep the microphone muted while the tutor is speaking to eliminate background noise.
Tutor Marking Rationale: Prevents disruptive ambient noises from echoing to other participants.
Step 2: Camera / participation advice [B1]
Use the 'Raise Hand' button or type questions into the in-call chat rather than talking over the tutor.
Tutor Marking Rationale: Maintains orderly, respectful online classroom etiquette.
Tutor Abdul's Exam Golden Rule: In Entry 3 exams, questions test whether you understand the icons: microphone icon with a diagonal line through it means MUTED.

Pearson Edexcel Benchmark Solution

1. Click the meeting link in your calendar or email invite to launch the video application. 2. Test that your microphone is working and keep it muted when entering the call. 3. Turn on your webcam and position yourself in good lighting facing the camera.
Official Marking Criteria:
  • [1 Mark]: Clicking the invitation link to join the meeting.
  • [1 Mark]: Managing microphone (muting) and webcam appropriately.
