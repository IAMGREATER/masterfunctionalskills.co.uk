Official Level 1 Specification & Scope (L1.H.27)

Regulated Ofqual Functional Skills Mathematics Level 1 learning outcomes (Open Awards, Pearson Edexcel, City & Guilds, NCFE & AQA):

  • Extract and interpret information accurately from lists, tally charts, and two-way tables.
  • Complete missing row totals, column totals, and internal cells in two-way workplace tables.
  • Cross-check that all row totals and all column totals add up to the same grand total.

Core Level 1 Golden Rules: Reading & Completing Data Tables & Two-Way Tables (Level 1)

Golden Rule 1: Start With Rows or Columns That Only Have ONE Missing Cell!

Never guess when a row has two blanks—find a row or column with only 1 blank, subtract from its total, and unlock the rest like Sudoku!

Level 1 Key Rule: Known Row Total − Known Cells = Missing Cell
Golden Rule 2: Verify the Grand Total Both Ways

In every two-way table, the sum of the Row Totals MUST equal the sum of the Column Totals.

Level 1 Key Rule: Horizontal Sum = Vertical Sum = Grand Total
Golden Rule 3: Five-Bar Gate Tally Rule

Four vertical strokes crossed with a diagonal fifth stroke (||||) equals 5—count in 5s first, then add single strokes!

Level 1 Key Rule: 3 gates + 2 strokes = 15 + 2 = 17

Core Skill 2: Extracting Percentages & Fractions from Two-Way Tables

Selecting the right cell and the right total denominator.

  • Fraction of ALL staff who are Night RNs: 15 / 60 = 1/4 (25%).
  • Percentage of RNs who work Nights: (15 ÷ 35) × 100 = 42.9% (Denominator is 35 RNs, not 60!).
Level 1 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 3: Triangular Distance / Mileage Charts & Multi-Criteria Tariff Tables

Intersecting row and column headers in depot mileage grids.

To read a triangular mileage table between two towns (e.g. Oxford and Bristol):

  1. Locate Oxford along the horizontal row and Bristol along the vertical column.
  2. Follow both lines straight until they meet at the single intersecting cell.
  3. For a multi-stop round trip (Depot → Town A → Town B → Depot), extract all three leg distances and add them together.
Level 1 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Common Level 1 Examiner Traps to Avoid

Level 1 Examiner Trap: Reading From the Total Column Instead of a Specific Row
Underline BOTH labels in the question (e.g. 'Afternoon' AND 'Part-Time') and put your finger where that row and column cross!
Level 1 Examiner Trap: Counting a 5-Bar Tally Gate as 6
Remember the diagonal slash IS the 5th mark, not a 6th mark onto 5 vertical lines!

Interactive Level 1 Exam Practice Questions

Level 1 Practice 1: Holiday Pricing Matrix 2 Marks
A campsite table charges £28 per night for a pitch plus £6 per night for electric hook-up. Find the total cost for 4 nights with electric hook-up.
Level 1 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
Daily total = £28 + £6 = £34 (M1): 4 × £34
4-night total (A1): £136
Final Answer: £136
Level 1 Practice 2: Canteen Meal Tally 2 Marks
Lunch orders show: Soup = 3 full tally gates + 4 strokes; Salad = 2 full tally gates + 1 stroke. How many orders were placed altogether?
Level 1 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
Soup = 19; Salad = 11 (M1): 19 + 11
Combined orders (A1): 30 orders
Final Answer: 30 orders
