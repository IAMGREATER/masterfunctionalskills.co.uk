Official Level 1 Specification & Scope (L1.N.8 / L1.N.9)

Regulated Ofqual Functional Skills Mathematics Level 1 learning outcomes (Open Awards, Pearson Edexcel, City & Guilds, NCFE & AQA):

  • Read, write, order, and compare common fractions and mixed numbers (e.g. 1½, 2¾) using common denominators.
  • Find fractions of whole-number quantities and measurements (e.g. ⅗ of £240 or ¾ of 800 g).
  • Simplify fractions to their simplest form by dividing top and bottom by common factors.

Core Level 1 Golden Rules: Fractions and Fractions of Amounts (Level 1)

Golden Rule 1: Divide by the Bottom, Times by the Top

To find ¾ of £120: first find 1 part (120 ÷ 4 = 30), then multiply by 3 parts (30 × 3 = £90).

Level 1 Key Rule: ÷ Denominator → × Numerator
Golden Rule 2: Match Bottom Numbers to Compare Sizes

Never compare ⅔, ⅚, and ¾ by eye! Convert all three to a common denominator of 12 (&frac812;, &frac1012;, &frac912;) first.

Level 1 Key Rule: ⅔ = 8/12, ¾ = 9/12, ⅚ = 10/12
Golden Rule 3: Read carefully: Fraction OF vs Fraction OFF

If a coat is ⅓ OFF £90, find ⅓ (£30) and subtract it (£90 − £30 = £60)!

Level 1 Key Rule: OFF means subtract from the original price!

STEP 1 Divide by the Denominator (Bottom)
£630 ÷ 9 = £70

STEP 2 Multiply by the Numerator (Top)
£70 × 5 = £350
The numerator (5) tells you how many units you need. Multiply 5 units by £70 to get the final answer: £350 ✓

Reverse Fraction Formula (Divide by Top, Times by Bottom)
Step 1: £240 ÷ 3 = £80 (Value of 1/5 unit)
Step 2: £80 × 5 = £400 (Total 5/5 budget)
STEP 1 Divide by the Numerator (Top Number)
£240 ÷ 3 = £80
Since £240 represents 3 parts of the budget, dividing by 3 reveals that exactly 1 part (1/5) equals £80.
STEP 2 Multiply by Denominator for Whole Total
£80 × 5 = £400
The total budget consists of all 5 parts (5/5). Multiply £80 by 5: Total Budget = £400 ✓
Sonya's Visual Quick-Check: Forward vs. Reverse Fractions

"Remember this rhythm in exams: Forward fraction (find 3/5 of £200) → divide by bottom, times by top. Reverse fraction (£240 IS 3/5) → divide by top to find 1 part, then times by bottom to find the full 100% total!"

Core Skill 2: Adding & Subtracting Fractions & Mixed Numbers

Converting mixed numbers, finding the Lowest Common Multiple (LCM), and combining numerators.

WORKED METHOD: 4-STAGE FRACTION SUBTRACTION
Worked Calculation: 234 − 125
Stage 1: Convert to Top-Heavy (Improper) Fractions
234 = (2×4)+34
= 114
125 = (1×5)+25
= 75
Stage 2 & 3: Lowest Common Denominator (LCM = 20)
11 × 54 × 5 − 7 × 45 × 4
= 5520 − 2820 = 2720
= 1720 ✓
STEP 1 Make Fractions Top-Heavy
2 3/4 → 11/4  |  1 2/5 → 7/5
Multiply whole number by denominator, add the numerator: 2 × 4 + 3 = 11. Keeps calculations foolproof!
STEP 2 Find Common Denominator (LCM)
LCM of 4 and 5 is 20
Multiply 11/4 by 5 top and bottom (55/20). Multiply 7/5 by 4 top and bottom (28/20). Denominators are now matched!
STEP 3 Subtract Numerators Only
55 − 28 = 27 (over 20)
The denominator stays 20 — never subtract denominators! Result: 27/20.
STEP 4 Convert to Mixed Number
27 ÷ 20 = 1 remainder 7 → 1 7/20
20 goes into 27 once with 7 left over. Final accredited answer: 1 7/20 ✓
Subi's Assessor Golden Rule: Never Subtract Denominators!

"A classic examiner trap is writing 55/20 − 28/20 = 27/0! Denominators simply tell you the size of each slice. When the slice sizes match, only subtract the top numbers."

Level 1 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 3: Multiplying & Dividing Fractions (Keep • Change • Flip)

Fast rules for direct multiplication, reciprocal division (KCF), and cross-simplifying.

MULTIPLICATION: DIRECT
Worked: 34 × 25
34 × 25 = 3 × 24 × 5 = 620
Simplify (÷ 2): 310 ✓
Rule: Multiply top × top and bottom × bottom directly. No common denominators needed! Always simplify by dividing top and bottom by their common factor (2).
DIVISION: KEEP-CHANGE-FLIP
Worked: 34 ÷ 25
1. KEEP 3/4 2. CHANGE to × 3. FLIP to 5/2
34 × 52 = 3 × 54 × 2 = 158
Mixed Number: 178 ✓
Rule: Keep first fraction intact. Turn division into multiplication. Flip second fraction upside down (reciprocal). Multiply across and convert to mixed number.
Sonya's Fraction Rule: Mixed Numbers MUST Become Improper First!

"If an exam question asks you to multiply or divide mixed numbers (e.g. 2 1/2 × 1 3/4), NEVER try to multiply the whole numbers and fractions separately! Always turn both into top-heavy fractions (5/2 × 7/4 = 35/8 = 4 3/8) to guarantee all method marks [M1]."

Vocational Context: Community Allotment Area Allocation

A community garden measures 180 m². Exactly ⅖ of the garden is used for vegetables, and ⅓ is used for fruit bushes. The rest is a grass seating area. Work out the area of the grass seating area.
Step
Level 1 Method & Working
Marking Focus
Step 1 Find ⅖ of 180 m² (vegetables)
180 ÷ 5 = 36; 36 × 2 = 72 m²
→ 72 m²
[M1] Correct method ÷ 5 × 2
Step 2 Find ⅓ of 180 m² (fruit)
180 ÷ 3 = 60 m²
→ 60 m²
[M1] Correct ⅓ of 180
Step 3 Subtract both from 180 m²
180 − (72 + 60) = 180 − 132
→ 48 m²
[A1] Exact remaining grass area
Final Exam Answer: 48 m²

Common Level 1 Examiner Traps to Avoid

Level 1 Examiner Trap: Dividing by the Top Instead of the Bottom
Doing 180 ÷ 2 × 5 = 450 (bigger than the whole garden!) instead of 180 ÷ 5 × 2 = 72.
Level 1 Examiner Trap: Stopping After Finding the Discount
Writing £30 when asked for the sale price after '⅓ off £90' instead of subtracting £90 − £30 = £60.

Interactive Level 1 Exam Practice Questions

Level 1 Practice 1: Sale Coat Price 2 Marks
A winter jacket normally costs £84. In a sale it has ¼ off. What is the sale price?
Level 1 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
Find 1/4 of £84 = £21 (M1): 84 ÷ 4 = 21
Subtract discount from £84 (A1): 84 − 21 = £63
Final Answer: £63
Level 1 Practice 2: Ordering Timber Lengths 2 Marks
Put these three plank widths in order from narrowest to widest: ¾ m, ⅝ m, ½ m.
Level 1 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
Convert all to eighths (/8) (M1): ¾ = 6/8, ⅝ = 5/8, ½ = 4/8
Order smallest to largest (A1): ½ m, ⅝ m, ¾ m
Final Answer: ½ m, ⅝ m, ¾ m
