Level 2 Maths FAQ's

Is a functional skills in maths level 2 easier than GCSE?

Many learners find Functional Skills maths Level 2 slightly easier than GCSE for a few reasons. The maths is more practical and focused on everyday situations, which makes it easier to understand and apply. There is also less content in the Functional Skills maths Level 2 curriculum, meaning it generally takes less time to revise for the exam.

Are all the resources for functional skills maths level 2 online?

Alongside our online Functional Skills maths Level 2 resources, you can also order physical revision materials to be delivered directly to your door.

  • Functional skills revision cards  
  • Level 2 maths practice papers 
  • Past papers (you can print)

Can I sit the Functional Skills Maths Level 2 exam in 2026?

Yes, Functional Skills Maths Level 2 exams are available to book in 2026 and beyond. Once you have booked your exam, you can choose from a range of available dates, including dates well into the future. Depending on availability, you may also be able to sit your exam with as little as one day’s notice. Booking further in advance can also give you access to discounted exam prices.

Is functional skills maths the same as level 2 numeracy and key skills maths?

Yes functional skills maths is often called the following:

  • Level 2 numeracy
  • Key skills maths level 2 or key skills level 2 maths
  • Maths GCSE equivalent

All of these descriptions are describing the same level 2 maths qualification.

How do I get my functional skills certificate?

Once you have passed your online level 2 Functional Skills Maths exam, we will email you a digital copy of your functional skills mathematics certificate.
