Presentational Features & Typographical Devices Pearson Edexcel: L1.14 • L2.16 • L2.17
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Foundational Concept Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Level 1 Identifying Presentational Features in a Safety Notice) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Look at this formatted extract from a workshop sign:
EYE PROTECTION ZONE
• Safety goggles must be worn past this line.
• Report scratched visors to the storeman.
WARNING: Flying metal swarf can cause permanent blindness.

Identify two presentational features used in this sign and state which words or details they highlight.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Feature 1: Bold, capitalised, underlined heading ('EYE PROTECTION ZONE') to announce the mandatory safety zone [A1]
2. Feature 2: Bullet points (listing the two goggle rules) OR Bold/italic warning text ('WARNING: Flying metal swarf...') [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1. Bold underlined capitals on the heading ('EYE PROTECTION ZONE'). 2. Bullet points listing the two rules (or bold italics on the blindness warning).
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Naming 'imperative verbs' or 'emotive language' — those are language features, not presentational features!
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Can you see bold, underlining, capitals, italics, and bullet points visually? Yes.
Question 2 (Level 1 Explaining the Effect of a Shaded Callout Box) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A council council-tax leaflet prints a grey shaded rectangular box at the bottom of the page reading:
STRUGGLING TO PAY? Call our confidential Hardship Team free on 0800 111 2222 (Mon–Fri, 9am–5pm).

Explain why the designer placed this information inside a shaded box with bold capital letters.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Shaded box effect: Visually separates the helpline number from the main body text so worried residents can find support details instantly [A1]
2. Bold capitals effect: 'STRUGGLING TO PAY?' grabs the immediate attention of residents in financial difficulty [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: The shaded box separates the free 0800 helpline number from the main text for instant lookup, while bold capitals ('STRUGGLING TO PAY?') grab the attention of residents needing help.
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Writing 'to make it stand out' without mentioning the helpline number or residents struggling to pay.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Always link 'stands out' to the specific content (the 0800 hardship helpline).
Section B: Functional Workplace Application Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1/2 Webpage Presentational Features) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A veterinary clinic webpage includes:
1. A top horizontal bar reading: [ Home ]   [ Book a Vet ]   [ Repeat Prescriptions ]   [ 24hr Emergency ]
2. Underlined blue text in the main paragraph: 'View our 2026 Vaccination Price List.'
3. Customer review ratings showing: ★★★★★ (4.9 / 5 from 320 pet owners).

Name each of these three webpage features and explain how each one helps a pet owner using the site.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Navigation bar / menu tabs: Lets pet owners click straight to key pages like '24hr Emergency' or 'Book a Vet' [A1]
2. Hyperlink ('2026 Vaccination Price List'): Allows users to click directly to the price list without searching [A1]
3. Star rating graphic (5 stars / 4.9): Gives instant visual reassurance that 320 pet owners trust the clinic [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1. Navigation menu bar (quick switching to bookings/emergencies). 2. Hyperlink (one-click access to vaccination prices). 3. Star rating (instant visual proof of high customer trust).
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Calling a hyperlink 'underlined words' without explaining that clicking it opens the price list page.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Menu bar + Hyperlink + Star rating = 3 distinct digital presentational features.
Question 4 (Level 2 Analysing Pull Quotes and Straplines in Articles) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A trade magazine article is formatted with:
• Main Headline: SOLAR ROOFS: THE BRIGHT SOLUTION FOR WAREHOUSES
• Strapline (Sub-headline underneath): How three Midlands logistics depots cut their summer electricity bills by 60%.
• Boxed Pull Quote in large italics mid-page: '"Our solar panels paid for themselves in under four years!" — Depot Director'.

Explain how the strapline and the pull quote work together with the main headline to engage business readers.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Role of strapline: Clarifies the pun in the main headline by summarizing the exact factual topic (Midlands depots cutting electricity bills by 60%) [A1]
2. Role of pull quote: Breaks up the columns of text and highlights a dramatic real-world financial endorsement ('paid for themselves in under four years!') [A1]
3. Combined effect on business readers: Hooks skimming warehouse managers with proven financial savings so they read the full article [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: The strapline clarifies the headline by stating the 60% electricity saving, while the boxed pull quote breaks up the text and highlights the 4-year payback quote to persuade skimming managers to read on.
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Analysing the pun 'Bright Solution' (a language feature) instead of the functional role of the strapline and pull quote.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Strapline = clarifies headline topic (60% saving); Pull quote = highlights key testimonial.
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Exam-Style Analysis & Evaluation Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Diagnosing Presentational vs Language Feature Confusion) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Read this extract from a health campaign poster:
STOP SMOKING TODAY — PROTECT YOUR LUNGS, YOUR HEART, AND YOUR WALLET!
• Free nicotine patches delivered to your door
• 1-to-1 support from local NHS advisors

An exam candidate is asked for two presentational features and writes: '1. Rule of three (lungs, heart, wallet). 2. Direct address (your).'
(a) Explain why the candidate scored 0 marks. [2 Marks]
(b) Identify two genuine presentational features from the extract and explain their effect. [2 Marks]
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. (a) Diagnose error: 'Rule of three' and 'Direct address' are LANGUAGE features (word/grammar choices), not visual presentational features [M1]
2. (a) Clarify distinction: Presentational features must refer to visual formatting and layout on the poster [A1]
3. (b) Correct Feature 1 & Effect: Bold ALL-CAPS heading ('STOP SMOKING TODAY...') visually commands attention and conveys urgency [A1]
4. (b) Correct Feature 2 & Effect: Bullet points (and italics) separate the two free NHS support benefits into quick, easy-to-scan points [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) Rule of three and direct address are language features, not visual formatting. (b) 1. Bold capital letters ('STOP SMOKING TODAY') grab attention and show urgency. 2. Bullet points clearly list the free patches and 1-to-1 NHS support.
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Failing to explain the effect of the bold capitals and bullet points in part (b).
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Language = words; Presentational = bold capitals, bullet points, italics.
Question 6 (Level 2 Evaluating How Multiple Presentational Features Support Purpose) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A food allergy product recall notice in a supermarket uses:
1. A thick red-and-white striped hazard border around the edge of the poster
2. A colour photograph of the exact 'Crunchy Oat Granola 500g' packet
3. Bold underlined text on the batch code: Batch Code: L4492 — Best Before: Oct 2026
4. A shaded green footer box stating: 'Return to any customer service desk for a full £3.20 cash refund (no receipt needed).'

Evaluate how these four presentational features work together to achieve the notice's purpose.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Red-and-white hazard border: Immediately signals an urgent safety alert to passing shoppers [A1]
2. Product photograph: Allows customers to visually recognise the 'Crunchy Oat Granola' box in their kitchen cupboard instantly [A1]
3. Bold underlined batch code: Draws the eye to the exact batch ('L4492') and date so shoppers can verify if their packet is affected [A1]
4. Shaded green footer box: Separates the reassuring action step (getting a full £3.20 refund with no receipt) [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1. Hazard border grabs attention for a safety alert. 2. Photo helps shoppers recognise the granola box. 3. Bold underlining highlights Batch Code L4492 for checking. 4. Green box separates the £3.20 refund instructions.
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Listing the four features without linking each one to how a shopper uses it (spotting alert -> recognising packet -> checking batch -> claiming refund).
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: All 4 features explained with specific content links = 4 marks.
