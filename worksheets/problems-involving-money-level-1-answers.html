Section A: Core Skills (Warm-Up) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Level 1 Foreign Currency Exchange) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A radiographer exchanges £650 into US Dollars ($) before a training trip.
The exchange rate is £1 = $1.28, and the travel desk deducts a flat $12.00 service fee after conversion.
How many US Dollars ($) does the radiographer receive?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Convert GBP to USD: 650 × 1.28 = $832.00 [M1]
2. Deduct $12.00 fee: $832.00 - $12.00 = $820.00 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: $820.00
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Dividing £650 by 1.28 instead of multiplying.
🔄 Quick Check: ($820 + $12) ÷ 1.28 = $832 ÷ 1.28 = £650.
Question 2 (Level 1 Domestic Gas Meter Bill) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A household gas bill over a 60-day period shows 1,400 kWh of gas used.
Gas is charged at 7.5p per kWh plus a standing charge of 30p per day.
Calculate the total pre-VAT charge in pounds (£).
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Usage charge: 1,400 × £0.075 = £105.00; Standing charge: 60 × £0.30 = £18.00 [M1]
2. Total pre-VAT charge: £105.00 + £18.00 = £123.00 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £123.00
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Using 0.75 instead of 0.075 for 7.5p per kWh.
🔄 Quick Check: (£123 - £18) ÷ £0.075 = £105 ÷ 0.075 = 1,400 kWh.
Section B: Everyday & Workplace Maths Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 Van Hire Mileage Tariff) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A hospital charity hires a Luton van for 4 days to collect donated furniture.
• Daily hire rate: £45.00 per day (includes 80 free miles per day).
• Excess mileage charge: 25p for every mile over the total free allowance.
The driver covers a total of 460 miles over the 4 days. Calculate the total hire cost.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Base hire for 4 days: 4 × £45.00 = £180.00 [M1]
2. Free mileage = 4 × 80 = 320 miles → Excess miles = 460 - 320 = 140 miles [P1]
3. Excess charge = 140 × £0.25 = £35.00 → Total cost = £180.00 + £35.00 = £215.00 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £215.00
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Deducting only 80 free miles instead of 4 × 80 = 320 free miles.
🔄 Quick Check: (£215 - £180) ÷ £0.25 = 140 excess miles + 320 free miles = 460 miles.
Question 4 (Level 1 Group Conference Catering Package) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A training centre hosts a clinical governance day for 35 delegates.
• Venue room hire: £220.00
• Lunch & refreshments: £14.00 per delegate
• Promotional offer: 10% discount on food only when booking for 30 or more delegates.

Calculate the total cost of the event.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Gross food cost: 35 × £14.00 = £490.00 [M1]
2. Discounted food cost (10% off food): £490.00 - £49.00 = £441.00 [P1]
3. Add room hire: £441.00 + £220.00 = £661.00 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £661.00
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Taking 10% off the £220 room hire as well, when the discount applies to food only.
🔄 Quick Check: £661 - £220 = £441; £441 ÷ 0.90 = £490 = 35 × £14.
