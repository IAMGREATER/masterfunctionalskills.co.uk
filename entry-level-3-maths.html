Functional Skills Maths Entry Level 3 — Frequently Asked Questions

Essential guidance on syllabus requirements, exam format, calculator rules, and progression pathways.

What is Functional Skills Maths Entry Level 3?

Entry Level 3 is a nationally recognised qualification regulated by Ofqual. It is designed for adult learners and students building their core foundation in everyday practical mathematics, preparing for Level 1 study, college entry, or employment.

What topics are tested in Entry 3 Maths?

The curriculum covers three main content domains: Using Numbers (whole numbers up to 1000, addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, simple fractions and decimals), Common Measures, Shape and Space (money, time, metric measurements, perimeter, 2D and 3D shapes), and Handling Information and Data (reading tables, tally charts, bar charts, and simple line graphs).

Can I use a calculator in the exam?

Entry Level 3 Maths exams consist of two distinct sections: a short Non-Calculator section (testing basic mental and written arithmetic methods) and a longer Calculator section where you can use a standard scientific or four-function calculator.

How do I take the Entry Level 3 exam?

You can sit your Entry Level 3 exam online from home with secure remote invigilation or at an accredited test centre. Exams can be booked with Master Functional Skills 6 days a week with fast turnaround results.

What can I do after passing Entry Level 3?

Passing Entry Level 3 demonstrates readiness for Functional Skills Maths Level 1, which is the direct stepping stone to Level 2 (the GCSE Grade 4/C equivalence needed for university courses, apprenticeships, and healthcare or nursing pathways).

What revision resources are included?

Every single one of our 24 Entry 3 topics includes an in-depth lesson guide, a printable PDF question worksheet, a full model answer sheet, and an expert tutor video walkthrough breaking down problem solving step-by-step.
