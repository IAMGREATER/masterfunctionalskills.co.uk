Section A: Core Skills (Warm-Up) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Place Value & Rounding Large Integers) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
An regional NHS logistics hub dispatched 348,650 sterile dressings in Quarter 1.
Write down the value represented by the digit 4, and round 348,650 to the nearest ten thousand.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Value of digit 4 in ten-thousands column: 40,000 (forty thousand) [M1]
2. Round 348,650 to nearest 10,000 (thousands digit is 8 ≥ 5): 350,000 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Value = 40,000 | Rounded = 350,000
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Rounding to the nearest thousand (349,000) instead of ten thousand (350,000).
🔄 Quick Check: 348,650 is 1,350 away from 350,000 vs 8,650 away from 340,000.
Question 2 (Level 1 Foundation — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Write the number four thousand, and sixty-nine in figures, and state the value of the digit 6.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Thousands = 4, Hundreds = 0, Tens = 6, Units = 9 → 4,069. [M1]
2. Digit 6 is in the tens column → 60 (sixty). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 4,069; value of 6 is 60
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Writing 75 underneath the hundreds and tens columns of 240 instead of the tens and units columns.
🔄 Quick Check: Include the 0 in the hundreds column so the 4 stays in the thousands place.
Section B: Everyday & Workplace Maths Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Care Home Resident Laundry Bag Count (Entry 3 / Level 1 Foundation)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A laundry assistant receives three deliveries of clean bath towels: 240 towels, 185 towels, and 75 towels.
Calculate the total number of towels received and show a reverse subtraction check.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Add the three delivery quantities in aligned columns: 240 + 185 = 425 towels. | 425 + 75 = 500 towels. [M1 / A1]
2. Show a reverse operation check: 500 − 75 = 425; 425 − 185 = 240. [B1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 500 towels (Check: 500 − 75 − 185 = 240)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Always align the rightmost units digits vertically.
🔄 Quick Check: Spot number bonds to 100: 185 + 75 = 260, and 240 + 260 = 500.
Question 4 (Workplace Application — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A catering supplier packs soup tins in trays of 25 tins. A care home orders 14 full trays, plus 18 loose tins. How many soup tins are delivered in total?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Tins in trays = 14 × 25 = 350 tins. [M1]
2. Add loose tins = 350 + 18 = 368 tins. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 368 tins
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Writing "three thousand and fifty" as 350 instead of 3,050.
🔄 Quick Check: 4 trays of 25 = 100, so 12 trays = 300, and 14 trays = 350.
Section C: Exam-Style Problem Solving Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Hospital Central Stores PPE Stock Audit & Reorder Threshold (Level 1/2 Multi-Step)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
At the start of the month, a hospital store holds 3,050 boxes of disposable aprons.
• Week 1 issue: 685 boxes | Week 2 issue: 740 boxes | Week 3 issue: 825 boxes
• The procurement system automatically orders a restock of 1,500 boxes if remaining stock falls below 850 boxes.
Determine whether the automatic restock is triggered at the end of Week 3, and calculate the final stock after any restock.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Sum the boxes issued across Weeks 1, 2, and 3: 685 + 740 + 825 = 2,250 boxes issued. [M1]
2. Subtract issued stock from starting stock (3,050): 3,050 − 2,250 = 800 boxes remaining. [M1]
3. Compare 800 with the 850 threshold and add 1,500 restock: Since 800 < 850, YES, the restock is triggered. | Final stock = 800 + 1,500 = 2,300 boxes. [M1 / A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Yes, restock triggered (800 < 850) — Final stock = 2,300 boxes
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Check that a thousand number always has 4 digits (3,050).
🔄 Quick Check: Notice how 685 + 825 = 1,510, plus 740 = 2,250.
Question 6 (Level 2 Multi-Step Problem Solving — 3 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Four regional clinics report their monthly patient registrations: Clinic A: 1,408, Clinic B: 1,084, Clinic C: 1,480, Clinic D: 1,048.
(a) Arrange the four numbers in ascending order (smallest to largest).
(b) Calculate the difference between the highest and lowest clinic registrations.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Ascending order: 1,048, 1,084, 1,408, 1,480. [M1]
2. Difference = 1,480 − 1,048 = 432 patients. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) 1,048, 1,084, 1,408, 1,480 | (b) 432
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Stating a conclusion without showing the full numerical comparison values.
🔄 Quick Check: Compare the hundreds column first (0 vs 4), then compare the tens column.
