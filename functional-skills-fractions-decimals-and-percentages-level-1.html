Official Level 1 Specification & Scope (L1.N.16)

Regulated Ofqual Functional Skills Mathematics Level 1 learning outcomes (Open Awards, Pearson Edexcel, City & Guilds, NCFE & AQA):

  • Recognise and calculate equivalences between common fractions, decimals, and percentages (e.g. ½ = 0.5 = 50%, ¼ = 0.25 = 25%, ¾ = 0.75 = 75%, ⅕ = 0.2 = 20%, &frac110; = 0.1 = 10%).
  • Compare mixed offers written in different forms (e.g. '¼ off' vs '20% off' vs '0.3 of the budget').
  • Convert any fraction to a decimal via Top ÷ Bottom and then × 100 for a percentage.

Core Level 1 Golden Rules: Equivalences Between Fractions, Decimals and Percentages (Level 1)

Golden Rule 1: Convert Everything into Percentages (%) to Compare

When comparing ⅕, 0.25, and 18%, turn them all into percentages first: 20%, 25%, and 18%—now ordering is instant!

Level 1 Key Rule: 18% < ⅕ (20%) < 0.25 (25%)
Golden Rule 2: Memorise the Core 5 Level 1 Benchmarks

½ = 0.5 = 50% • ¼ = 0.25 = 25% • ¾ = 0.75 = 75% • ⅕ = 0.2 = 20% • &frac110; = 0.1 = 10%.

Level 1 Key Rule: 3/5 = 3 × 20% = 60% = 0.6
Golden Rule 3: Decimal → Percentage = × 100

Watch single-digit percentages carefully: 0.05 = 5%, whereas 0.5 = 50%!

Level 1 Key Rule: 0.08 = 8% (NOT 80%!)

Core Skill 2: Converting Any Fraction to a Percentage (Scaling vs. Bus-Stop)

Two failsafe non-calculator methods for converting unmemorized fractions into percentages.

METHOD A: SCALE TO 100
For Denominators That Divide Into 100
Use when denominator is 2, 4, 5, 10, 20, 25, or 50. Multiply top and bottom by the multiplier that makes the bottom 100!
Worked: Convert 1725 to a percentage
17 × 425 × 4 = 68100 = 68% ✓
Multiplier Key: 20 × 5 = 100 • 25 × 4 = 100 • 50 × 2 = 100.
METHOD B: SHORT DIVISION
For Denominators Like 8, 16, or Odd Numbers
Divide numerator by denominator under the bus stop with decimals (top ÷ bottom), then multiply by 100.
Worked: Convert 58 to a percentage
8 into 5.000 = 0.625
0.625 × 100 = 62.5% ✓
Step: Add trailing decimal zeros (5.000) under the bus stop and shift decimal point 2 places right for %.

Core Skill 3: Converting Decimals & Percentages to Simplest Fractions

Writing over 10, 100, or 1,000 and cancelling down using Highest Common Factors (HCF).

WORKED METHOD: CANCELLING COMMON FACTORS
Worked Conversions: 36% and 0.375 into Simplest Fractions
Example A: 2-Digit Decimal / Percentage (36% or 0.36)
Step 1: Write over 100 → 36100
Step 2: Both divide by 4 (HCF = 4):
36 ÷ 4100 ÷ 4 = 925 ✓
Example B: 3-Digit Decimal (0.375)
Step 1: 3 decimal places = over 1,000 → 3751000
Step 2: Cancel in steps (÷ 25, then ÷ 5):
375 ÷ 1251000 ÷ 125 = 38 ✓
Subi's Assessor Golden Rule: Always Cancel to 'Simplest Form'

"Writing 36/100 or 375/1000 will bank the method mark [M1], but to get the accuracy mark [A1], you must divide down until no common factors remain. If you are stuck, keep dividing by 2 or 5 in steps until you can't divide anymore!"

Vocational Context: Comparing Three Promotional Discounts

Three DIY stores sell the same £200 lawnmower with different promotions: Store A offers ⅕ off, Store B offers 25% off, and Store C reduces the price by 0.15 of the ticket price. Put the three discounts in order from smallest discount to largest discount.
Step
Level 1 Method & Working
Marking Focus
Step 1 Convert Store A (⅕) to a percentage
100% ÷ 5 = 20%
→ Store A = 20% off
[M1] ⅕ = 20%
Step 2 Convert Store C (0.15) to a percentage
0.15 × 100 = 15%
→ Store C = 15% off
[M1] 0.15 = 15%
Step 3 Order smallest to largest
15% (0.15), 20% (⅕), 25% (Store B)
→ Store C (0.15), Store A (⅕), Store B (25%)
[A1] Correct ordered comparison
Final Exam Answer: Smallest to largest discount: Store C (0.15 = 15%), Store A (⅕ = 20%), Store B (25%)

Common Level 1 Examiner Traps to Avoid

Level 1 Examiner Trap: Writing ⅕ = 0.15 or 15%
Remember fifths go up in 20s: ⅕ = 20% = 0.20, ⅖ = 40% = 0.40, ⅗ = 60% = 0.60!
Level 1 Examiner Trap: Confusing 0.05 with 50%
0.05 × 100 = 5%, whereas 0.50 × 100 = 50%.

Interactive Level 1 Exam Practice Questions

Level 1 Practice 1: Survey Results Comparison 2 Marks
In a staff travel survey, ⅖ drive, 35% take the bus, and 0.25 cycle. Which travel method is most popular?
Level 1 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
Convert all to %: 2/5 = 40%, bus = 35%, 0.25 = 25% (M1): ⅖ = 40%, 0.25 = 25%
Identify highest percentage (A1): Driving (40%) is most popular
Final Answer: Driving (⅖ = 40%)
Level 1 Practice 2: Fraction to Percentage 2 Marks
A learner scores 18 out of 25 on a safety quiz. Write this score as a percentage.
Level 1 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
Scale 18/25 × 4 to reach /100 (M1): 18 × 4 = 72
State percentage (A1): 72%
Final Answer: 72%
