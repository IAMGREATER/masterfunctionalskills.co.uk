Estimating the Mean from Grouped Data Pearson Edexcel: Pearson Edexcel L1 & L2 Mathematics
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Procedural Fluency Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Finding Midpoints & fx in Grouped Data) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A pharmacy tracks patient waiting times (t minutes) in two intervals:
• 0 < t ≤ 10 mins: 12 patients
• 10 < t ≤ 20 mins: 18 patients

Using interval midpoints, calculate the estimated mean waiting time across these 30 patients.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Midpoints: 5 and 15. Multiply Σ(f × x) = (12 × 5) + (18 × 15) = 60 + 270 = 330 [M1]
2. Divide Σ(fx) by total frequency (30): 330 ÷ 30 = 11 minutes [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 11 minutes
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Dividing 330 by 2 (the number of rows) instead of 30 (total patients).
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 11 mins lies sensibly between the two midpoints (5 and 15), closer to 15.
Question 2 (Level 1 Foundation — 4 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A courier depot records the weights of 25 parcels:
• 0 < w ≤ 2 kg: 5 parcels
• 2 < w ≤ 4 kg: 12 parcels
• 4 < w ≤ 6 kg: 8 parcels
Calculate an estimate of the mean parcel weight.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Midpoints: 1 kg, 3 kg, 5 kg. [M1]
2. fx: (5 × 1 = 5) + (12 × 3 = 36) + (8 × 5 = 40) = 81 kg. [M1]
3. Total frequency = 25. [M1]
4. Estimated Mean = 81 ÷ 25 = 3.24 kg. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 3.24 kg
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Computing 2,460 ÷ 4 = 615 seconds is impossible because no call lasted longer than 120 seconds!
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Sum fx = 81; divide by 25 parcels = 3.24 kg.
Section B: Workplace & Functional Scenarios Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Care Home Call Bell Response Times Estimated Mean (Level 2 Core)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A care home audits the response times in seconds for 40 emergency call bells:
• 0 to 30s: 6 calls
• 30 to 60s: 14 calls
• 60 to 90s: 12 calls
• 90 to 120s: 8 calls
Calculate an estimate of the mean call bell response time.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Find the midpoint (x) of each interval: 0–30: 15s | 30–60: 45s | 60–90: 75s | 90–120: 105s. [M1]
2. Multiply each Midpoint by Frequency (fx): (15 × 6 = 90) + (45 × 14 = 630) + (75 × 12 = 900) + (105 × 8 = 840). [M1]
3. Sum fx (Σfx) and verify total frequency (Σf): Σfx = 90 + 630 + 900 + 840 = 2,460. | Total frequency Σf = 6 + 14 + 12 + 8 = 40 calls. [M1]
4. Divide Σfx by Σf: Estimated Mean = 2,460 ÷ 40 = 61.5 seconds. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 61.5 seconds
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Always cross-out the midpoint total—only sum the Frequency (f) column and the (fx) column.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 61.5 seconds sits right in the middle of the 0–120s range, confirming the calculation is sensible.
Question 4 (Workplace Application — 4 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A solar array logs daily energy output over 30 days:
• 10 to 20 kWh: 6 days
• 20 to 30 kWh: 15 days
• 30 to 40 kWh: 9 days
Calculate an estimate of the mean daily energy output.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Midpoints: 15, 25, 35. [M1]
2. fx: (6 × 15 = 90) + (15 × 25 = 375) + (9 × 35 = 315) = 780 kWh. [P1]
3. Estimated Mean = 780 ÷ 30 = 26 kWh. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 26 kWh
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Doing 40 ÷ 4 = 10 completely ignores the time intervals and scores 0 marks.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 780 ÷ 30 = 26 kWh.
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Problem Solving & Justification Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Outpatient Pharmacy Prescription Wait Target Decision (Level 2 Multi-Step)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A hospital pharmacy charter states that the mean prescription waiting time must be under 18 minutes. An audit of 50 prescriptions shows:
• 0 < t ≤ 10 mins: 12 prescriptions
• 10 < t ≤ 20 mins: 23 prescriptions
• 20 < t ≤ 30 mins: 10 prescriptions
• 30 < t ≤ 50 mins: 5 prescriptions
Calculate an estimate of the mean waiting time and state whether the pharmacy met its charter target.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate midpoints (noting the wider 30–50 final interval!): Midpoints: 5, 15, 25, and (30 + 50) ÷ 2 = 40 mins. [M1]
2. Compute fx and sum Σfx: (12 × 5 = 60) + (23 × 15 = 345) + (10 × 25 = 250) + (5 × 40 = 200) = 855. [M1]
3. Divide by total prescriptions (50) and compare with 18 mins: Estimated Mean = 855 ÷ 50 = 17.1 minutes. | Since 17.1 mins < 18 mins, YES, the pharmacy met its charter target. [M1 / A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Estimated Mean = 17.1 minutes — Yes, the pharmacy met its target (< 18 mins)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: You must calculate Midpoint × Frequency for every row.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Watch the last interval carefully: 30 to 50 has a width of 20, so its midpoint is 40, not 35!
Question 6 (Level 2 Multi-Step Problem Solving — 4 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A call center audits 80 customer calls:
• 0 < t ≤ 4 mins: 20 calls
• 4 < t ≤ 8 mins: 36 calls
• 8 < t ≤ 12 mins: 16 calls
• 12 < t ≤ 20 mins: 8 calls
Calculate an estimate of the mean call duration in minutes.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Midpoints: 2, 6, 10, 16 (note 12 to 20 midpoint is 16!). [M1]
2. fx: (20 × 2 = 40) + (36 × 6 = 216) + (16 × 10 = 160) + (8 × 16 = 128) = 544. [P1]
3. Estimated Mean = 544 ÷ 80 = 6.8 minutes. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 6.8 minutes
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Stating a conclusion without showing the full numerical comparison values.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Check the final group (12 to 20): (12 + 20) ÷ 2 = 16.
