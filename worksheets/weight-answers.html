Weight & Mass Pearson Edexcel: L1.18 • L2.20 • L2.21
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Procedural Fluency Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Level 1 Parcel Gross Weight in Kilograms) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A medical dispatch box weighs 450 grams when empty.
A technician packs 8 bottles of saline (each weighing 325 grams) into the box.
Calculate the total gross weight of the packed box in kilograms (kg).
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Total weight in grams: (8 × 325 g) + 450 g = 2,600 g + 450 g = 3,050 g [M1]
2. Convert to kilograms (÷ 1,000): 3,050 ÷ 1,000 = 3.05 kg [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 3.05 kg
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Forgetting to add the 450 g empty box weight.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 3.05 kg = 3,050 g; (3,050 - 450) ÷ 8 = 325 g.
Question 2 (Level 2 Milligram to Gram Tablet Batch Mass) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Each ibuprofen tablet contains 400 mg of active ingredient.
How many grams (g) of active ingredient are needed to manufacture a batch of 2,500 tablets?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Total mg: 2,500 × 400 mg = 1,000,000 mg [M1]
2. Convert mg to grams (÷ 1,000): 1,000,000 ÷ 1,000 = 1,000 g (or 1 kg) [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1,000 g
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Dividing by 100 instead of 1,000 when converting mg to g.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 400 mg = 0.4 g; 2,500 × 0.4 g = 1,000 g.
Section B: Workplace & Functional Scenarios Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 Laundry Roll-Cage Safe Load) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A hospital laundry roll-cage has a maximum gross weight limit of 250 kg.
The empty roll-cage weighs 28 kg.
It is loaded with laundry bags that each weigh 12 kg.

What is the maximum number of full laundry bags that can be loaded into the cage without exceeding the 250 kg limit?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Subtract empty cage tare weight: 250 kg - 28 kg = 222 kg available for bags [M1]
2. Divide available weight by 12 kg per bag: 222 ÷ 12 = 18.5 bags [P1]
3. Round DOWN for safety: 18 full bags [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 18 bags
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Rounding up to 19 bags (19 × 12 + 28 = 256 kg, which exceeds the 250 kg safety limit!).
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: (18 × 12 kg) + 28 kg = 216 + 28 = 244 kg ≤ 250 kg.
Question 4 (Level 2 Catering Meat Joint Roasting Time) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A care home chef is roasting a beef joint weighing 3.25 kg.
The cooking instructions state: 'Roast at 190°C for 20 minutes per 500 g, plus an extra 25 minutes.'
If the beef must come out of the oven at 12:45 pm, at what time must the chef put it into the oven?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Find number of 500 g (0.5 kg) units in 3.25 kg: 3.25 ÷ 0.5 = 6.5 units [M1]
2. Calculate cooking time: (6.5 × 20 mins) + 25 mins = 130 + 25 = 155 minutes (2 hours 35 minutes) [P1]
3. Subtract 2 hrs 35 mins from 12:45 pm: 12:45 - 2 hrs 35 mins = 10:10 am [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 10:10 am
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Multiplying 3.25 × 20 mins instead of finding how many 500 g blocks are in 3,250 g (6.5 blocks).
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 10:10 am + 2 hrs 35 mins = 12:45 pm.
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Problem Solving & Justification Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Delivery Van Weighbridge Axle & Payload Compliance) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A 3.5-tonne Luton delivery van has a maximum legal Gross Vehicle Weight (GVW) of 3.5 tonnes.
• The unladen (empty) van weighs 2,140 kg.
• The driver and passenger weigh 165 kg combined, and a sack barrow weighs 25 kg.
• The van is being loaded with 28 identical crates of medical equipment, each weighing 42.5 kg.

Will the fully loaded van be within the legal 3.5-tonne weight limit? Show exact figures in kilograms.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Convert 3.5 tonnes limit to kg: 3.5 × 1,000 = 3,500 kg [P1]
2. Calculate weight of 28 crates: 28 × 42.5 kg = 1,190 kg [M1]
3. Sum total gross weight: 2,140 (van) + 165 (crew) + 25 (barrow) + 1,190 (crates) = 3,520 kg [P1]
4. Compare and conclude: 3,520 kg > 3,500 kg. No, the van is overweight by 20 kg. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: No, overweight by 20 kg (Total weight is 3,520 kg vs 3,500 kg limit)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Omitting the 165 kg crew weight or 25 kg sack barrow weight.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 3,520 - 3,500 = 20 kg over limit.
Question 6 (Level 2 Paediatric Intravenous Dosage by Body Weight) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A paediatrician prescribes an intravenous medication for a child weighing 44 lbs.
• Clinical guidelines specify a daily dosage of 15 mg per kg of body weight, divided into 3 equal doses per day.
• The medication solution has a concentration of 25 mg per ml.
• Use: 1 kg = 2.2 lbs.

How many millilitres (ml) of solution should the nurse administer for one single dose?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Convert child weight from lbs to kg: 44 lbs ÷ 2.2 = 20 kg [P1]
2. Calculate total daily dosage in mg: 20 kg × 15 mg/kg = 300 mg per day [M1]
3. Divide by 3 for one single dose: 300 mg ÷ 3 = 100 mg per dose [P1]
4. Convert 100 mg to ml using concentration (25 mg/ml): 100 ÷ 25 = 4 ml [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 4 ml
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Giving the full daily volume (12 ml) instead of dividing by 3 for a single dose.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 4 ml × 25 mg/ml = 100 mg/dose × 3 = 300 mg/day ÷ 20 kg = 15 mg/kg.
