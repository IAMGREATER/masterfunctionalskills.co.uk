Section A: Core Skills (The Basics) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Using Discourse Markers to Link Contrasting Ideas) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Combine these two short, disconnected sentences into one sophisticated complex sentence using a contrasting connective (e.g. Although, Whereas, or Despite):
'The new electric ambulances cost 15% more to purchase. They reduce annual fuel bills by £4,200 per vehicle.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Open with a subordinating contrasting conjunction ('Although' or 'While') [A1]
2. Punctuate the subordinate clause with a comma before the main clause [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Although the new electric ambulances cost 15% more to purchase, they reduce annual fuel bills by £4,200 per vehicle.
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Joining the two sentences with a comma alone (comma splice) instead of a conjunction.
🔄 Quick Check: Subordinate clause + comma + main clause.
Question 2 (Core Skill Application — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Combine these two choppy sentences into one cohesive Level 2 sentence using a semicolon and an appropriate contrasting discourse marker (e.g. nevertheless, however):
'The night-shift care team experienced two unexpected staff sickness absences. They completed all resident medication rounds safely and on schedule.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Place a semicolon after the first independent clause ('...sickness absences;'). [M1]
2. Insert 'nevertheless,' or 'however,' followed by a comma and the second clause. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'The night-shift care team experienced two unexpected staff sickness absences; nevertheless, they completed all resident medication rounds safely and on schedule.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: While better than no markers at all, starting every paragraph with 'Firstly... Secondly... Thirdly...' feels robotic at Level 2.
🔄 Quick Check: Using 'semicolon + discourse marker + comma' (; nevertheless, / ; however, / ; consequently,) is one of the highest-scoring punctuation structures in Level 2 English.
Section B: Real-Life & Workplace Practice Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 Model: Inserting Paragraph Breaks & Linking Words in a Letter) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
This draft extract is written as one unbroken block of short sentences. Divide it into two logical paragraphs and insert appropriate linking words (However, Furthermore, Therefore):
'I visited your leisure centre on Tuesday evening for the beginner swimming class. The receptionist was very welcoming and showed me to the changing rooms. The changing room floors were covered in muddy footprints. Two of the three hairdryers were broken and had exposed wires. You need to inspect the changing rooms daily.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Identify the topic shift from positive reception to changing room hazards: Start Paragraph 1 with the visit and positive reception; start Paragraph 2 where the complaint about the changing rooms begins. [A1]
2. Insert a contrasting marker ('However,') at the start of Paragraph 2: Use 'However,' to pivot from the polite receptionist to the unhygienic changing rooms. [A1]
3. Insert 'Furthermore,' and 'Therefore,' to link the electrical hazard and action: Connect the muddy floor to the dangerous hairdryers and final recommendation. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: I visited your leisure centre on Tuesday evening to attend the beginner adult swimming class. Upon arrival, the front-desk receptionist was extremely welcoming and kindly directed me to the poolside facilities.

However, I was disappointed to find that the changing room floors were covered in muddy footprints. Furthermore, two of the three wall-mounted hairdryers were out of order with exposed electrical wiring, presenting a serious safety hazard. Therefore, I ask that centre management conduct daily hygiene and electrical safety inspections.
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Link paragraphs conceptually using thematic bridges: 'In addition to these clinical safety benefits, the financial implications must also be considered.'
🔄 Quick Check: Starting Paragraph 2 with 'However,' creates a smooth bridge between your praise in Paragraph 1 and your complaint in Paragraph 2.
Question 4 (Exam-Style Task — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Choose the most logical discourse marker from the list (Consequently / Conversely / Furthermore) to fill each gap below:
1. Replacing halogen bulbs with LED panels will cut electricity usage by 45%. __________, the new panels have a ten-year lifespan that reduces maintenance callouts.
2. The north car park lighting timer failed at 17:00 yesterday. __________, staff leaving the evening shift had to walk to their vehicles in complete darkness.
3. Online training modules allow staff to study flexibly at home. __________, practical manual-handling skills can only be assessed safely in person.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Sentence 1 adds a second positive benefit → Furthermore. [M1]
2. Sentence 2 shows the direct result/effect of the timer failing → Consequently. [M1]
3. Sentence 3 contrasts online study with in-person assessment → Conversely. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1. Furthermore | 2. Consequently | 3. Conversely
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Writing 'The course was cheap. However, everyone enjoyed it' makes no sense because 'cheap' and 'enjoyed it' are both positive points, not a contrast!
🔄 Quick Check: Test the relationship between the two sentences first: Addition = Furthermore; Result = Consequently; Contrast = Conversely.
Section C: Exam-Style Tasks Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Model: Community Health Hub Expansion PEEL Paragraph) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Write one fully cohesive body paragraph arguing why extending GP clinic opening hours until 20:00 will alleviate evening A&E overcrowding, using the PEEL method and at least two Level 2 discourse markers.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Draft Topic Sentence (Point): State clearly that extending primary care hours until 20:00 directly relieves emergency hospital congestion. [A1]
2. Incorporate Evidence & Explanation with 'Furthermore,': Use 'Furthermore,' to introduce audit data (38% of evening A&E visits are minor ailments) and explain how nurse practitioners can treat them. [A1]
3. Link back to overarching clinical strategy with 'Consequently,': Use 'Consequently,' to connect local evening access with reduced hospital waiting times and NHS savings. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'Extending our community clinic opening hours until 20:00 on weekdays will directly alleviate severe congestion at the neighbouring hospital Emergency Department. Furthermore, recent regional audit data reveals that 38% of non-urgent evening A&E attendances involve minor infections or sprains that could be treated rapidly by our primary care nurse practitioners. Consequently, providing local evening appointments not only prevents vulnerable patients from enduring six-hour hospital waits, but also frees acute trauma teams to focus on life-threatening emergencies.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Only use 'However' or 'Conversely' when the second idea genuinely opposes or contrasts with the first.
🔄 Quick Check: Notice how 'Furthermore,' introduces supporting data and 'Consequently,' drives the logical conclusion home. This is textbook Level 2 cohesion.
Question 6 (Level 2 Mastery Challenge — 4 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Write a complete 4-sentence PEEL body paragraph for a formal letter to your College Principal arguing why the college library should remain open until 20:00 on Tuesdays and Thursdays for adult evening-class learners.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Point: Topic sentence stating that extending library hours until 20:00 is essential for adult learners. [M1]
2. Evidence: Cite that over 120 adult apprentices work full-time daytime shifts and have no quiet study space or Wi-Fi at home. [M1]
3. Explain (with 'Furthermore'): Explain how access to textbooks and computers after work improves exam pass rates. [M1]
4. Link (with 'Consequently'): Conclude that keeping one librarian on duty for two extra hours will significantly boost student retention. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'Extending college library opening hours until 20:00 on Tuesdays and Thursdays is essential to support our adult evening-class learners. Currently, the library closes at 17:00, which means over 120 adult apprentices who work full-time daytime shifts are completely locked out of textbook and computer resources. Furthermore, many learners live in shared accommodation without quiet study spaces or reliable broadband to complete their coursework. Consequently, funding just six additional hours of library staffing per week will directly improve functional skills pass rates and student retention.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Failing to proofread spelling, punctuation, and grammar after completing the draft.
🔄 Quick Check: Every sentence in this paragraph has a clear job: Point → Evidence → Explanation → Link.
