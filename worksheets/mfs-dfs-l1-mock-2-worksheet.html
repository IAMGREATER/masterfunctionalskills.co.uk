Task C • Bike Workshop & Hire Revenue Spreadsheet Analysis (15 marks)
Context: Calculate total sessions and weekly revenue across 8 bike services in BIKE WORKSHOP SALES.xlsx, apply currency formatting, sort data, and create an informative chart.
  1. Merge and centre cells A1:H1, set font size to 20 pt, bold, and shade cells A1:H1 soft green.
  2. In cell G4, enter a formula to calculate Total Sessions for Standard Commuter Bike Hire across Mon-Fri: =SUM(C4:F4).
  3. In cell H4, calculate Total Revenue: =G4*B4 (Total Sessions × Hourly / Hire Rate).
  4. Replicate formulas down columns G and H for all 8 workshop services (rows 4 to 11).
  5. In row 12, calculate the Daily Average Bookings: =AVERAGE(C4:C11) and replicate across columns C to F.
  6. In cell H12, calculate Grand Total Revenue using =SUM(H4:H11).
  7. Format all hire rates (column B) and revenue amounts (column H and cell H12) as British Pounds (£) with 2 decimal places.
  8. Sort the services table (rows A4:H11) in descending order of Total Revenue (Column H).
  9. Create a 2D Clustered Column or Bar Chart comparing Service / Hire Item (A3:A11) against Total Sessions (G3:G11). Add an informative chart title, axis titles, and place the chart neatly below the data table or on a separate sheet.
