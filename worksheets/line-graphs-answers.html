Line Graphs & Trend Analysis Pearson Edexcel: Pearson Edexcel L1 & L2 Mathematics
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Procedural Fluency Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Calculating Rate of Change from a Line Graph) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A vaccine cold-storage line graph shows the internal temperature rising from 2.4°C at 08:00 to 6.0°C at 11:00 during a power cut.
Calculate the mean rate of temperature rise in °C per hour.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Temperature increase: 6.0 - 2.4 = 3.6°C over 3 hours (08:00 to 11:00) [M1]
2. Rate per hour: 3.6 ÷ 3 = 1.2°C per hour [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1.2°C per hour
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Dividing 6.0°C by 3 hours without subtracting the starting temperature of 2.4°C.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 2.4°C + (3 × 1.2°C) = 2.4 + 3.6 = 6.0°C.
Question 2 (Level 1 Foundation — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A depot diesel tank line graph shows 420 litres at 08:00 and 180 litres at 16:00, decreasing at a steady linear rate. How many litres were in the tank at 12:00?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. 12:00 is halfway between 08:00 and 16:00. [M1]
2. Midpoint = (420 + 180) ÷ 2 = 600 ÷ 2 = 300 litres. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 300 litres
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Vague trend statements without figures from the axes score 0 marks.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Average the 08:00 and 16:00 readings (drops by 30 litres per hour).
Section B: Workplace & Functional Scenarios Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Hospital ICU Patient Body Temperature Monitoring Line Graph (Level 1/2 Foundation)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A clinical line graph records an ICU patient's temperature every 2 hours from 08:00 to 18:00:
• 08:00: 38.8°C | 10:00: 38.4°C | 12:00: 37.8°C | 14:00: 37.2°C | 16:00: 36.9°C | 18:00: 37.0°C
(a) Describe the overall trend in the patient's temperature over the 10-hour period.
(b) Estimate the patient's temperature at 11:00.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. State overall trend direction with start and end figures: Steady downward trend, falling from 38.8°C at 08:00 to 36.9°C at 16:00 before stabilising at 37.0°C at 18:00. [A1]
2. Interpolate halfway between 10:00 (38.4°C) and 12:00 (37.8°C): (38.4 + 37.8) ÷ 2 = 76.2 ÷ 2 = 38.1°C. [M1]
3. State estimated temperature with units: 38.1°C. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) Downward trend from 38.8°C at 08:00 to 37.0°C at 18:00 | (b) 38.1°C
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Always write: "Decreased from [Start Value] in [Start Time] to [End Value] in [End Time]."
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Always include starting and ending figures when describing a line graph trend.
Question 4 (Workplace Application — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A dual line graph compares quarterly sales (£000s) for Store A and Store B:
• Store A: Q1 = 40, Q2 = 55, Q3 = 50, Q4 = 75
• Store B: Q1 = 50, Q2 = 45, Q3 = 65, Q4 = 60
In which quarter was the difference in sales between the two stores greatest, and what was that difference in £?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Differences: Q1 = 10k; Q2 = 10k; Q3 = 65 − 50 = 15k; Q4 = 75 − 60 = 15k. [M1]
2. Greatest difference is in Q3 and Q4, with a difference of £15,000. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Q3 and Q4 (£15,000 difference)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: When asked to draw a line graph, drawing vertical bars loses the format mark.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Remember the axis is in £000s (thousands), so 15 represents £15,000!
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Problem Solving & Justification Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Cold-Room Vaccine Fridge Power Outage Threshold Analysis (Level 2 Multi-Step)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
During a power cut, a vaccine fridge temperature is logged every hour:
• 01:00: 3.0°C | 02:00: 4.5°C | 03:00: 6.5°C | 04:00: 9.5°C | 05:00: 7.0°C | 06:00: 4.0°C
• Vaccines must be quarantined if the temperature stays above 8.0°C for more than 45 minutes.
Using linear interpolation between the plotted points, determine the exact times when the fridge was above 8.0°C and decide whether the vaccines must be quarantined.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Find when temperature crossed 8.0°C rising between 03:00 (6.5°C) and 04:00 (9.5°C): Rise from 6.5°C to 9.5°C is 3.0°C in 60 mins (1°C every 20 mins). | From 6.5°C to 8.0°C is 1.5°C → 1.5 × 20 mins = 30 mins → Crosses 8.0°C at 03:30. [M1]
2. Find when temperature dropped back to 8.0°C between 04:00 (9.5°C) and 05:00 (7.0°C): Drop from 9.5°C to 7.0°C is 2.5°C in 60 mins. | Drop from 9.5°C to 8.0°C is 1.5°C → (1.5 ÷ 2.5) × 60 = 36 mins → Drops to 8.0°C at 04:36. [M1]
3. Calculate total minutes above 8.0°C and compare with 45-minute limit: From 03:30 to 04:36 = 30 + 36 = 66 minutes. | Since 66 mins > 45 mins, YES, the vaccines must be quarantined. [M1 / A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Above 8.0°C for 66 minutes (03:30 to 04:36) — Yes, vaccines must be quarantined (> 45 mins)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Plot neat crosses (×) at each coordinate and connect them point-to-point with a ruler.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Draw a horizontal line across the graph at 8.0°C to see exactly where the graph rises above and drops below the threshold.
Question 6 (Level 2 Multi-Step Problem Solving — 3 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Using the Store A sales figures from Question 2 (Q1: £40k, Q2: £55k, Q3: £50k, Q4: £75k), calculate the mean quarterly sales for Store A in £.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Sum = 40 + 55 + 50 + 75 = 220 (£000s) = £220,000. [M1]
2. Mean = £220,000 ÷ 4 = £55,000. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £55,000
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Stating a conclusion without showing the full numerical comparison values.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 220 ÷ 4 = 55 thousand pounds (£55,000).
