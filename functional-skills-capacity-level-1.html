Core Level 1 Golden Rules: Metric Capacity — Millilitres, Centilitres and Litres (Level 1)

Golden Rule 1: 1 Litre = 1,000 ml = 100 cl

Multiply litres by 1,000 to get millilitres (2.5 L = 2,500 ml); divide ml by 1,000 to get litres.

Level 1 Key Rule: 1 cl = 10 ml (so 33 cl can = 330 ml)
Golden Rule 2: Convert Urns to ml Before Dividing by Cup Size

To pour 200 ml cups from a 4.5 L urn: convert 4.5 L → 4,500 ml, then 4,500 ÷ 200 = 22.5 → 22 full cups!

Level 1 Key Rule: Round DOWN for full cups poured!
Golden Rule 3: Cuboid Tank Link: 1,000 cm³ = 1 Litre

Once you find a rectangular fish tank's volume in cm³ (L × W × H), divide by 1,000 to convert into litres.

Level 1 Key Rule: 24,000 cm³ ÷ 1,000 = 24 litres

Core Skill 1: Converting Between Litres (L), Centilitres (cl) & Millilitres (ml)

Keep all liquid volumes in the same unit before dividing or adding.

Litres to Millilitres (× 1,000)
3.5 L × 1,000 = 3,500 ml
Millilitres to Litres (÷ 1,000)
750 ml ÷ 1,000 = 0.75 L
Centilitres to Millilitres (× 10)
75 cl bottle × 10 = 750 ml

Vocational Context: Conference Refreshment Juice Cartons

An organiser needs to serve 45 delegates one 200 ml glass of orange juice each. Orange juice is sold in 2-litre cartons. How many 2-litre cartons must she buy?
Step
Level 1 Method & Working
Marking Focus
Step 1 Calculate total juice required in ml
45 × 200 ml = 9,000 ml
→ 9,000 ml
[M1] Total liquid needed in ml
Step 2 Convert 9,000 ml into litres (÷ 1,000)
9,000 ÷ 1,000 = 9 litres
→ 9 litres
[M1] Accurate ml → L conversion
Step 3 Divide by 2 L per carton and round UP
9 ÷ 2 = 4.5 → 5 cartons
→ 5 cartons
[A1] Rounded up to 5 whole cartons
Final Exam Answer: 5 cartons (gives 10 litres to cover the 9 litres needed)

Common Level 1 Examiner Traps to Avoid

Level 1 Examiner Trap: Dividing 1 Litre by 100 Instead of 1,000 for ml
Remember 'milli' means thousandth: 1 L = 1,000 ml (so 0.5 L = 500 ml, not 50 ml).
Level 1 Examiner Trap: Rounding Down Bottles to Buy
Seeing 4.5 cartons and rounding down to 4—always check whether you are buying supplies (round UP) or pouring full cups (round DOWN).

Interactive Level 1 Exam Practice Questions

Level 1 Practice 1: Cleaning Concentrate Doses 2 Marks
A janitor uses 40 ml of floor cleaner per bucket. How many full buckets can he make from a 1.5-litre bottle?
Level 1 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
Convert 1.5 L = 1,500 ml; 1,500 ÷ 40 = 37.5 (M1): 1,500 ÷ 40 = 37.5
Round down to full buckets (A1): 37 full buckets
Final Answer: 37 full buckets
Level 1 Practice 2: Fish Tank Litre Capacity 3 Marks
A rectangular glass tank measures 50 cm long, 30 cm wide, and 40 cm deep. Using 1,000 cm³ = 1 litre, find its full capacity in litres.
Level 1 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
Volume = 50 × 30 × 40 = 60,000 cm³ (M1): 60,000 cm³
Divide by 1,000 cm³ per litre (M1): 60,000 ÷ 1,000
Capacity in litres (A1): 60 litres
Final Answer: 60 litres
