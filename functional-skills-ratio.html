Specification & Learning Outcomes

Candidates must demonstrate mastery of the following regulated Functional Skills Mathematics outcomes:

  • L1.11 / L2.11 – Understand and calculate ratio, direct proportion, and scaling.
  • L2.11 – Divide an amount into a given ratio of two or three parts.

Golden Rules for Ratio & Sharing into Parts

Golden Rule 1: The 3-Step Ratio Sharing Rule (Add, Divide, Multiply)

1. Add the ratio numbers together to find the total number of parts.
2. Divide the total money or items by the total parts to find the value of 1 part.
3. Multiply the 1-part value by each ratio number.

Golden Rule / Method: 1. Add Parts → 2. Divide Total by Parts → 3. Multiply by Each Part
Golden Rule 2: Dilutions and Ratios: Concentrate vs Water

In a 1 : 4 dilution ratio, there is 1 part concentrate and 4 parts water, giving 5 TOTAL parts (Concentrate is 1/5 of the total liquid, NOT 1/4).

Golden Rule / Method: Ratio 1 : 4 = 1 + 4 = 5 Total Parts

Core Skill 1: Skill 1: Simplifying Ratios & Converting to Unitary Form (1 : n)

Dividing by Highest Common Factors and expressing ratios with a leading 1.

  • Simplifying to Lowest Whole Terms: Divide both sides by their Highest Common Factor (HCF): 24 : 36 = 2 : 3 (dividing both by 12).
  • Unitary Ratio (1 : n): Divide BOTH numbers by the first number: 4 : 18 = 1 : 4.5 (dividing both by 4).
  • Match Units First: Never simplify mixed units! Convert to the smaller unit first: 50p : £2.50 → 50p : 250p = 1 : 5.
Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 2: Skill 2: Sharing a Total into a Two-Part or Three-Part Ratio (ADM Method)

When you know the grand total and need to find each share.

To share £720 in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4:

  1. Add parts: 2 + 3 + 4 = 9 total parts.
  2. Divide total by parts: £720 ÷ 9 = £80 per part.
  3. Multiply each part: 2 × £80 = £160 | 3 × £80 = £240 | 4 × £80 = £320.
  4. Sanity Check: £160 + £240 + £320 = £720.
Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 3: Skill 3: Scaling from One Known Part or the Difference Between Parts

When the question gives you ONE share or the difference, NOT the grand total.

Given Information How to Find 1 Part Worked Example (Ratio 3 : 7)
Known Share (e.g. First share = £60) Divide known value by its own parts (3) 1 part = £60 ÷ 3 = £20 → Second share = 7 × £20 = £140
Known Difference (e.g. B gets £80 more than A) Divide difference by difference in parts (7 - 3 = 4) 1 part = £80 ÷ 4 = £20 → Total = 10 × £20 = £200
Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Pearson Edexcel Step-by-Step Worked Models

Benchmark Exam Model 1 3 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 1/2)

Vocational Context: Clinical Decontamination Protocol

An infection control housekeeper must prepare 15 litres of sanitizing solution. The clinical guidelines require hospital disinfectant concentrate and water to be mixed in the ratio 1 : 4.
(a) How many litres of disinfectant concentrate are needed?
(b) How many litres of water are needed?
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Add the ratio parts to find the total parts
1 part concentrate + 4 parts water = 5 total parts.
Criteria: M1: Total parts calculation
Step 2 Find the volume of 1 part
15 litres ÷ 5 parts = 3 litres per part.
Criteria: M1: Value of 1 part division
Step 3 Multiply by each ratio term
Concentrate = 1 × 3 L = 3 litres.
Water = 4 × 3 L = 12 litres.
Criteria: A1: Accurate component volumes
Final Accredited Answer: Disinfectant Concentrate = 3 litres | Water = 12 litres
Method Checklist & Marking Scheme Guidance
Quick check: 3 L + 12 L = 15 L total. 3 : 12 simplifies to 1 : 4. Both conditions verified.
Benchmark Exam Model 2 4 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 2 Problem Solving)

Vocational Context: Healthcare Workforce Compliance

CQC inspection guidelines for an acute dementia unit require a minimum ratio of senior nurses to healthcare assistants (HCAs) of 2 : 5, and at least 1 staff member for every 4 residents.
On Saturday, there are 84 residents on the unit. The roster currently has 6 senior nurses booked.
(a) Based on the 6 senior nurses and the 2 : 5 ratio, how many HCAs should be rostered?
(b) Will this combined team of senior nurses and HCAs meet the overall 1 : 4 staff-to-resident requirement?
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Use the 2 : 5 ratio to find the number of HCAs matching 6 senior nurses
2 parts = 6 senior nurses → 1 part = 6 ÷ 2 = 3.
HCAs (5 parts) = 5 × 3 = 15 HCAs.
Criteria: M1: Scaling from known part (6 ÷ 2 × 5 = 15)
Step 2 Calculate total staff on the unit
Total staff = 6 senior nurses + 15 HCAs = 21 staff members.
Criteria: M1: Summing total rostered staff
Step 3 Calculate minimum staff required for 84 residents (1 : 4)
84 residents ÷ 4 = 21 staff members required.
Criteria: M1: Resident ratio division
Step 4 Compare and conclude
21 rostered staff = 21 minimum required staff. Yes, 15 HCAs are needed and the combined team of 21 staff meets the 1 : 4 requirement.
Criteria: A1: Accurate HCA count (15) and justified confirmation
Final Accredited Answer: (a) 15 HCAs | (b) Yes, total staff is 6 + 15 = 21, which exactly meets the 84 ÷ 4 = 21 requirement.
Method Checklist & Marking Scheme Guidance
Notice that in part (a), 6 is the number of senior nurses (2 parts), NOT the total staff!

Critical Examiner Traps & Common Pitfalls

Examiner Pitfall: Dividing by One of the Ratio Terms Instead of the Total
When sharing a total of 15 L in the ratio 1 : 4, a common blunder is dividing 15 L by 4 (= 3.75 L). You must divide by the SUM of the parts (1 + 4 = 5).
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Always write: 'Total parts = [part 1] + [part 2]' before touching your calculator.

Interactive Exam Practice Questions

Exam Practice Question 1 3 Marks
A construction firm mixes concrete using cement, sand, and gravel in the ratio 1 : 2 : 4 by weight. The builder needs to prepare 560 kg of concrete.
Calculate the weight of sand required.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Total parts = 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 parts.
2. 1 part = 560 ÷ 7 = 80 kg.
3. Sand has 2 parts: 2 × 80 kg = 160 kg.
Final Correct Answer: 160 kg of sand
Examiner Tip: Identify which term in the ratio corresponds to sand (the middle term, 2 parts).
Exam Practice Question 2 2 Marks
A landscaping nursery mixes compost and topsoil in the ratio 400 g : 1.8 kg. Write this ratio in its simplest whole-number form, and also in the unitary form 1 : n.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Convert to common units (grams): 400 g : 1,800 g.
2. Divide both by 200 to simplify: 2 : 9.
3. Divide both by 2 for 1 : n form: 1 : 4.5.
Final Correct Answer: Simplest form = 2 : 9 | Unitary form = 1 : 4.5
Examiner Tip: Always convert 1.8 kg to 1,800 g before simplifying!
Exam Practice Question 3 3 Marks
Two business partners, Maya and Liam, share annual profits in the ratio 3 : 7. This year, Liam receives £14,000 more than Maya. Calculate the total profit made by the business.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Difference in ratio parts = 7 - 3 = 4 parts.
2. Value of 1 part = £14,000 ÷ 4 = £3,500.
3. Total parts = 3 + 7 = 10 parts → Total profit = 10 × £3,500 = £35,000.
Final Correct Answer: £35,000
Examiner Tip: When given the difference ("£14,000 more"), divide by the difference in parts (7 - 3 = 4).
