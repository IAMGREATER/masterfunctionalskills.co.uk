Specification & Learning Outcomes

Candidates must demonstrate mastery of the following regulated Functional Skills Mathematics outcomes:

  • E3.7 / L1.24 – Extract, compare, and use numerical information from tables, price lists, and schedules.

Golden Rules for Data Tables & Information Retrieval

Golden Rule 1: Check Header Units and Multipliers First (e.g. "in £000s" or "per 100")

Always read the table title, column headers, and footnotes before picking numbers! If a header says "Cost (£ per box of 50)", then ordering 100 items requires 2 boxes.

Golden Rule / Method: Read Row Header • Read Column Header • Check Footnotes & Units
Golden Rule 2: Trace Rows and Columns with a Straight Edge

In dense pricing or timetable grids, place your ruler horizontally under the target row so your eyes never slip onto the row above or below.

Golden Rule / Method: Intersect Target Row and Target Column Precisely

Core Skill 1: Two-Condition Pricing Matrices (Size × Delivery Speed)

Finding the exact charge where a weight band row meets a service level column.

Parcel Weight Standard (48h) Express (24h) Saturday Special
Up to 2 kg£3.95£5.50£8.20
2.01 kg to 5 kg£5.40£7.25£10.50
5.01 kg to 10 kg£7.80£9.90£13.40

Example: A 3.6 kg parcel sent by Express (24h) sits in row 2, column 3 = £7.25.

Core Skill 2: Reading Bus, Train & Shift Timetables

Following a single vehicle down a column and calculating journey durations.

  • Each Column is One Bus/Train: Find the column that arrives at your destination on or before your appointment time, then read up that same column to find when it departs your starting station.
  • Dash (—) in a Timetable: Means that specific bus or train does NOT stop at that station!
Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 3: Comparing Multi-Supplier Quotes from a Table

Including delivery charges and bulk discounts when comparing table rows.

Never pick the supplier with the lowest unit price without checking the Delivery Fee column! Calculate (Quantity × Unit Price) + Delivery Fee for each row to prove which supplier is cheapest overall.

Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Benchmark Exam Model 1 2 Marks (Entry Level 3 / Level 1)

Vocational Context: Clinical Ward Operations

A table shows nurse staffing across 3 hospital wards:
• Ward A: Morning 6, Afternoon 5, Night 3
• Ward B: Morning 8, Afternoon 6, Night 4
• Ward C: Morning 5, Afternoon 4, Night 2
(a) Which ward has the highest Afternoon staffing?
(b) What is the total number of Night nurses across all three wards?
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Compare the Afternoon numbers (5, 6, 4)
Highest is Ward B with 6 nurses.
Criteria: A1: Ward B
Step 2 Sum the Night numbers (3 + 4 + 2)
3 + 4 + 2 = 9 night nurses.
Criteria: A1: 9 night nurses
Final Accredited Answer: (a) Ward B (6 nurses) | (b) 9 night nurses
Method Checklist & Marking Scheme Guidance
Circle the specific shift name (Afternoon / Night) before adding.
Benchmark Exam Model 2 4 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 1/2)

Vocational Context: GP Surgery Procurement

A practice manager needs to order 8 digital blood pressure monitors. Three suppliers quote in a table:
• MedEquip: £24.00 per monitor | Delivery: £12.00
• HealthDirect: £25.50 per monitor | Delivery: Free on orders over £150 (otherwise £15.00)
• CarePlus: £23.00 per monitor | Delivery: £22.00
Calculate the total cost for each supplier and identify the cheapest option.
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Calculate MedEquip total for 8 monitors
(8 × £24.00) + £12.00 = £192.00 + £12.00 = £204.00.
Criteria: M1: MedEquip total (£204.00)
Step 2 Calculate HealthDirect total (checking free delivery threshold)
8 × £25.50 = £204.00 (Over £150, so delivery is £0) → Total = £204.00.
Criteria: M1: HealthDirect total (£204.00)
Step 3 Calculate CarePlus total and compare all three
(8 × £23.00) + £22.00 = £184.00 + £22.00 = £206.00.
MedEquip and HealthDirect are tied as the cheapest at £204.00 each (£2.00 cheaper than CarePlus).
Criteria: M1 / A1: CarePlus (£206.00) and correct comparison
Final Accredited Answer: MedEquip and HealthDirect are joint cheapest at £204.00 (CarePlus costs £206.00)
Method Checklist & Marking Scheme Guidance
Notice that CarePlus had the cheapest monitor (£23), but its £22 delivery fee made it the most expensive overall!

Critical Examiner Traps & Common Pitfalls

Examiner Pitfall: Ignoring Table Footnotes (e.g. "All prices exclude 20% VAT")
Missing a line below the table stating that delivery or VAT must be added.
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Always read the small text directly underneath a table before starting your working.
Examiner Pitfall: Picking a Bus That Arrives AFTER the Appointment Time
If an appointment is at 09:15 and buses arrive at 09:02 and 09:20, picking the 09:20 bus makes the passenger 5 minutes late!
How to Avoid This Pitfall: You must arrive ON or BEFORE the appointment time (pick the 09:02 arrival).

Interactive Exam Practice Questions

Exam Practice Question 1 2 Marks
Using the Parcel Weight table in Core Skill 1 above, calculate the total postage cost to send one 1.5 kg parcel by Standard (48h) and one 6.8 kg parcel by Saturday Special.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. 1.5 kg (Up to 2 kg) Standard = £3.95.
2. 6.8 kg (5.01 to 10 kg) Saturday Special = £13.40.
3. Total = £3.95 + £13.40 = £17.35.
Final Correct Answer: £17.35
Examiner Tip: Match each parcel weight to its correct row before reading across to the service column.
Exam Practice Question 2 3 Marks
A hospital shuttle bus timetable shows:
• Bus 1: Departs Station 07:45 → Arrives Hospital 08:18
• Bus 2: Departs Station 08:15 → Arrives Hospital 08:48
• Bus 3: Departs Station 08:40 → Arrives Hospital 09:13
A nurse starts her shift at 08:45 and needs 10 minutes to walk from the bus stop to her ward. Which is the latest bus she can catch, and what time does it depart the Station?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Latest arrival at Hospital bus stop = 08:45 − 10 mins = 08:35.
2. Bus 2 arrives at 08:48 (too late). She must catch Bus 1 (arrives 08:18).
3. Bus 1 departs Station at 07:45.
Final Correct Answer: Bus 1 (departs Station at 07:45)
Examiner Tip: Subtract the 10-minute walk from 08:45 first: she must arrive at the bus stop by 08:35.
Exam Practice Question 3 2 Marks
In Question 2, how many minutes does the Bus 1 journey take from Station (07:45) to Hospital (08:18)?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. From 07:45 to 08:00 = 15 minutes.
2. From 08:00 to 08:18 = 18 minutes.
3. Total = 15 + 18 = 33 minutes.
Final Correct Answer: 33 minutes
Examiner Tip: Bridge across the hour (08:00): 15 mins + 18 mins = 33 mins.
