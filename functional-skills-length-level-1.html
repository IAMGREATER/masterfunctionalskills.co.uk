Official Level 1 Specification & Scope (L1.M.20)

Regulated Ofqual Functional Skills Mathematics Level 1 learning outcomes (Open Awards, Pearson Edexcel, City & Guilds, NCFE & AQA):

  • Convert measurements between millimetres (mm), centimetres (cm), metres (m), and kilometres (km) within the metric system.
  • Match all units BEFORE adding, subtracting, or finding how many pieces fit along a wall or shelf.
  • Know the three core metric length bridges: 10 mm = 1 cm, 100 cm = 1 m, and 1,000 m = 1 km.

Core Level 1 Golden Rules: Metric Length Conversions — mm, cm, m and km (Level 1)

Golden Rule 1: Memorise 10 — 100 — 1,000

1 cm = 10 mm • 1 m = 100 cm • 1 km = 1,000 m.

Level 1 Key Rule: 2.4 m = 240 cm • 3,500 m = 3.5 km
Golden Rule 2: Big Unit → Small Unit = MULTIPLY

Metres are bigger than centimetres, so there are MORE centimetres: 3.6 m × 100 = 360 cm. Small → Big = DIVIDE.

Level 1 Key Rule: 85 cm ÷ 100 = 0.85 m
Golden Rule 3: Convert Before Dividing 'How Many Fit?'

To find how many 45 cm boxes fit along a 3.6 m shelf, convert 3.6 m → 360 cm first, then 360 ÷ 45 = 8 boxes!

Level 1 Key Rule: Never divide 3.6 ÷ 45!

Core Skill 1: Length Measurement Benchmarks & Unit Selection

Real-world physical benchmarks for rapid estimation and unit checking.

  • Millimetres (mm): Precision engineering, timber thickness, tablet diameters (10 mm = 1 cm).
  • Centimetres (cm): Furniture dimensions, shelving, clothing sizes (100 cm = 1 m).
  • Metres (m): Room dimensions, garden fencing, corridor lengths (1,000 m = 1 km).
  • Kilometres (km): Road transport distances and courier routes.
Level 1 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 2: Cutting Lengths from Timber / Pipe Stock (No-Joining Rule)

Why you must divide each stock length individually when offcuts cannot be glued together.

Suppose you need 10 shelves each measuring 90 cm, and timber planks are sold in 240 cm lengths:

  1. Check how many full shelves fit into 1 plank: 240 cm ÷ 90 cm = 2.66... → 2 full shelves per plank (with a 60 cm offcut that is too short for a 3rd shelf).
  2. Divide required shelves by shelves per plank: 10 shelves ÷ 2 per plank = 5 planks needed.
Level 1 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 3: Spacing Items with Gaps Along a Wall or Fence

Calculating posts, shelving brackets, or hospital bed bays with mandatory clearance gaps.

When placing 4 lockers (each 60 cm wide) along a 3.5 m (350 cm) wall with a 15 cm gap between each locker:

  • 4 lockers have 3 internal gaps between them (Gaps = Items - 1).
  • Total width used: (4 × 60 cm) + (3 × 15 cm) = 240 cm + 45 cm = 285 cm.
  • Remaining wall space: 350 cm - 285 cm = 65 cm.
Level 1 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Vocational Context: Joinery Shelving Cut List

A carpenter has a timber board that is 2.4 metres long. He cuts three shelves each measuring 65 cm long. How many centimetres of timber are left over?
Step
Level 1 Method & Working
Marking Focus
Step 1 Convert 2.4 m into centimetres (× 100)
2.4 × 100 = 240 cm
→ 240 cm
[M1] Metric conversion 2.4 m = 240 cm
Step 2 Find total length of the 3 shelves
3 × 65 cm = 195 cm
→ 195 cm used
[M1] Total cut length
Step 3 Subtract from 240 cm
240 − 195 = 45 cm
→ 45 cm (or 0.45 m)
[A1] Accurate offcut length with units
Final Exam Answer: 45 cm left over

Common Level 1 Examiner Traps to Avoid

Level 1 Examiner Trap: Using 100 Instead of 10 for mm ↔ cm
Look at a standard ruler: there are only 10 mm in 1 cm (not 100!). So 45 mm = 4.5 cm.
Level 1 Examiner Trap: Subtracting Mixed Units Directly
Doing 195 − 2.4 without converting 2.4 m to 240 cm first.

Interactive Level 1 Exam Practice Questions

Level 1 Practice 1: Running Track Distance 2 Marks
A park loop is 850 metres long. Jo runs 4 laps. Write the total distance in kilometres (km).
Level 1 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
Total metres = 4 × 850 = 3,400 m (M1): 3,400 m
Divide by 1,000 for km (A1): 3,400 ÷ 1,000 = 3.4 km
Final Answer: 3.4 km
Level 1 Practice 2: Storage Crates Along a Wall 2 Marks
How many 60 cm wide filing cabinets can stand side-by-side along a 4.5 m office wall?
Level 1 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
Convert 4.5 m = 450 cm; divide 450 ÷ 60 = 7.5 (M1): 450 ÷ 60 = 7.5
Round down to whole cabinets that fit (A1): 7 cabinets
Final Answer: 7 filing cabinets
