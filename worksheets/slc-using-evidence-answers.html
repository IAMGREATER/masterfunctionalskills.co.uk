Using Evidence & Persuasive Data in Speech Pearson Edexcel: Pearson Edexcel Functional Skills English (SLC)
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Core Skills (The Basics) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Strengthening a Vague Claim with Spoken Statistics and Examples) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Upgrade this weak, unsupported spoken opinion by incorporating the data provided in brackets:
'Lots of appointments get wasted when people forget to turn up, so text reminders are really good.'
(Data: 140 missed appointments per month; SMS reminders reduced Did-Not-Attend rates by 38% in the pilot clinic).
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Cite the baseline problem statistic ('Currently, our practice loses 140 appointments every month to Did-Not-Attends...') [A1]
2. Cite the pilot outcome statistic ('...however, our pilot clinic proved that automated 24-hour SMS reminders cut missed appointments by 38%.') [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'Currently, our practice loses 140 clinical appointments every month to Did-Not-Attends; however, evidence from our pilot clinic proves that automated 24-hour SMS reminders reduce missed appointments by 38%.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Saying 'a lot of appointments' when exact figures (140 per month, 38% reduction) are available.
🔄 Quick Check: Includes both the 140 baseline figure and the 38% improvement figure.
Question 2 (Core Skill Application — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
You are presenting an argument for installing chilled water dispensers on hospital wards for nursing staff. Combine one physiological fact and one operational benefit into a 40-word spoken statement.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Cite an authoritative source and physiological fact (e.g. Royal College of Nursing research showing a 2% drop in hydration reduces concentration by 15%). [M1]
2. Connect hydration directly to reduced medication charting errors and lower mid-shift fatigue. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'Clinical studies cited by the Royal College of Nursing show that even a 2% drop in hydration impairs concentration and cognitive focus by 15%. Installing chilled water dispensers on every ward will directly combat mid-shift fatigue and reduce prescription charting errors.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Saying 'Everyone hates the new uniform' or 'It is obvious this will save millions' does not count as persuasive evidence under Criterion L2.4.
🔄 Quick Check: Citing recognised bodies like the Royal College of Nursing (RCN), HSE, or NICE immediately elevates your spoken authority.
Section B: Real-Life & Workplace Practice Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Model 1 (Level 1): Supporting an Opinion with Practical Examples) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
In a group discussion about boosting Saturday trade for local High Street shops, support the idea of offering '2 Hours Free Parking' in council car parks using a clear reason and a real-world example.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. State the core opinion clearly: 'I believe introducing two hours of free Saturday parking in the town centre car park is the best way to help independent shops.' [A1]
2. Give the financial reason why shoppers currently avoid the High Street: 'Currently, families have to pay £4.50 just to park for ninety minutes, so many drive to the out-of-town retail park where parking is free.' [A1]
3. Provide a concrete neighbouring town example: 'When our neighbouring town, Market Drayton, introduced two hours of free Saturday parking last summer, High Street footfall increased by a quarter.' [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'I believe introducing two hours of free Saturday parking in the town centre is the best way to boost local trade. Currently, drivers pay £4.50 for ninety minutes, which pushes families toward out-of-town retail parks with free parking. When our neighbouring town trialled two hours of free Saturday parking last summer, High Street visitor numbers rose by 25%.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Replace 'everyone' with a specific percentage or survey source: 'In last week's poll, 78% of ward staff reported...'
🔄 Quick Check: Comparing current costs (£4.50) with a successful trial in a neighbouring town makes your point grounded and persuasive.
Question 4 (Exam-Style Task — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Upgrade this weak, unsubstantiated spoken opinion into a persuasive Level 2 evidenced contribution for a college meeting:
'We should make all textbooks available online as PDFs because heavy books are annoying to carry and cost too much money.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Replace 'annoying to carry and cost too much' with concrete financial data (e.g. £140 average textbook cost per student). [M1]
2. Add accessibility evidence (screen-reader compatibility and zoom functions for learners with dyslexia or visual impairments). [M1]
3. State the impact on course completion. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'Providing free digital PDF textbooks on the college portal would remove a major financial barrier for adult learners, who currently spend an average of £140 per term on printed manuals. Furthermore, digital editions allow learners with dyslexia to adjust font sizes and use text-to-speech software on their phones, which increased assignment submission rates by 22% in the Business department last year.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Reciting a string of numbers without explaining what they mean for patients, staff, or customers leaves listeners cold.
🔄 Quick Check: Notice how pairing a financial figure (£140 per term) with an inclusivity benefit (dyslexia software) makes the argument twice as strong.
Section C: Exam-Style Tasks Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Model 2 (Level 2): Defending a Capital Funding Request for Electric Pallet Trucks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A finance director questions why the depot needs £18,000 for three electric pallet trucks when manual pump trucks cost only £300 each. Deliver an evidenced spoken business case.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. State the core claim focusing on long-term return on investment: 'While manual pump trucks have a low upfront price, investing £18,000 in three powered pallet trucks delivers substantial health and productivity savings.' [A1]
2. Introduce internal HSE accident and agency cost data: 'According to our HSE accident log, manual-handling back strains caused 64 lost working days last year, costing the depot £9,200 in temporary agency cover alone.' [A1]
3. Project turnaround efficiency and payback period: 'Furthermore, trials in Bay 1 showed powered trucks cut lorry unloading times from 45 minutes to 22 minutes. Consequently, the £18,000 investment pays for itself within fourteen months while protecting our operatives from injury.' [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'While manual trucks have a lower upfront cost, electric pallet trucks deliver substantial long-term savings. According to our HSE accident log, manual-handling strain injuries caused 64 lost working days last year, costing £9,200 in temporary agency cover. Furthermore, trials showed lorry turnaround times dropped from 45 minutes to 22 minutes. The investment pays for itself within 14 months while protecting staff wellbeing.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Always follow a statistic with 'which means that...' to translate data into real-world impact.
🔄 Quick Check: Comparing the hidden cost of staff injury leave (£9,200/year) against the equipment price creates an unarguable Level 2 business case.
Question 6 (Level 2 Mastery Challenge — 3 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
During a discussion on workplace security, a colleague says: 'Replacing our lost visitor paper book with an electronic tablet sign-in costs £600—that's a waste of money.'
Deliver a 3-sentence counter-argument using legal/regulatory evidence (GDPR & fire safety) to prove why the £600 tablet is necessary.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Acknowledge the £600 cost. [M1]
2. Cite GDPR data protection law (open paper visitor books let anyone read previous visitors' names, phone numbers, and car registrations, which breaches GDPR). [M1]
3. Cite fire safety compliance (instant cloud evacuation roll-call access on a warden's phone). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'While £600 is an upfront outlay, our current open paper visitor book leaves every visitor's full name, mobile number, and vehicle registration visible to the public, which directly breaches GDPR privacy regulations. An electronic tablet encrypts visitor details immediately and prints a photo lanyard badge. Furthermore, during a fire alarm, wardens can access the live digital visitor list on their phones at the assembly point in seconds.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Failing to proofread spelling, punctuation, and grammar after completing the draft.
🔄 Quick Check: Whenever a peer objects on cost, counter with GDPR data protection or Fire Safety compliance—legal requirements always trump minor costs!
