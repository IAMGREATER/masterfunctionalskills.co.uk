Entry Level 3 Specification & Learning Outcomes (E3.SLC.1 – E3.SLC.7)

Regulated Ofqual Entry Level 3 English outcomes (Open Awards, Pearson Edexcel, City & Guilds & NCFE):

  • E3.SLC.1 & E3.SLC.2 – Identify relevant information from explanations/presentations and make requests / ask concise questions.
  • E3.SLC.3, E3.SLC.4 & E3.SLC.5 – Communicate information and opinions clearly on familiar topics and respond appropriately to questions.
  • E3.SLC.6 & E3.SLC.7 – Follow the main points of discussions, respect turn-taking rights, and make relevant contributions.
Entry Level 3 Focus: Taking part confidently in two relaxed, supportive Entry Level 3 group discussions (~5–10 minutes each in a small group of 3–4 learners) on everyday topics, asking and answering simple questions politely.

Core Entry Level 3 Golden Rules: Entry Level 3 Speaking, Listening & Communicating Guide

Golden Rule 1: Entry 3 SLC Is Friendly & Supportive—No Scary Solo Lecture Required!

Most awarding bodies assess Entry Level 3 through two short small-group discussions (one informal familiar topic, one straightforward workplace/community topic) where you share views, ask questions, and listen.

Entry Level 3 Key Rule: Check your tutor brief—you can keep 3–4 short keyword prompts in front of you!
Golden Rule 2: Ask at Least Two Clear Questions & Answer in Full Sentences

Show E3.SLC.2 and E3.SLC.5 by asking group members simple questions ('Which day works best for you?') and replying with more than just 'Yes/No'.

Entry Level 3 Key Rule: 'Yes, Saturday morning works well for me because I'm off work.'
Golden Rule 3: Take Turns Politely & Stay on the Topic

Wait until another learner finishes their sentence before speaking, nod to show you are listening, and bring your points back to the topic.

Entry Level 3 Key Rule: Polite phrases like 'I agree with Sam because...' work brilliantly!

Core Skill 1: Sharing Clear Points & Opinions (E3.SLC.3 & E3.SLC.4)

Use 'I think... because...' on familiar everyday topics.

Give a Simple Reason
'I think the community park needs better lighting because it gets dark at 16:30 in winter.'
Stay Clear & Audible
Speak at a steady pace so everyone in the room or video call can hear you clearly.

Common Entry Level 3 Examiner Traps to Avoid

Entry Level 3 Examiner Trap: Only Answering With One Word ('Yes' / 'Fine')
Add one short 'because...' reason so your assessor can hear clear spoken communication.
Entry Level 3 Examiner Trap: Forgetting to Ask Any Questions
Make a mental note to ask at least TWO questions across the two short discussions!

Interactive Entry Level 3 Exam Practice Questions

Entry Level 3 Practice 1: Add a Reason & Question 2 Marks
In a discussion on 'Ways to Stay Healthy on a Budget', upgrade 'Walking is good' by adding a reason AND a question for your group.
Entry Level 3 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
Opinion + reason (1 Mark): 'I think brisk walking for 30 minutes a day is great because it's completely free and boosts your mood.'
Question (1 Mark): 'Does anyone use a free step-counter app on their phone?'
Final Answer: 'Walking for 30 minutes a day is great because it costs nothing and keeps you fit—does anyone use a step-counter app?'
Entry Level 3 Practice 2: Polite Turn-Taking 1 Marks
What should you do if you and another learner start speaking at the exact same moment?
Entry Level 3 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
Polite action (1 Mark): Smile, pause, and say 'Sorry—please go ahead first, I'll come in after you!'
Final Answer: Pause politely and invite the other speaker to go ahead first.
