Step-by-Step Practical Guide

1
1. Reading the Grid, Formula Bar & Fixing Clipped ### Columns

Columns run top-to-bottom by Letter (A, B, C...); Rows run left-to-right by Number (1, 2, 3...). Where they meet is a Cell Reference such as B4.

  • Click once on a cell and look at the white fx Formula Bar near the top — the grid cell displays the finished number (£29.70), whereas the Formula Bar reveals the real formula behind it (=B4*C4)!
  • If a column shows ### or cuts off words, double-click the dividing line between column letters (e.g. between C and D) to auto-fit column width instantly.
Tutor Tip: To merge a title across A1:D1 cleanly, highlight A1:D1 and click Merge & Centre.
2
2. Typing Core Formulas & Dragging with the Fill Handle (+)

EVERY formula starts with an equals sign (=) — NEVER use your phone calculator and type the plain answer by hand!

  • Multiply / Subtract / Divide / Add: Click answer cell → type =B5*C5 (multiply uses star *, divide uses slash /) → press Enter.
  • Drag Formulas Down (Fill Handle): Click the finished formula cell once, hover over the tiny solid square at its bottom-right corner until your cursor turns into a thin black cross (+), then hold Left-Click and drag down cleanly! Excel/Calc updates row numbers (B6*C6, B7*C7...) automatically.
  • Range Functions: =SUM(D5:D9) adds; =AVERAGE(D5:D9) finds the mean; =MIN(...) lowest; =MAX(...) highest; =COUNT(...) counts numeric entries; =IF(C5<B5,"Reorder","OK") tests a condition.
Tutor Tip: Remember: ranges use a colon (D5:D9 means 'from D5 through D9'), NOT a minus sign or comma!
3
3. Formatting (£), Sorting Whole Tables & Moving Charts to a New Sheet Tab

These three practical steps catch out learners in exams — follow them word for word:

  1. Currency Formatting: Highlight money cells → click the £ Currency icon (ensuring 2 decimal places like £4.50, not 4.5).
  2. Safe Table Sorting (Never Sort 1 Column Alone!): Highlight the ENTIRE table including headers (e.g. A4:D9 — leave out any Grand Total row!) → click Data → Sort → ensure Range contains column labels / My data has headers is ticked → choose sort column and Ascending/Descending.
  3. Inserting a Chart on a NEW Sheet Tab: Highlight category names (e.g. A4:A9), hold Ctrl while highlighting values (e.g. D4:D9) — DO NOT include the Total row! Click Insert → Column / Bar Chart, add a clear Chart Title + Axis Labels, then move/place it onto a new sheet tab (in Excel: Chart Design → Move Chart → New sheet; in Calc: cut chart Ctrl+X, click + bottom-left to add a new sheet, double-click tab to rename Sales Chart, and Ctrl+V paste!).
Tutor Tip: Review all 6 starter workbooks inside the downloadable Practice Pack above — each workbook has its own built-in Instructions tab.

Three Common Beginner Traps

Avoid Trap: Sorting only Column D without selecting Columns A–C

Breaks the rows apart so prices and totals end up next to the wrong item! Always highlight the entire table (excluding Totals).

Avoid Trap: Including the Grand Total row inside a Bar/Column chart

Creates one giant 'Total' bar that squashes every real item into tiny slivers — exclude Totals before clicking Insert > Chart.

Avoid Trap: Typing plain answers instead of = formulas

Even if your arithmetic looks right, hardcoded numbers don't update dynamically and miss spreadsheet formula requirements.
