Section A: Core Skills (Warm-Up) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Ordering & Subtracting Decimals Without a Calculator) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A laboratory digital scale records three chemical sample weights: 4.08 g, 4.8 g, and 4.085 g.
Write the weights in order from smallest to largest, and calculate the difference between the largest and smallest weight.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Pad to 3 decimal places (4.080, 4.800, 4.085) → Order: 4.08 g, 4.085 g, 4.8 g [M1]
2. Difference (largest minus smallest): 4.800 - 4.080 = 0.72 g [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Order: 4.08 g, 4.085 g, 4.8 g | Difference: 0.72 g
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Assuming 4.085 is larger than 4.8 because it has more digits.
🔄 Quick Check: 4.08 + 0.72 = 4.80 g.
Question 2 (Level 1 Foundation — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Arrange these four parcel weights in ascending order (lightest to heaviest): 2.4 kg, 2.08 kg, 2.45 kg, 2.39 kg.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Pad to 2 decimal places: 2.40, 2.08, 2.45, 2.39. [M1]
2. Order from smallest to largest: 2.08 kg, 2.39 kg, 2.4 kg, 2.45 kg. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 2.08 kg, 2.39 kg, 2.4 kg, 2.45 kg
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Failing to convert 85p into £0.85 before adding to £2.65.
🔄 Quick Check: Writing 2.4 as 2.40 makes it easy to see that 2.39 is lighter than 2.40.
Section B: Everyday & Workplace Maths Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Care Home Tea Club Petty Cash Receipt & Change (Entry 3 / Level 1 Foundation)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A care home activity worker buys biscuits for £1.45, tea bags for £2.80, and milk for 95p.
(a) Calculate the total cost in pounds (£).
(b) How much change will she receive from a £10.00 note?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Convert 95p to £0.95 and add all three items: £1.45 + £2.80 + £0.95 = £5.20. [M1]
2. Subtract £5.20 from £10.00: £10.00 − £5.20 = £4.80. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) Total = £5.20 | (b) Change = £4.80
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Convert every pence figure into £0.XX before starting your calculation.
🔄 Quick Check: Always convert pence (95p) into pounds (£0.95) BEFORE adding to pound amounts!
Question 4 (Workplace Application — 2 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Round £27.46:
(a) To the nearest whole pound (£1).
(b) To 1 decimal place (nearest 10p).
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Nearest £1: tenths digit is 4 (< 5), so it rounds down to £27.00. [M1]
2. Nearest 10p (1 dp): hundredths digit is 6 (≥ 5), so 4 tenths rounds up to 5 tenths → £27.50. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) £27.00 (or £27) | (b) £27.50 (or 27.5)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: £0.5 is £0.50 (50 pence)! Single-digit pence require a zero after the decimal point.
🔄 Quick Check: When rounding to the nearest £1, look only at the tenths digit (4), not the 6!
Section C: Exam-Style Problem Solving Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Hospital Staff Canteen Meal Deal Comparison (Level 1/2 Multi-Step)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A hospital canteen sells items individually:
• Sandwich: £2.65 | Fruit Pot: £1.35 | Bottled Water: 85p
• A "Healthy Shift Meal Deal" includes all three items for £4.20.
If a nurse buys this meal deal every shift for 5 shifts, how much money does she save in total compared to buying the items separately?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Convert 85p to £0.85 and sum the separate item prices: £2.65 + £1.35 + £0.85 = £4.85 per day. [M1]
2. Calculate daily saving with the £4.20 Meal Deal: £4.85 − £4.20 = £0.65 (65p) saved per shift. [M1]
3. Multiply daily saving by 5 shifts: 5 × £0.65 = £3.25. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £3.25 saved over 5 shifts
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Remember: 10p = £0.10, while 1p = £0.01.
🔄 Quick Check: Convert 85p to £0.85 first so you never add 85 whole pounds by mistake!
Question 6 (Level 2 Multi-Step Problem Solving — 3 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A receptionist buys 3 packs of pens at £1.75 each and 1 stapler for £3.40. How much change does he get from a £10.00 note?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Pens = 3 × £1.75 = £5.25. [M1]
2. Total spent = £5.25 + £3.40 = £8.65. [P1]
3. Change = £10.00 − £8.65 = £1.35. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £1.35
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Stating a conclusion without showing the full numerical comparison values.
🔄 Quick Check: Check: £8.65 + £1.35 = £10.00.
