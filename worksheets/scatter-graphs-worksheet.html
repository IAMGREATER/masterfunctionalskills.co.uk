Top Tip for Scatter Graphs, Correlation & Lines of Best Fit: Golden Rule 1: Positive (Upward) • Negative (Downward) • Zero (Random): Positive Correlation: As X increases, Y increases (points slope upwards left-to-right). Negative Correlation: As X increases, Y decreases (points slope downwards left-to-right). Zero Correlation: No relationship (random cloud).
