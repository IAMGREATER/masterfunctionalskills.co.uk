Section A: Core Skills (The Basics) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Level 1 Extracting Information from an Image Caption) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A workplace fire safety leaflet includes a photograph of a silver CO2 fire extinguisher with a black horn nozzle. Beneath the photo, the caption reads:
'Figure 2: Carbon Dioxide (CO2) extinguishers (black label) are safe for live electrical server fires but must NEVER be used on chip-pan grease fires.'

Using the caption, state: (a) what colour label a CO2 extinguisher has, and (b) which type of fire it must never be used on.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. (a) Extract label colour from brackets in caption: Black label [A1]
2. (b) Extract prohibited fire type: Chip-pan grease fires [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) Black label; (b) Chip-pan grease fires.
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Answering 'silver' (the cylinder body colour in the stem) instead of 'black label' as stated in the caption.
🔄 Quick Check: Check the exact bracketed text '(black label)' and 'chip-pan grease fires' in the caption.
Question 2 (Level 1 Explaining the Purpose of an Instructional Diagram) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A flat-pack office desk manual includes a step-by-step line diagram showing how Cam Lock A twists 90 degrees clockwise using a crosshead screwdriver to lock Pin B into the wooden side panel.

Give two reasons why the writer included this diagram alongside the written instructions.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Visual clarity: Shows the exact physical orientation and 90-degree clockwise twist of Cam Lock A and Pin B more clearly than words alone [A1]
2. Error prevention: Helps the person assembling the desk identify the correct fittings and avoid damaging the wooden panel [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1. Visually shows how Cam Lock A and Pin B fit together and turn 90 degrees. 2. Prevents assembly mistakes and damage.
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Writing vague answers like 'it makes the manual look nice' or 'fills up space'.
🔄 Quick Check: Does your answer refer specifically to assembling the desk parts (Cam Lock A / Pin B)?
Section B: Real-Life & Workplace Practice Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1/2 Analysing Emotive Photographs in Charity Appeals) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A winter homelessness appeal leaflet features the headline 'No One Should Freeze on Our Streets Tonight' alongside a black-and-white photograph of an elderly man huddled in a doorway under a thin wet blanket in the snow.

Explain how this photograph supports the headline and helps achieve the leaflet's purpose. (3 marks)
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Identify specific visual detail: Shows a vulnerable elderly man shivering in snow under a thin wet blanket (sombre black-and-white tone) [A1]
2. Identify emotional impact: Provokes immediate empathy, sympathy, shock, and moral guilt in the reader [A1]
3. Link to headline & purpose: Visually proves the urgency of 'No One Should Freeze' and persuades readers to donate money immediately [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: The photo of a vulnerable elderly man in the snow reinforces the headline 'No One Should Freeze', evoking sympathy and urgency that persuades readers to donate.
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Simply stating 'it shows a homeless man' without explaining the emotional response (sympathy/urgency) and fundraising purpose.
🔄 Quick Check: Visual detail + Emotional effect + Link to donating = 3 marks.
Question 4 (Level 2 Interpreting Data from a Embedded Bar Chart) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A council waste report includes a bar chart titled 'Household Recycling Rates by Ward (%)':
• Oakfield Ward: 64% (Green bar)
• Central Ward: 28% (Red bar)
• Riverside Ward: 51% (Amber bar)
Caption: 'Target statutory minimum across all wards: 50%.'

Using the chart and caption, identify which ward is failing the statutory target, by how many percentage points it falls short, and explain why the designer colour-coded the bars.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Identify failing ward: Central Ward (28% is below the 50% statutory minimum in the caption) [A1]
2. Calculate exact shortfall: 50% - 28% = 22 percentage points (22%) short [A1]
3. Explain colour coding: Traffic-light colours (red/amber/green) allow readers to spot failing (red) vs high-performing (green) wards at a glance [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Central Ward fails by 22 percentage points (28% vs 50% target); traffic-light colours let readers spot underperforming wards instantly.
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Missing the 50% target printed in the caption or forgetting to explain the red/amber/green colour coding.
🔄 Quick Check: 50 - 28 = 22%; Central Ward is the red bar.
Section C: Exam-Style Tasks Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Evaluating Dual Images — Before and After Contrast) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
An advert for ' HydroClean Patio Restoration' displays two side-by-side photographs:
• Photo 1 (Left — Labelled 'BEFORE'): Dull, black moss-covered paving slabs with slippery green algae.
• Photo 2 (Right — Labelled 'AFTER 1 HOUR'): Bright, clean golden sandstone slabs gleaming in the sun.

Analyse how the company uses these paired images and labels to persuade homeowners to book their service.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Visual contrast: Places the dirty, slippery moss-covered slabs directly next to the gleaming golden sandstone to dramatise the transformation [M1]
2. Evidence of effectiveness: Provides tangible visual proof that the cleaning treatment works thoroughly [A1]
3. Impact of the label ('AFTER 1 HOUR'): Emphasises how rapid and effortless the results are [A1]
4. Overall persuasive impact: Builds customer trust and desire to transform their own garden patio [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Side-by-side 'BEFORE' and 'AFTER 1 HOUR' photos dramatise the contrast between slippery moss and gleaming stone, proving the service is both fast (1 hour) and highly effective.
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Describing only one photo instead of explaining the persuasive power of the side-by-side 'Before vs After' contrast.
🔄 Quick Check: Did you mention both the visual transformation AND the '1 HOUR' speed label?
Question 6 (Level 2 Critiquing Image Suitability for Audience & Purpose) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A college is designing a leaflet to recruit mature adults aged 50+ onto a beginner's Digital Skills & Online Banking course. The draft leaflet includes two possible cover images:
• Image Option A: A cartoon graphic of a gaming teenager wearing neon VR goggles surrounded by binary code.
• Image Option B: A photograph of a smiling woman in her sixties comfortably using a tablet at a kitchen table with a cup of tea, captioned: 'Master everyday online banking safely at your own pace.'

Evaluate why Image Option B is suitable for the target audience and purpose, and why Image Option A is unsuitable.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Evaluate Option B (Audience match): Shows a relatable adult in her sixties in a calm home setting, directly mirroring the 50+ target demographic [M1]
2. Evaluate Option B (Purpose & Caption): The tablet and reassuring caption ('safely at your own pace') reduce tech anxiety and match the online banking topic [A1]
3. Critique Option A (Audience mismatch): A teenager in neon VR gaming goggles alienates older learners and makes them feel the course is not for them [M1]
4. Critique Option A (Topic mismatch): VR gaming and binary code look overly complex and intimidating rather than practical everyday banking [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Option B is suitable because it shows a relatable older learner in a relaxed setting with a reassuring caption ('at your own pace'). Option A is unsuitable because a teenager in VR goggles alienates 50+ adults and makes the course look intimidating and irrelevant.
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Only praising Option B without explaining why Option A fails the target audience.
🔄 Quick Check: Check that both age demographic (50+) and course subject (everyday online banking) are addressed.
