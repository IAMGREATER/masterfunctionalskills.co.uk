Section A: Core Skills (The Basics) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Eliminating Verbal Filler and Structuring a Clear Announcement) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Rewrite this hesitant, filler-heavy spoken announcement into a crisp, authoritative two-sentence briefing:
'Um, hi guys, basically the fire alarm test is like happening at ten o'clock today, so yeah, don't panic or leave the building unless it rings for more than thirty seconds.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Strip all verbal fillers ('Um', 'basically', 'like', 'so yeah') and informal 'hi guys' [A1]
2. State the exact time (10:00) and clear 30-second evacuation threshold [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'Good morning everyone. Please note that a routine fire alarm test will take place at 10:00 today; you do not need to evacuate unless the siren sounds continuously for more than thirty seconds.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Omitting the crucial 30-second safety rule when shortening the announcement.
🔄 Quick Check: Zero filler words; clear time and action threshold.
Question 2 (Core Skill Application — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Rewrite this rambling, filler-heavy spoken announcement so it is clear, concise, and professional for a GP surgery waiting room:
'Um, excuse me everyone, basically Dr Evans is running like half an hour late this morning because there was an emergency callout, so yeah, sorry about that, if you can't wait just tell the desk.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Remove all filler words ('Um', 'basically', 'like', 'so yeah'). [M1]
2. Structure clearly: Polite greeting → Factual reason & 30-minute delay → Actionable option at reception. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'Good morning everyone, may I have your attention please. Due to an urgent medical callout, Dr Evans's morning clinic is currently running approximately thirty minutes behind schedule. We sincerely apologise for the delay; if you are unable to wait, please speak to our reception team to rebook your appointment for a convenient time.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Repeating 'like' or 'basically' every five words weakens your authority and distracts the assessor from the substance of your argument.
🔄 Quick Check: Always state the exact delay time ('thirty minutes') and give listeners a clear next step.
Section B: Real-Life & Workplace Practice Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Model 1 (Level 1): Care Assistant Shift Handover Briefing) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Deliver a clear 45-second spoken handover informing the incoming afternoon care assistant that Resident Mrs Patel (Room 14) had a minor uninjured fall yesterday, requires a two-person walking transfer until 15:00, and has an updated chart on the tablet.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Identify the resident, room number, and background clearly: 'Before we start the afternoon round, please note an important update for Mrs Patel in Room 14, who had a minor uninjured slip yesterday evening.' [A1]
2. State the exact mandatory safety instruction: 'Until the physiotherapist reviews her at 15:00 today, Mrs Patel requires two care assistants to support her whenever she transfers from her chair to her walking frame.' [A1]
3. Confirm documentation location: 'I have logged her morning observations on the ward tablet under Room 14. Do you have any questions before we begin?' [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'Before we start the afternoon round, please note an important update for Mrs Patel in Room 14, who had a minor uninjured slip yesterday evening. Until the physiotherapist reviews her at 15:00 today, she requires two care assistants to support her whenever she transfers to her walking frame. I have updated her mobility chart on the ward tablet.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Slow your speaking pace by 15%. Whenever you need to think of your next word, close your mouth and take a calm breath instead of saying 'um'.
🔄 Quick Check: In healthcare handovers, stating Who (Mrs Patel, Room 14), Why (minor fall yesterday), and What action (2-person assist until 15:00) in chronological order prevents errors.
Question 4 (Exam-Style Task — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Use the 4-step SBAR framework (Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation) to write a 50-word spoken telephone report to your IT Helpdesk explaining that the main reception card-payment terminal has frozen.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Situation: Identify yourself and state that the main reception PDQ card machine has frozen. [M1]
2. Background: Explain that it displayed 'Error 404' at 09:15 and restarting the router did not fix it. [M1]
3. Assessment: Customers cannot pay by card and queues are building. [M1]
4. Recommendation: Request an urgent replacement handset from IT stores. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'Hello IT Support, this is Sam at Main Reception. Our primary card-payment terminal has frozen completely. It displayed Error 404 at 09:15 this morning, and rebooting the terminal has not resolved the fault. Consequently, we cannot process customer card payments and a queue is building. Could a technician bring out the spare replacement terminal within the next fifteen minutes please?'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Ending statements with '...so yeah, that's basically it' or letting your voice drop to a mumble makes your conclusion sound weak.
🔄 Quick Check: SBAR works in every industry—it gives your listener all the facts they need in four logical sentences.
Section C: Exam-Style Tasks Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Model 2 (Level 2): Delivering a Health & Safety Depot Toolbox Briefing) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Deliver a 60-second verbal briefing to forklift operators regarding newly designated pedestrian safety walkways in Bay 3, indicating where vocal emphasis and pauses should be used.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Opening Hook & Clear Purpose: 'Good morning team. Please give me your full attention for sixty seconds on a critical safety update regarding Loading Bay 3.' [A1]
2. Key Factual Directive with Vocal Stress & Pause: 'As of 06:00 today, the yellow cross-hatched lanes are strictly pedestrian-only. [Pause for 2 seconds] Forklift trucks must not cross or park on these lines under any circumstances.' [A1]
3. Actionable Consequence & Sign-Off: 'Breaching this walkway zone triggers an automatic HSE incident review. Please sign the briefing register on the clipboard before collecting your vehicle keys. Thank you.' [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'Good morning team. Please give me your full attention for sixty seconds on a critical safety update regarding Loading Bay 3. As of 06:00 today, the yellow cross-hatched lanes are strictly pedestrian-only. [2-second pause] Forklift trucks must not cross or park on these lines under any circumstances. Breaching this policy triggers an automatic HSE incident review. Please sign the briefing register before collecting your keys.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Finish every speaking turn with a complete, crisp sentence or a direct handover question.
🔄 Quick Check: Notice how pausing for 2 seconds right after 'strictly pedestrian-only' makes the safety rule sink in immediately.
Question 6 (Level 2 Mastery Challenge — 3 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Look at this sentence from a formal presentation:
'Last year, avoidable packaging waste cost our distribution centre forty-two thousand pounds—enough to fund two full-time apprenticeships.'
Explain two specific vocal techniques (pace, pause, volume, or emphasis) you would use when speaking this sentence aloud to maximise its impact on the audience.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Technique 1 (Vocal Emphasis & Slowed Pace): Slow down and stress the words 'forty-two thousand pounds' to highlight the scale of the financial loss. [M1]
2. Technique 2 (Deliberate Pause): Pause for two full seconds at the dash before delivering 'enough to fund two full-time apprenticeships'. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1. Slow the speaking pace and place strong vocal stress on each word of 'forty-two thousand pounds' so the financial figure stands out clearly.
2. Insert a deliberate two-second pause at the dash before delivering the comparison ('enough to fund two full-time apprenticeships') to let the audience absorb the human impact.
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Failing to proofread spelling, punctuation, and grammar after completing the draft.
🔄 Quick Check: Numbers and statistics always land harder when you slow down and pause right after stating them.
