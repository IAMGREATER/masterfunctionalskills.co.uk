Specification & Learning Outcomes

Candidates must demonstrate mastery of the following regulated Functional Skills Mathematics outcomes:

  • L2.30 – Estimate the mean of a grouped frequency distribution from discrete or continuous data using class midpoints.

Golden Rules for Estimating the Mean from Grouped Data

Golden Rule 1: The 4-Step Grouped Mean Formula (Σfx ÷ Σf)

1. Find the Midpoint (x) of each group: (Lower + Upper) ÷ 2. 2. Multiply each Midpoint by its Frequency: f × x = fx. 3. Add up the fx column (Σfx). 4. Divide Σfx by the Total Frequency (Σf).

Golden Rule / Method: Estimated Mean = Total fx (Σfx) ÷ Total Frequency (Σf)
Golden Rule 2: NEVER Divide by the Number of Rows/Groups!

If there are 4 class intervals and 50 total patients, you MUST divide by 50 (the total frequency Σf), NEVER by 4! Your final answer must lie comfortably inside the lowest and highest group boundaries.

Golden Rule / Method: Divide by Total Frequency (Σf), NEVER by Number of Rows

Core Skill 1: Finding Class Midpoints (x) & Why It Is an Estimate

Averaging the lower and upper bounds of each interval.

To find the midpoint of any interval, add the two boundary numbers and divide by 2:

  • 0 < t ≤ 15: (0 + 15) ÷ 2 = 7.5
  • 15 < t ≤ 30: (15 + 30) ÷ 2 = 22.5
  • Why is it an "Estimate"? We do not know the exact individual values inside each group, so we assume every item in a group equals that group's midpoint.

Core Skill 3: Unequal Class Widths & Comparing Estimated Means to Targets

Handling wider final intervals (e.g. 40 to 80) and evaluating SLA performance.

  • Watch Out for Unequal Intervals: Exam tables often widen the last row (e.g. 0–10, 10–20, 20–40, 40–80). Never guess midpoints by pattern—always compute (Lower + Upper) ÷ 2 for every single row!
  • Plausibility Check: If your groups run from 0 to 80, your estimated mean MUST be between 0 and 80.

Pearson Edexcel Step-by-Step Worked Models

Benchmark Exam Model 1 4 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 2)

Vocational Context: Residential Care Quality Auditing

A care home audits the response times in seconds for 40 emergency call bells:
• 0 to 30s: 6 calls
• 30 to 60s: 14 calls
• 60 to 90s: 12 calls
• 90 to 120s: 8 calls
Calculate an estimate of the mean call bell response time.
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Find the midpoint (x) of each interval
0–30: 15s | 30–60: 45s | 60–90: 75s | 90–120: 105s.
Criteria: M1: Correct class midpoints
Step 2 Multiply each Midpoint by Frequency (fx)
(15 × 6 = 90) + (45 × 14 = 630) + (75 × 12 = 900) + (105 × 8 = 840).
Criteria: M1: Multiplying midpoints by frequencies
Step 3 Sum fx (Σfx) and verify total frequency (Σf)
Σfx = 90 + 630 + 900 + 840 = 2,460.
Total frequency Σf = 6 + 14 + 12 + 8 = 40 calls.
Criteria: M1: Summing fx (2,460)
Step 4 Divide Σfx by Σf
Estimated Mean = 2,460 ÷ 40 = 61.5 seconds.
Criteria: A1: Accurate estimated mean (61.5s)
Final Accredited Answer: 61.5 seconds
Method Checklist & Marking Scheme Guidance
61.5 seconds sits right in the middle of the 0–120s range, confirming the calculation is sensible.
Benchmark Exam Model 2 4 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 2)

Vocational Context: Hospital Outpatient Pharmacy SLA Audit

A hospital pharmacy charter states that the mean prescription waiting time must be under 18 minutes. An audit of 50 prescriptions shows:
• 0 < t ≤ 10 mins: 12 prescriptions
• 10 < t ≤ 20 mins: 23 prescriptions
• 20 < t ≤ 30 mins: 10 prescriptions
• 30 < t ≤ 50 mins: 5 prescriptions
Calculate an estimate of the mean waiting time and state whether the pharmacy met its charter target.
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Calculate midpoints (noting the wider 30–50 final interval!)
Midpoints: 5, 15, 25, and (30 + 50) ÷ 2 = 40 mins.
Criteria: M1: Accurate midpoints including 40
Step 2 Compute fx and sum Σfx
(12 × 5 = 60) + (23 × 15 = 345) + (10 × 25 = 250) + (5 × 40 = 200) = 855.
Criteria: M1: Accurate Σfx = 855
Step 3 Divide by total prescriptions (50) and compare with 18 mins
Estimated Mean = 855 ÷ 50 = 17.1 minutes.
Since 17.1 mins < 18 mins, YES, the pharmacy met its charter target.
Criteria: M1 / A1: 17.1 minutes and valid YES conclusion
Final Accredited Answer: Estimated Mean = 17.1 minutes — Yes, the pharmacy met its target (< 18 mins)
Method Checklist & Marking Scheme Guidance
Watch the last interval carefully: 30 to 50 has a width of 20, so its midpoint is 40, not 35!

Critical Examiner Traps & Common Pitfalls

Examiner Pitfall: Dividing by the Number of Groups (4) Instead of Total Frequency (40)
Computing 2,460 ÷ 4 = 615 seconds is impossible because no call lasted longer than 120 seconds!
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Always cross-out the midpoint total—only sum the Frequency (f) column and the (fx) column.
Examiner Pitfall: Dividing Total Frequency by Number of Groups
Doing 40 ÷ 4 = 10 completely ignores the time intervals and scores 0 marks.
How to Avoid This Pitfall: You must calculate Midpoint × Frequency for every row.

Interactive Exam Practice Questions

Exam Practice Question 1 4 Marks
A courier depot records the weights of 25 parcels:
• 0 < w ≤ 2 kg: 5 parcels
• 2 < w ≤ 4 kg: 12 parcels
• 4 < w ≤ 6 kg: 8 parcels
Calculate an estimate of the mean parcel weight.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Midpoints: 1 kg, 3 kg, 5 kg.
2. fx: (5 × 1 = 5) + (12 × 3 = 36) + (8 × 5 = 40) = 81 kg.
3. Total frequency = 25.
4. Estimated Mean = 81 ÷ 25 = 3.24 kg.
Final Correct Answer: 3.24 kg
Examiner Tip: Sum fx = 81; divide by 25 parcels = 3.24 kg.
Exam Practice Question 2 4 Marks
A solar array logs daily energy output over 30 days:
• 10 to 20 kWh: 6 days
• 20 to 30 kWh: 15 days
• 30 to 40 kWh: 9 days
Calculate an estimate of the mean daily energy output.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Midpoints: 15, 25, 35.
2. fx: (6 × 15 = 90) + (15 × 25 = 375) + (9 × 35 = 315) = 780 kWh.
3. Estimated Mean = 780 ÷ 30 = 26 kWh.
Final Correct Answer: 26 kWh
Examiner Tip: 780 ÷ 30 = 26 kWh.
Exam Practice Question 3 4 Marks
A call center audits 80 customer calls:
• 0 < t ≤ 4 mins: 20 calls
• 4 < t ≤ 8 mins: 36 calls
• 8 < t ≤ 12 mins: 16 calls
• 12 < t ≤ 20 mins: 8 calls
Calculate an estimate of the mean call duration in minutes.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Midpoints: 2, 6, 10, 16 (note 12 to 20 midpoint is 16!).
2. fx: (20 × 2 = 40) + (36 × 6 = 216) + (16 × 10 = 160) + (8 × 16 = 128) = 544.
3. Estimated Mean = 544 ÷ 80 = 6.8 minutes.
Final Correct Answer: 6.8 minutes
Examiner Tip: Check the final group (12 to 20): (12 + 20) ÷ 2 = 16.
