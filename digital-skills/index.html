The 5 Statutory DFSQ Skill Areas

Both Entry 3 and Level 1 qualifications assess learners across the five statutory UK Department for Education digital skill areas:

Area 1: Using Devices

Hardware, OS, Wi-Fi, hierarchies, troubleshooting & backups.

Area 2: Creating & Editing

Word processing, formulas, spreadsheets, charts & slides.

Area 3: Communicating

Professional email, CC/BCC, calendars, video calls & cloud sharing.

Area 4: Transacting

Online forms, shopping, MFA banking & document portal uploads.

Area 5: Being Safe Online

Passwords, malware defence, phishing, UK GDPR & ergonomics.
