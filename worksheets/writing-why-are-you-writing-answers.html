Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Core Skills (The Basics) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Matching Language Choices to Persuasive vs Informative Purpose) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Write one informative sentence and one persuasive sentence about a hospital's new Staff Cycle-to-Work Scheme.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Informative sentence (neutral facts): e.g. 'The Staff Cycle-to-Work Scheme launches on 1 May and provides up to £1,000 towards a new bicycle.' [A1]
2. Persuasive sentence (direct address/benefits): e.g. 'Join the scheme today to slash your commuting costs by 32% and beat morning traffic jams!' [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Informative: The scheme launches on 1 May with grants up to £1,000. | Persuasive: Sign up today to save 32% on a brand-new bike and transform your morning commute!
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Using emotional exclamation marks in a neutral informative notice.
🔄 Quick Check: Informative = factual and neutral; Persuasive = compelling and benefit-led.
Question 2 (Core Skill Application — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Identify the primary purpose (To Persuade / To Inform / To Instruct) of each sentence below:
1. 'Isolate the mains electricity switch before removing the inspection cover.'
2. 'Can our community afford to lose its only youth sports field to another luxury housing development?'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Sentence 1 uses an imperative command verb ('Isolate') for a safety step → To Instruct. [M1]
2. Sentence 2 uses an emotive rhetorical question to rally opposition → To Persuade. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1. To Instruct | 2. To Persuade
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: If a prompt asks you to write an informational guide explaining a new recycling system, filling it with emotional exaggeration and exclamation marks misjudges the purpose.
🔄 Quick Check: Imperative verbs ('Isolate', 'Wash', 'Press') always signal instructional writing; rhetorical questions signal persuasive writing.
Section B: Real-Life & Workplace Practice Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 Model: Advisory Email to a New Apprentice Starting Next Week) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Write a supportive 3-paragraph email to a new administrative apprentice, Leo, advising him on what to bring on his first day (Monday 9 November at 09:00), dress code, and lunch arrangements.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Warm welcome and clear arrival information (Inform): State start time (09:00 on Monday 9 November) and who to ask for at reception. [A1]
2. Practical advice on dress code and ID documents (Advise): Use supportive modal verbs ('you should wear', 'please remember to bring') for smart-casual attire and passport/ID. [A1]
3. Reassuring guidance on lunch and first-day buddy support: Explain kitchen facilities and reassure Leo that his mentor will guide him through the day. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: To: leo.davies@email.co.uk
Subject: Welcome to Oakfield Medical Practice – First Day Advice (Monday 9 November)

Dear Leo,

We are delighted that you are joining our administration team next Monday, 9 November. Please arrive at the main reception desk at 09:00, where I will meet you and issue your security pass.

For your first day, we advise wearing smart-casual office clothing, such as tailored trousers and a collared shirt. You should also bring your passport or driving licence so that HR can complete your identification check.

Finally, our staff kitchen has a fridge and microwave if you wish to bring a packed lunch, or we can visit the café across the road together during your break. Please reply if you have any questions at all!

Best wishes,
Hannah Reed, Senior Administrator
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Match your emotional temperature to the prompt verb: 'Inform' and 'Explain' require calm, factual authority.
🔄 Quick Check: Notice how the tone is warm, practical, and reassuring—perfectly matching the 'Advise and Inform' purpose.
Question 4 (Exam-Style Task — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Write a 2-sentence counter-argument and rebuttal for a persuasive letter to your manager asking to allow administrative staff to work from home one day a week.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Sentence 1 (Acknowledge the manager's likely objection): Mention concern about telephone cover or productivity. [M1]
2. Sentence 2 (Rebut with evidence): Explain how cloud phone forwarding and rotating rota days maintain 100% office cover while boosting output. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'Some managers may be concerned that allowing remote working one day per week could leave the main reception telephone lines understaffed; however, rotating home-working days across the five-person team ensures four administrators remain on site at all times. Furthermore, our cloud telephone system routes calls seamlessly to staff laptops, guaranteeing uninterrupted client service.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Writing 'According to a survey, 99.9% of people hate buses' sounds childish and unconvincing.
🔄 Quick Check: Using 'Some may be concerned that...; however,...' shows the examiner you can evaluate multiple viewpoints at Level 2.
Section C: Exam-Style Tasks Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Model: NHS Smoking Cessation Campaign Leaflet (Advise & Persuade)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Write the concluding two paragraphs of a public health leaflet intended to ADVISE and PERSUADE long-term smokers to enrol in a free local NHS stop-smoking clinic.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Combine empathetic advisory guidance with clinical evidence: Acknowledge how challenging quitting is alone, then cite the 400% higher success rate with specialist support. [A1]
2. Deploy Rule of Three and financial/health incentives: Highlight saving £2,000 a year and improving breathing within 72 hours. [A1]
3. Deliver a clear, motivating call to action: Provide the free helpline number and drop-in clinic hours. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'Quitting smoking is undeniably challenging, but you do not have to face cravings through willpower alone. We strongly recommend booking a free 15-minute consultation with our friendly NHS Stop Smoking advisers, who can prescribe tailored nicotine replacement patches and gum free of charge. Clinical studies prove that combining one-to-one support with dual nicotine therapy makes you four times more likely to quit permanently.

Within just 72 hours of your final cigarette, your lung capacity begins to recover—and quitting a twenty-a-day habit puts over £3,500 back into your pocket every year. Take the first step toward a healthier, wealthier, and smoke-free future by calling our confidential helpline on 0800 012 345 today.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Use realistic, believable figures in persuasive writing (e.g. 'According to our recent staff survey, 68% of commuters...').
🔄 Quick Check: Blending supportive advice ('we strongly recommend') with hard persuasive figures ('four times more likely', '£3,500 a year') hits every Level 2 marking descriptor.
Question 6 (Level 2 Mastery Challenge — 4 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A prompt instructs: 'Write an article for your college website explaining why apprenticeships offer a valuable alternative to university and advising school leavers on how to apply.'
Draft a 4-sentence paragraph that fulfils both the Explain and Advise purposes.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Sentences 1–2 (Explain): Explain why apprenticeships are valuable (earning a salary while gaining an accredited qualification without £40,000 of student debt). [M1]
2. Sentences 3–4 (Advise): Advise school leavers on practical application steps (registering on the government apprenticeship portal and tailoring their CV). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'Higher and degree apprenticeships offer a compelling alternative to traditional university degrees because learners earn a full-time salary while gaining practical industry experience and a recognized qualification, graduating with zero student loan debt. Consequently, over 85% of apprentices secure permanent employment immediately upon completing their programme. If you are considering this route, we strongly advise registering on the official Find an Apprenticeship portal before the spring recruitment window opens. Additionally, you should ask your college careers adviser to review your CV and arrange a mock interview.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Failing to proofread spelling, punctuation, and grammar after completing the draft.
🔄 Quick Check: Notice how 'because' and 'consequently' drive the explanation, while 'we strongly advise' and 'you should' deliver the advice.
