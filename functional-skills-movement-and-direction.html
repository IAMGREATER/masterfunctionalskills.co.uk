Specification & Learning Outcomes

Candidates must demonstrate mastery of the following regulated Functional Skills Mathematics outcomes:

  • E3.8 / L1.21 – Use cardinal and intercardinal compass directions and turns (quarter/90°, half/180°, three-quarter/270°, full/360°) to describe movement and position.

Golden Rules for Movement, Direction & Compass Bearings

Golden Rule 1: Never Eat Shredded Wheat (Clockwise N → E → S → W)

North is at the top (0°), East is to the right (90°), South is at the bottom (180°), and West is to the left (270°). Clockwise follows the hands of a clock (N → E → S → W); Anti-clockwise goes backwards (N → W → S → E).

Golden Rule / Method: Clockwise: N → E → S → W • Anti-Clockwise: N → W → S → E
Golden Rule 2: 1/4 Turn = 90° • 1/2 Turn = 180° • 3/4 Turn = 270°

A quarter turn is 1 main compass step (90° right angle). A half turn (180°) always faces the exact opposite direction regardless of whether you turn clockwise or anti-clockwise!

Golden Rule / Method: 1/4 Turn = 90° • 1/2 Turn = 180° (Opposite) • 3/4 Turn = 270°

Core Skill 1: The 8-Point Compass & Opposite Directions

Cardinal and intercardinal directions and their 180° opposites.

  • North (N) ↔ South (S) are exact opposites (180° apart).
  • East (E) ↔ West (W) are exact opposites (180° apart).
  • North-East (NE) ↔ South-West (SW) are exact opposites.
  • North-West (NW) ↔ South-East (SE) are exact opposites.
Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 2: Left/Right Turns vs Compass Directions

Why "Turn Right" depends on which way you are currently facing!

Crucial Distinction:

  • Turning Right = 90° Clockwise from your current heading (e.g. if walking South and you turn Right, you are now facing West!).
  • Turning Left = 90° Anti-Clockwise from your current heading (e.g. if walking South and you turn Left, you are now facing East!).
Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 3: Following & Reversing Grid Route Instructions

Tracing warehouse AGV paths and reversing directions for the return journey.

To reverse a journey from B back to A, invert both the order of steps AND every direction (North becomes South, East becomes West, Left becomes Right).

Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Pearson Edexcel Step-by-Step Worked Models

Benchmark Exam Model 1 2 Marks (Entry Level 3 / Level 1)

Vocational Context: Facility Fire Safety Navigation

A care clinic porter is pushing a wheelchair facing North down Main Corridor. At the junction, she turns a quarter turn (90°) clockwise, walks 30 metres, then turns a three-quarter turn (270°) clockwise.
(a) In which compass direction is she facing after the first turn?
(b) In which compass direction is she facing after the second turn?
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Quarter turn (90°) clockwise from North
North + 90° clockwise = EAST.
Criteria: A1: East
Step 2 Three-quarter turn (270°) clockwise from East
From East: 1/4 = South, 2/4 = West, 3/4 = NORTH.
Criteria: A1: North
Final Accredited Answer: (a) East | (b) North
Sonya’s Examiner Tip Examiner Method
A 3/4 turn clockwise is the exact same final position as a 1/4 turn anti-clockwise!
Benchmark Exam Model 2 3 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 1/2)

Vocational Context: Logistics Warehouse Floor Grid

On a warehouse floor grid where 1 square = 5 metres, a forklift starts at Loading Bay S and follows these instructions:
1. Drive 6 squares North.
2. Turn 90° clockwise and drive 4 squares.
3. Turn 90° clockwise and drive 2 squares.
(a) How many metres in total has the forklift driven?
(b) How many squares North and how many squares East is the forklift from its starting point S?
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Sum total squares driven and multiply by 5 metres
Total squares = 6 + 4 + 2 = 12 squares.
Total distance = 12 × 5 m = 60 metres.
Criteria: M1 / A1: 60 metres driven
Step 2 Track net North/South and East/West position
Step 1 goes 6 North; Step 2 goes 4 East; Step 3 turns clockwise from East to go 2 South.
Net North = 6 − 2 = 4 squares North; Net East = 4 squares East.
Criteria: A1: 4 squares North and 4 squares East
Final Accredited Answer: (a) 60 metres total driven | (b) 4 squares North and 4 squares East of S
Subi’s Assessor Pro-Tip Assessor Guidance
Sketch the path on scrap grid paper with arrows so you can see that the third leg goes South.

Critical Examiner Traps & Common Pitfalls

Examiner Pitfall: Assuming "Turn Right" Always Means East
If a person is walking South down a map and turns Right, they turn towards the West (left side of the page), NOT East!
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Imagine yourself standing in the person’s shoes facing their direction before turning left or right.
Examiner Pitfall: Confusing Clockwise with Anti-Clockwise
Turning West from North when asked to turn clockwise.
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Draw a small clock face: 12 → 3 → 6 → 9 is Clockwise (N → E → S → W).

Interactive Exam Practice Questions

Exam Practice Question 1 2 Marks
You are facing South-East (SE). You make a half turn (180°). Which compass direction are you now facing?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. A half turn (180°) points in the exact opposite direction.
2. The opposite of South-East (SE) is North-West (NW).
Final Correct Answer: North-West (NW)
Examiner Tip: Swap both words: South becomes North, East becomes West → North-West!
Exam Practice Question 2 2 Marks
A security guard is facing West. Through how many degrees clockwise must he turn to face South?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Clockwise from West (270°): West → North (90°) → East (180°) → South (270°).
2. Angle = 270° clockwise (a three-quarter turn).
Final Correct Answer: 270°
Examiner Tip: Going clockwise from West to South passes through North and East (3 quarter turns = 270°).
Exam Practice Question 3 3 Marks
A delivery driver leaves the depot, drives 3.5 km East, turns left (90° anti-clockwise) and drives 2.0 km, then turns left again and drives 3.5 km. In which compass direction is she now facing, and how far is she directly from the depot?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Starts East → turns left to face North (2.0 km) → turns left to face West (3.5 km).
2. The 3.5 km West cancels out the 3.5 km East, leaving her exactly 2.0 km North of the depot.
Final Correct Answer: Facing West; 2.0 km North of the depot
Examiner Tip: Draw the 3 sides of the rectangle: 3.5 km East, 2.0 km North, 3.5 km West.
