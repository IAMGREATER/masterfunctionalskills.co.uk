Section A: Core Skills (Warm-Up) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Calculating Median & Range of an Even Data Set) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Six outpatient physiotherapy sessions lasted the following times (in minutes):
28, 42, 35, 24, 39, 48
Calculate the median session duration and the range.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Order data (24, 28, 35, 39, 42, 48) → Median is average of 35 and 39 = 37 mins [M1]
2. Range = Highest (48) - Lowest (24) = 24 mins [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Median = 37 mins, Range = 24 mins
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Finding the middle of the unordered list (35 and 24) without sorting first.
🔄 Quick Check: 37 is halfway between 35 and 39; 24 + 24 = 48.
Question 2 (Level 1 Foundation — 3 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Find the median, mode, and range for these 8 daily parcel deliveries: 14, 18, 12, 14, 22, 19, 14, 16.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Ordered: 12, 14, 14, 14, 16, 18, 19, 22. [M1]
2. Median (4th & 5th values: 14 and 16): (14 + 16) ÷ 2 = 15. [M1]
3. Mode (most frequent) = 14. [M1]
4. Range = 22 − 12 = 10. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Median = 15, Mode = 14, Range = 10
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Selecting 55 or 38 from the raw unsorted list instead of sorting from smallest to largest first forfeits all median marks.
🔄 Quick Check: For 8 numbers, average the 4th and 5th ordered values for the median.
Section B: Everyday & Workplace Maths Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Paramedic Crew Response Time Audit (Level 1 Foundation)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
An ambulance station logs response times (in minutes) for 7 emergency calls:
8, 12, 7, 15, 8, 11, 9
(a) Find the mode.
(b) Find the median.
(c) Calculate the mean and the range.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Identify the mode and sort data in ascending order: Ordered list: 7, 8, 8, 9, 11, 12, 15. | Mode (most frequent) = 8 minutes. [A1]
2. Extract the middle (4th) value for the median: The 4th value in the ordered list of 7 numbers is 9 minutes. [A1]
3. Calculate Mean (Sum ÷ 7) and Range (High − Low): Sum = 7 + 8 + 8 + 9 + 11 + 12 + 15 = 70. | Mean = 70 ÷ 7 = 10 minutes. | Range = 15 − 7 = 8 minutes. [M1 / A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) Mode = 8 mins | (b) Median = 9 mins | (c) Mean = 10 mins, Range = 8 mins
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Always write "Ordered:" and tick off each number as you rewrite it.
🔄 Quick Check: Always write your ordered list down and count that you still have all 7 numbers before picking the middle value!
Question 4 (Workplace Application — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A student needs a mean score of 75 marks across 5 mock tests to qualify for an award. Their first 4 test scores are 72, 68, 81, and 74. Calculate the score they must achieve on the 5th test.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Required total across 5 tests = 5 × 75 = 375 marks. [M1]
2. Sum of first 4 tests = 72 + 68 + 81 + 74 = 295 marks. [P1]
3. Required 5th test score = 375 − 295 = 80 marks. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 80 marks
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: The range measures SPREAD (consistency), not average performance.
🔄 Quick Check: Use Total = Mean × Count (75 × 5 = 375) and subtract the known 4 scores.
Section C: Exam-Style Problem Solving Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Hospital Emergency A&E Triage Waiting Times Comparison (Level 2 Multi-Step)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A hospital trust records the waiting times in minutes for 6 triage patients on Monday and compares them with Tuesday’s summary:
• Monday: 45, 62, 38, 55, 70, 42
• Tuesday Summary: Mean = 48 minutes, Range = 15 minutes.
Calculate the mean and range for Monday, and write two comparative statements comparing waiting times on Monday and Tuesday.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate Monday Mean (Sum ÷ 6): Sum = 45 + 62 + 38 + 55 + 70 + 42 = 312 minutes. | Mean = 312 ÷ 6 = 52 minutes. [M1]
2. Calculate Monday Range (Highest − Lowest): Range = 70 − 38 = 32 minutes. [A1]
3. Statement 1 — Compare Averages in Context: Tuesday had a lower mean waiting time than Monday (48 mins vs 52 mins), so patients waited less time on average on Tuesday. [A1]
4. Statement 2 — Compare Consistency using Range: Tuesday had a smaller range than Monday (15 mins vs 32 mins), meaning waiting times on Tuesday were much more consistent. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Monday Mean = 52 mins, Range = 32 mins. Tuesday had shorter average waits (48 vs 52 mins) and more consistent wait times (range 15 vs 32 mins).
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Never write "The average range was..."; write "The range was..., showing greater consistency."
🔄 Quick Check: Smaller range ALWAYS means MORE CONSISTENT. Cite the actual numbers (52 vs 48 and 32 vs 15) in your sentences.
Question 6 (Level 2 Multi-Step Problem Solving — 3 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A clinic employs 4 receptionists earning £21,000, £22,000, £22,000, and £23,000, plus 1 consultant surgeon earning £112,000.
(a) Calculate the mean salary and the median salary of the 5 staff.
(b) Explain which average is fairer to represent a typical worker at the clinic.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Total salary = 21,000 + 22,000 + 22,000 + 23,000 + 112,000 = £200,000. Mean = £200,000 ÷ 5 = £40,000. [M1]
2. Median (3rd ordered value) = £22,000. [P1]
3. The median (£22,000) is fairer because the mean (£40,000) is heavily skewed upward by the consultant’s £112,000 outlier salary. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) Mean = £40,000, Median = £22,000 | (b) Median, as it is not distorted by the £112,000 outlier
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Stating a conclusion without showing the full numerical comparison values.
🔄 Quick Check: Whenever there is an extreme outlier, the median is the best average to choose.
