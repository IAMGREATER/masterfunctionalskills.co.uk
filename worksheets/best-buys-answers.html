Best Buys & Unit Pricing Pearson Edexcel: L1.15 • L2.17
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Core Skills (Warm-Up) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Level 1 Unit Price per Litre) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A catering supplier sells sunflower oil in two drums:
• Drum A: 5 litres for £11.50
• Drum B: 8 litres for £17.60
Calculate the cost per litre for each drum and state which is better value.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Drum A = £11.50 ÷ 5 = £2.30/L; Drum B = £17.60 ÷ 8 = £2.20/L [M1]
2. Conclude: Drum B is better value (£2.20/L is 10p per litre cheaper than £2.30/L) [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Drum B (£2.20/L vs Drum A £2.30/L)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Choosing Drum A because £11.50 is a lower total ticket price than £17.60.
🔄 Quick Check: 8 × £2.20 = £17.60; 5 × £2.30 = £11.50.
Question 2 (Level 2 Mixed Metric Units Best Buy) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Hand sanitiser gel is sold in two dispenser sizes:
• Standard Pump: 400 ml for £3.20
• Bulk Refill: 1.5 litres for £11.25
Work out the cost per 100 ml for both options to determine the best buy.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Standard (400 ml = 4 × 100 ml): £3.20 ÷ 4 = £0.80 (80p) per 100 ml. Bulk (1.5 L = 15 × 100 ml): £11.25 ÷ 15 = £0.75 (75p) per 100 ml [M1]
2. Conclude: Bulk Refill (1.5 L) is better value at 75p per 100 ml (vs 80p per 100 ml) [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Bulk Refill 1.5 L (75p per 100 ml vs 80p per 100 ml)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Dividing £11.25 by 1.5 without converting 400 ml to 0.4 litres.
🔄 Quick Check: £0.75 × 15 = £11.25; £0.80 × 4 = £3.20.
Section B: Everyday & Workplace Maths Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 Three-Way Pack Size Comparison) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A care home buys decaffeinated tea bags. Three box sizes are sold:
• Small Box: 80 tea bags for £2.40
• Medium Box: 160 tea bags for £4.48
• Large Box: 240 tea bags for £6.96

Which box gives the lowest cost per tea bag? Show the cost per bag (in pence) for all three boxes.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Small: 240p ÷ 80 = 3.0p per bag [M1]
2. Medium: 448p ÷ 160 = 2.8p per bag; Large: 696p ÷ 240 = 2.9p per bag [P1]
3. Conclude: The Medium Box (160 bags) is best value at 2.8p per bag [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Medium Box (2.8p per bag vs 2.9p Large and 3.0p Small)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Assuming the 240-bag Large Box is cheapest without checking the arithmetic.
🔄 Quick Check: Scale all to 480 bags: Small = 6 × £2.40 = £14.40; Medium = 3 × £4.48 = £13.44; Large = 2 × £6.96 = £13.92.
Question 4 (Level 2 Multi-Buy Promotional Bundle Comparison) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A hospital ward sister needs to buy 12 boxes of tissues.
• Chemist A: Normal price £1.80 per box, on a '3 for the price of 2' offer.
• Chemist B: Normal price £1.60 per box, on a 'Buy one, get a second half price' offer.

Which chemist is cheaper for 12 boxes, and by how much?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Chemist A (4 groups of 3 = pay for 8 boxes): 8 × £1.80 = £14.40 [M1]
2. Chemist B (6 pairs of 2 at £1.60 + £0.80 = £2.40 per pair): 6 × £2.40 = £14.40 [P1]
3. Wait — let's check: both cost exactly £14.40! State that both chemists cost the exact same (£14.40, difference £0.00) [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Both cost exactly £14.40 (Difference = £0.00 / £1.20 per box at both)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Misinterpreting 'Buy 1 get 2nd half price' as half price on all 12 boxes instead of every second box.
🔄 Quick Check: Chemist A: £3.60 for 3 = £1.20/box. Chemist B: £2.40 for 2 = £1.20/box. 12 × £1.20 = £14.40.
Section C: Exam-Style Problem Solving Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Printer Toner Cost per 1,000 Pages) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
An Healthcare administration office compares two laser toner cartridges:
• Standard Cartridge: Costs £49.50 and prints 2,200 pages.
• High-Yield Cartridge: Costs £84.00 (with a 10% corporate discount) and prints 4,000 pages.

Calculate the cost per 1,000 pages for each cartridge and state how much money the office saves over 20,000 pages by choosing the better-value cartridge.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Standard cost for 20,000 pages (or per 1,000): £49.50 ÷ 2.2 = £22.50 per 1,000 pages (× 20 = £450.00) [P1]
2. High-Yield discounted price: £84.00 × 0.90 = £75.60 for 4,000 pages [P1]
3. High-Yield cost per 1,000 pages: £75.60 ÷ 4 = £18.90 per 1,000 pages (× 20 = £378.00 for 5 cartridges) [M1]
4. Saving over 20,000 pages: £450.00 - £378.00 = £72.00 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Saves £72.00 (Standard = £22.50/1,000 pages vs High-Yield = £18.90/1,000 pages)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Forgetting to apply the 10% corporate discount to the High-Yield cartridge.
🔄 Quick Check: (£22.50 - £18.90) × 20 = £3.60 × 20 = £72.00.
Question 6 (Level 2 Wholesale Tile Pack Optimisation) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A facilities manager needs exactly 36 m² of ceiling tiles. Two suppliers offer different pack sizes:
• Supplier P: Packs covering 2.4 m² at £31.20 per pack, plus a flat £25.00 delivery fee.
• Supplier Q: Packs covering 3.0 m² at £42.00 per pack, with free delivery and a 'Buy 10 packs, get 2 packs free' deal.

Which supplier is cheaper for the 36 m² job, and by how much?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Supplier P packs needed: 36 ÷ 2.4 = 15 packs [P1]
2. Supplier P total cost: (15 × £31.20) + £25.00 = £468.00 + £25.00 = £493.00 [M1]
3. Supplier Q packs needed: 36 ÷ 3.0 = 12 packs. With 'Buy 10 get 2 free', pay for 10 packs: 10 × £42.00 = £420.00 [P1]
4. Compare and conclude: Supplier Q (£420.00) is cheaper than Supplier P (£493.00) by £73.00. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Supplier Q is cheaper by £73.00 (£420.00 vs £493.00)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Multiplying 12 × £42.00 = £504.00 for Supplier Q without deducting the 2 free packs.
🔄 Quick Check: £493.00 - £420.00 = £73.00.
