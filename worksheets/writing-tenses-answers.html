Consistency of Tense Pearson Edexcel: Pearson Edexcel Functional Skills English (WRITING)
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Foundational Concept Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Eliminating Mid-Sentence Tense Drift) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Read this incident report sentence where the writer accidentally drifts between past and present tense:
'Yesterday morning, the fire alarm sounded at 09:15 and all staff evacuate the building before the fire brigade arrives.'
Rewrite the sentence so it remains consistently in the past tense throughout.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Change present tense 'evacuate' to past tense 'evacuated' to match 'Yesterday morning... sounded' [A1]
2. Change present tense 'arrives' to past tense 'arrived' [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Yesterday morning, the fire alarm sounded at 09:15 and all staff evacuated the building before the fire brigade arrived.
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Changing 'sounded' to present tense when the time marker 'Yesterday morning' requires past tense.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Check all three verbs: sounded, evacuated, arrived (all past tense).
Question 2 (Core Skill Application — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Correct the two tense errors in this sentence from an audit report:
'Yesterday morning, the pharmacy auditor inspected the controlled drugs cabinet and finds that two signature entries are missing from Tuesday's log.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. The time marker 'Yesterday morning' and verb 'inspected' establish the past tense. [M1]
2. Change 'finds' to 'found' and 'are missing' to 'were missing'. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'Yesterday morning, the pharmacy auditor inspected the controlled drugs cabinet and found that two signature entries were missing from Tuesday's log.'
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: In casual speech, people often say: 'So I walked up to the counter and the manager says to me...' In Functional Skills writing, mixing 'walked' and 'says' is penalised as a tense error.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: When a sentence begins with a past time marker like 'Yesterday morning' or 'Last week', every verb that follows in that recount must stay in the past tense.
Section B: Functional Workplace Application Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 Model: Paramedic Callout Log Tense Repair) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Identify and correct the three tense errors in this paramedic incident log:
'We arrived at the residential address at 03:15 and find the patient conscious on the hallway floor. The lead paramedic administers oxygen while the technician checked the ECG monitor, and then we takes the patient to St Jude’s Hospital.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Identify the anchor tense of the incident log: The callout happened in the past ('arrived at 03:15', 'checked'). All main action verbs must be in the PAST tense. [A1]
2. Locate the three present-tense slips: 1. 'find' → 'found' | 2. 'administers' → 'administered' | 3. 'takes' → 'took' (or 'transferred'). [A1]
3. Write out the fully consistent past-tense log: Ensure smooth past-tense narration throughout. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'We arrived at the residential address at 03:15 and found the patient conscious on the hallway floor. The lead paramedic administered oxygen while the technician checked the ECG monitor, and then we transferred the patient to St Jude’s Hospital.'
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Always keep past narratives strictly in the past tense: 'I walked up to the counter and the manager said to me...'
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Read your incident log aloud in your head: shifting from 'arrived' to 'find' and 'administers' immediately sounds jarring.
Question 4 (Exam-Style Task — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Replace the three non-standard verb forms in this witness statement with correct Standard English past-tense or past-participle verbs:
'When I come onto the ward at 07:00, I seen that the medication fridge door had broke during the night.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Change 'come' (present) to 'came' (or 'arrived'). [M1]
2. Change 'I seen' to 'I saw' (or 'I noticed'). [M1]
3. Change 'had broke' to 'had broken'. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'When I came onto the ward at 07:00, I saw that the medication fridge door had broken during the night.'
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Writing 'I seen the broken window' or 'We done the risk assessment' is non-standard grammar and loses immediate SPaG marks.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: After 'had', 'have', or 'has', always use the past participle ('broken', 'taken', 'written', 'seen'), never the simple past ('broke', 'took', 'wrote').
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Exam-Style Analysis & Evaluation Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Model: Multi-Tense Audit Report (Past Perfect, Present Perfect & Future)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Write a 3-sentence executive summary for a safety report that:
1. Uses the past perfect (had + past participle) to state what had gone wrong prior to last month's inspection.
2. Uses the present perfect (have/has + past participle) to state what remedial steps have been completed since then.
3. Uses a future modal verb to state the projected outcome for next quarter.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Sentence 1: Past Perfect for pre-inspection background: Use 'had deteriorated' and 'had not received' to show conditions before the October audit. [A1]
2. Sentence 2: Present Perfect for recent completed actions: Use 'has repainted' and 'have completed' to connect recent fixes to the current state. [A1]
3. Sentence 3: Future Modal for projected safety outcome: Use 'will eliminate' and 'should ensure' to project next quarter's compliance. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'Prior to our internal audit on 1 October, pedestrian walkway markings in Bay 3 had faded severely and twelve temporary operatives had not completed their forklift awareness induction. Over the past fortnight, the facilities team has repainted all high-visibility floor lanes and all agency staff have passed mandatory safety assessments. Consequently, these interventions will reduce vehicle-pedestrian near-misses to zero during the upcoming peak winter quarter.'
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Use 'I saw' / 'We did' for simple past, or add 'have/had': 'I have seen' / 'We have done'.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Moving logically from Past Perfect ('had faded') → Present Perfect ('has repainted') → Future ('will reduce') demonstrates effortless Level 2 grammatical command.
Question 6 (Level 2 Mastery Challenge — 3 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Rewrite this paragraph so that it maintains consistent present tense appropriate for a permanent Staff Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) manual:
'All laboratory technicians must wear safety goggles before entering Room 4. You checked the fume cupboard airflow gauge, and then you will record the pressure reading in the yellow logbook.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Identify the anchor tense for an SOP manual: Present tense (or imperative instruction). [M1]
2. Change past tense 'checked' and future tense 'will record' into present simple verbs ('check' and 'record') to match the routine procedure. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'All laboratory technicians must wear safety goggles before entering Room 4. Technicians check the fume cupboard airflow gauge and then record the pressure reading in the yellow logbook.'
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Failing to proofread spelling, punctuation, and grammar after completing the draft.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Standard Operating Procedures describe permanent rules, so keep all verbs in the present simple tense.
