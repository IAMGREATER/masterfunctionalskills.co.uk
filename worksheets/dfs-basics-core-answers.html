Digital Skills Basics (Units 1–7) Core Practice Exercises

Laptop Start-Up, Passwords, Windows Controls, Files/Search, Shortcuts, Web Search & Email
Q1 (Mouse & Keyboard)
You need to type an email address containing '@' on a UK laptop keyboard. Which two keys do you press together, and what mouse action opens a folder sitting on the Desktop?
Worked Answer: Hold Shift + ' (apostrophe key near Enter) for @. Use Double Left-Click on a Desktop folder icon to open it.
Q2 (Forgotten Password)
You failed to sign in twice and see 'Incorrect password'. Write 4 calm recovery steps without locking your account or falling for a fake text scam.
Worked Answer: 1) Check Caps Lock is OFF and click the Eye icon to check your typing. 2) Click 'Forgotten password?' on the real login page. 3) Wait 1–2 mins for the reset email/SMS and check Junk/Spam. 4) Choose a fresh 3-random-word passphrase + number.
Q3 (Windows Controls)
What is the exact difference between clicking Minimise (_) and clicking the top-right red X (Close) inside Word or Excel?
Worked Answer: Minimise (_) tucks the window cleanly down to the bottom Taskbar while keeping everything open; Close (X) shuts the programme completely (always Ctrl+S before closing).
Q4 (Folders & Finding Lost Files)
You downloaded a utility form 5 minutes ago in your browser, but it is not visible on the Desktop. Where should you look first, and how do you move it cleanly into Documents > Coursework?
Worked Answer: Open the Downloads folder in File Explorer (Win + E). Create a 'Coursework' folder inside Documents (Right-click > New > Folder), then Cut (Ctrl+X) and Paste (Ctrl+V) the file inside.
Q5 (Universal Shortcuts)
Write what each shortcut does across EVERY programme: a) Ctrl + C, b) Ctrl + X, c) Ctrl + V, d) Ctrl + Z, e) Ctrl + S, f) Ctrl + F.
Worked Answer: a) Copy, b) Cut (Move), c) Paste, d) Undo last step, e) Save now, f) Find word on page/document.
Q6 (Web Search & Official Forms)
What short keywords find official UK minimum wage rules cleanly, why skip 'Sponsored' links, and what date format do most UK forms demand?
Worked Answer: Search 'national minimum wage UK gov'. Skip 'Sponsored' results (paid adverts) and pick .gov.uk links. Enter dates as DD/MM/YYYY with a clean uppercase postcode.
