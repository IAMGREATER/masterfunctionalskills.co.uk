Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Foundational Concept Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Extracting Actionable Facts from a Spoken Handover) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Listen to (read) this spoken shift handover transcript:
'Before I finish, please remember that Bed 4 needs a repeat blood glucose check at 14:30, the pharmacy courier is collecting the controlled drugs box at 15:15, and Mrs Khan in Bed 9 has already had her discharge letter signed.'
Extract the two timed tasks that the incoming nurse must complete.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Extract 14:30 task: Repeat blood glucose check for Bed 4 [A1]
2. Extract 15:15 task: Hand over controlled drugs box to pharmacy courier (ignore Bed 9 as already completed) [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1. 14:30 — Repeat blood glucose check for Bed 4; 2. 15:15 — Controlled drugs box collection by pharmacy courier.
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Listing Mrs Khan's discharge letter as a pending task when the speaker stated it is already signed.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Matches exact times (14:30 and 15:15) and actions.
Question 2 (Core Skill Application — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A warehouse supervisor announces:
'We've had two near-misses in Loading Bay 4 this morning due to hydraulic oil spills. All operatives must wear steel-toecap boots and high-visibility vests at all times, and Loading Bay 4 is closed for steam cleaning until 14:30.'
Extract (a) the mandatory PPE rule and (b) the operational restriction.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Identify the mandatory PPE requirement. [M1]
2. Identify the specific location and time of the closure. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) Mandatory PPE Rule: All operatives must wear steel-toecap boots and high-visibility vests at all times.
(b) Operational Restriction: Loading Bay 4 is closed for steam cleaning until 14:30.
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: If you try to write down every word a speaker says, you will miss their next two points AND lose marks for lack of eye contact.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Be exact with locations ('Loading Bay 4') and times ('until 14:30') when summarising safety briefings.
Section B: Functional Workplace Application Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Model 1 (Level 1): Extracting Key Details from a Voicemail Transcript) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Listen to / read this voicemail transcript left on the garage answerphone:
'Hi there, it's Mrs Anita Shah calling about my silver Ford Fiesta, registration BD68 KLP. Sorry I missed your call earlier—I was stuck in the dentist's chair! Anyway, please go ahead and fit the two new front tyres you quoted £140 for, and I will come in to collect the car at 4:30 pm today. My mobile is 07700 900412 if there's any problem.'
Extract the four essential workshop action details and ignore the filler.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Filter out irrelevant personal anecdote: Ignore the comment about being stuck in the dentist's chair. [A1]
2. Extract Customer, Vehicle & Authorised Work: Customer: Mrs Anita Shah (07700 900412) | Vehicle: Silver Ford Fiesta (BD68 KLP) | Authorised work: Fit 2 new front tyres at £140. [A1]
3. Extract Deadline Time: Ready for customer collection at 16:30 today. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1. Customer & Contact: Mrs Anita Shah (07700 900412)
2. Vehicle: Silver Ford Fiesta (Reg: BD68 KLP)
3. Authorised Action: Fit two new front tyres at the agreed quote of £140
4. Deadline: Ready for collection by 16:30 today.
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Write max 3–4 words per line (e.g. 'CQC 17 Nov | Audit 16:00 | CD 2 sigs') while keeping your head up.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Always double-check registration numbers, prices (£140), and exact collection times (16:30) when extracting spoken messages.
Question 4 (Exam-Style Task — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Read this spoken pitch from a colleague in a meeting:
'Switching all customer support entirely to an AI chatbot will save our company £30,000 a year in call-centre wages and give instant 24/7 replies.'
1. Identify the speaker's core line of argument.
2. Identify one critical customer-service risk the speaker has ignored.
3. Write an open question exposing that risk.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Core argument: Replace human call handlers with an AI chatbot to save £30,000/year and offer 24/7 responses. [M1]
2. Ignored risk: Complex complaints, elderly/vulnerable customers who struggle with chatbots, and customer frustration. [M1]
3. Open question probing how complex or distressed customer calls will be handled. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1. Line of Argument: Automating customer support via an AI chatbot saves £30,000 annually while providing 24/7 instant replies.
2. Overlooked Risk: Elderly or vulnerable customers and complex complaints that automated bots cannot resolve.
3. Probing Question: 'While the £30,000 saving is attractive, how would an automated chatbot support elderly customers without internet access or handle complex complaints that require human empathy?'
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Asking 'How much will it cost?' when the speaker clearly stated 'The total cost is £800' proves to the assessor that you were NOT listening.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Referencing the speaker's exact figure ('While the £30,000 saving is attractive...') before probing the flaw proves Level 2 active listening.
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Exam-Style Analysis & Evaluation Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Model 2 (Level 2): Extracting Compliance Directives & Formulating a Probe from a Ward Briefing) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Read the transcript of the Ward Manager's briefing:
'Good morning team. First, thank you for covering the busy weekend shifts. A crucial reminder that our CQC inspection window opens next Monday, 17 November. All environmental cleaning audits must be uploaded to the digital portal by 16:00 daily without exception. Second, pharmacy reported that three controlled-drug entries lacked a second signature last Thursday; therefore, anyone administering Schedule 2 medications must log two practitioner signatures at the bedside immediately. Finally, the staff summer barbecue has been moved to 18 July.'
1. Extract the two mandatory clinical compliance directives.
2. Formulate one relevant follow-up question to clarify implementation.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Filter out social notices and pleasantries: Ignore weekend thanks and the July barbecue date. [A1]
2. Extract Compliance Directives 1 & 2 with exact times: Directive 1: Upload environmental cleaning audits to the digital portal by 16:00 daily (ahead of CQC window on Mon 17 Nov). | Directive 2: Log two practitioner countersignatures immediately at the bedside for all Schedule 2 controlled drugs. [A1]
3. Formulate a logical follow-up clarifying question: Ask what backup procedure applies if the digital audit portal is offline at 16:00 or when only one registered nurse is on the bay during night break rotations. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Directive 1: Upload daily environmental cleaning audits to the portal by 16:00 (effective immediately ahead of 17 Nov CQC window).
Directive 2: Mandatory immediate bedside dual-signature logging for all Schedule 2 controlled drugs.
Follow-Up Question: 'During night-shift break rotations when only one registered nurse is on Bay B, should we call the night coordinator across to witness and countersign Schedule 2 administrations at the bedside?'
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Refer to the figure they gave and probe deeper: 'You mentioned the total cost is £800—does that include installation?'
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Notice how the follow-up question directly tests a real practical edge-case of Directive 2—proving both accurate listening and critical thinking.
Question 6 (Level 2 Mastery Challenge — 3 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Convert this 55-word spoken briefing extract into 3 concise shorthand notepad lines (using arrows/symbols) that take less than 10 seconds to jot down:
'Starting next Monday 9 November, our main staff car park will close for five days so contractors can install six electric vehicle chargers. During next week, all staff must park at the Leisure Centre overflow car park on Mill Road and show their staff ID badge to the barrier attendant.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Line 1: Date & closure (Mon 9 Nov, 5 days: Main car park CLOSED → 6 EV chargers). [M1]
2. Line 2: Alternative parking (Park at Leisure Centre overflow, Mill Rd). [M1]
3. Line 3: Requirement (Show Staff ID badge at barrier). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: • Mon 9 Nov (5 days): Main car park CLOSED → 6 EV chargers installed
• Temp parking: Leisure Centre overflow (Mill Rd)
• Action: Must show Staff ID badge at barrier
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Failing to proofread spelling, punctuation, and grammar after completing the draft.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Short keyword notes let you capture 100% of the facts while keeping your eyes on the speaker.
