Searching, Buying and Booking Services Online

Learn how to search for products and services online, apply filters, manage a digital shopping basket, and verify secure payment pages using the padlock icon and HTTPS.

Use search filters (price, size, rating) to find specific items or services online Add items to a shopping basket and review order totals before purchasing Identify the secure padlock icon and https:// in the browser address bar before paying
On This Page:
1. Key Concepts 2. Worked Exam Model 3. Interactive Quiz 4. Practice Exam Task

Step-by-Step Teaching & Key Concepts

Core Knowledge
1
Searching and Filtering Online

When booking a ticket or shopping on an e-commerce website:

  1. Type what you need into the Search Bar (e.g. 'First Aid Kit Wall Mounted').
  2. Use Filters on the side menu to narrow down your choices: filter by Price (Low to High), Customer Rating, or Delivery Date.
  3. Click on an item to read the detailed description, specifications, and customer reviews.
Focus Browser Search: Ctrl + E • Reload Webpage: F5
2
Managing the Shopping Basket and Checkout

When you decide to purchase:

  • Click Add to Basket / Cart. You can choose the quantity (e.g. 2).
  • Click the Basket icon at the top right to review your order. Check that quantities and prices are correct.
  • Click Proceed to Checkout.
  • Enter your delivery address and choose your delivery speed (Standard vs Express).
3
Verifying Secure Payment: Padlocks and HTTPS

Before typing credit card details or making a payment, STOP and check the address bar:

  • The Padlock Symbol: A closed padlock icon must appear next to the web address.
  • HTTPS: The web address must begin with https:// (the 'S' stands for Secure). This encrypts your financial data so hackers cannot intercept your card numbers.
Browser Address Bar Security Check
https://secure.medicalsupplies.co.uk/checkout
Never Pay on HTTP!: If a website only shows http:// (without the S) or displays 'Not Secure' with an open red lock, never enter your card details!

Step-by-Step Exam Worked Model

Accredited Model

Here is how this skill is tested in the real Pearson Edexcel onscreen digital assessment, broken down with official examiner mark codes ([B1] knowledge, [M1] process, [A1] accuracy).

Exam Scenario Prompt: A worker is booking train tickets online for an official training seminar. Before typing their debit card number on the payment page, what two security indicators must they look for in the browser to ensure the page is safe?
Step 1: Identify first security indicator [B1]
A closed Padlock icon next to the web address
Tutor Marking Rationale: Confirms an active SSL/TLS encrypted connection.
Step 2: Identify second security indicator [B1]
The address starts with 'https://' (with the letter 's')
Tutor Marking Rationale: Indicates Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure.
Tutor Abdul's Exam Golden Rule: In Entry 3 exams, the two answers for payment security are ALWAYS: 1. Padlock symbol; 2. 'https://'.

You are purchasing stationery for your office on a supplier website. Explain the four stages of making this transaction from finding the items to finalising the order.

Accredited Exam Benchmark Solution

1. Search for the stationery using the search bar and filters. 2. Add the items to the shopping basket and review the quantities and total cost. 3. Verify that the payment page has a closed padlock and 'https://' in the address bar. 4. Enter the delivery address and payment details securely, then confirm the order.
