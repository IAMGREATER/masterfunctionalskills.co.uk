Official Level 1 Specification & Scope (L1.M.18 / L1.M.20)

Regulated Ofqual Functional Skills Mathematics Level 1 learning outcomes (Open Awards, Pearson Edexcel, City & Guilds, NCFE & AQA):

  • Solve multi-step money problems converting cleanly between pence (p) and pounds (£).
  • Calculate household bills, hourly wages, overtime pay, and multi-buy discounts.
  • Format all currency answers with £ and 2 decimal places (e.g. £18.40) and never mix £ and p on one figure.

Core Level 1 Golden Rules: Problems Involving Money, Budgets and Pence/Pounds (Level 1)

Golden Rule 1: Convert Pence to Pounds BEFORE Adding to Pounds

Divide pence by 100: 45p = £0.45 and 8p = £0.08. Never add 28 (pence) directly to £14.50!

Level 1 Key Rule: 300 units × 25p = 7,500p ÷ 100 = £75.00
Golden Rule 2: Never Write '£0.65p' or '£12.5'

Use EITHER £0.65 OR 65p (never both symbols together!), and always pad tenths to two decimal places: £12.50.

Level 1 Key Rule: £12.5 on calculator → write £12.50
Golden Rule 3: 3-for-2 Deals: Group Into Sets of 3

For 12 items on a '3 for the price of 2' offer: 12 ÷ 3 = 4 sets, so you only pay for 4 × 2 = 8 items!

Level 1 Key Rule: Pay for 2 out of every 3

Core Skill 1: Foreign Currency Exchange Calculations (Buying & Selling)

Converting between GBP (£) and foreign currencies (€ / $) with commission.

  • GBP (£) to Foreign Currency: MULTIPLY by the exchange rate: £500 × 1.16 = €580.00.
  • Foreign Currency back to GBP (£): DIVIDE by the exchange rate: €464 ÷ 1.16 = £400.00.
  • Handling Commission: If a travel bureau charges 2% commission, deduct 2% before converting (or multiply by 0.98).
Level 1 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 2: Household & Commercial Utility Bills (Electricity & Gas Meters)

Calculating unit consumption from meter readings, standing charges, and 5% energy VAT.

  1. Units Used (kWh): Present Meter Reading − Previous Meter Reading.
  2. Usage Charge: Units Used × Cost per kWh (convert pence to pounds by dividing by 100!).
  3. Standing Charge: Number of Days × Daily Standing Charge.
  4. Total Bill: Sum Usage + Standing Charge, then add 5% domestic energy VAT (× 1.05).
Level 1 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 3: Group Booking Tariffs & Family/Multi-Ticket Combinations

Checking whether a family/group pass is cheaper than individual tickets.

When booking for a mixed group (e.g. 2 Adults and 3 Children where Adult = £18, Child = £11, and a Family Ticket for 2 Adults + 2 Children = £48):

  • Option 1 (All Individual): (2 × £18) + (3 × £11) = £36 + £33 = £69.00.
  • Option 2 (1 Family Ticket + 1 Extra Child): £48 + £11 = £59.00 (Saves £10.00!).
Level 1 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Vocational Context: Electricity Meter Bill Calculation (Pence & Pounds)

A community workshop used 420 units (kWh) of electricity in March. Electricity costs 28p per unit, plus a fixed standing charge of £16.50 for the month. Does a monthly budget of £130 cover the bill?

Final Exam Answer: No — total bill is £134.10 (£4.10 over the £130 budget)

Common Level 1 Examiner Traps to Avoid

Level 1 Examiner Trap: Adding Pence Directly to Pounds
Calculating 420 × 28 = 11,760 and adding 16.50 = £11,776.50! Always divide pence by 100.
Level 1 Examiner Trap: Writing £134.1 Without the Zero
Always write two digits after the decimal point for money: £134.10.

Level 1 Practice 1: Weekly Wage & Overtime 2 Marks
Sam works 35 regular hours at £12.00/hr plus 4 overtime hours at £16.50/hr. Find Sam's total pay.
Level 1 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
35 × 12 = £420 and 4 × 16.50 = £66 (M1): £420 + £66
Total pay (A1): £486.00 (or £486)
Final Answer: £486
Level 1 Practice 2: 3-for-2 Lever Arch Files 2 Marks
Folders cost £3.40 each on a '3 for the price of 2' offer. How much do 9 folders cost?
Level 1 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
9 folders = 3 sets of 3 → pay for 6 folders (M1): 6 × £3.40
Exact 2 d.p. price (A1): £20.40
Final Answer: £20.40
