Official Entry Level 3 Scope (E3.M.12)

What you need for Entry Level 3 Maths — and what is saved for Level 1 so you never waste time on harder topics:

Included at Entry Level 3
Analogue & digital clocks (12-hour am/pm and 24-hour format), Roman numerals I to XII on clock faces, calendars (days, weeks, months, years), and adding/finding simple time intervals.
Saved for Level 1 (Not Tested Here)

3 Simple Entry 3 Golden Rules: Time

Golden Rule 1: 60 Minutes = 1 Hour (Never Roll Over at 100!)

Clocks reset at :60, NOT :100! Adding 30 minutes to 10:45 jumps 15 mins to 11:00, then 15 mins to 11:15 (never 10:75!).

Entry 3 Example: Quarter past = :15 | Half past = :30 | Quarter to = :45.
Golden Rule 2: am vs pm & the +12 Afternoon Rule

am = midnight (00:00) to before noon • pm = noon (12:00) to midnight. For afternoon 24-hour times after 12:59 pm, add 12 to the hours!

Entry 3 Example: 2:30 pm → 14:30 | 7:15 pm → 19:15 | 9:05 am → 09:05.
Golden Rule 3: Know Your Calendar Facts & UK Date Order

7 days = 1 week • 12 months = 1 year • 52 weeks = 1 year. UK dates always go DD/MM/YYYY (Day / Month / Year).

Entry 3 Example: 14th March 2026 = 14/03/2026.

Visual Analogue Clock (Roman Numerals I–XII) & Jump Timeline

Clock Reading: Quarter past 2
• Short hour hand just past II (2)
• Long minute hand on III (15 mins)
• Morning = 2:15 am (02:15)
• Afternoon = 2:15 pm (14:15)
Timeline Jump (09:40 → 11:15):
09:40 (+20m) → 10:00 (+1h) → 11:00 (+15m) → 1 hr 35 mins!

Core Skill 1: Roman Numerals on Clock Faces (I to XII)

Entry Level 3 explicitly includes reading clock faces marked with Roman numerals!

I=1, II=2, III=3, IV=4, V=5, VI=6
VII=7, VIII=8, IX=9, X=10, XI=11, XII=12.
Big hand on VI = :30 (half past)
Big hand on IX = :45 (quarter to the NEXT hour!).

Core Skill 2: Using Calendars & Counting Days/Weeks

30 days hath September, April, June, and November — all the rest have 31 (except February with 28, or 29 in a leap year).

2 weeks from 5th May
Add 14 days: 5 + 14 = 19th May!
Sonya’s Entry 3 Exam Tip
Never use column subtraction on clock times (11:15 − 09:45 gives 170 if you forget 60 mins = 1 hour!). Always draw a quick straight line and hop to the next whole o'clock!

Entry Level 3 Step-by-Step Worked Exam Question

Entry Level 3 Exam Model 3 Marks (Non-Calculator)

Everyday Scenario: Dental Appointment Finish Time

Mr Patel arrives for his dental check-up at 2:40 pm. He waits 15 minutes and his check-up takes 30 minutes.
Write the time Mr Patel finishes in 24-hour clock format.
Step
Clear Working Out
Mark Focus
Step 1 Add total minutes: 15 + 30 = 45 mins
Start at 2:40 pm and add 45 minutes
[M1] Add 45 mins to 2:40 pm
Step 2 Bridge through 3:00 pm cleanly
2:40 pm + 20 mins = 3:00 pm; + 25 mins = 3:25 pm
[M1] 3:25 pm
Step 3 Convert pm to 24-hour (+12 hours)
3 + 12 = 15 → 15:25
[A1] 15:25
Final Exam Answer: 15:25
Sonya’s Check Reminder: Always include units (£, p, cm, g, ml, or °C) and double-check you answered the exact question asked!

Common Entry 3 Mistakes to Avoid

Common Entry 3 Mistake: Writing 2:40 + 45 mins = 2:85
Remember 60 minutes make a new hour! Bridge to 3:00 first.
Common Entry 3 Mistake: Confusing US and UK Dates
In the UK, 06/11/2026 means 6th November 2026 (Day first, Month second!).

Interactive Entry Level 3 Practice Questions

Entry 3 Practice 1: 24-Hour Shift Start 2 Marks
A twilight warehouse shift starts at 4:45 pm and ends at 8:45 pm.
(a) Write 4:45 pm in 24-hour format.
(b) How many hours long is the shift?
Entry Level 3 Worked Solution:
(a) +12 to hours (1 Mark): 16:45
(b) Duration (1 Mark): 4 hours
Final Answer: (a) 16:45, (b) 4 hours
Entry 3 Practice 2: Calendar Delivery Date 2 Marks
An online sofa order is placed on 4th October and takes 3 weeks to arrive. On what date in October will the sofa arrive?
Entry Level 3 Worked Solution:
3 weeks = 21 days (1 Mark): 3 × 7 = 21
Add to 4th Oct (1 Mark): 4 + 21 = 25th October
Final Answer: 25th October
