Section A: Procedural Fluency Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Calculating Sector Angles & Proportions) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A pie chart represents 90 staff members surveyed about hospital transport.
If the sector for Cycle to Work measures an angle of 108°, how many staff members cycle to work?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Find fraction of full circle: 108° ÷ 360° = 0.3 (or 3/10) [M1]
2. Multiply by total staff (90): 0.3 × 90 = 27 staff members [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 27 staff members
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Dividing 360° by 90 (= 4° per person) and multiplying instead of dividing 108° by 4°.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 360° ÷ 90 = 4° per person; 27 × 4° = 108°.
Question 2 (Level 1 Foundation — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
In a pie chart representing 120 staff members, the "Full-Time" sector has an angle of 210°. How many staff members work full-time?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Fraction of circle = 210° ÷ 360° = 7/12. [M1]
2. Number of staff = (210 ÷ 360) × 120 = 70 staff members. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 70 staff members
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Calculating percentages (out of 100) and plotting those directly as degrees leaves two-thirds of the circle empty!
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: (210 ÷ 360) × 120 = 70.
Section B: Workplace & Functional Scenarios Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Hospital Trust Energy Consumption Breakdown Pie Chart (Level 1/2 Foundation)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A hospital records its annual energy consumption of 1,000 MWh by source:
• Gas: 500 MWh | Electricity: 300 MWh | Solar: 120 MWh | Biomass: 80 MWh
Calculate the sector angle in degrees for each of the four energy sources on a pie chart.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate degrees per MWh (360° ÷ 1,000): 360° ÷ 1,000 MWh = 0.36° per MWh. [M1]
2. Multiply each energy quantity by 0.36°: Gas = 500 × 0.36° = 180° | Electricity = 300 × 0.36° = 108° | Solar = 120 × 0.36° = 43.2° | Biomass = 80 × 0.36° = 28.8° [M1]
3. Check that all four angles sum to 360°: 180° + 108° + 43.2° + 28.8° = 360°. Confirmed. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Gas = 180°, Electricity = 108°, Solar = 43.2°, Biomass = 28.8°
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: A full circle is 360°, not 100°.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Always add your final angles together to verify they sum to 360°.
Question 4 (Workplace Application — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A care home survey of 90 residents shows: 38 chose Gardening, 27 chose Music, and 25 chose Bingo. Calculate the pie chart sector angle for each activity.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Multiplier = 360° ÷ 90 = 4° per resident. [M1]
2. Gardening = 38 × 4° = 152°. [P1]
3. Music = 27 × 4° = 108°. [P1]
4. Bingo = 25 × 4° = 100°. [P1]
5. Check: 152° + 108° + 100° = 360°. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Gardening = 152°, Music = 108°, Bingo = 100°
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: When drawing a pie chart with a protractor, you must measure each new sector starting from the line of the PREVIOUS sector you just drew.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 360° ÷ 90 = 4° per resident makes the multiplications super fast!
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Problem Solving & Justification Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Comparing Two Regional Depots’ Electric Van Fleet Pie Charts (Level 2 Multi-Step)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Two regional delivery depots display pie charts of their vehicle fleets:
• North Depot (Total = 180 vehicles): The Electric Van sector angle is 100°.
• South Depot (Total = 300 vehicles): The Electric Van sector angle is 72°.
The fleet director claims: "North Depot has a greater number of Electric Vans than South Depot because its pie chart sector angle is larger (100° vs 72°)."
Is the fleet director correct? Show full calculations for both depots.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate number of Electric Vans at North Depot: North = (100° ÷ 360°) × 180 = 50 electric vans. [M1 / A1]
2. Calculate number of Electric Vans at South Depot: South = (72° ÷ 360°) × 300 = 0.2 × 300 = 60 electric vans. [M1]
3. Compare counts and state conclusion: No, the director is incorrect. North Depot has a higher proportion, but South Depot has 60 electric vans compared to 50 at North Depot (10 more vans). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Incorrect — South Depot has 60 electric vans vs 50 at North Depot
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Rotate your protractor so its zero line sits on the edge of the sector you just completed.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Pie chart angles only show PROPORTIONS. You must multiply by each depot’s total fleet size to compare actual vehicle counts!
Question 6 (Level 2 Multi-Step Problem Solving — 3 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
On a budget pie chart, the "IT Software" sector has an angle of 54° and represents £13,500. Calculate the total budget represented by the entire 360° pie chart.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Value per degree = £13,500 ÷ 54° = £250 per degree. [M1]
2. Total 360° budget = £250 × 360 = £90,000. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £90,000
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Stating a conclusion without showing the full numerical comparison values.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Divide £13,500 by 54° to find the value of 1° (£250), then multiply by 360°.
