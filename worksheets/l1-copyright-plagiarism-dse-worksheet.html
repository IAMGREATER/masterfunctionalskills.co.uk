Copyright, Fair Use, DSE and Ergonomics

5. Being Safe and Responsible Online • Standard Code: L1.5.4
Style 2: Practice (4 Tasks)
Task 1 — Core Workplace Scenario Task [3 Marks]

An office worker is experiencing persistent wrist and finger pain after typing for long hours each day. Name the condition they may be developing, and describe two ergonomic adjustments that can prevent this workplace health issue.

Task 2 — Applied Workplace Problem [3 Marks]

A medical training coordinator is preparing a printed trainee handbook. They download medical diagrams from a commercial medical website and paste them into the handbook without contacting the creator. Explain whether this is permitted, and state one legal way the coordinator could find images they are allowed to use.

Task 3 — Troubleshooting & Error Resolution [3 Marks]

During a busy workplace shift, a team member makes an error while handling Plagiarism & Citing Online Sources. Identify one risk associated with this mistake, and describe the exact troubleshooting procedure required to correct it.

Task 4 — Quality Assurance & Workplace Policy [3 Marks]

Before confirming completion of any task involving Copyright, Fair Use, DSE and Ergonomics, the organisation requires all staff to verify their work. Consider Tutor Abdul's standard rule: "In Level 1 exams, never assume images online are free. Always mention 'Copyright permission' or 'Creative Commons licences'." State two quality checks an employee must complete to verify their work meets workplace data security and operational standards.
