Part 1: Quick-Check Answer Key
Quick answers for practice questions
Q1 Question 1: Distinguishing Metric Dimension Units
(a) The skirting board around a room → Length (m)
(b) The carpet needed to cover an office floor → Area (m²)
(c) The water needed to fill a swimming pool → Volume (m³ or litres)
(d) The fencing around a community garden → Length (m)
(e) The grass seed needed for a lawn → Area (m²)
(f) The air flow ventilation in a medical clinic → Volume (m³/hour)
Q2 Question 2: Material Pack Coverage Calculations
(a) Floor area = 28 m². Tile packs cover 2.5 m² each. Packs needed? → 12 packs (28 ÷ 2.5 = 11.2)
(b) Wall area = 45 m². Paint tins cover 12 m² each. Tins needed? → 4 tins (45 ÷ 12 = 3.75)
(c) Trench length = 32 m. Drainage pipe sold in 3 m lengths. Pipes needed? → 11 pipes (32 ÷ 3 = 10.67)
(d) Planter volume = 1.2 m³. Compost sold in 60-litre bags (0.06 m³). Bags? → 20 bags (1.2 ÷ 0.06)
Q3 Question 3: Compound Geometric Calculations (Perimeter, Area & Cost)
(a) Lawn 12m × 8m. Perimeter for fencing = ? → 40 m (2×12 + 2×8)
(b) Fence panels cost £15 per metre. Total fencing cost for 40m? → £600.00 (40 × 15)
(c) Lawn area = 12m × 8m = 96 m². Turf costs £6/m². Total turf cost? → £576.00 (96 × 6)
(d) Room 5m × 4m. Carpet tiles 50cm × 50cm (0.25 m²). Tiles needed? → 80 tiles (20 ÷ 0.25)
(e) Wall 6m × 3m = 18 m². Tiles 20cm × 20cm (0.04 m²). Tiles needed? → 450 tiles (18 ÷ 0.04)
(f) Swimming pool: 10m × 5m × 2m. Volume = ? → 100 m³
(g) Pool capacity in litres (1 m³ = 1000 litres)? → 100,000 litres
(h) Water flow rate is 5,000 L/hour. Hours to fill 100,000 L pool? → 20 hours (100,000 ÷ 5000)
(i) Garden flower bed: 4m × 2m × 0.2m deep. Compost volume in m³? → 1.6 m³
(j) Compost bags hold 50 litres (0.05 m³). Bags needed for 1.6 m³? → 32 bags (1.6 ÷ 0.05)
Q4 Question 4: Dimension Matching & Unit Consistency Rules
(a) Rectangle 2.4 m by 80 cm. Area in m² (2.4 × 0.8) → 1.92 m²
(b) Rectangle 1.5 m by 60 cm. Area in cm² (150 × 60) → 9,000 cm²
(c) Cuboid: 2 m by 50 cm by 40 cm. Volume in m³ (2 × 0.5 × 0.4) → 0.4 m³
(d) Cuboid volume 0.4 m³ converted to cm³ (0.4 × 1,000,000) → 400,000 cm³
(e) Ribbon needed for 6 gifts: each gift needs 85 cm. Total metres? → 5.1 m (510 cm)
(f) Planks 2.4m each. Total length of 15 planks in metres? → 36 m
(g) Table top 1.8m × 0.9m. Varnish covers 8 m²/litre. Litres needed for 1 coat? → 0.2 litres (1.62 ÷ 8)
(h) Packaging box: 30cm × 20cm × 15cm. How many in crate 120cm × 80cm × 60cm? → 64 boxes ((120÷30) × (80÷20) × (60÷15) = 4×4×4)
(i) Why must all dimensions be in identical units before multiplying? → To ensure unit consistency and avoid dimensional errors
(j) If Length is in metres and Width is in metres, unit of Area is? → Square metres (m²)
Part 2: Real-Life Problem Solutions
Step-by-step methods and mark breakdown
Question 1 Worked Solution Estates Refurbishment Consultation Room
[4 Marks Available]
An estates team refits a clinical consultation room.
The room is rectangular, measuring 5 metres long by 4 metres wide with a ceiling height of 2.6 metres.
The team carries out two jobs:
• Job 1: Install vinyl flooring at £18.50 per m².
• Job 2: Paint the four walls. The total wall area (after subtracting doors and windows) is 42 m². Paint tins cost £22.00 each and cover 10 m² per tin.
Work out the total cost for the flooring and paint.
Floor area: 5 × 4 = 20 m²
[M1]
Flooring cost: 20 m² × £18.50 = £370.00
[M1]
Paint tins needed: 42 ÷ 10 = 4.2 → 5 whole tins; Paint cost: 5 × £22.00 = £110.00
[M1]
Combined total cost: £370.00 + £110.00 = £480.00
[A1]
Correct Final Answer: £480.00 total cost
Quick Check Method: £480 - £370 = £110; £110 ÷ £22 = 5 tins.
Common Mistake to Avoid: Rounding paint down to 4 tins (leaving 2 m² of wall unpainted) or using the ceiling height in the floor area.
