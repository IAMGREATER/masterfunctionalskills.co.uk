Section A: Core Skills (The Basics) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (High-Frequency Functional Skills Exam Spelling Corrections) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Correct the four misspelled words in this formal sentence:
'The hospital accomodation committee will definately recieve the maintenance scedule tomorrow.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Correct 'accomodation' → 'accommodation' (double c, double m) and 'definately' → 'definitely' (-ite-, not -ate-) [A1]
2. Correct 'recieve' → 'receive' (i before e except after c) and 'scedule' → 'schedule' (sch-) [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: accommodation, definitely, receive, schedule
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Spelling 'definitely' with an 'a' or 'accommodation' with only one 'm'.
🔄 Quick Check: Memory hooks: Double C, Double M in accommodation; 'finite' inside definitely; 'c' followed by 'ei' in receive.
Question 2 (Core Skill Application — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Select the correct spelling from each bracket in this clinical sentence:
'The senior consultant gave the apprentice helpful [advice / advise] and encouraged her to [practice / practise] taking blood pressure readings on the training mannequin.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. 'helpful [advice]' is a noun ('a piece of ice') → advice (with a c). [M1]
2. 'to [practise]' is an infinitive verb ('to do something') → practise (with an s). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1. advice (noun) | 2. practise (verb)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Examiners report that thousands of candidates lose marks by misspelling words like 'community', 'accommodation', or 'questionnaire' when those exact words are printed in the exam question above!
🔄 Quick Check: Substitute 'guidance' (noun) vs 'train' (verb): 'helpful guidance' = advice (C); 'to train' = to practise (S).
Section B: Real-Life & Workplace Practice Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 Model: Pharmacy Stock-Take Spelling & Homophone Audit) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Identify and correct the three spelling/homophone errors in this dispensary notice:
'It is neccessary to seperate all expired medication bottles and return them to there original manufacturer by Friday.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Correct 'neccessary': Apply the '1 Collar, 2 Sleeves' rule: one 'c' and double 'ss' → necessary. [A1]
2. Correct 'seperate': Apply the 'a rat' memory hook: sep-a-rat-e → separate. [A1]
3. Correct the homophone 'there': The sentence requires the possessive determiner belonging to the bottles → their. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1. neccessary → necessary | 2. seperate → separate | 3. there → their.
Corrected sentence: 'It is necessary to separate all expired medication bottles and return them to their original manufacturer by Friday.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Always check your spelling of key topic words against the exam prompt paper.
🔄 Quick Check: Keep a personal flashcard of these exact three words—they appear in almost every Functional Skills mock and live exam!
Question 4 (Exam-Style Task — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Select the correct homophone or near-homophone in each workplace sentence below:
1. All delivery vans must remain completely [stationary / stationery] while the loading bay barrier is raised.
2. The new shift pattern will positively [affect / effect] staff morale across the depot.
3. The college can [accommodate / acommodate] up to thirty learners in the new IT suite.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Not moving ('parked car') = stationary (with an 'a'). [M1]
2. Action verb ('will influence') = affect (RAVEN: Affect = Verb). [M1]
3. Double 'cc' and double 'mm' = accommodate. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1. stationary | 2. affect | 3. accommodate
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Writing 'We will loose customers' instead of 'We will lose customers' is a classic phonetic slip ('loose' with double o means not tight; 'lose' with one o means to forfeit).
🔄 Quick Check: Stationary with 'ar' = parked car; Stationery with 'er' = writing paper.
Section C: Exam-Style Tasks Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Model: Specialist Vocational Spelling & C/S Verb Distinction) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Identify and correct the four spelling errors in this Level 2 report extract:
'Following the recent enviromental audit, we reccomend that the GP practise upgrades its heating maintainance schedule and advises staff accordingly.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Correct 'enviromental': Insert the silent 'n' before the suffix '-mental' → environmental. [A1]
2. Correct 'reccomend': Prefix 're-' + 'commend' = one 'c', double 'mm' → recommend. [A1]
3. Correct 'GP practise': Here 'GP practice' is a NOUN (the medical surgery), so it must use a 'c' → practice. [A1]
4. Correct 'maintainance': Root shifts to '-ten-' → maintenance. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1. enviromental → environmental | 2. reccomend → recommend | 3. GP practise → GP practice | 4. maintainance → maintenance.
Corrected sentence: 'Following the recent environmental audit, we recommend that the GP practice upgrades its heating maintenance schedule and advises staff accordingly.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Remember: If you LOSE something, it has lost one of its O's!
🔄 Quick Check: Notice how 'GP practice' (noun = C) and 'advises staff' (verb = S) sit in the same sentence—getting both right proves total command of British English spelling.
Question 6 (Level 2 Mastery Challenge — 4 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Proofread this customer service email extract and correct all four spelling errors:
'We sincerely apologise that you did not recieve your furniture delivery on Wednesday. Unfortunatly, our courier experienced an unexpected vehicle breakdown, and we would like to offer you a full refund on your delivery reciept at your earliest conveniance.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. 'recieve' → 'receive' (i before e except after c). [M1]
2. 'Unfortunatly' → 'Unfortunately' (keep the 'e' in fortunate + ly). [M1]
3. 'reciept' → 'receipt' (after c use ei + silent p). [M1]
4. 'conveniance' → 'convenience' (-ience ending). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1. recieve → receive | 2. Unfortunatly → Unfortunately | 3. reciept → receipt | 4. conveniance → convenience.
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Failing to proofread spelling, punctuation, and grammar after completing the draft.
🔄 Quick Check: When adding '-ly' to words ending in '-te' (fortunate, immediate, definite, separate), never drop the 'e': fortunately, immediately, definitely, separately.
