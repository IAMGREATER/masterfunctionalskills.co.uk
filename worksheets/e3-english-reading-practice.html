Oakfield Library — Autumn Opening Hours & Free Computer Desk
Monday to Friday: 09:30 – 17:30 | Saturday: 10:00 – 14:00 | Sunday: Closed.
• Book a free 45-minute computer slot at the front desk using your library card.
• Black-and-white printing costs 10p per sheet (colour printing costs 30p per sheet).
• Please switch mobile phones to silent inside the Quiet Study Area.
