What are Functional Skills?

Functional skills qualifications in Maths and English demonstrate that you possess the essential skills necessary for career success. Functional Skills give you the tools to communicate effectively and solve problems, two key elements of success in your job. In many cases you will need Functional Skills Level 2 or GCSE qualifications in Maths and English too apply for a new role or promotion. Man UK employers also value commitment to continuous professional development, such as obtaining better qualifications. This can lead to promotions and new opportunities within your current company. Whether you have a role in mind or are still weighing up your options, it can be smart to get a head start on your application and complete your Functional Skills qualifications now. These skills will also help you achieve success in your career, improving you competency in Maths and English.

Benefits of Functional Skills

What are the benefits of Functional Skills Level 2 qualifications over GCSEs?

  • Functional Skills exams can be sat online, whereas GCSEs must be sat in-person

  • Functional Skills assessments are offered throughout the year, while GCSE exams are held only twice

  • Functional Skills qualifications generally take less time to complete compared to GCSEs

  • Functional Skills emphasize practical applications of Mathematics and English that are useful in everyday life

  • These skills can be conveniently learned online, enhancing their flexibility

Maths

The Functional Skills Maths exam is sat virtually under the supervision of an online invigilator. It takes 2 hours, and is split into 2 parts. The first part is a 30-minute non-calculator section and the second is a 90-minute calculator section. Upon passing your exam you will receive your certification to show that you have achieved your Functional Skills Maths qualification.

English

The Functional Skills English exam is sat as three separate exams. These exams test three different components: reading, writing, and SLC (speaking, listening and communication). The reading and writing exams take 1 hour each to complete and are typically sat one after the other in a 2 hour slot. The SLC is often sat separately and takes around 2 hours to complete.

GCSEs For Careers FAQs

Why are GCSEs or functional skills required for my career?

Most courses and highly-skilled jobs require GCSE passes in both maths and English as a minimum, or Functional Skills Level 2. This demonstrates that you have the essential skills and can carry out tasks required in your role.

What GCSEs do you need to be a nurse?

Careers in nursing require GCSEs at grade 4/C in maths and English as a minimum, or Functional Skills Level 2. Some nursing courses may also require GCSE Science or GCSE Biology. It is sensible to check the specific GCSE requirements with your chosen university or training provider before taking a qualification.

Can I get a job without any Level 2 qualifications?

Some entry-level roles, like being a soldier in the army, have no GCSE requirements. However, for the majority of jobs, you will need to complete training courses which often require a minimum of GCSEs in both maths and English or an equivalent functional skills qualification.

Can I take a GCSE equivalent instead?

Yes, you can take an equivalent qualification such as a functional skills level 2 qualification in maths and English to meet the requirements for certain career paths. Always check any entry requirements for your career with the relevant admissions team to ensure your alternative is accepted. If you are unsure if functional skills might unlock career paths for you, contact our support team who can discuss your options with you.

Will I need both maths and English GCSEs?

Maths and English GCSEs are often the core requirements for many different roles across many sectors. This is because achieving GCSEs in these subjects proves that you have core skills in numeracy and literacy required to succeed at work and beyond. Functional Skills Maths and Functional Skills English are another option for building these skills, with content that focuses more on real-life applications, making them more accessible to those less familiar with the education setting.

What grades do I need in my GCSE exams?

The grade you need in your GCSEs will depend on the role you are applying to. If an application asks for a GCSE in any subject, you will need to ensure at least a passing grade of 4/C. Grade 4/C at GCSE is equivalent to Functional Skills Level 2. Prospective surgeons, for example, will be expected to achieve high grades to prove that they are more likely to meet the demands of the job.
