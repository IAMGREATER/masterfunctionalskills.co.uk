Time, 24-Hour Clocks & Timetables Pearson Edexcel: L1.16 • L1.17 • L2.18
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Core Skills (Warm-Up) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Level 1 Elapsed Shift Duration) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A phlebotomist starts her morning clinic at 07:45 and finishes at 13:20.
How long is her clinic session in hours and minutes?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Bridge across hours: 07:45 to 08:00 = 15 mins; 08:00 to 13:00 = 5 hours; 13:00 to 13:20 = 20 mins [M1]
2. Sum duration: 5 hours 35 minutes (or 335 minutes) [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 5 hours 35 minutes
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Subtracting 13.20 - 7.45 as base-100 decimals to get 5 hours 75 minutes.
🔄 Quick Check: 07:45 + 5 hrs = 12:45; 12:45 + 35 mins = 13:20.
Question 2 (Level 2 Decimal Hours Conversion) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A flight data recorder logs a total airborne time of 3.65 hours.
Convert 3.65 hours into hours and minutes.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Multiply decimal fraction of an hour by 60: 0.65 × 60 = 39 minutes [M1]
2. Combine with whole hours: 3 hours 39 minutes [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 3 hours 39 minutes
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Writing 3 hours 65 minutes (or 4 hours 5 minutes) by treating .65 as 65 minutes.
🔄 Quick Check: 39 ÷ 60 = 0.65; 3 + 0.65 = 3.65 hours.
Section B: Everyday & Workplace Maths Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 Multi-Stage Surgical Theatre Schedule) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
An operating theatre starts pre-op preparation at 08:50.
• Pre-op preparation takes 35 minutes.
• The surgical procedure lasts 2 hours 40 minutes.
• Post-op theatre deep-cleaning takes 25 minutes.

At what time (in 24-hour clock format) is the theatre ready for the next patient?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Sum total minutes: 35 mins + 160 mins (2h 40m) + 25 mins = 220 minutes [M1]
2. Convert 220 minutes to hours and minutes: 220 ÷ 60 = 3 hours 40 minutes [P1]
3. Add to 08:50 start time: 08:50 + 3 hrs = 11:50; 11:50 + 40 mins = 12:30 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 12:30
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Adding 50 mins + 40 mins = 90 mins and writing 11:90 instead of carrying 60 minutes into 12:30.
🔄 Quick Check: 12:30 - 3 hrs 40 mins = 08:50.
Question 4 (Level 2 Overnight Shift Pay Calculation) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A night security officer works from 20:45 on Friday until 06:15 on Saturday.
Each shift includes an unpaid break of 30 minutes.
The officer is paid £13.60 per paid hour.

Calculate the officer's gross pay for this night shift.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Total shift duration across midnight: 20:45 to 24:00 = 3 hrs 15 mins; 00:00 to 06:15 = 6 hrs 15 mins → Total = 9 hrs 30 mins [M1]
2. Deduct 30-minute unpaid break: 9 hrs 30 mins - 30 mins = 9.0 paid hours [P1]
3. Multiply paid hours by hourly rate: 9 × £13.60 = £122.40 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £122.40
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Forgetting to deduct the 30-minute unpaid break (paying for 9.5 hours = £129.20).
🔄 Quick Check: £122.40 ÷ £13.60 = 9 paid hours + 0.5 hr break = 9.5 hrs (20:45 to 06:15).
Section C: Exam-Style Problem Solving Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Timetable Backward Planning) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A medical rep must attend a hospital presentation in Birmingham starting at 11:15.
• It takes 18 minutes to walk from Birmingham New Street station to the hospital.
• Train departures from London Euston (and arrival times at Birmingham) are:
– Train A: Departs 08:40 → Arrives 10:02
– Train B: Departs 09:10 → Arrives 10:32
– Train C: Departs 09:40 → Arrives 11:02
• It takes the rep 35 minutes to travel from home to London Euston, and she wants to arrive at Euston at least 10 minutes before her train departs.

What is the latest time she can leave home to arrive at the hospital on time?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Latest station arrival at Birmingham: 11:15 - 18 mins walk = 10:57 [P1]
2. Select latest valid train: Train C (arrives 11:02) is 5 mins too late; must catch Train B (departs 09:10, arrives 10:32) [M1]
3. Latest arrival at London Euston: 09:10 - 10 mins buffer = 09:00 [P1]
4. Subtract 35 mins travel from home: 09:00 - 35 mins = 08:25 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 08:25 (catching Train B at 09:10)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Selecting Train C (11:02 arrival + 18 mins walk = 11:20, which is 5 minutes late).
🔄 Quick Check: 08:25 + 35m = 09:00 (+10m buffer = 09:10 Train B) → arrives 10:32 + 18m walk = 10:50 (before 11:15).
Question 6 (Level 2 Clinic Appointment Scheduling Capacity) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
An optometrist runs a testing clinic from 09:00 to 16:30.
• Each patient appointment lasts 25 minutes, followed by a mandatory 5-minute room sanitisation turnaround before the next patient.
• The optometrist takes a 45-minute lunch break and two 15-minute admin breaks during the day.

The receptionist has booked 13 patients for today. Does the schedule have enough time to see all 13 patients?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Total clinic window (09:00 to 16:30): 7 hours 30 minutes = 450 minutes [P1]
2. Total breaks: 45 + 15 + 15 = 75 minutes → Available clinical time = 450 - 75 = 375 minutes [P1]
3. Time per patient slot (appointment + turnaround): 25 + 5 = 30 minutes per patient [M1]
4. Compare: 13 patients × 30 mins = 390 mins needed > 375 mins available (or 375 ÷ 30 = 12.5 → max 12 patients). No, there is not enough time (short by 15 minutes). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: No (13 patients require 390 mins [or 385 mins if last turnaround omitted], exceeding the 375 available minutes)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Dividing 375 by 25 minutes and forgetting the 5-minute sanitisation turnaround per patient.
🔄 Quick Check: 12 patients × 30 mins = 360 mins + 75 mins breaks = 435 mins (fits in 450 mins; 13th needs 465 mins).
