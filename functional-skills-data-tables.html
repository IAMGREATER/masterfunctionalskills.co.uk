Golden Rules for Data Tables & Two-Way Tables

Golden Rule 1: Row Totals and Column Totals Must Cross-Check to the Grand Total

In every two-way table, the sum of the row totals MUST equal the sum of the column totals in the bottom-right Grand Total cell. Start with any row or column that has only ONE missing number.

Golden Rule / Method: Sum of Row Totals = Sum of Column Totals = Grand Total
Golden Rule 2: Read Conditional Probability Denominators Carefully

If asked for the probability from "all staff", divide by the Grand Total. If asked "Given that a nurse works the Night Shift...", divide ONLY by the Night Shift row/column total!

Golden Rule / Method: "Of all..." → Divide by Grand Total • "Of the Night Shift..." → Divide by Subgroup Total

Core Skill 2: Extracting Percentages & Fractions from Two-Way Tables

Selecting the right cell and the right total denominator.

  • Fraction of ALL staff who are Night RNs: 15 / 60 = 1/4 (25%).
  • Percentage of RNs who work Nights: (15 ÷ 35) × 100 = 42.9% (Denominator is 35 RNs, not 60!).

Core Skill 3: Triangular Distance / Mileage Charts & Multi-Criteria Tariff Tables

Intersecting row and column headers in depot mileage grids.

To read a triangular mileage table between two towns (e.g. Oxford and Bristol):

  1. Locate Oxford along the horizontal row and Bristol along the vertical column.
  2. Follow both lines straight until they meet at the single intersecting cell.
  3. For a multi-stop round trip (Depot → Town A → Town B → Depot), extract all three leg distances and add them together.
Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Pearson Edexcel Step-by-Step Worked Models

Benchmark Exam Model 1 3 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 1/2)

Vocational Context: Healthcare Staffing Workforce Management

A hospital ward employs 60 nursing staff across Day and Night shifts. 35 staff are Registered Nurses (RNs) and the rest are Healthcare Assistants (HCAs). 20 RNs work Day shifts. In total, 38 staff work Day shifts.
(a) Find how many HCAs work Night shifts.
(b) What percentage of the total 60 staff work Night shifts (to 1 decimal place)?
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Calculate total HCAs and Day HCAs
Total HCAs = 60 − 35 = 25 HCAs.
Day HCAs = 38 − 20 = 18 HCAs.
Criteria: M1: Row/column subtraction
Step 2 Calculate Night HCAs
Night HCAs = 25 − 18 = 7 HCAs (Check: Total Night = 60 − 38 = 22; Night RNs = 35 − 20 = 15; 15 + 7 = 22).
Criteria: A1: Accurate Night HCA count (7)
Step 3 Calculate percentage of total staff on Night shift
(22 ÷ 60) × 100 = 36.66...% → 36.7%.
Criteria: A1: Accurate percentage (36.7%)
Final Accredited Answer: (a) 7 HCAs work Night shifts | (b) 36.7%
Method Checklist & Marking Scheme Guidance
Always cross-check that both row totals (35 + 25 = 60) and column totals (38 + 22 = 60) add up to the Grand Total.
Benchmark Exam Model 2 4 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 2)

Vocational Context: Clinical Mandatory Training Compliance

120 staff sat a mandatory resuscitation assessment after choosing either In-Person Training or Online E-Learning:
• 75 staff chose In-Person Training, and 66 of them passed.
• Overall across both methods, 96 staff passed in total.
Compare the percentage pass rate of In-Person Training against Online E-Learning.
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Calculate Online E-Learning total candidates and passes
Online Total = 120 − 75 = 45 staff.
Online Passes = 96 − 66 = 30 staff.
Criteria: M1: Deducting online subgroup totals (30 out of 45)
Step 2 Calculate In-Person percentage pass rate
(66 ÷ 75) × 100 = 88%.
Criteria: M1: In-Person pass rate (88%)
Step 3 Calculate Online percentage pass rate and compare
(30 ÷ 45) × 100 = 66.7%.
In-Person Training achieved a much higher pass rate (88% vs 66.7%).
Criteria: M1 / A1: Online pass rate (66.7%) and comparative conclusion
Final Accredited Answer: In-Person Pass Rate = 88% vs Online Pass Rate = 66.7% (In-Person was higher by 21.3%)
Method Checklist & Marking Scheme Guidance
To find the pass rate OF In-Person candidates, divide 66 by 75 (not 120!).

Critical Examiner Traps & Common Pitfalls

Examiner Pitfall: Dividing by the Grand Total Instead of the Subgroup Total
When asked "What percentage of the Night Shift staff are RNs?", dividing 15 by 60 instead of 15 by 22.
How to Avoid This Pitfall: The group immediately following "of the..." is always your bottom denominator!
Examiner Pitfall: Leaving Blank Cells in a Two-Way Table Question
Even if the question only asks for one specific cell at the end, examiners award method marks for filling in the entire table.
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Fill in every single cell in the printed two-way table and check all row and column sums.

Interactive Exam Practice Questions

Exam Practice Question 1 2 Marks
In a logistics hub of 100 drivers, 65 drive daytime routes and 35 drive night routes. 40 of the daytime drivers operate articulated lorries, and 20 of the night drivers operate articulated lorries. The rest drive rigid vans. How many night drivers operate rigid vans?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Total night drivers = 35.
2. Night articulated drivers = 20.
3. Night rigid van drivers = 35 − 20 = 15 drivers.
Final Correct Answer: 15 drivers
Examiner Tip: Subtract the 20 night articulated drivers from the 35 total night drivers.
Exam Practice Question 2 3 Marks
A café records 200 drink orders (Hot or Cold, Eat-In or Takeaway). 130 drinks are Hot. 85 orders are Eat-In, of which 60 are Hot.
Calculate how many Cold Takeaway drinks were ordered.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Total Cold drinks = 200 − 130 = 70.
2. Eat-In Cold drinks = 85 − 60 = 25.
3. Takeaway Cold drinks = 70 − 25 = 45 drinks.
Final Correct Answer: 45 Cold Takeaway drinks
Examiner Tip: Sketch a quick 2×2 grid to keep Hot/Cold and Eat-In/Takeaway clear.
Exam Practice Question 3 3 Marks
Using the café data in Question 2, if one Eat-In customer is chosen at random, what is the probability that they ordered a Cold drink? Give your answer as a simplified fraction.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Total Eat-In customers = 85.
2. Eat-In Cold customers = 25.
3. Probability = 25 / 85 = 5 / 17 (dividing top and bottom by 5).
Final Correct Answer: 5/17
Examiner Tip: Since the customer is chosen from Eat-In customers, the denominator is 85, not 200.
