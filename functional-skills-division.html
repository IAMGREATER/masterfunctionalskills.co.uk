Specification & Learning Outcomes

Candidates must demonstrate mastery of the following regulated Functional Skills Mathematics outcomes:

  • L1.3 / L2.3 – Divide whole numbers and decimals by whole numbers up to 2-digit divisors.
  • L1.8 / L2.4 – Interpret remainders appropriately depending on context (e.g. packaging crates, transport minibuses).

Golden Rules for Division & Remainders

Golden Rule 1: Practical Context Dictates How You Treat Remainders

If booking minibuses, buying paint tins, or ordering tile packs, you MUST round UP so nothing is left behind or uncovered. If calculating how many complete boxes can be filled or items afforded on a fixed budget, round DOWN.

Golden Rule / Method: People / Tins / Packs → Round UP | Complete Boxes / Affordability → Round DOWN
Golden Rule 2: Turn Remainders into Decimals with .00

To convert a remainder into an exact decimal or money figure, place a decimal point and add trailing zeros (.00) to the dividend under the bus stop.

Golden Rule / Method: £145 ÷ 4 = 145.00 ÷ 4 = £36.25

Core Skill 1: Skill 1: The Short Division Bus-Stop Method with Decimals

Dividing step-by-step with carrying and trailing decimal zeros.

To calculate £518 ÷ 4 without a calculator:

  1. 4 into 5 goes 1 time, remainder 1 (carry 1 to make 11).
  2. 4 into 11 goes 2 times (8), remainder 3 (carry 3 to make 38).
  3. 4 into 38 goes 9 times (36), remainder 2. Add decimal point and trailing zeros: .00 (carry 2 to make 20).
  4. 4 into 20 goes 5 times, 0 into 0. Result: £129.50.
Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 2: Skill 2: Dividing by 2-Digit Numbers Using Chunking / Multiples List

Failsafe method for non-calculator division by numbers like 15, 16, or 24.

Before dividing by a 2-digit divisor (e.g. 432 ÷ 18), write a quick multiples ladder in the margin:

  • 1 × 18 = 18 | 2 × 18 = 36 | 4 × 18 = 72 | 10 × 18 = 180 | 20 × 18 = 360
  • Subtract the largest chunk: 432 - 360 (20 × 18) = 72 remaining.
  • Spot the remaining chunk: 72 = 4 × 18.
  • Combine chunks: 20 + 4 = 24.
Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 3: Skill 3: Contextual Remainder Decision Matrix (Round Up vs Round Down)

How to score the final accuracy mark on contextual division problems.

Workplace Scenario Raw Division Correct Action Final Answer
Minibuses for 142 patients (16 seats) 142 ÷ 16 = 8.875 Round UP (cannot leave 14 behind) 9 minibuses
Full boxes of 12 from 490 bottles 490 ÷ 12 = 40.833 Round DOWN (only full boxes count) 40 full boxes

Pearson Edexcel Step-by-Step Worked Models

Benchmark Exam Model 1 3 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 1/2)

Vocational Context: Hospital Patient Shuttle Service

An NHS outpatient centre needs to transport 142 patients to an off-site clinic. Each patient shuttle minibus carries a maximum of 16 passengers.
(a) How many minibuses must be booked?
(b) How many empty seats will be on the final minibus?
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Divide total patients by bus capacity
142 ÷ 16 = 8 remainder 14 (or 8.875)
Criteria: M1: Division setup
Step 2 Interpret remainder in practical transport context
8 buses carry 128 passengers, leaving 14 patients stranded. Therefore, 9 minibuses must be booked.
Criteria: A1: Correctly rounding UP to 9 buses
Step 3 Calculate empty seats on final minibus
Total capacity: 9 × 16 = 144 seats.
Empty seats: 144 - 142 = 2 empty seats (or 16 - 14 = 2).
Criteria: B1: Correct empty seat calculation
Final Accredited Answer: (a) 9 minibuses must be booked | (b) 2 empty seats on the final minibus.
Method Checklist & Marking Scheme Guidance
In passenger transport problems, you can NEVER leave patients behind. You must always round up to the next whole vehicle.
Benchmark Exam Model 2 4 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 2 Problem Solving)

Vocational Context: Pharmaceutical Packaging Line

A pharmaceutical batch produces 15,500 paracetamol caplets.
• Each blister strip holds 16 caplets.
• Every carton holds 6 complete blister strips.
• A hospital pharmacy orders 160 cartons.

Does this single batch produce enough complete cartons to fulfil the hospital order?
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Calculate total caplets required per carton
16 caplets/strip × 6 strips/carton = 96 caplets per carton.
Criteria: M1: Caplets per carton calculation
Step 2 Divide total batch caplets by caplets per carton
15,500 ÷ 96 = 161.458... cartons.
Criteria: M1: Division of batch total by 96
Step 3 Round down to complete cartons and find leftover caplets
Only 161 complete cartons can be packed (161 × 96 = 15,456 caplets used; 44 left over).
Criteria: M1: Contextual rounding down to 161 complete cartons
Step 4 Compare against 160-carton order and conclude
161 complete cartons > 160 cartons ordered. Yes, the batch is sufficient with 1 complete carton (and 44 loose caplets) to spare.
Criteria: A1: Valid conclusion supported by 161 complete cartons
Final Accredited Answer: Yes, sufficient (Produces 161 complete cartons, exceeding the 160-carton order by 1 carton).
Method Checklist & Marking Scheme Guidance
You can also check by multiplying: 160 cartons × 96 caplets = 15,360 caplets needed, which is less than 15,500!

Critical Examiner Traps & Common Pitfalls

Examiner Pitfall: Writing Decimal Remainders for Indivisible Units
Answering '8.875 minibuses' earns zero marks for the final answer because you cannot book 0.875 of a vehicle.
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Always round to a whole integer when dealing with people, vehicles, or physical packaging.

Interactive Exam Practice Questions

Exam Practice Question 1 2 Marks
A factory produces 490 bottles of hand sanitizer. The bottles are packed into shipping boxes of 12 bottles each. How many complete boxes can be shipped, and how many bottles are left over?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. 490 ÷ 12 = 40 remainder 10 (since 40 × 12 = 480).
2. Complete boxes = 40; Leftover bottles = 10.
Final Correct Answer: 40 complete boxes (10 bottles left over)
Examiner Tip: Notice the question asks for 'complete boxes', so here you round DOWN to 40.
Exam Practice Question 2 2 Marks
Six colleagues win a syndicate prize of £813 and split it equally. How much does each person receive? Give your answer in pounds and pence.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Set up short division with decimals: 813.00 ÷ 6.
2. 813 ÷ 6 = 135 remainder 3; 3.00 ÷ 6 = 0.50.
3. Each colleague receives £135.50.
Final Correct Answer: £135.50
Examiner Tip: Always include the trailing zero (£135.50, never £135.5).
Exam Practice Question 3 3 Marks
A decorator needs to tile a clinic wall with an area of 26 m². Tiles are sold in boxes that cover 1.5 m² each and cost £24.00 per box. Calculate the total cost of the boxes required.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Number of boxes = 26 ÷ 1.5 = 260 ÷ 15 = 17.333... boxes.
2. Round UP to 18 whole boxes so the wall is fully tiled.
3. Total cost = 18 × £24.00 = £432.00.
Final Correct Answer: £432.00 (18 boxes)
Examiner Tip: Rounding down to 17 boxes would only cover 25.5 m², leaving 0.5 m² bare!
