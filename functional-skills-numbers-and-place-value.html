Specification & Learning Outcomes

Candidates must demonstrate mastery of the following regulated Functional Skills Mathematics outcomes:

  • L1.1 / L2.1 – Read, write, order and compare large positive whole numbers up to 1,000,000 and beyond.
  • L2.2 – Recognise and use numbers given in figures and in words in workplace documents.

Golden Rules for Numbers & Place Value

Golden Rule 1: The Zero Placeholder Protects Positional Value

A digit's value is determined entirely by its column. If a column has no value, you MUST write a zero as a placeholder (e.g. two million and five thousand is 2,005,000, NOT 2,5000).

Golden Rule / Method: Millions | H-Th | T-Th | Th | Hundreds | Tens | Units
Golden Rule 2: Group Digits in Threes from the Decimal Point

When reading or writing large numbers, work in blocks of three digits separated by commas or thin spaces (e.g. 14,250,800).

Golden Rule / Method: 14,250,800 = 14 million, 250 thousand, 800

Core Skill 2: Translating Between Words and Figures Using Period Blocks

How to convert complex written figures into exact digits without dropping zeros.

Always split large written numbers at the words "million" and "thousand" into three 3-digit blocks:

  1. Millions Block: Write the number of millions (e.g. "Six million" → 6,___,___).
  2. Thousands Block (3 digits): Pad with leading zeros if under 100 thousand (e.g. "nine thousand" → 009, giving 6,009,___).
  3. Units Block (3 digits): Pad with leading zeros if under 100 (e.g. "and forty-five" → 045, giving 6,009,045).
Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 3: Multiplying & Dividing by Powers of 10 (10, 100, 1,000)

Shifting digits across place value columns in non-calculator papers.

Operation Place Value Shift Worked Example
Multiply by 10 / 100 / 1,000 Shift digits 1, 2, or 3 places LEFT 42.5 × 1,000 = 42,500
Divide by 10 / 100 / 1,000 Shift digits 1, 2, or 3 places RIGHT 3,800 ÷ 100 = 38
Converting "Million" Figures Multiply decimal millions by 1,000,000 (shift 6 places) £2.45 million = £2,450,000

Pearson Edexcel Step-by-Step Worked Models

Benchmark Exam Model 1 3 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 1 Non-Calculator)

Vocational Context: Healthcare Capital Expenditure

A hospital procurement circular states the capital investment in surgical imaging equipment is £4,082,500.
(a) Write this amount in words.
(b) State the exact value of the digit 8 in this number.
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Break the number into three-digit periods
4 millions, 082 thousands, 500 units.
Criteria: M1: Place value period identification
Step 2 Write in formal words
Four million, eighty-two thousand, five hundred pounds.
Criteria: A1: Accurate word translation
Step 3 Determine the specific value of digit 8
The digit 8 is in the ten-thousands column: 8 × 10,000 = 80,000 (eighty thousand).
Criteria: A1: Correct digit value
Final Accredited Answer: (a) Four million, eighty-two thousand, five hundred pounds | (b) Eighty thousand (£80,000).
Method Checklist & Marking Scheme Guidance
Notice the hundred-thousands column has a 0. Do NOT say 'four million, eight hundred thousand'; it is eighty-two thousand.
Benchmark Exam Model 2 4 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 2 Problem Solving)

Vocational Context: Public Sector Budget Reconciliation

A regional health authority receives a total grant of £3.4 million.
• Hospital A is allocated one million, six hundred and fifty thousand pounds (£1,650,000).
• Hospital B is allocated £985,000.
• Hospital C requests eight hundred thousand pounds from the remaining balance.

Does the authority have enough remaining funding to approve Hospital C's full request? Show your working in figures.
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Convert all written amounts into full standard figures
Total grant: £3.4 million = £3,400,000.
Hospital A = £1,650,000.
Hospital C request = £800,000.
Criteria: M1: Accurate conversion of decimal/word millions into digits
Step 2 Calculate total allocated to Hospital A and Hospital B
£1,650,000 + £985,000 = £2,635,000.
Criteria: M1: Combined allocation addition
Step 3 Calculate remaining balance and compare with Hospital C request
Remaining = £3,400,000 - £2,635,000 = £765,000.
Compare: £765,000 < £800,000 (shortfall of £35,000).
Criteria: M1: Subtraction and comparison
Step 4 State final justified conclusion
No, the authority does not have enough funding; only £765,000 remains, which is £35,000 less than the £800,000 requested.
Criteria: A1: Clear decision supported by exact figures
Final Accredited Answer: No (Only £765,000 remains, leaving a shortfall of £35,000 against the £800,000 request).
Method Checklist & Marking Scheme Guidance
Converting £3.4 million to £3,400,000 first prevents decimal alignment mistakes when subtracting hundreds of thousands.

Critical Examiner Traps & Common Pitfalls

Examiner Pitfall: Omitting Placeholder Zeros
Writing 'four million and twelve' as 4,12 or 4012 instead of 4,000,012 loses the accuracy mark entirely.
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Always sketch 7 column dashes (_ , _ _ _ , _ _ _) and populate from the units column.

Interactive Exam Practice Questions

Exam Practice Question 1 2 Marks
A logistics firm handled 1,050,402 parcels in November.
(a) Write this number in words.
(b) State the value of the digit 5.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. In words: One million, fifty thousand, four hundred and two.
2. Digit 5 is in the ten-thousands column: 50,000 (fifty thousand).
Final Correct Answer: (a) One million, fifty thousand, four hundred and two | (b) 50,000
Examiner Tip: Check that you do not omit the placeholder zero in the hundred-thousands and tens columns.
Exam Practice Question 2 2 Marks
A council recycling report states that two million, three hundred and six thousand, and eighty glass bottles were collected. Write this number in figures and state what happens to the value of the digit 3 if the number is divided by 10.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Millions block: 2 | Thousands block: 306 | Units block: 080 → 2,306,080.
2. The digit 3 currently represents 300,000. Divided by 10, it shifts one column right to represent 30,000.
Final Correct Answer: 2,306,080 (Digit 3 changes value from 300,000 to 30,000)
Examiner Tip: Remember to write 080 for "eighty" in the final three-digit block.
Exam Practice Question 3 3 Marks
A community healthcare vaccination hub receives 1.25 million syringes. In Quarter 1 it distributes 480,000 syringes, and in Quarter 2 it distributes five hundred and five thousand syringes. How many syringes are left in central storage?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Convert to figures: Total = 1,250,000; Q1 = 480,000; Q2 = 505,000.
2. Total distributed = 480,000 + 505,000 = 985,000.
3. Remaining = 1,250,000 - 985,000 = 265,000 syringes.
Final Correct Answer: 265,000 syringes
Examiner Tip: 1.25 million has 6 digits after the 1: 1,250,000.
