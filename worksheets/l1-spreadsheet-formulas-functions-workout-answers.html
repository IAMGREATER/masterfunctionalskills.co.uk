Task 1 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

Which formula correctly calculates the arithmetic mean of values in cells B2 to B8?

A. =AVERAGE(B2:B8) (CORRECT)
B. =MEAN(B2:B8)
C. =SUM(B2:B8)/COUNT
D. =TOTAL(B2:B8)
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

=AVERAGE() is the built-in spreadsheet function used to find the mean value.

Task 2 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What keyboard symbol is used in spreadsheets to MULTIPLY two numbers or cell references?

A. * (Asterisk) (CORRECT)
B. x (Letter x)
C. • (Bullet point)
D. & (Ampersand)
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

The asterisk (*) is the mathematical operator for multiplication in spreadsheets and programming.

Task 3 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What happens when you drag the Fill Handle down from a cell containing '=B2*C2'?

A. The formula copies down, automatically adjusting to =B3*C3, =B4*C4, etc. (CORRECT)
B. It deletes all formulas in the column
C. It turns the cells into pie charts
D. It freezes the computer
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Relative cell references increment automatically when copied with the Fill Handle.
