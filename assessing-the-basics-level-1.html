Specification & Learning Outcomes

Candidates must demonstrate mastery of the following regulated Functional Skills English outcomes:

  • L1.17 – Punctuate writing correctly using a wide range of punctuation markers (full stops, commas, apostrophes, colons, semicolons).
  • L1.18 – Use correct grammar including subject-verb agreement, consistent verb tense, and standard English modality.
  • L1.19 – Spell words used in work, study, and daily life accurately, including specialist vocabulary and homophones.

Golden Rules for Core Literacy Baseline: Grammar, Punctuation & Spelling

Golden Rule 1: Master the Non-Negotiable Homophone & Apostrophe Trio

Examiners immediately spot errors in: (1) They're / Their / There, (2) You're / Your, and (3) It's / Its. Remember: 'It's' ALWAYS means 'It is' or 'It has'. Possessive 'its' (belonging to it) NEVER takes an apostrophe.

Golden Rule / Technique: It's = It is | Its = Belonging to it (NO apostrophe!) | They're = They are
Golden Rule 2: Enforce Subject-Verb & Tense Agreement

A singular subject takes a singular verb ('The team of nurses IS ready'); a plural subject takes a plural verb ('The nurses ARE ready'). Never write 'we was' or 'patients was'.

Golden Rule / Technique: Singular: The box of gloves IS full | Plural: The gloves ARE in the box

Core Skill 1: The Core Punctuation Toolkit (Level 1)

Exact rules for the five punctuation marks that determine your SPaG band.

Punctuation Mark Core Rule Correct Workplace Example
Full Stop (.) Ends a complete independent statement. "The audit finished at 4 p.m. All records matched."
Comma (,) Separates list items, follows fronted adverbials, or brackets extra info. "However, before starting the shift, staff must sign the log."
Apostrophe (') Shows omission (don't) or possession (singular 's vs plural s'). "One patient's chart" (1 patient) vs "The nurses' locker room" (multiple nurses).
Colon (:) Follows a complete clause to introduce a list, explanation, or verdict. "Bring three items: photo ID, steel-toe boots, and a pad."
Semicolon (;) Links two closely related complete sentences without a conjunction. "The ward is full; elective admissions have been paused."
Level 1 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 2: Singular vs Plural Possessive Apostrophes Made Simple

How to place the apostrophe accurately every single time.

Ask yourself: Who owns the item — one person/thing, or more than one?

  • Singular Owner (Add 's): the manager's office (one manager), Mr James's van, the company's profits.
  • Regular Plural Owner Ending in -s (Add ' after the s): the residents' lounge (for many residents), four weeks' notice, employees' pensions.
  • Irregular Plural Owner NOT Ending in -s (Add 's): children's ward, men's changing room, women's health clinic, people's views.
Level 1 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 3: High-Frequency Vocational Spelling Hit-List

Memorize the top 12 words adult learners misspell under exam pressure.

  • Ac-comm-o-da-tion (double C, double M)
  • Nec-ess-ary (1 Collar, 2 Sleeves: 1 C, 2 S)
  • Rec-omm-end (1 C, double M)
  • Opp-or-tun-ity (double P)
  • En-vir-on-ment (silent N before M)
  • Gov-ern-ment (silent N before M)
  • Sep-a-rate ('there is A RAT in separate')
  • Def-i-nite-ly (finite, no 'A'!)
  • Sin-cere-ly (keep the E before LY)
  • Im-med-i-ate-ly (double M)
  • Col-league (double L, ends in -gue)
  • Main-ten-ance (ten, not tain!)
Level 1 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Pearson Edexcel Step-by-Step Worked Models

Benchmark Exam Model 1 3 Marks (Pearson Edexcel SPaG Check)

Vocational Context: Reception Desk Handover Note

Read this handover note and correct three errors in homophones or apostrophes:
'Please tell the visitors that there permits are at reception. The childrens play area is closed because it's floor is being polished.' [3 Marks]
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Fix Homophone Error (there vs their)
'there permits' refers to permits belonging to the visitors, so it must be spelled 'their permits'.
Criteria: 1 Mark [Homophone]
Step 2 Fix Irregular Plural Possessive Apostrophe
'childrens play area' requires an apostrophe before the s because 'children' is already plural: 'children's play area'.
Criteria: 1 Mark [Possessive Apostrophe]
Step 3 Fix Possessive Pronoun 'its'
'it's floor' means 'it is floor', which is incorrect. Possessive 'its' has no apostrophe: 'its floor'.
Criteria: 1 Mark [Its vs It's]
Final Accredited Response: 'Please tell the visitors that their permits are at reception. The children's play area is closed because its floor is being polished.'
Marking Scheme Guidance & Assessment Criteria
Because 'children', 'men', and 'women' are already plural without an 's', the apostrophe always goes BEFORE the 's' ('children's', 'women's').

Critical Examiner Traps & Common Pitfalls

Examiner Pitfall: Confusing 'Should of' with 'Should have'
Writing 'we should of ordered more PPE' is a spoken phonetic habit that heavily damages SPaG marks. The correct modal verb phrase is always 'should have'.
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Always write 'should have', 'could have', and 'would have' in full.
Examiner Pitfall: Putting Apostrophes in Simple Plural Nouns (Greengrocer's Apostrophe)
Writing 'Two lorry's arrived with ten box's of apples' is incorrect because nobody owns anything — they are simple plurals.
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Never use an apostrophe just to make a word plural: write 'lorries' and 'boxes'.

Interactive Exam Practice Questions

Exam Practice Question 1 3 Marks
Insert the missing punctuation and correct the homophone in this sentence:
'The nurses station requires three items sanitiser gloves and aprons however its currently out of stock.'
Marking Scheme Criteria:
1. Possessive apostrophe: 'nurses' station' (or 'nurse's station') [1 Mark].
2. Colon and list commas: '...requires three items: sanitiser, gloves, and aprons' [1 Mark].
3. Sentence boundary & contraction: '; however, it's (or it is) currently out of stock.' [1 Mark].
Final Model Answer: 'The nurses' station requires three items: sanitiser, gloves, and aprons; however, it is currently out of stock.'
Examiner Tip: Here 'its currently out of stock' means 'it is currently out of stock', so expand it to 'it is' (or 'it's') and precede 'however' with a semicolon or full stop.
Exam Practice Question 2 3 Marks
Correct the three spellingerrors in this workplace sentence:
'We definately recomend booking overnight accomodation before the conference.'
Marking Scheme Criteria:
1. 'definately' -> 'definitely' (spelled with an 'i', from finite) [1 Mark].
2. 'recomend' -> 'recommend' (one c, double m) [1 Mark].
3. 'accomodation' -> 'accommodation' (double c, double m) [1 Mark].
Final Model Answer: 'We definitely recommend booking overnight accommodation before the conference.'
Examiner Tip: Definitely, recommend, and accommodation are three of the most frequently tested spellings across all awarding bodies.
Exam Practice Question 3 2 Marks
Choose the correct verb form in brackets for each sentence:
(a) 'The box of sterile syringes (is / are) stored in the locked cabinet.'
(b) 'Neither the supervisor nor the operatives (was / were) informed of the delay.'
Marking Scheme Criteria:
1. Sentence (a): The head noun of the subject is singular ('The box'), not 'syringes', so the verb must be singular: 'is' [1 Mark].
2. Sentence (b): With 'neither... nor', the verb agrees with the closer plural noun ('operatives'), so the verb is plural: 'were' [1 Mark].
Final Model Answer: (a) is ('The box... is stored'). | (b) were ('...the operatives were informed').
Examiner Tip: In sentence (a), cross out the prepositional phrase 'of sterile syringes' — you are left with 'The box is stored', which makes the singular subject obvious!
