Fraction Basics: Halves, Quarters & Thirds Pearson Edexcel: Pearson Edexcel L1 & L2 Mathematics
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Core Skills (Warm-Up) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Unit & Non-Unit Fractions of a Ward Budget) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A rehabilitation unit has a weekly refreshment budget of £180.
The unit spends 1/4 of the budget on fresh fruit and 1/3 of the budget on tea and coffee.
How much money is spent in total on these two items?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Fruit = 180 ÷ 4 = £45; Tea/Coffee = 180 ÷ 3 = £60 [M1]
2. Combined spend: £45 + £60 = £105 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £105
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Dividing £180 by 7 (adding denominators 4 + 3).
🔄 Quick Check: £105 / £180 = 7/12 = 3/12 + 4/12.
Question 2 (Level 1 Foundation — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Calculate 2/5 of 85 metres.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Find 1/5: 85 ÷ 5 = 17 metres. [M1]
2. Find 2/5: 17 × 2 = 34 metres. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 34 metres
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: To find 3/4 of 60, dividing 60 by 3 (= 20) instead of dividing 60 by 4 (= 15).
🔄 Quick Check: 85 ÷ 5 = 17, then 17 × 2 = 34.
Section B: Everyday & Workplace Maths Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Hospital Kitchen Fruit Crate Allocation (Entry 3 / Level 1 Foundation)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A hospital kitchen receives a crate of 72 apples. The chef uses 3/4 of the apples for patient lunch trays.
How many apples does the chef use, and how many are left over?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Divide total apples (72) by denominator (4) to find 1/4: 72 ÷ 4 = 18 apples. [M1]
2. Multiply by numerator (3) and find remainder: Used = 18 × 3 = 54 apples. | Left over = 72 − 54 = 18 apples. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 54 apples used, 18 apples left over
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Always memorise: "Divide by the BOTTOM, multiply by the TOP."
🔄 Quick Check: Notice that if 3/4 are used, 1/4 (18 apples) is left over!
Question 4 (Workplace Application — 2 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Out of 40 staff members on a ward, 28 are female. Write the fraction of staff who are female in its simplest form.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Initial fraction = 28 / 40. [M1]
2. Divide top and bottom by 4: 28 ÷ 4 = 7; 40 ÷ 4 = 10 → 7/10. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 7/10
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: When a whole is cut into more pieces, each piece is smaller! 1/2 (50%) is larger than 1/3 (33.3%), and 1/3 is larger than 1/4 (25%).
🔄 Quick Check: Divide both numerator and denominator by their highest common factor (4).
Section C: Exam-Style Problem Solving Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Ward Shift Budget Allocation Across Three Expense Categories (Level 1/2 Multi-Step)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A clinic manager has a monthly consumables budget of £960.
• She spends 1/3 of the budget on sterile dressings.
• She spends 3/8 of the original £960 budget on PPE gloves.
• The rest of the money is kept for diagnostic test strips.
Calculate how much money (£) is kept for diagnostic test strips.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate 1/3 of £960 for sterile dressings: £960 ÷ 3 = £320. [M1]
2. Calculate 3/8 of £960 for PPE gloves: (£960 ÷ 8) × 3 = £120 × 3 = £360. [M1]
3. Subtract both expenses from £960: £960 − (£320 + £360) = £960 − £680 = £280. [M1 / A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £280 kept for diagnostic test strips
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: For unit fractions (top number 1), the smaller the bottom number, the bigger the slice.
🔄 Quick Check: Check by adding all three parts: £320 + £360 + £280 = £960.
Question 6 (Level 2 Multi-Step Problem Solving — 3 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A charity fun run is 24 km long. A paramedic cyclist has completed 5/8 of the route. How many kilometres does she still have left to cycle?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Completed = (24 ÷ 8) × 5 = 3 × 5 = 15 km. [M1]
2. Remaining = 24 − 15 = 9 km (or 3/8 of 24 = 9 km). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 9 km
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Stating a conclusion without showing the full numerical comparison values.
🔄 Quick Check: Read carefully: the question asks for the distance LEFT to cycle (3/8 of 24 = 9 km).
