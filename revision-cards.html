#1 Area of a Rectangle
Shape & Space
Area = Length × Width
Multiply length by width. Area measures flat 2D space inside a border and is always written in square units.
★ Golden Rule: Always state square units (cm² or m²) for area!
⚠️ Watch Out: Don't add all four sides — that calculates Perimeter, not Area!
