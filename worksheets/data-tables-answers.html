Data Tables & Two-Way Tables Pearson Edexcel: Pearson Edexcel L1 & L2 Mathematics
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Core Skills (Warm-Up) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Completing a Two-Way Clinical Audit Table) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A clinic records 150 patient appointments across Morning and Afternoon sessions:
• Total Morning appointments = 85 (of which 72 attended on time).
• Total Late arrivals across the whole day = 28.

Calculate how many Afternoon appointments attended on time.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Afternoon total = 150 - 85 = 65; Morning late = 85 - 72 = 13 [M1]
2. Afternoon late = 28 - 13 = 15; Afternoon on-time = 65 - 15 = 50 patients [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 50 patients
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Subtracting total late (28) directly from Afternoon total (65).
🔄 Quick Check: On-time total = 72 + 50 = 122; 122 + 28 late = 150 total.
Question 2 (Level 1 Foundation — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
In a logistics hub of 100 drivers, 65 drive daytime routes and 35 drive night routes. 40 of the daytime drivers operate articulated lorries, and 20 of the night drivers operate articulated lorries. The rest drive rigid vans. How many night drivers operate rigid vans?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Total night drivers = 35. [M1]
2. Night articulated drivers = 20. [M1]
3. Night rigid van drivers = 35 − 20 = 15 drivers. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 15 drivers
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: When asked "What percentage of the Night Shift staff are RNs?", dividing 15 by 60 instead of 15 by 22.
🔄 Quick Check: Subtract the 20 night articulated drivers from the 35 total night drivers.
Section B: Everyday & Workplace Maths Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Hospital Clinical Shift Staff Attendance Two-Way Table (Level 1/2 Foundation)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A hospital ward employs 60 nursing staff across Day and Night shifts. 35 staff are Registered Nurses (RNs) and the rest are Healthcare Assistants (HCAs). 20 RNs work Day shifts. In total, 38 staff work Day shifts.
(a) Find how many HCAs work Night shifts.
(b) What percentage of the total 60 staff work Night shifts (to 1 decimal place)?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate total HCAs and Day HCAs: Total HCAs = 60 − 35 = 25 HCAs. | Day HCAs = 38 − 20 = 18 HCAs. [M1]
2. Calculate Night HCAs: Night HCAs = 25 − 18 = 7 HCAs (Check: Total Night = 60 − 38 = 22; Night RNs = 35 − 20 = 15; 15 + 7 = 22). [A1]
3. Calculate percentage of total staff on Night shift: (22 ÷ 60) × 100 = 36.66...% → 36.7%. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) 7 HCAs work Night shifts | (b) 36.7%
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: The group immediately following "of the..." is always your bottom denominator!
🔄 Quick Check: Always cross-check that both row totals (35 + 25 = 60) and column totals (38 + 22 = 60) add up to the Grand Total.
Question 4 (Workplace Application — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A café records 200 drink orders (Hot or Cold, Eat-In or Takeaway). 130 drinks are Hot. 85 orders are Eat-In, of which 60 are Hot.
Calculate how many Cold Takeaway drinks were ordered.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Total Cold drinks = 200 − 130 = 70. [M1]
2. Eat-In Cold drinks = 85 − 60 = 25. [P1]
3. Takeaway Cold drinks = 70 − 25 = 45 drinks. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 45 Cold Takeaway drinks
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Even if the question only asks for one specific cell at the end, examiners award method marks for filling in the entire table.
🔄 Quick Check: Sketch a quick 2×2 grid to keep Hot/Cold and Eat-In/Takeaway clear.
Section C: Exam-Style Problem Solving Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Medical Training Exam Pass Rate Conditional Audit (Level 2 Multi-Step)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
120 staff sat a mandatory resuscitation assessment after choosing either In-Person Training or Online E-Learning:
• 75 staff chose In-Person Training, and 66 of them passed.
• Overall across both methods, 96 staff passed in total.
Compare the percentage pass rate of In-Person Training against Online E-Learning.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate Online E-Learning total candidates and passes: Online Total = 120 − 75 = 45 staff. | Online Passes = 96 − 66 = 30 staff. [M1]
2. Calculate In-Person percentage pass rate: (66 ÷ 75) × 100 = 88%. [M1]
3. Calculate Online percentage pass rate and compare: (30 ÷ 45) × 100 = 66.7%. | In-Person Training achieved a much higher pass rate (88% vs 66.7%). [M1 / A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: In-Person Pass Rate = 88% vs Online Pass Rate = 66.7% (In-Person was higher by 21.3%)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Fill in every single cell in the printed two-way table and check all row and column sums.
🔄 Quick Check: To find the pass rate OF In-Person candidates, divide 66 by 75 (not 120!).
Question 6 (Level 2 Multi-Step Problem Solving — 3 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Using the café data in Question 2, if one Eat-In customer is chosen at random, what is the probability that they ordered a Cold drink? Give your answer as a simplified fraction.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Total Eat-In customers = 85. [M1]
2. Eat-In Cold customers = 25. [P1]
3. Probability = 25 / 85 = 5 / 17 (dividing top and bottom by 5). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 5/17
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Stating a conclusion without showing the full numerical comparison values.
🔄 Quick Check: Since the customer is chosen from Eat-In customers, the denominator is 85, not 200.
