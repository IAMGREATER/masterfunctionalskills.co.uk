Section A: Core Skills (Warm-Up) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Converting Map Distance to Real-World Metres) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A hospital campus map is drawn to a scale of 1 : 2,500.
The straight-line distance on the map between the Ambulance Bay and the Radiology Entrance measures 6.4 cm.
Calculate the actual real-world distance in metres.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Multiply map distance by scale factor: 6.4 cm × 2,500 = 16,000 cm [M1]
2. Convert centimetres to metres (÷ 100): 16,000 ÷ 100 = 160 m [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 160 m
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Dividing by 1,000 instead of 100 when converting centimetres to metres.
🔄 Quick Check: 160 m = 16,000 cm; 16,000 ÷ 2,500 = 6.4 cm.
Question 2 (Level 1 Foundation — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
On an Ordnance Survey map with scale 1 : 50,000, the straight-line distance between two medical clinics measures 7.4 cm. What is the actual distance in kilometres?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Real distance in cm = 7.4 × 50,000 = 370,000 cm. [M1]
2. Convert cm to km (÷ 100,000): 370,000 ÷ 100,000 = 3.7 km (or 1 cm = 0.5 km → 7.4 × 0.5 = 3.7 km). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 3.7 km
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Writing that a hospital room is "700 m" or "700 cm" when the answer line specifies metres forfeits the accuracy mark.
🔄 Quick Check: On a 1 : 50,000 map, 1 cm = 500 m = 0.5 km.
Section B: Everyday & Workplace Maths Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Hospital Acute Ward Refurbishment Scale Floor Plan (Level 1 Foundation)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A hospital architect produces a ward floor plan using a scale of 1 : 50.
(a) On the drawing, Bay 3 measures 14 cm long by 9 cm wide. What are the actual real-world dimensions in metres?
(b) A clinical consultation desk is 2.5 metres long in reality. What length should it be drawn on the scale plan in cm?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Multiply map measurements by scale factor (50): Real length = 14 cm × 50 = 700 cm. | Real width = 9 cm × 50 = 450 cm. [M1]
2. Convert real dimensions from centimetres to metres (÷ 100): 700 cm ÷ 100 = 7.0 metres. | 450 cm ÷ 100 = 4.5 metres. [A1]
3. Calculate drawing length for the 2.5 m consultation desk: Real length = 2.5 m = 250 cm. | Drawing length = 250 cm ÷ 50 = 5 cm. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) Real dimensions = 7.0 m long by 4.5 m wide | (b) Drawing length = 5 cm
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Remember: 100 cm = 1 m, and 100,000 cm = 1 km.
🔄 Quick Check: Sanity check: Real room dimensions should be in metres (7 m × 4.5 m); paper drawings should be in centimetres (5 cm).
Question 4 (Workplace Application — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A landscape gardener draws a rectangular lawn measuring 16 m long by 11 m wide using a scale of 1 : 200. Calculate the length and width of the lawn on the scale drawing in cm.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Convert real dimensions to cm: 16 m = 1,600 cm; 11 m = 1,100 cm. [M1]
2. Divide by 200: Length = 1,600 ÷ 200 = 8 cm; Width = 1,100 ÷ 200 = 5.5 cm. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 8 cm long by 5.5 cm wide
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Multiplying 14 cm × 9 cm = 126 cm² and then multiplying by 50 gives the wrong area (area scales by 50², not 50).
🔄 Quick Check: Convert metres to cm (× 100) before dividing by the scale factor (200).
Section C: Exam-Style Problem Solving Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Ordnance Survey Map Ambulance Route & Journey Time (Level 2 Multi-Step)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
On a regional map with a ratio scale of 1 : 25,000, the road route from St Mary’s Hospital to the Community Rehabilitation Centre measures 18 cm.
• The patient transfer ambulance travels at an average speed of 36 km/h.
Calculate the journey time in minutes.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate real road distance in cm and convert to km: Real distance = 18 cm × 25,000 = 450,000 cm. | Convert cm to m (÷ 100) = 4,500 m. | Convert m to km (÷ 1,000) = 4.5 km. [M1 / A1]
2. Calculate journey time in hours (Time = Distance ÷ Speed): Time = 4.5 km ÷ 36 km/h = 0.125 hours. [M1]
3. Convert hours to minutes (× 60): 0.125 × 60 = 7.5 minutes. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 7.5 minutes (Real distance = 4.5 km)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Always convert length and width into real metres BEFORE multiplying them together for area!
🔄 Quick Check: On a 1 : 25,000 map, every 1 cm = 0.25 km (or 4 cm = 1 km). So 18 cm ÷ 4 = 4.5 km immediately!
Question 6 (Level 2 Multi-Step Problem Solving — 4 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
On a floor plan drawn to a scale of 1 : 50, a rectangular office measures 12 cm by 8 cm. Carpet tiles cost £15.00 per m². Calculate the total cost to carpet the real office.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Real length = 12 × 50 = 600 cm = 6.0 m. [M1]
2. Real width = 8 × 50 = 400 cm = 4.0 m. [P1]
3. Real area = 6.0 m × 4.0 m = 24.0 m². [P1]
4. Total carpet cost = 24.0 × £15.00 = £360.00. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £360.00
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Stating a conclusion without showing the full numerical comparison values.
🔄 Quick Check: Convert 12 cm and 8 cm into real metres (6 m and 4 m) FIRST, then multiply for area (24 m²).
