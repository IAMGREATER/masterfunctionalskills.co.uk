Symmetry & 2D Shapes Pearson Edexcel: Pearson Edexcel L1 & L2 Mathematics
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Procedural Fluency Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Lines of Symmetry & Rotational Order) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Compare a rectangle (non-square) and a rhombus (non-square).
Write down the number of lines of symmetry and the order of rotational symmetry for a rectangle.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Lines of symmetry of a non-square rectangle: 2 (horizontal and vertical bisectors; diagonals are NOT lines of symmetry) [M1]
2. Order of rotational symmetry: Order 2 (matches at 180° and 360°) [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 2 lines of symmetry; rotational symmetry of order 2
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Claiming a rectangle has 4 lines of symmetry by counting the two diagonals.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Folding a rectangular A4 sheet along its diagonal does not align the corners.
Question 2 (Level 1 Foundation — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
State the number of lines of reflective symmetry and the order of rotational symmetry for a standard parallelogram.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Lines of reflective symmetry = 0. [M1]
2. Order of rotational symmetry = 2 (matches at 180° and 360°). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 0 lines of symmetry; Rotational symmetry of order 2
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: There is no such thing as rotational symmetry of order 0! Every shape looks identical after a full 360° turn, so the minimum order is 1.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: A parallelogram is the classic shape with 0 mirror lines but rotational symmetry of order 2!
Section B: Workplace & Functional Scenarios Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Safety Warning Symbol Symmetry Analysis (Entry 3 / Level 1 Foundation)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A blank hazard warning sign is shaped as an equilateral triangle, while a first-aid cross has 4 equal arms.
(a) How many lines of reflective symmetry does the equilateral triangle have?
(b) What is the order of rotational symmetry of the 4-armed first-aid cross?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. State reflective symmetry of an equilateral triangle: An equilateral triangle has 3 equal sides and 3 lines of symmetry. [A1]
2. State rotational symmetry order of the 4-armed equal cross: A 4-armed equal cross matches itself every 90° (4 times in 360°) → Order 4. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) 3 lines of symmetry | (b) Rotational symmetry of order 4
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Reflective lines of symmetry can be 0, but Rotational Order is ALWAYS at least 1.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: For any regular shape with n equal sides or arms, lines of symmetry = rotational order = n.
Question 4 (Workplace Application — 2 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A regular polygon has an interior angle of 120° and 6 equal sides. How many lines of symmetry does it have, and through how many degrees do you rotate it before it first matches its outline?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. A 6-sided regular hexagon has 6 lines of symmetry. [M1]
2. Angle of rotation = 360° ÷ 6 = 60°. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 6 lines of symmetry; 60° rotation
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: A rectangle has only 2 lines of symmetry (horizontal and vertical).
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Divide 360° by the rotational order (6) to find the smallest rotation angle (60°).
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Problem Solving & Justification Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Symmetrical Courtyard Tile Pattern Grid Completion & Costing (Level 1/2 Multi-Step)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A designer creates a square floor mosaic that is symmetrical across both the vertical midline and the horizontal midline (4 quadrants).
• In the top-left quadrant, the pattern uses 6 navy tiles and 9 cream tiles.
• Navy tiles cost £2.50 each and cream tiles cost £1.80 each.
Calculate the total number of navy and cream tiles needed for the entire 4-quadrant mosaic, and work out the total tile cost.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Multiply top-left quadrant counts by 4 for the full symmetrical pattern: Total Navy tiles = 4 × 6 = 24 tiles. | Total Cream tiles = 4 × 9 = 36 tiles. [M1 / A1]
2. Calculate cost of navy tiles and cream tiles: Navy cost = 24 × £2.50 = £60.00. | Cream cost = 36 × £1.80 = £64.80. [M1]
3. Sum for total mosaic cost: £60.00 + £64.80 = £124.80. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 24 navy tiles, 36 cream tiles — Total Cost = £124.80
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Only a square or rhombus has diagonal lines of symmetry.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Symmetry across both horizontal and vertical midlines means all 4 quarters of the design have identical tile counts!
Question 6 (Level 2 Multi-Step Problem Solving — 2 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
On a coordinate grid, point P is at (2, 5). What are the coordinates of the reflected point P′ when reflected across the vertical Y-axis (x = 0)?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Point P(2, 5) is 2 units to the right of the Y-axis. [M1]
2. Reflecting across the Y-axis places P′ 2 units to the left at the same height: (−2, 5). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (−2, 5)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Stating a conclusion without showing the full numerical comparison values.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Reflecting across the vertical Y-axis flips the sign of the x-coordinate while keeping y unchanged.
