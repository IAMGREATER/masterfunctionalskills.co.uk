Welcome to the Comparing Two Sets of Data blueprint. In Pearson Edexcel Functional Skills (Levels 1 and 2), examiners require you to make two distinct comparison statements in context: (1) compare an average (mean or median) to explain who performed higher or faster on average, and (2) compare the spread (range) to determine which group was more consistent!

Specification & Learning Outcomes

Candidates must demonstrate mastery of the following regulated Functional Skills Mathematics outcomes:

  • L2.29 – Compare two data sets using an appropriate measure of average (mean, median, or mode) and the range, making contextual conclusions.

Golden Rules for Comparing Data Sets & Distribution Consistency

Golden Rule 1: Always Write TWO Distinct Comparison Statements (Average + Range)

Full marks on any Level 2 comparison question require: Statement 1 comparing the Average (mean or median) in context, AND Statement 2 comparing the Range and explaining what it means for consistency.

Golden Rule / Method: Statement 1: Compare Average • Statement 2: Compare Range / Consistency
Golden Rule 2: Smaller Range = MORE Consistent (Less Variable)

A smaller range means all values are clustered closely together (more reliable/consistent). A larger range means values are widely spread out (less consistent/more unpredictable).

Golden Rule / Method: Smaller Range = More Consistent • Larger Range = More Variable

Core Skill 1: The Full-Mark 2-Sentence Comparison Template

Word-for-word sentence structures that guarantee full marks on Pearson Edexcel papers.

  • Sentence 1 (Average in Context): "On average, [Group A] had a higher/lower [context metric] than [Group B], because its mean/median was [X] compared to [Y]."
  • Sentence 2 (Range & Consistency): "[Group B] was more consistent than [Group A] because its range was smaller ([R₂] compared to [R₁])."

Core Skill 2: When "Lower Mean" Means "Better Performance"

Distinguishing waiting times and defect rates from sales and test scores.

Metric Type Which Average is Better? Which Range is Better?
Sales, Exam Scores, OutputHigher Mean/Median is betterSmaller Range = more consistent
Waiting Time, Delivery Delay, ErrorsLower Mean/Median is better (faster/fewer errors)Smaller Range = more predictable

Core Skill 3: Calculating Raw Data Stats Against a Pre-Calculated Summary Table

How exam questions give raw numbers for Team A and a summary table for Team B.

If Team B's summary gives the Median and Range, then you should calculate the Median and Range for Team A so you are comparing like-for-like measures!

Pearson Edexcel Step-by-Step Worked Models

Benchmark Exam Model 1 3 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 2)

Vocational Context: Clinical Pharmacy Audit

A clinical audit compares dispensing accuracy rates across Day and Night shifts:
• Day Shift: Mean = 96.5%, Range = 4.0%
• Night Shift: Mean = 94.0%, Range = 9.0%
Compare the dispensing accuracy of the two shifts using both the mean and the range.
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Compare the Mean in context
Mean Comparison (Average):
Day Shift had a higher average dispensing accuracy than Night Shift (mean 96.5% vs 94.0%).
Criteria: A1: Valid contextual mean comparison
Step 2 Compare the Range in terms of consistency
Range Comparison (Consistency):
Day Shift was more consistent than Night Shift because its range was smaller (4.0% vs 9.0%).
Criteria: A1: Valid range/consistency comparison
Step 3 State overall recommendation
Day Shift performed better overall, achieving higher average accuracy and greater operational consistency.
Criteria: A1: Supporting figures cited clearly
Final Accredited Answer: Day Shift had higher average accuracy (mean 96.5% vs 94.0%) and was more consistent (smaller range of 4.0% vs 9.0%).
Sonya’s Examiner Tip Examiner Method
Always include the actual numbers (96.5% vs 94.0% and 4.0% vs 9.0%) inside your comparison sentences.
Benchmark Exam Model 2 4 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 2)

Vocational Context: Medical Pathology Sample Courier Selection

A pathology lab tests two courier firms over 6 urgent sample deliveries (time in minutes):
• SwiftMed (Raw times): 28, 35, 24, 31, 42, 26
• BioExpress (Summary): Mean = 34 minutes, Range = 8 minutes.
Calculate the mean and range for SwiftMed, and write two comparisons between the two couriers.
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Calculate SwiftMed Mean (Sum ÷ 6)
Sum = 28 + 35 + 24 + 31 + 42 + 26 = 186 minutes
SwiftMed Mean = 186 ÷ 6 = 31 minutes
Criteria: M1: SwiftMed Mean (31 mins)
Step 2 Calculate SwiftMed Range (Highest − Lowest)
SwiftMed Range = 42 − 24 = 18 minutes
Criteria: A1: SwiftMed Range (18 mins)
Step 3 Compare Averages in context
SwiftMed was faster on average, with a lower mean delivery time (31 mins vs 34 mins).
Criteria: A1: Valid average comparison
Step 4 Compare Consistency using Range
However, BioExpress had more consistent delivery times because its range was smaller (8 mins vs 18 mins).
Criteria: A1: Valid consistency comparison
Final Accredited Answer: SwiftMed Mean = 31 mins, Range = 18 mins. SwiftMed is faster on average (31 vs 34 mins), but BioExpress is more consistent (range 8 vs 18 mins).
Subi’s Assessor Pro-Tip Assessor Guidance
Notice the trade-off: SwiftMed has a faster average, whereas BioExpress is more consistent!

Critical Examiner Traps & Common Pitfalls

Examiner Pitfall: Stating "Range is Smaller" Without Explaining It Means "More Consistent"
Writing "Day Shift has a smaller range of 4%" without interpreting what a smaller range means in real life loses the interpretation mark.
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Always add the words "...which means their performance is more consistent."
Examiner Pitfall: Comparing Mean of Group A with Median of Group B
You must compare like with like (Mean vs Mean, or Median vs Median).
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Calculate the same type of average that is given for the second group.

Interactive Exam Practice Questions

Exam Practice Question 1 2 Marks
Depot A: Mean daily parcels = 420, Range = 45. Depot B: Mean daily parcels = 465, Range = 130. Write two sentences comparing the daily parcel deliveries of Depot A and Depot B.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Average: Depot B delivers more parcels per day on average (mean 465 vs 420).
2. Consistency: Depot A has more consistent daily delivery numbers because its range is smaller (45 vs 130).
Final Correct Answer: Depot B has a higher average (465 vs 420), but Depot A is more consistent (smaller range of 45 vs 130).
Examiner Tip: Always write one sentence for the mean and one sentence for the range.
Exam Practice Question 2 3 Marks
Nurse A records 5 blood pressure check times (in mins): 6, 9, 5, 12, 8. Nurse B has a median of 7 mins and a range of 4 mins. Calculate the median and range for Nurse A and state which nurse is more consistent.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Nurse A ordered: 5, 6, 8, 9, 12 → Median = 8 mins.
2. Nurse A Range = 12 − 5 = 7 mins.
3. Nurse B is more consistent because Nurse B has a smaller range (4 mins vs 7 mins).
Final Correct Answer: Nurse A Median = 8 mins, Range = 7 mins; Nurse B is more consistent (range 4 vs 7 mins)
Examiner Tip: Since Nurse B’s summary uses the median, calculate the median for Nurse A.
Exam Practice Question 3 3 Marks
Machine X produces bolts with a mean length of 50.0 mm (Range = 0.4 mm). Machine Y produces bolts with a mean length of 50.0 mm (Range = 1.8 mm). If the engineering specification requires bolts to be as close to 50.0 mm as possible, which machine should the factory keep and why?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Both machines have the exact same mean length (50.0 mm).
2. Machine X has a much smaller range (0.4 mm vs 1.8 mm), meaning its bolt lengths are much more consistent and closer to 50.0 mm.
3. Keep Machine X.
Final Correct Answer: Keep Machine X because it has a much smaller range (0.4 mm vs 1.8 mm), making bolt lengths far more consistent.
Examiner Tip: When averages are equal, the range (consistency) decides the winner!
