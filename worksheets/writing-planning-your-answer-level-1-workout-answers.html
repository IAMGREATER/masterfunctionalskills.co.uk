Part 1: Quick-Check Answer Key
Quick answers for practice questions
Q1 Question 1: Structuring a 4-Paragraph Response
(a) Paragraph 1 (Introduction) → State reason for writing, establish tone, acknowledge context
(b) Paragraph 2 (Main Argument 1) → Address prompt bullet 1 with supporting evidence and explanation
(c) Paragraph 3 (Main Argument 2 / Counter) → Address prompt bullet 2 with detailed examples or solutions
(d) Paragraph 4 (Conclusion & Action) → Summarize main points, request action or state next steps
Q2 Question 2: Time Management in the Writing Exam
(a) Task 1 (15 marks): Recommended writing time? → 20 to 25 minutes
(b) Task 2 (21 marks): Recommended writing time? → 35 to 40 minutes
(c) How much time should be reserved at the end for proofreading? → 5 minutes per task
Q3 Question 3: Question 2: Deconstructing the Writing Prompt & TAP Analysis
(a) Prompt: Write an email to your line manager requesting permission to attend a three-day first aid course. → Text: Workplace Email | Audience: Line Manager | Purpose: Persuade / Request approval
(b) Prompt: Write an article for a local newspaper arguing against the proposed closure of the town sports centre. → Text: Newspaper Feature Article | Audience: Local community residents & council | Purpose: Persuade / Campaign
(c) Prompt: Write a formal letter of complaint to a travel company regarding a ruined package holiday. → Text: Formal Letter of Complaint | Audience: Customer Relations Director | Purpose: Complain & claim compensation
(d) Prompt: Write an informational leaflet advising new apprentices about health and safety rules in the workshop. → Text: Information Leaflet | Audience: New vocational apprentices | Purpose: Inform & instruct
(e) Prompt: Write a speech to deliver at a team meeting proposing ideas to reduce workplace single-use plastic waste. → Text: Formal Speech Script | Audience: Workplace colleagues & supervisors | Purpose: Persuade & inspire action
(f) Prompt: Write an official review of an online training platform for your company's staff intranet. → Text: Evaluative Review | Audience: Work colleagues & training managers | Purpose: Evaluate & advise
(g) Prompt: Write a report evaluating three possible locations for the company's new regional distribution depot. → Text: Business Feasibility Report | Audience: Senior company executives | Purpose: Analyze & recommend
(h) Prompt: Write a blog post sharing tips on how to prepare mentally and physically for a long shift at work. → Text: Web Blog Post | Audience: Shift workers / General online readers | Purpose: Advise & encourage
Q4 Question 4: Question 3: Brainstorming & Outlining Balanced Content Plans
(a) Topic: Should workplaces implement a 4-day working week? Outline 2 pros and 2 cons. → 1. Pro: Better work-life balance and lower burnout; 2. Pro: Reduced commuting costs and lower carbon emissions; 3. Con: Risk of longer, exhausting 10-hour daily shifts; 4. Con: Operational scheduling difficulties for 24/7 services.
(b) Topic: Introducing electric vehicles for fleet transport. Outline 2 benefits and 2 challenges. → 1. Benefit: Zero tailpipe emissions and cleaner air; 2. Benefit: Lower running and maintenance costs per mile; 3. Challenge: High initial vehicle purchase price; 4. Challenge: Limited rural charging infrastructure and charging downtime.
(c) Topic: Moving from physical paper files to digital cloud records. Outline 2 advantages and 2 risks. → 1. Advantage: Instant multi-user access and reduced physical storage; 2. Advantage: Automated backups prevent physical loss; 3. Risk: Vulnerability to cyberattacks or network outages; 4. Risk: High initial staff training and system transition costs.
(d) Topic: Self-service checkouts in supermarkets. Outline 2 customer benefits and 2 concerns. → 1. Benefit: Faster checkout times for small baskets; 2. Benefit: Shorter overall store queues; 3. Concern: Job losses for human checkout staff; 4. Concern: Frustrating barcode errors and accessibility barriers for elderly.
(e) Topic: Remote working vs Office working. Outline 2 home pros and 2 office pros. → 1. Home: No stressful commute and quiet focused environment; 2. Home: Greater flexibility for family duties; 3. Office: Spontaneous team collaboration and creative brainstorming; 4. Office: Clear mental separation between work and home life.
(f) Topic: Banning mobile phones in college classrooms. Outline 2 arguments for and 2 against. → 1. For: Eliminates major social media distractions; 2. For: Fosters active classroom discussion; 3. Against: Restricts access to digital educational research tools; 4. Against: Prevents students receiving urgent family communications.
(g) Topic: Mandatory uniform for hospital administrative staff. Outline 2 benefits and 2 objections. → 1. Benefit: Clear professional identification for patients; 2. Benefit: Promotes team unity and equality; 3. Objection: Personal discomfort and lack of individual expression; 4. Objection: Cost and inconvenience of maintaining specific attire.
(h) Topic: Charging staff for hospital car parking. Outline 2 justifications and 2 criticisms. → 1. Justification: Generates revenue to maintain car parks and lighting; 2. Justification: Encourages public transit and cycling; 3. Criticism: Unfair financial burden on low-wage clinic staff; 4. Criticism: Public transport often unavailable for unsocial night shifts.
Q5 Question 5: Question 4: Time Management & Mark Allocation Strategy
(a) How much time should you allocate to Task 1 overall? → 24 minutes (40% of 60 minutes)
(b) How many minutes of Task 1 should be spent planning before writing? → 4 to 5 minutes
(c) How many minutes of Task 1 should be spent writing the draft? → 15 to 16 minutes
(d) How many minutes of Task 1 must be reserved for proofreading and SPaG checks? → 3 to 4 minutes
(e) How much time should you allocate to Task 2 overall? → 36 minutes (60% of 60 minutes)
(f) How many minutes of Task 2 should be spent on TAP analysis and planning? → 5 to 6 minutes
(g) How many minutes of Task 2 should be spent writing the main body? → 24 to 25 minutes
(h) How many minutes of Task 2 must be reserved for thorough final editing and proofreading? → 5 minutes
Part 2: Real-Life Problem Solutions
Step-by-step methods and mark breakdown
Question 1 Worked Solution Pearson Edexcel Writing Task Planning Scaffold
[3 Marks Available]
Exam Task Prompt: 'Write a letter to your regional facilities director proposing the creation of a staff quiet room for rest breaks during intense shifts. You should explain the current problem, suggest a suitable location, and outline the benefits for staff wellbeing.'

Produce a concise 4-part paragraph plan covering all three bullet points from the prompt.
Introduction plan: Purpose of letter + identify current issue (lack of restorative space during 12-hour shifts)
[W1]
Body Paragraph 1 & 2 plan: Suggest underutilized disused office on 2nd floor + outline specific wellbeing benefits (reduced stress, fewer fatigue errors)
[W1]
Conclusion plan: Propose next steps, site walkaround visit, polite professional sign-off
[A1]
Correct Final Answer: Para 1: Intro & shift fatigue issues | Para 2: Proposal for Room 14 conversion & low-cost furniture | Para 3: Measurable benefits on retention & safety | Para 4: Call to action for site meeting & sign-off.
Quick Check Method: Directly maps to all bullet points in the prompt.
Common Mistake to Avoid: Planning only 1 paragraph and forgetting to address the location or benefits.
