▶4-Step Level 1 Practice & Checking Routine
1. Plan Using TAP (5 Mins): Check the Text format layout, Audience (polite formal vs friendly informal), and Purpose, then plan one clear paragraph for each bullet point.
2. Use the 10-Word Vocabulary Bank: Each task brief gives you 10 helpful Level 1 words. Try to include at least 4–5 of these words naturally in your writing.
3. Join Ideas & Punctuate Clearly: Link sentences using because, although, however, and in addition, and check capital letters, full stops, question marks, and apostrophes.
4. Check Against Model Solutions: After finishing each task or mock paper, compare your work with the Reference Section guidance and full-marks model answers.

Level 1 Functional Writing Glossary (A–C)

Address
The recipient and sender postal details placed at the top of a formal letter.
Adjective
A describing word that gives more detail about a noun (e.g. 'cold rain', 'heavy boxes').
Adverb
A word that describes a verb, indicating how, when, or where an action occurs (e.g. 'quickly', 'yesterday').
Apostrophe
A punctuation mark (') used to show omission (don't = do not) or possession (the manager's desk).
Article
A piece of writing for a newspaper, magazine, or community website with a headline, subheadings, and paragraphs.
Audience
The person or group of people you are writing for (e.g. a store manager, local council, or friend).
Bullet points
Symbols used to list key facts, actions, or recommendations clearly in reports, leaflets, and emails.
Byline
The line underneath an article headline that gives the writer's name (e.g. 'By Sam Taylor').
Capital letter
An uppercase letter used for the first word in a sentence, proper nouns (names, days, places), and 'I'.
Comma
A punctuation mark (,) used to separate items in a list or mark a brief pause after a starter clause.

Level 1 Functional Writing Glossary (C–N)

Complex sentence
A sentence that joins a main clause and a supporting clause using a joining word like 'because', 'although', or 'when'.
Conjunction
A linking word used to join words, phrases, or clauses together (e.g. 'and', 'but', 'because', 'although').
Drafting
Writing out your response using your 5-minute plan before proofreading and checking.
Email
An electronic message containing a To: line, Subject: line, greeting, clear paragraphs, and sign-off.
Exclamation mark
A punctuation mark (!) used to express strong emotion, welcome, or warning. Use sparingly in formal writing.
Formal letter
A structured letter written to a business, landlord, or council with both addresses, date, and formal sign-offs.
Full stop
A punctuation mark (.) used to mark the end of a complete statement sentence.
Headline
The catchy main title at the top of an article or newsletter designed to grab the reader's attention.
Homophones
Words that sound the same when spoken but have different spellings and meanings (e.g. 'their', 'there', and 'they're').
Leaflet
An information guide using a bold heading, subheadings, and action-verb bullet points to advise readers.

Level 1 Functional Writing Glossary (P–V)

Noun
A word that names a person, place, or thing (e.g. 'student', 'Greendale', 'computer').
Paragraph
A group of sentences about one main point, separated from the next paragraph by a clear line space.
Proofreading
Carefully checking your finished writing in the last 5 minutes to fix spelling, punctuation, and grammar mistakes.
Purpose
The main reason why you are writing a text (e.g. to complain, apply, inform, persuade, or advise).
Question mark
A punctuation mark (?) placed at the end of any sentence that asks a direct or rhetorical question.
Review
A balanced piece of writing that rates a café, service, or product and gives a final recommendation.
Salutation
The opening greeting at the start of a letter, email, or talk (e.g. 'Dear Mr Green,', 'Good morning everyone,').
Sign-off
The polite closing words of a letter or email ('Yours sincerely' when named, 'Yours faithfully' when 'Sir/Madam').
Subject line
A short heading at the top of an email or formal letter ('Re:') summarising what the message is about.
Verb
An action, doing, or being word (e.g. 'deliver', 'inspect', 'organise', 'were').
