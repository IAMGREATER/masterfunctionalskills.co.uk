Video Calling and Digital Messaging — Answer Key

3. Communicating • Standard Code: E3.3.3
Style 1: Solutions (6 Drills)
Task 1 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What does a microphone icon with a line through it indicate during a video call?

A. Your microphone is MUTED and other people cannot hear you (CORRECT)
B. Your computer speakers are broken
C. The meeting has ended
D. Your webcam is switched on
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

A line through the microphone symbol indicates that you are muted.

Task 2 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What feature allows you to ask a question in a large meeting without talking over the speaker?

A. Click the 'Raise Hand' button or use the meeting chat (CORRECT)
B. Turn off your computer monitor
C. Leave the meeting and rejoin five times
D. Unmute and shout loudly
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Raising your virtual hand or posting in the chat alerts the host without disrupting the presentation.

Task 3 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

Why should you use 'Background Blur' during a workplace video call from home?

A. To protect your home privacy and prevent distracting background movements (CORRECT)
B. Because it makes your voice louder
C. To hide from your boss that you are in a meeting
D. It speeds up your internet download speed
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Background blur conceals private home surroundings and keeps the focus on the participant.

Task 4 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

During a multi-person video call on Microsoft Teams or Zoom, what should you do when someone else is presenting?

A. Mute your microphone to eliminate background noise and echo (CORRECT)
B. Leave your microphone unmuted and play music in the background
C. Turn off your Wi-Fi router
D. Repeatedly click the share screen button
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Muting your microphone while others are speaking cuts out ambient room noise, typing sounds, and echo, ensuring high audio clarity for all attendees.

Task 5 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

If you wish to ask a question during a large formal company presentation without interrupting the speaker, which feature should you use?

A. The 'Raise Hand' button or meeting chat panel (CORRECT)
B. Unmute and speak loudly over the presenter
C. Hang up and call the presenter's mobile phone
D. Toggle your camera rapidly on and off
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Using the 'Raise Hand' feature signals the host that you have a contribution, allowing them to invite you to speak at a suitable break in the presentation.

Task 6 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

When attending a professional video conference from a home or shared office, why should you enable background blur?

A. To protect privacy and prevent visual distractions behind you (CORRECT)
B. To make the video stream download faster
C. To hide your face from the camera
D. To improve computer battery life
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Background blur conceals personal domestic environments, passersby, and confidential notices on walls, maintaining privacy and professional meeting standards.
