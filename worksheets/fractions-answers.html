Fractions Pearson Edexcel: L1.9 • L2.8 • L2.9
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Procedural Fluency Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Level 1 Simplifying & Equivalence) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Simplify the fraction 36/48 to its simplest form.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Identify Highest Common Factor (HCF = 12) or divide numerator and denominator successively by 2, 2, and 3 [M1]
2. 36 ÷ 12 = 3; 48 ÷ 12 = 4; Result: 3/4 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 3/4
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Stopping at 18/24 or 9/12 without reducing fully to lowest terms.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 3/4 × 12/12 = 36/48.
Question 2 (Level 2 Adding Mixed Numbers) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Work out: 2 1/3 + 1 3/4 without a calculator.
Give your answer as a mixed number in its simplest form.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Convert to improper fractions (7/3 + 7/4) with common denominator 12: 28/12 + 21/12 = 49/12 [M1]
2. Convert improper fraction to mixed number: 49 ÷ 12 = 4 remainder 1 → 4 1/12 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 4 1/12
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Adding numerators and denominators directly (3 4/7). Denominators must be matched first!
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 4 1/12 - 1 3/4 = 49/12 - 21/12 = 28/12 = 7/3 = 2 1/3.
Section B: Workplace & Functional Scenarios Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 Hospital Pharmacy Dispatches) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A central hospital pharmacy stocks 720 vials of specialized antibiotic.
• 3/8 of the vials are dispatched to Paediatric Intensive Care.
• 2/5 of the vials are dispatched to Emergency Assessment.

How many vials remain in central pharmacy stock?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. PICU dispatches: (720 ÷ 8) × 3 = 90 × 3 = 270 vials [M1]
2. Emergency dispatches: (720 ÷ 5) × 2 = 144 × 2 = 288 vials [M1]
3. Remaining stock: 720 - (270 + 288) = 720 - 558 = 162 vials [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 162 vials
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Calculating 2/5 of the remaining stock (450) rather than 2/5 of the original 720 vials.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 270 + 288 + 162 = 720 vials.
Question 4 (Level 2 Apprentice Construction Timber Cut) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A site carpenter cuts roof struts from a 4.8 metre timber beam.
The first strut requires 1 1/4 metres, the second requires 1 3/5 metres, and 1/10 metre is lost as saw-cut waste.

Calculate the exact length of timber remaining on the beam. Give your answer in metres.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Convert fractions to decimals (or twentieths): 1 1/4 = 1.25 m; 1 3/5 = 1.60 m; 1/10 = 0.10 m [M1]
2. Sum used and wasted timber: 1.25 + 1.60 + 0.10 = 2.95 m [P1]
3. Subtract from total beam length: 4.80 - 2.95 = 1.85 m (or 1 17/20 m) [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1.85 m (or 1 17/20 m)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Forgetting to deduct the 1/10 m saw-cut waste, which yields 1.95 m.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 1.85 + 1.25 + 1.60 + 0.10 = 4.80 m.
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Problem Solving & Justification Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Ward Dietary Survey) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
In an acute medical unit with 96 patients:
• 5/12 require diabetic meal plans.
• 1/4 require low-sodium meal plans.
• 1/6 require texture-modified soft diets.
• The remaining patients receive standard hospital meals.

How many patients receive standard hospital meals?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Diabetic meals: (96 ÷ 12) × 5 = 8 × 5 = 40 patients [P1]
2. Low-sodium: 96 ÷ 4 = 24 patients; Soft diet: 96 ÷ 6 = 16 patients [P1]
3. Sum specialist diets: 40 + 24 + 16 = 80 patients [M1]
4. Standard meals: 96 - 80 = 16 patients [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 16 patients
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Adding denominators instead of using a common denominator of 12 (5/12 + 3/12 + 2/12 = 10/12).
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 40 + 24 + 16 + 16 = 96 patients.
Question 6 (Level 2 Trust Target Justification) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A community trust aims for at least 7/10 of all clinical incident reviews to be completed within 14 days.
Last month, 185 out of 250 incident reviews were completed within 14 days.
The quality manager claims: 'We achieved our target fraction with room to spare.'

Is the quality manager correct? Show full step-by-step working and state a clear conclusion.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate target number of reviews: 7/10 of 250 = (250 ÷ 10) × 7 = 175 reviews [M1]
2. Or convert actual to percentage/decimal: 185 ÷ 250 = 0.74 (74% vs 70% target) [P1]
3. Compare actual against target: 185 > 175 (or 74% > 70%) [M1]
4. Conclude: Yes, the manager is correct; 185 reviews (74%) exceeds the 7/10 target of 175 reviews (70%) by 10 reviews. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Yes, correct (Achieved 185 reviews / 74% vs target of 175 reviews / 70%)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Ticking 'Yes' without showing numerical values for both the target and actual performance.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 185/250 = 37/50 = 74/100 = 0.74 > 0.70.
