Part 1: Quick-Check Answer Key
Quick answers for practice questions
Q1 Determining Axis Scales and Intervals
(a) 0 to 50 on y-axis marked across 10 grid squares. Value per square? → 5 units (50 ÷ 10)
(b) 0 to 100 on y-axis marked across 5 major squares. Value per square? → 20 units (100 ÷ 5)
(c) 0 to 20 on y-axis marked across 10 grid squares. Value per square? → 2 units (20 ÷ 10)
(d) 10°C to 15°C marked across 5 squares. Value per square? → 1°C per square
Q2 Describing Trends and Rate of Change
(a) Patient visits rise from 40 in Jan to 85 in June. Trend? → Increasing / upward trend
(b) Medication errors fall from 12 in Q1 to 2 in Q4. Trend? → Decreasing / downward trend
(c) A line graph is completely horizontal. What does this mean? → No change / value remained constant
(d) Between which two hours is change steepest: 08:00-09:00 (+5) or 09:00-10:00 (+18)? → 09:00 to 10:00 (steeper gradient)

Question 1 Worked Solution Pharmacy Cold Storage Temperature Tracking
[3 Marks Available]
An electronic data logger tracks the temperature inside a clinical vaccine storage unit over 6 hours:
• 08:00: 3.2°C
• 10:00: 3.8°C
• 12:00: 5.4°C
• 14:00: 6.8°C
(a) Describe the general trend in temperature between 08:00 and 14:00.
(b) Work out the total temperature increase from 08:00 to 14:00.
(c) Safe vaccine storage requires temperature to stay strictly below 8.0°C. Did the temperature remain safe?
Identify trend: Upward / increasing trend over time
[M1]
Calculate total increase: 6.8°C - 3.2°C = 3.6°C increase
[M1]
Compare against limit: 6.8°C < 8.0°C, so Yes, it remained within the safe limit
[A1]
Correct Final Answer: (a) Upward trend | (b) 3.6°C increase | (c) Yes, stayed under 8.0°C
Quick Check Method: 3.2 + 3.6 = 6.8°C.
Common Mistake to Avoid: Omitting the word 'increase' or failing to state the units.
