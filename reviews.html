Speak to a Functional Skills Specialist

Our Functional Skills Experts are on hand to answer any queries you may have. The first thing they will look to do is ensure that functional skills is the best option for the career path you are choosing. Our staff specialise in specific career paths, so whether you are looking to become a nurse, teacher, police officer or simply get into University, we can direct you to support staff with the right knowledge and experience. They will then look to explore how much support you need. Don’t worry if you really struggle with maths or English is a second language, we have people from all backgrounds and all ages use our service and we are experienced in helping!

We can also ensure we work to your timelines. We have some learners who sit their exams in a matter of days and others who take a year or so. The recommended study time is around 50 hours per course but this can vary from person to person. We will work with your deadline to help you achieve the result you need in the time you have to do it.

We work with employers and Universities across the UK and have a dedicated Training Provider service.
