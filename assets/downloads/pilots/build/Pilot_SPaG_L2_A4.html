Its or It's?

Sam
A  The idea
it's is short for it is or it has.
its means belonging to it – like his or her. No apostrophe!
★B  The Decisive Test: Its vs It's
it's = it is / it hasits = belonging to it
Say 'it is' out loud in the gap.
  • If it makes sense, write it's: It's cold in the server room.
  • If not, write its: The firm changed its logo.
✓The 'It Is' Mantra
If you can't say 'it is' or 'it has' in that spot, rub out the apostrophe.
!Watch Out!
its' is never correct. It does not exist!
C  ✗ Wrong
The uninterruptible power supply failed because it's battery had reached the end of it's operational life.
✓ Right
The uninterruptible power supply failed because its battery had reached the end of its operational life.
IT technicians write reports where its / it's matters.

Apostrophes for Owners

Priya
A  The idea
An apostrophe can show that something belongs to someone: the manager's office = the office of the manager.
★B  The Three Rules of Possessive Apostrophes
one owner: 'splural ending in s: s'plural without s: 's
  • One tenant → the tenant's lease
  • Many tenants → the tenants' committee
  • Irregular plural → the children's play area, the men's room
✓The Cover Test
Cover the apostrophe with your finger. The word on the left must name the owner.
!Watch Out!
Never use an apostrophe for a simple plural: six pallet's ✗ → six pallets ✓
C  ✗ Wrong
All contractor's must park their vehicle's in the visitors car park.
✓ Right
All contractors must park their vehicles in the visitors' car park.
Signs, notices and contracts all use possessive apostrophes.

Workplace Words: Prefixes

A prefix goes at the start of a word and changes its meaning.

antivirus (anti- = opposed to)
Install antivirus software on every laptop.
bilateral (bi- = two)
The two companies signed a bilateral deal.
collaborate (co- / con- = together)
Our teams collaborate on the project.
de-escalate (de- = reverse)
Stay calm to de-escalate an angry customer.
forecast (fore- = before)
The sales forecast for May looks good.
malfunction (mal- = bad)
The printer had a malfunction again.
miscalculate (mis- = incorrect)
Don't miscalculate the delivery cost.
multidisciplinary (multi- = many)
A multidisciplinary NHS team met today.
subcontractor (sub- = under)
A subcontractor fitted the new windows.
