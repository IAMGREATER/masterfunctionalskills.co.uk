Email Attachments and Contacts List

Learn how to attach documents and photos to emails, open and save received attachments safely, and create contacts in your email address book for quick communication.

Attach documents and photos to an outgoing email using the paperclip icon Open, preview, and safely save received email attachments Add and organise email contacts in your address book

Step-by-Step Teaching & Key Concepts

Core Knowledge
1
Adding an Attachment to an Email

An Attachment is a separate digital file (such as a Word document, spreadsheet, or photo) sent alongside your email message:

  1. In your email compose window, click the Paperclip icon () labelled Attach File.
  2. Browse through your computer's folders to select your file.
  3. Click Open / Insert.
  4. The file name and file size will appear below the subject line. Always check that the correct file is attached before clicking Send.

2
Opening and Saving Received Attachments Safely

When you receive an email with a paperclip symbol, it contains an attachment:

  • Click the attachment to Preview it inside your browser or email app.
  • Click Download / Save As to save a permanent copy onto your computer (e.g. into your Documents folder).
Crucial Cybersecurity Warning: NEVER open an attachment from an unknown or suspicious sender! Email attachments from scammers can contain harmful viruses and malware that lock your computer.
3
Creating and Using Email Contacts

Instead of memorising complicated email addresses, save people into your Contacts / People address book:

  1. Open the Contacts tab in your email program.
  2. Click New Contact.
  3. Type the person's Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
  4. Click Save. Next time you type their name in the 'To' box, their address will automatically appear!

Step-by-Step Exam Worked Model

Accredited Model

Here is how this skill is tested in the real Pearson Edexcel onscreen digital assessment, broken down with official examiner mark codes ([B1] knowledge, [M1] process, [A1] accuracy).

Exam Scenario Prompt: A dental receptionist needs to email a price list PDF to a patient. Describe the step-by-step procedure to attach the file 'Price_List_2026.pdf' from their Documents folder to the email.
Step 1: Locate and click the attachment tool [B1]
Click the Paperclip icon (or 'Attach File' button) in the email window.
Tutor Marking Rationale: Identifies the standard universal attachment command.
Step 2: Select and attach the file [M1]
Browse to the 'Documents' folder, click 'Price_List_2026.pdf', and click 'Open' or 'Insert'.
Tutor Marking Rationale: Correctly navigates to folder and selects the required file.
Tutor Abdul's Exam Golden Rule: In Entry 3 exams, questions often show a screenshot of an email ribbon and ask: 'Which icon attaches a file?' The answer is always the Paperclip icon.
