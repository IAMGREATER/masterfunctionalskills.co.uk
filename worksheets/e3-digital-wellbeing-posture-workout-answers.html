Digital Wellbeing, Screen Breaks and Online Footprints — Answer Key

5. Being Safe and Responsible Online • Standard Code: E3.5.2
Style 1: Solutions (6 Drills)
Task 1 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What is the 20-20-20 rule designed to prevent when using digital devices?

A. Eye strain and headaches (CORRECT)
B. Low battery power
C. Computer viruses
D. Slow internet speeds
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Looking at an object 20 feet away for 20 seconds every 20 minutes relaxes eye muscles.

Task 2 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

Where should the top of your computer monitor be positioned relative to your head?

A. At eye level roughly an arm's length away (CORRECT)
B. Down on the floor near your feet
C. Way above your head so you have to look up
D. Directly touching your nose
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Positioning the top of the monitor at eye level prevents neck hyperextension and strain.

Task 3 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What is meant by your 'Digital Footprint'?

A. The permanent trail of information, posts, and records left behind when you use the internet (CORRECT)
B. The size of your computer mouse
C. How heavy your laptop is
D. The amount of electricity your printer uses
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Your digital footprint is the traceable history of your online actions, posts, and browsing data.

Task 4 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

According to ergonomic guidelines, what is the '20-20-20 rule' recommended to prevent digital eye strain?

A. Every 20 minutes, look at an object at least 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds (CORRECT)
B. Work for 20 hours, take 20 minutes break, drink 20 glasses of water
C. Blink 20 times every 20 seconds at 20% brightness
D. Keep your computer monitor 20 inches from the ceiling
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

The 20-20-20 rule allows the ciliary muscles in your eyes to relax by shifting focus to a distant object (20 feet) for 20 seconds every 20 minutes of continuous screen use.

Task 5 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

How should an office workstation chair and computer monitor be positioned to support healthy posture?

A. Feet flat on the floor, thighs parallel to ground, top of monitor at eye level (CORRECT)
B. Slouching backwards with monitor placed far to the left side
C. Looking straight up at a high ceiling-mounted screen with feet dangling
D. Sitting cross-legged on the desk surface
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Proper DSE ergonomics require feet flat on the floor (or footrest), lower back supported, forearms horizontal, and the top of the monitor positioned at or slightly below eye level.

Task 6 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What is an individual's 'digital footprint'?

A. The trail of data, search history, comments, and posts left behind when using the internet (CORRECT)
B. The physical dimensions of a computer mouse pad
C. The amount of electricity consumed by a laptop charger
D. The brand of printer installed in the workplace
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

A digital footprint consists of all active and passive traces left by a user online, including public comments, social media uploads, account registrations, and browsing history.

  • Rule 1: Healthy Posture and Ergonomics — Sitting at a computer for hours can cause neck pain, backache, and repetitive strain injury (RSI) unless you set up your workstation correct
  • Rule 2: The 20-20-20 Rule for Eye Strain — Regular screen breaks make you more productive and alert. In exams, questions will ask how to prevent eye strain: 'Take regular screen breaks' is always an accredited answer.
