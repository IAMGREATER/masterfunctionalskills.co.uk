Temperature, Negative Numbers & Thermometer Scales Pearson Edexcel: Pearson Edexcel L1 & L2 Mathematics
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Procedural Fluency Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Negative Temperature Differences Across Zero) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A laboratory deep-freezer is maintained at -18.5°C, while a chilled blood fridge is kept at +4.2°C.
Calculate the temperature difference between the two storage units.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate distance across zero: 4.2 - (-18.5) = 4.2 + 18.5 [M1]
2. Evaluate sum: 22.7°C [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 22.7°C
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Subtracting 18.5 - 4.2 = 14.3°C by ignoring the negative sign.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: -18.5°C + 22.7°C = +4.2°C.
Question 2 (Level 1 Foundation — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Overnight, the outside temperature at an ambulance station drops from 4.5°C by 11.2°C. What is the new temperature?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Drop 4.5°C to reach 0°C. [M1]
2. Remaining drop below zero = 11.2 − 4.5 = 6.7°C. [M1]
3. New temperature = −6.7°C. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: −6.7°C
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Ignoring the minus sign on −19°C and doing 22 − 19 = 3°C instead of 22 + 19 = 41°C.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 4.5 − 11.2 = −6.7°C.
Section B: Workplace & Functional Scenarios Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Hospital Catering Walk-In Freezer vs Kitchen Temperature Difference (Level 1 Foundation)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A catering supervisor checks the digital thermometer in the walk-in freezer, which reads −19°C. The main kitchen preparation room is at 22°C.
(a) Work out the difference in temperature between the walk-in freezer and the kitchen.
(b) A defrost cycle raises the freezer temperature by 5°C. What is the new freezer temperature, and is it still at or below the mandatory −18°C safety limit?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Bridge across 0°C for total temperature difference: From −19°C to 0°C = 19°C; from 0°C to 22°C = 22°C. | Total difference = 19 + 22 = 41°C. [M1 / A1]
2. Add 5°C rise to −19°C and compare with −18°C: −19°C + 5°C = −14°C. | No, −14°C is warmer than −18°C, so it breaches the safety limit. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) 41°C difference | (b) −14°C — No, warmer than the −18°C limit
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: If one temperature is negative and one is positive, ADD their distances from 0°C!
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: When temperature rises from −19°C by 5°C, it moves UP towards zero to −14°C (not −24°C!).
Question 4 (Workplace Application — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Five Cryo-Freezer compartments record temperatures of: −16.4°C, −21.0°C, −18.5°C, −15.9°C, −20.2°C.
(a) Arrange these temperatures from coldest to warmest.
(b) Find the range of these temperatures.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Coldest (most negative) to warmest: −21.0°C, −20.2°C, −18.5°C, −16.4°C, −15.9°C. [M1]
2. Range = difference between −21.0°C and −15.9°C = 21.0 − 15.9 = 5.1°C. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) −21.0°C, −20.2°C, −18.5°C, −16.4°C, −15.9°C | (b) Range = 5.1°C
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: In freezer safety questions, −14°C is WARMER than −18°C (closer to 0°C), which means the freezer is too warm!
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Both extremes are below zero, so the range is 21.0 − 15.9 = 5.1°C.
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Problem Solving & Justification Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (US Medical Autoclave Sterilisation Temperature Conversion (Level 2 Multi-Step)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
An American surgical autoclave manual states that instruments must be sterilised at a minimum temperature of 250°F.
• The UK hospital autoclave displays temperatures in Celsius (°C) and is currently set to 122°C.
• Use the formula F = 1.8C + 32.
Determine whether the UK setting of 122°C meets the minimum 250°F requirement.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Substitute C = 122 into 1.8 × C: 1.8 × 122 = 219.6. [M1]
2. Add 32 to find temperature in °F: 219.6 + 32 = 251.6°F. [M1 / A1]
3. Compare 251.6°F with 250°F minimum: Yes, 251.6°F is above the 250°F minimum requirement (by 1.6°F). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Yes — 122°C equals 251.6°F, which exceeds the 250°F minimum
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Draw a vertical thermometer: 0°C at the top, then −14°C, then −18°C below it.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Follow BIDMAS in F = 1.8C + 32: always multiply 1.8 by C FIRST, then add 32!
Question 6 (Level 2 Multi-Step Problem Solving — 3 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Use the formula C = (F − 32) ÷ 1.8 to convert a patient fever reading of 102.2°F into degrees Celsius (°C).
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Brackets first: 102.2 − 32 = 70.2. [M1]
2. Divide by 1.8: 70.2 ÷ 1.8 = 39.0°C. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 39°C (or 39.0°C)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Stating a conclusion without showing the full numerical comparison values.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Subtract 32 FIRST before dividing by 1.8!
