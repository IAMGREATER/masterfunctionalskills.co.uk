Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Procedural Fluency Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Identifying Modal Class & Median Class Interval) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A grouped frequency table records the ages of 40 volunteer blood donors:
• 18–29 yrs: 9 | 30–39 yrs: 16 | 40–49 yrs: 10 | 50–59 yrs: 5

Write down the modal class interval and state what percentage of donors are aged under 40.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Modal class (highest frequency of 16): 30–39 yrs [M1]
2. Donors under 40 = 9 + 16 = 25; Percentage = (25 ÷ 40) × 100 = 62.5% [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Modal class: 30–39 yrs | Under 40: 62.5%
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Writing '16' as the modal class instead of the interval '30–39 yrs'.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 62.5% of 40 = 0.625 × 40 = 25 donors (9 + 16).
Question 2 (Level 1 Foundation — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A logistics hub records parcel weights: 0 < w ≤ 5 kg (freq 12), 5 < w ≤ 10 kg (freq 29), 10 < w ≤ 15 kg (freq 19).
(a) State the modal class.
(b) In which group should a parcel weighing exactly 5.0 kg be recorded?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Highest frequency is 29 → Modal class is 5 < w ≤ 10 kg. [M1]
2. Because 0 < w ≤ 5 kg has the "≤" (less than or equal to) sign on 5, 5.0 kg belongs in 0 < w ≤ 5 kg. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) 5 < w ≤ 10 kg | (b) 0 < w ≤ 5 kg
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Answering "38" instead of "10 < t ≤ 20" scores zero marks.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: The ≤ symbol shows which interval owns the exact boundary value.
Section B: Workplace & Functional Scenarios Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Hospital Phlebotomy Outpatient Waiting Time Distribution (Level 1/2 Foundation)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A blood testing clinic records waiting times for 80 outpatients:
• 0 < t ≤ 10 mins: 14 patients
• 10 < t ≤ 20 mins: 38 patients
• 20 < t ≤ 30 mins: 22 patients
• 30 < t ≤ 40 mins: 6 patients
(a) State the modal class interval.
(b) In which class interval does the median waiting time lie?
(c) What percentage of patients waited more than 20 minutes?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Identify the interval with highest frequency (38): Modal class = 10 < t ≤ 20 mins. [A1]
2. Find median position (40th) using cumulative frequency: Group 1 ends at 14; Group 2 ends at 14 + 38 = 52. The 40th patient lies in 10 < t ≤ 20 mins. [A1]
3. Calculate percentage waiting > 20 mins: Patients > 20 mins = 22 + 6 = 28. | Percentage = (28 ÷ 80) × 100 = 35%. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) Modal Class: 10 < t ≤ 20 | (b) Median Class: 10 < t ≤ 20 | (c) 35%
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Modal CLASS means the name of the group.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Always copy the exact inequality interval (10 < t ≤ 20) rather than writing the frequency (38).
Question 4 (Workplace Application — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A staff age table shows: 20–29 (11 staff), 30–39 (18 staff), 40–49 (14 staff), 50–59 (7 staff). Total = 50 staff.
Find the class interval that contains the median age.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Median position = 50 ÷ 2 = 25th staff member. [M1]
2. Cumulative frequencies: Group 1 = 11; Group 1 + Group 2 = 11 + 18 = 29. [P1]
3. The 25th staff member falls inside the 30–39 interval. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 30–39
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Putting 20 into "20 < t ≤ 30" instead of "10 < t ≤ 20".
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Add frequencies from the top row until you pass N ÷ 2.
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Problem Solving & Justification Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Raw Call Duration Data Tally & SLA Compliance Audit (Level 2 Multi-Step)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
An IT helpdesk logs the resolution time (in minutes) for 15 support tickets:
8, 14, 20, 6, 25, 12, 10, 18, 32, 15, 9, 22, 11, 28, 16
• Group 1: 0 < t ≤ 10 | Group 2: 10 < t ≤ 20 | Group 3: 20 < t ≤ 35
(a) Determine the frequency for each of the three groups.
(b) State the modal class and determine whether at least 70% of tickets were resolved within 20 minutes or less.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Sort or classify the 15 values carefully (noting 10 goes in Group 1 and 20 goes in Group 2): 0 < t ≤ 10: {6, 8, 9, 10} → Frequency = 4. | 10 < t ≤ 20: {11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 18, 20} → Frequency = 7. | 20 < t ≤ 35: {22, 25, 28, 32} → Frequency = 4. | (Check sum: 4 + 7 + 4 = 15). [M1 / A1]
2. Identify the modal class: Highest frequency is 7 → Modal class is 10 < t ≤ 20. [A1]
3. Calculate percentage ≤ 20 minutes and compare with 70%: Tickets ≤ 20 mins = 4 + 7 = 11 tickets. | (11 ÷ 15) × 100 = 73.3%. Yes, 73.3% exceeds the 70% target. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) Frequencies: 4, 7, 4 | (b) Modal class: 10 < t ≤ 20; Yes (73.3% resolved in ≤ 20 mins)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Look for the line under the inequality sign (≤ means "includes this number").
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Pay close attention to boundary numbers: 10 belongs in 0 < t ≤ 10, and 20 belongs in 10 < t ≤ 20!
Question 6 (Level 2 Multi-Step Problem Solving — 3 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
In a speed survey of 120 vehicles: 0–20 mph (18), 21–30 mph (66), 31–40 mph (27), 41–50 mph (9). Calculate the percentage of vehicles exceeding the 30 mph speed limit.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Vehicles over 30 mph = 27 + 9 = 36 vehicles. [M1]
2. Percentage = (36 ÷ 120) × 100 = 30%. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 30%
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Stating a conclusion without showing the full numerical comparison values.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Combine both groups above 30 mph (27 + 9 = 36) before dividing by 120.
