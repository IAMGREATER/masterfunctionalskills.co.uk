Three Non-Negotiable Exam Golden Rules

Golden Rule 1: 6x6 Concise Bullets Rule — Prompts Only, Never Essays!

Slides anchor audience attention visually; aim for concise ~6 bullet points max (short scannable phrases), moving detailed verbal scripts into **Speaker Notes** underneath.

✗ Pasting 180-word paragraphs directly onto Slide 3 in tiny 11pt font
✓ 3–5 crisp bullet takeaways (24–28pt font) + detailed talk script in Notes Pane
Golden Rule 2: High Colour Contrast & >= 24pt Body Font Across All Slides

Keep slide body text >= 24pt and headings >= 36pt with crisp foreground/background contrast (dark navy text on cream/white, OR white on deep navy).

✗ Yellow text over bright cyan gradient backgrounds
✓ Consistent corporate Slide Master template, dark font on light uncluttered slide
Golden Rule 3: Restrained Professional Transitions & Consistent Slide Layout

Pick one subtle slide transition (e.g. Fade) applied consistently across slides; avoid spinning flying word-art distractions.

✗ Different 3-second bouncing sound effects on every slide element
✓ Clean Fade transition + clear Slide Title + visual chart/diagram balance

Step-by-Step Teaching & Key Concepts

1
Choosing Slide Layouts

When adding a new slide (Ctrl + M), always select a layout suited to your content:

  • Title Slide: First slide of your deck. Contains presentation title, subtitle, and speaker name.
  • Title and Content: Standard slide layout. Has a title banner at the top and a large content box for bullet points, a table, or a chart.
  • Two Content / Comparison: Features two columns side by side — ideal for comparing pros and cons or placing a photo next to text.
New Slide: Ctrl + M • Start Slide Show: F5
2
Principles of Professional Slide Design

Avoid cluttered slides that overwhelm the audience:

  • The 6×6 Rule: Aim for no more than 6 bullet points per slide, with roughly 6 words per bullet.
  • High Contrast: Dark text on light backgrounds (or white text on deep navy) ensures clear legibility from the back of a meeting room.
  • Consistent Theme: Use the same fonts, brand colours, and layout styling across every slide in the deck.
Tutor Abdul's Golden Presentation Tip: Slides are visual prompts for the audience, not a teleprompter for you to read word-for-word! Use concise bullets on screen and write detailed explanations in your Speaker Notes.
3
Adding Speaker Notes & Slide Transitions

To present with confidence:

  • Speaker Notes: Click the Notes button at the bottom of PowerPoint. Type your private talking points here. When using Presenter View on a projector, only you can see the notes on your laptop screen!
  • Slide Transitions: The animation when moving from one slide to the next (e.g. Fade or Push). Keep transitions subtle and consistent across all slides; avoid spinning or noisy effects.

Worked Model 1 • Core StandardStep-by-Step Accredited Guide
Exam Scenario Prompt: A trainer is designing a 5-slide presentation on 'Infection Control'. Explain two design rules they should follow to ensure the slides are accessible and professional, and state where the trainer should type their personal talking notes.
Step 1: First design rule [B1]
Use concise bullet points (short key phrases rather than huge paragraphs of text).
Tutor Marking Rationale: Prevents cognitive overload and maintains visual clarity.
Step 2: Second design rule [B1]
Ensure strong colour contrast between text and background (e.g. dark text on light cream/white background).
Tutor Marking Rationale: Ensures legibility and accessibility for audience members with visual impairments.
Step 3: Location for private notes [B1]
In the Speaker Notes pane at the bottom of the slide window.
Tutor Marking Rationale: Speaker notes are visible only to the presenter.
Tutor Abdul's Exam Golden Rule: In Level 1 exams, presentation questions test layout simplicity: 'Concise bullets', 'High contrast', and 'Speaker notes'.
Worked Model 2 • Applied Workplace Scenario Exam Scenario Model 2 • Reworking a Wall-of-Text Slide Using Speaker Notes
Exam Scenario Prompt 2: A trainer creates Slide 2 on Hand Hygiene with a 140-word paragraph at 12pt Times New Roman over a busy photo background. Identify three improvements to reach Pearson Level 1 Distinction.
Step 1: Distil Body Copy Into Scannable 24pt+ Bullets + Notes [B1][M1]
Cut the 140-word paragraph down to 4 concise bullet takeaways (e.g. 'Wash hands 20 seconds', 'Use alcohol gel on arrival') in 24pt+ sans-serif (Arial/Calibri) and paste the detailed speaking script into the Speaker Notes pane.
Tutor Marking Rationale: Solves readability immediately while preserving full speaker knowledge.
Step 2: Clean Layout Contrast & Balanced Visual Framing [B1]
Change layout to 'Two Content' (Text left, crisp single high-contrast photo right on a clean pale background) instead of placing words directly over busy photography.
Tutor Marking Rationale: Eliminates background noise and achieves clean visual hierarchy.
Tutor Abdul's Distinction Tip: Check Slide Footers (`Insert > Header & Footer`): adding consistent Slide Numbers (`1, 2, 3...`) and Title in Slide Master looks super polished!

Critical Examiner Traps & Common Pitfalls

Trap 1: Low-Contrast Fancy Gradient Text Boxes
Light grey text looks okay on a bright desk monitor yet disappears completely on overhead projectors.
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Verify crisp dark/light contrast before exporting.
Trap 2: Inconsistent Fonts & Slide Positions Between Slides
Slide 1 title sits top-left 40pt Georgia while Slide 2 sits centred 28pt Comic Sans.
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Stick to built-in slide Theme/Layout templates (`Title and Content`) without manually shifting every box.
Trap 3: Embedding Uncompressed Videos That Break Size Caps
Dropping a 100 MB raw video inside `.pptx` blocks portal upload.
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Link external reliable video or compress media inside File > Info > Compress Media when sharing slides.

Accredited Exam Benchmark Solution

New slide: On the Home tab, click the 'New Slide' dropdown and select 'Title and Content'. Content: Type 'Key Safety Rules' in the title box, and enter two concise bullet points in the main text box. Transition: Go to the 'Transitions' tab, click 'Fade', and click 'Apply To All' for consistency.
