Step-by-Step Teaching & Key Concepts

Core Knowledge
1
Starting Up and Logging In

To turn on a computer, press the physical Power Button once. When the lock screen appears, click or swipe up, then enter your Username and Password / PIN.

Never share your password with colleagues. If you walk away from your desk, lock the screen immediately to protect confidentiality.

Lock Computer Immediately: Win + L • Open Start Menu: Win Key
2
Sleep vs Restart vs Shut Down

Always use the software power menu rather than holding down the physical button:

  • Shut Down: Completely closes all open programs, saves system state, and turns off the electrical power safely. Use this at the end of the working day.
  • Restart: Closes all programs, shuts down the system, and immediately turns it back on. Essential when installing updates or fixing glitches.
  • Sleep Mode: Puts the computer into a low-power state. Open work remains in memory so you can resume work instantly when touching a key.
Exam Trap: Unsaved Work: Before choosing 'Shut Down' or 'Restart', always save your files! Shutting down without saving will cause you to lose unsaved progress.
3
Navigating the Desktop & Taskbar

The main screen after logging in is the Desktop. At the bottom is the Taskbar:

  • Start Button: Click to view all installed applications, files, and power options.
  • Pinned Apps: Frequently used icons kept on the taskbar for single-click opening.
  • System Tray / Notification Area: Located on the bottom right, displaying the clock, Wi-Fi status, volume, and battery percentage.

Authentic Pearson Edexcel Exam Model

Accredited Model

Here is how this skill is tested in the real Pearson Edexcel onscreen digital assessment, broken down with official examiner mark codes ([B1] knowledge, [M1] process, [A1] accuracy).

Exam Scenario Prompt: A clinic worker is leaving their desk for a 15-minute tea break. The computer contains confidential patient appointments. Explain what action the worker must take to keep the device secure while away from the desk.
Step 1: Select the correct security action [B1]
Lock the device (or press Windows Key + L)
Tutor Marking Rationale: Locking the device hides the active screen behind the password prompt while keeping programs open safely.
Step 2: Explain why this protects data [B1]
Prevents unauthorized people from viewing or accessing sensitive information without logging in.
Tutor Marking Rationale: Demonstrates understanding of data security and confidentiality standards.
Tutor Abdul's Exam Golden Rule: In Entry 3 exams, questions often ask what to do when stepping away briefly: the answer is 'Lock the device'. At the end of the shift, the answer is 'Save work and Shut Down'.
