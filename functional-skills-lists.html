Specification & Learning Outcomes

Candidates must demonstrate mastery of the following regulated Functional Skills Mathematics outcomes:

  • E3.7 / L1.24 – Sort and organise lists alphabetically and numerically and filter records against multi-part criteria.

Golden Rules for Lists: Sorting & Information Extraction

Golden Rule 1: Alphabetical Ordering: Compare 1st Letter → 2nd Letter → 3rd Letter

When words start with the same first letter, compare their second letters. If those match too, compare their third letters (e.g. Parker comes before Patel because r comes before t).

Golden Rule / Method: Compare 1st Letter → 2nd Letter → 3rd Letter (Shorter Word First: Hall before Halley)
Golden Rule 2: Multi-Criteria Filtering — Cross Off Excluded Items One Rule at a Time

When a question gives 3 requirements (e.g. "Must be available on Friday, hold a First Aid certificate, and cost under £150"), apply Rule 1 and cross out failures, then Rule 2, then Rule 3.

Golden Rule / Method: Eliminate Rule by Rule Until Only Matching Candidates Remain

Core Skill 1: Alphabetical Filing Rules for Surnames & Codes

Sorting patient records and stock codes accurately.

  • Identical Surnames: Sort by Surname first; if two people share the same Surname (e.g. Smith, David and Smith, Alice), compare their First Names (Smith, Alice comes first).
  • Alphanumeric Stock Codes: Sort the letters alphabetically first, then sort the numbers by numerical size (e.g. AB-104, AB-210, AC-050).
Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 2: Chronological Date & Time Sorting

Ordering expiry dates, appointments, and birthdates from earliest to latest.

To sort dates in chronological order (earliest first), compare in this exact order:

  1. Year first (e.g. 2025 before 2026)
  2. Month second (e.g. 03/2026 before 08/2026)
  3. Day third (e.g. 05/03/2026 before 19/03/2026)
Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 3: Filtering Lists with Multiple Workplace Constraints

Selecting the right room, vehicle, or staff member from a specification list.

Use a tick/cross checklist for each item against every constraint in the question so you can justify your choice clearly.

Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Benchmark Exam Model 1 2 Marks (Entry Level 3 / Level 1)

Vocational Context: Health Records Management

Arrange these five patient surnames in alphabetical order (A to Z):
Patel, Parker, Phillips, Price, Pearce
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Group by 2nd letter (Pa, Pe, Ph, Pr)
Pa: Parker, Patel | Pe: Pearce | Ph: Phillips | Pr: Price.
Criteria: M1: Grouping by second letter
Step 2 Compare 3rd letter of Parker (r) vs Patel (t)
r comes before t → Parker, Patel, Pearce, Phillips, Price.
Criteria: A1: Exact alphabetical order
Final Accredited Answer: Parker, Patel, Pearce, Phillips, Price
Method Checklist & Marking Scheme Guidance
Write down the 2nd and 3rd letters above each name to avoid slips.
Benchmark Exam Model 2 4 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 1/2)

Vocational Context: NHS Training Day Venue Selection

A training coordinator needs a venue for 45 delegates that has Wheelchair Access, includes Free Parking, and costs under £900 in total (Room Hire + Catering at £12 per delegate):
• Hall A: Capacity 60 | Wheelchair: Yes | Parking: Free | Room Hire: £320
• Hall B: Capacity 40 | Wheelchair: Yes | Parking: Free | Room Hire: £250
• Hall C: Capacity 50 | Wheelchair: Yes | Parking: Paid (£5) | Room Hire: £280
• Hall D: Capacity 55 | Wheelchair: Yes | Parking: Free | Room Hire: £390
Identify which hall meets all criteria and state its total cost.
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Eliminate venues failing capacity or parking criteria
Hall B fails capacity (40 < 45 delegates).
Hall C fails Free Parking.
Criteria: M1: Eliminating Halls B and C
Step 2 Calculate catering cost for 45 delegates at £12 each
45 × £12 = £540 catering cost.
Criteria: M1: Catering cost (£540)
Step 3 Check total cost for Hall A and Hall D against £900 limit
Hall A Total = £320 + £540 = £860 (< £900 — Passes).
Hall D Total = £390 + £540 = £930 (> £900 — Fails).
Select Hall A at £860.
Criteria: M1 / A1: Hall A selected with £860 total
Final Accredited Answer: Hall A (Total cost = £860, meeting all criteria)
Method Checklist & Marking Scheme Guidance
State clearly why each rejected option failed so you earn full process marks.

Critical Examiner Traps & Common Pitfalls

Examiner Pitfall: Sorting Dates by the Day Number Instead of Year/Month First
Putting 02/11/2026 before 18/04/2026 because 02 is smaller than 18!
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Always compare Year first, then Month (April = 04 comes before November = 11), then Day.
Examiner Pitfall: Missing One Constraint in a Multi-Criteria Question
Picking Hall B because it is the cheapest without noticing its capacity is only 40 (< 45 needed).
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Underline every single requirement in the question stem before checking the list.

Interactive Exam Practice Questions

Exam Practice Question 1 2 Marks
Arrange these five delivery towns in alphabetical order: Bolton, Bradford, Bristol, Bath, Barnsley.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Ba comes before Bo and Br: Barnsley (Bar) vs Bath (Bat) → r before t → Barnsley, Bath.
2. Bo: Bolton.
3. Br: Bradford (Bra) vs Bristol (Bri) → Bradford, Bristol.
4. Full order: Barnsley, Bath, Bolton, Bradford, Bristol.
Final Correct Answer: Barnsley, Bath, Bolton, Bradford, Bristol
Examiner Tip: Compare the 3rd letters for Barnsley (r) and Bath (t).
Exam Practice Question 2 2 Marks
A pharmacy technician checks four medicine batch expiry dates so the earliest expiry is used first (FEFO): 14/09/2026, 28/03/2026, 05/12/2025, 19/03/2026. List them from earliest to latest.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. 2025 comes first: 05/12/2025.
2. March 2026 (03/2026): 19/03/2026 then 28/03/2026.
3. September 2026: 14/09/2026.
Final Correct Answer: 05/12/2025, 19/03/2026, 28/03/2026, 14/09/2026
Examiner Tip: Sort by Year (2025 before 2026), then Month (03 before 09), then Day (19 before 28).
Exam Practice Question 3 2 Marks
From this list of staff: Ali (Band 5, Night, First Aid), Ben (Band 6, Day, First Aid), Cara (Band 6, Night, No First Aid), Dan (Band 6, Night, First Aid) — who meets ALL three rules: Band 6, Night Shift, and First Aid qualified?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Ali is Band 5 (Eliminated).
2. Ben is Day Shift (Eliminated).
3. Cara has No First Aid (Eliminated).
4. Dan meets all three criteria.
Final Correct Answer: Dan
Examiner Tip: Cross off candidates who fail any one of the three rules.
