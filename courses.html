Maths Level 2: Complete 39-Topic Curriculum

Number Systems • Measures, Shape & Space • Data & Statistics
Standard 1: Using Numbers & The Number System (14 Topics)
  • Positive and negative numbers in context
  • Fractions of amounts, addition & mixed numbers
  • Percentages, percentage change & reverse percentages
  • Ratio sharing and direct/inverse proportion
  • Compound interest, AER loans & personal finance
  • Estimation, rounding & order of operations (BIDMAS)
Standard 2: Measures, Shape & Space (15 Topics)
  • Perimeter, circumference & compound 2D area
  • Volume and surface area of prisms and cylinders
  • Scale drawings, floor plans & 3-figure bearings
  • Metric and imperial unit conversions
  • Compound measures: Speed, density & pressure
Standard 3: Handling Information & Data (10 Topics)
  • Mean, median, mode and range comparisons
  • Estimated mean from grouped frequency tables
  • Scatter graphs, correlation & lines of best fit
  • Probability outcomes, sample space & tree diagrams

English Level 2: Reading, Writing & SLC

Textual Comparison • 11 Workplace Writing Formats • Speaking & Listening
Component 1: Reading Comprehension (12 Topics)
  • Identifying implicit meaning, bias, tone and style
  • Multi-text comparison (pulling points together from Text A, B & C)
  • Organisational features (headings, subheadings, bullet points)
  • Language devices (rhetorical questions, rule of three, metaphors)
Component 2: Writing Composition & SPaG (11 Formats)
  • Formal letters, professional workplace emails, and incident reports
  • Articles, informational leaflets, speeches, and persuasive reviews
  • SPaG mastery: Colons, semicolons, apostrophes & sentence variation
  • Proofreading tips to avoid losing easy marks on spelling and punctuation
Component 3: Speaking, Listening & Communicating (SLC)
  • Formal presentation preparation with visual slides & handouts
  • Group discussions, asking clarifying questions & active listening

Digital Functional Skills Level 1: 5 Core Standards

Devices & Files • Digital Content • Communication • Safety & Problem Solving
  • Using Devices & Handling Information: File management, folders, searching and downloading files safely.
  • Creating & Editing: Word processing layouts, spreadsheet formulas (SUM, AVERAGE), and slide decks.
  • Communicating: Workplace emails, attachments, calendar invites, and video meeting etiquette.
  • Transacting: Online shopping, secure account logins, two-factor authentication, and digital forms.
  • Being Safe & Responsible Online: Phishing detection, strong passwords, data privacy, and digital well-being.
