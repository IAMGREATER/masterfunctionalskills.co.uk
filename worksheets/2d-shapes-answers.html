Section A: Core Skills (Warm-Up) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Polygon Interior & Exterior Properties) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A clinical courtyard paving slab is shaped as a regular hexagon with each side measuring 45 cm.
Calculate the total perimeter of one hexagonal slab in metres.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Multiply 6 equal sides by 45 cm: 6 × 45 = 270 cm [M1]
2. Convert centimetres to metres: 270 ÷ 100 = 2.7 m [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 2.7 m
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Multiplying by 5 (pentagon) or 8 (octagon) instead of 6 for a hexagon.
🔄 Quick Check: 2.7 m = 270 cm; 270 ÷ 6 = 45 cm per side.
Question 2 (Level 1 Foundation — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A ceramic wall tile has 6 equal sides and 6 equal interior angles, each side measuring 8 cm.
(a) What is the full mathematical name of this shape?
(b) Calculate the perimeter of one tile.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. A 6-sided polygon with equal sides and equal angles is a Regular Hexagon. [M1]
2. Perimeter = 6 × 8 cm = 48 cm. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) Regular Hexagon | (b) 48 cm
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: A standard non-rectangular parallelogram has ZERO lines of reflective symmetry, even though it has rotational symmetry of order 2.
🔄 Quick Check: Include the word "Regular" whenever all sides and angles are equal.
Section B: Everyday & Workplace Maths Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Hospital Signboard Geometry & Lines of Symmetry (Level 1 Foundation)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
An emergency stop signboard is manufactured in the shape of a regular octagon.
(a) How many sides does an octagon have?
(b) How many lines of reflective symmetry does a regular octagon possess?
(c) State the order of rotational symmetry of a regular octagon.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. State the number of sides of an octagon: An octagon has 8 straight sides. [A1]
2. Determine lines of reflective symmetry: A regular polygon with n sides has n lines of symmetry. An 8-sided regular octagon has 8 lines of symmetry. [A1]
3. Determine rotational symmetry order: A regular octagon matches its outline 8 times in a 360° turn. Order = 8. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) 8 sides | (b) 8 lines of symmetry | (c) Rotational symmetry of order 8
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Fold it mentally: the corners do not align when folded horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
🔄 Quick Check: For ANY regular polygon, number of sides = lines of symmetry = order of rotational symmetry!
Question 4 (Workplace Application — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A quadrilateral has one pair of parallel sides of lengths 12 cm and 18 cm, and two equal non-parallel sloping sides of 7 cm each.
(a) Name this specific type of quadrilateral.
(b) How many lines of symmetry does it have?
(c) Calculate its perimeter.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. One pair of parallel sides and equal sloping sides = Isosceles Trapezium. [M1]
2. An isosceles trapezium has 1 vertical line of symmetry. [P1]
3. Perimeter = 12 + 18 + 7 + 7 = 44 cm. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) Isosceles Trapezium | (b) 1 line of symmetry | (c) 44 cm
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Only a square has 4 lines of symmetry. A rectangle has only 2 (vertical and horizontal midlines; folding along a diagonal does not line up the corners).
🔄 Quick Check: A standard trapezium has 0 lines of symmetry, but an isosceles trapezium (equal slanted sides) has 1.
Section C: Exam-Style Problem Solving Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Architectural Paving Slab Polygon Tessellation Audit (Level 2 Multi-Step)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A courtyard designer arranges regular hexagonal paving slabs of side length 25 cm around a central square feature.
• Twenty-four outer edges of the hexagons form the external boundary of the patio.
• Steel edging strips are sold in 1.5 m lengths at £9.20 per strip.
Calculate the total cost of the steel edging strips required for the 24 outer hexagon edges.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate total boundary perimeter in cm and convert to metres: 24 edges × 25 cm = 600 cm. | 600 cm ÷ 100 = 6.0 metres. [M1]
2. Calculate number of 1.5 m steel strips required: 6.0 m ÷ 1.5 m = 4 strips. [M1]
3. Calculate total cost of steel edging: 4 × £9.20 = £36.80. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £36.80 (4 strips needed for 6.0 m boundary)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Remember: Rectangle = 2 lines; Square = 4 lines.
🔄 Quick Check: Because the hexagons are regular, every single side measures exactly 25 cm.
Question 6 (Level 2 Multi-Step Problem Solving — 3 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A rectangular metal frame measures 1.2 m long by 0.8 m wide, with one diagonal brace running from corner to corner of length 1.44 m.
(a) How many right angles (90°) are in the outer rectangular frame?
(b) Calculate the total length of steel bar needed to make the outer frame plus the single diagonal brace.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. A rectangle has 4 right angles (90°). [M1]
2. Outer frame perimeter = 2 × (1.2 + 0.8) = 4.0 m. [P1]
3. Add diagonal brace: 4.0 m + 1.44 m = 5.44 m. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) 4 right angles | (b) 5.44 m
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Stating a conclusion without showing the full numerical comparison values.
🔄 Quick Check: Read carefully whether internal braces are included in the total steel length.
