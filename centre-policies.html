Quality Assurance & Regulatory Governance

Assessment Governance & Learner Policies

Transparent, learner-centred policies aligned with Ofqual General Conditions of Recognition to ensure fair access, robust exam security, neurodiverse accessibility, and clear learner rights.

1. Reasonable Adjustments & Special Consideration Policy

Master Functional Skills is built from the ground up for neurodiverse and dyslexic learners. Candidates with documented learning differences, medical conditions, or disabilities may apply for reasonable adjustments (including up to 25% extra time, supervised rest breaks, onscreen tinted overlays, reader/scribe support where permitted by the specification, and assistive technology) at least 15 working days prior to an assessment sitting.

2. Appeals & Enquiries About Results Policy

Every learner has the right to a transparent, fair assessment decision. If a candidate believes an assessment outcome or mark administration does not reflect their performance, they may request a Stage 1 Internal Review within 10 working days of results notification, followed where applicable by a formal Awarding Organisation Enquiry About Results or Appeal in accordance with Ofqual General Conditions of Recognition.

3. Malpractice, Maladministration & AI Integrity Policy

To protect the integrity and national recognition of Functional Skills certificates, all assessments are conducted under strict identity and invigilation controls. Prohibited conduct — including unauthorised mobile devices, screen-sharing, third-party assistance, impersonation, or use of generative AI tools during controlled assessments — is investigated promptly and reported to the relevant Awarding Organisation in line with JCQ and Ofqual regulations.

4. Candidate Identity Verification & Remote Invigilation Protocol

Prior to any online remote-invigilated assessment, candidates must present valid government-issued photographic identification (Passport, UK Driving Licence, or Biometric Residence Permit), complete a 360-degree room and desk environment check via webcam/smartphone, and ensure a quiet, private room with stable broadband connectivity.

5. Safeguarding, Prevent & Learner Welfare Policy

We are committed to providing a safe, respectful, and supportive online learning environment for adult learners, apprentices, and young people aged 16+. All tutors and assessment staff hold enhanced DBS clearance, complete annual Safeguarding and Prevent Duty training, and maintain clear escalation channels for learner wellbeing concerns.

6. Equality, Diversity & Inclusion (EDI) Policy

Master Functional Skills promotes equal access to education and assessment regardless of age, disability, gender reassignment, marriage and civil partnership, pregnancy and maternity, race, religion or belief, sex, or sexual orientation. All learning resources use plain, accessible British English and built-in dyslexia readability controls.

7. Learner Complaints & Service Standards Procedure

We aim to resolve any query regarding tuition, digital resources, or exam administration informally within 2 working days. Formal written complaints submitted via our Contact page are acknowledged within 3 working days and investigated by a senior quality lead with a full written outcome provided within 15 working days.

8. UK GDPR, Data Protection & Assessment Record Retention

Learner personal data, diagnostic outcomes, and assessment recordings are processed strictly in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and Data Protection Act 2018. Assessment registration records and invigilation evidence are encrypted and retained only for the statutory regulatory audit period required by Ofqual and the Awarding Organisation.
