Question 1 Worked Solution NHS Healthcare & Workplace Scenario
[3 Marks Available]
An NHS healthcare administrator manages resource scheduling involving Grouped Frequency Tables.
The baseline record requires evaluating three consecutive ward operations with values 120, 180, and 240 units.
Calculate the combined outcome and determine if the total satisfies the threshold limit of 500 units.
1. Sum the baseline resource values: 120 + 180 + 240 = 540 units
[M1]
2. Compare against threshold: 540 > 500 units
[M1]
3. State supported conclusion: Total exceeds threshold by 40 units
[A1]
Correct Final Answer: Threshold Exceeded (Total = 540 units, 40 over limit)
Quick Check Method: 540 - 240 - 180 = 120 units.
Common Mistake to Avoid: Omitting the comparison statement against the threshold limit.
Question 2 Worked Solution Commercial Trade & Logistics
[3 Marks Available]
A warehouse logistics supervisor reviews shipping schedules involving Grouped Frequency Tables.
Each pallet requires standard packaging costing £14.50 plus a handling surcharge of 12%.
Calculate the total cost to dispatch 20 pallets.
1. Calculate handling surcharge: £14.50 × 0.12 = £1.74
[M1]
2. Total cost per pallet: £14.50 + £1.74 = £16.24
[M1]
3. Multiply for 20 pallets: £16.24 × 20 = £324.80
[A1]
Correct Final Answer: £324.80
Quick Check Method: £324.80 ÷ 20 = £16.24; £16.24 ÷ 1.12 = £14.50.
Common Mistake to Avoid: Multiplying £14.50 by 20 and forgetting the 12% surcharge.
