Document Uploads and Digital Support Portals — Answer Key

4. Transacting • Standard Code: L1.4.3
Style 1: Solutions
Task 1 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

Why might an online government portal refuse to accept an uploaded photo of your passport?

A. The file size exceeds the maximum limit or the file format is not accepted (e.g. docx instead of pdf/jpg) (CORRECT)
B. The government website is sleeping
C. Passports cannot be stored on computers
D. The internet is only for text, not photos
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Portals reject files that violate strict format (.pdf, .jpg) or file size (MB) constraints.

Task 2 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What should you always retain as evidence after submitting an official document or cancelling a subscription online?

A. The confirmation reference number or confirmation email (CORRECT)
B. A photograph of your computer keyboard
C. Your computer power cable
D. The cardboard box your laptop arrived in
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Confirmation emails and reference numbers act as legal proof of transaction.

Task 3 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

How can you prevent a 30-day free software trial from charging your bank card automatically?

A. Set a calendar reminder and cancel the subscription in account settings before the trial period expires (CORRECT)
B. Throw away your computer monitor
C. Uninstall your web browser
D. Change your desktop colour to black
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Cancelling prior to the renewal date terminates the recurring billing instruction.
