Section A Mark Scheme — Knowledge & Understanding

10 Marks
Q1 • 2FA: App Tokens vs SMS Interception
CORRECT: A 1 Mark [B1]
Pearson Examiner Rationale: App tokens are computed on-device via time-synchronized algorithms, removing vulnerability to cellular SIM-swapping attacks.
Q2 • Repetitive Strain Injury (RSI) Prevention
CORRECT: A 1 Mark [B1]
Pearson Examiner Rationale: Gel wrist rests, neutral wrist postures, and frequent micro-stretches prevent cumulative tendon inflammation per HSE guidelines.
Q3 • Boolean Search Operators: Minus (-) Operator
CORRECT: A 1 Mark [B1]
Pearson Examiner Rationale: Prepending a hyphen or minus sign (-) strictly excludes matching pages from the search engine index.
Q4 • Digital Footprint & Location Telemetry
CORRECT: A 1 Mark [B1]
Pearson Examiner Rationale: Continuous GPS tracking generates comprehensive location footprints; GDPR mandates proportionality and off-duty privacy.
Q5 • Cybersecurity Threats: Keyloggers (Spyware)
CORRECT: A 1 Mark [B1]
Pearson Examiner Rationale: A keylogger covertly records keystroke sequences to intercept passwords and account credentials.
Q6 • Operating System Security Patches
CORRECT: A 1 Mark [B1]
Pearson Examiner Rationale: Security patches remediate discovered software vulnerabilities before malicious actors can exploit them.
Q7 • Bandwidth (Mbps) vs Storage Capacity (GB)
CORRECT: A 1 Mark [B1]
Pearson Examiner Rationale: Megabits per second (Mbps) quantifies transfer speed; Gigabytes (GB) quantifies total file storage volume.
Q8 • UK GDPR: Right to Rectification
CORRECT: A 1 Mark [B1]
Pearson Examiner Rationale: Under GDPR Article 16, individuals have the statutory right to have inaccurate personal records corrected without undue delay.
Q9 • Image Formats: Lossy JPEG vs Lossless PNG
CORRECT: A 1 Mark [B1]
Pearson Examiner Rationale: PNG provides lossless transparency for logos; JPEG provides high-ratio compression for bulk delivery condition photographs.
Q10 • Secure Disposal of Digital Media
CORRECT: B 1 Mark [B1]
Pearson Examiner Rationale: Formatting leaves data magnetically recoverable; secure disposal requires accredited physical shredding or degaussing.
