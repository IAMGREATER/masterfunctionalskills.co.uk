Examiner Rule for The Writer's Purpose, Sub-Purpose & Audience Adaptation: Never write 'To inform' or 'To persuade' on its own! Always use the complete Pearson Edexcel formula: 'To [Purpose Verb: inform / persuade / instruct / advise / review] [the target audience] about [the specific topic and desired action].'
