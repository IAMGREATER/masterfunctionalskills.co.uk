UK's #1 Courses with ESFA & Ofsted Reporting

The Master Functional Skills course has been built using learner and tutor feedback which is why our pass rates are so high. We have built and own the rights to the content and software which enables us to respond to feedback quickly and continually improve. We have maths and English functional skills courses for Level 2, Level 1 and Entry Level 3, all of which accurately track distance travelled for each learner, making use of our proprietary Current Level logic.

Each course starts with an initial assessment to determine which level a learner should enrol on. Once a learner is enrolled on a course they then complete a subject knowledge assessment to produce an individual learning plan. Learners work through the plan with their Current Level updating in real time.

All of the learner data produced during their course work through is aggregated and analysed to produce a series of reports that can be accessed in the Training Provider login. These reports can be generated for an individual or a cohort of learners showing the progress made in multiple contexts. These reports are extremely useful as evidence to comply with the ESFA funding rules but also for Ofsted inspections where our Training Providers excel in demonstrating the support they are giving learners and the progress that is being made.

ESFA Compliant

Our assessment tools are based on the National Standards for Adult Literacy and Numeracy which is an ESFA requirement to assess the current level of learners. Our advanced initial assessment and diagnostic tests help to determine a learners current level whilst producing an individualised learning plan. All of the data produced can be exported into Excel and PDF documents making it easy to provide the required evidence to the ESFA.
