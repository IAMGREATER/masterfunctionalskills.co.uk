Entry Level 3 English • Exam Overview

▶Assessment Structure

Entry Level 3 English comprises two main assessments:

  • Reading Assessment: 45 minutes • 24 marks • 3 short everyday workplace and community texts.
  • Writing Assessment: 50 minutes • 36 marks • Spelling (Annexe D list), punctuation, sentence writing, and two short functional tasks (an email and an informal letter or report).

56% of marks in Writing are awarded for accurate Spelling, Punctuation and Grammar (SPaG).

✓Reading Tip
Read the text twice before answering reading questions.
!Writing Rule
Always start every sentence with a capital letter and end with a full stop.
