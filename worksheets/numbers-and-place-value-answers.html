Numbers & Place Value Pearson Edexcel: L1.1 • L1.2 • L2.1
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Procedural Fluency Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Level 1 Standard) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Write the number two million, forty-five thousand, six hundred and eight in figures.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Identify millions place (2,000,000) and thousands place (045,000) [M1]
2. Complete number with hundreds, tens, units: 2,045,608 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 2,045,608
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Candidates often omit the placeholder zero in the hundred-thousands column, erroneously writing 2,45,608 or 245,608.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Break down by column: 2,000,000 + 40,000 + 5,000 + 600 + 8 = 2,045,608. Confirmed.
Question 2 (Level 1 Standard) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
In the number 4,839,271, state the value of the digit 3 both in words and as a number.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Identify position as ten-thousands column [M1]
2. State value as 30,000 (thirty thousand) [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 30,000 (Thirty Thousand)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Writing just 'ten thousands' (identifying the place instead of the value) or writing '3' loses the accuracy mark.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 3 × 10,000 = 30,000.
Section B: Workplace & Functional Scenarios Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 Workplace Stock Audit) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
An NHS central distribution warehouse holds the following quantities of medical masks across four regional depots:
• Depot A: 340,500
• Depot B: 304,850
• Depot C: 345,000
• Depot D: 340,095

Write these depot stock figures in order of size, starting with the smallest stock quantity.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Compare hundred-thousands and ten-thousands digits to identify lowest: Depot B (304,850) [P1]
2. Compare remaining three depots with 340k+: Depot D (340,095), Depot A (340,500), Depot C (345,000) [P1]
3. Correct full ascending list: 304,850; 340,095; 340,500; 345,000 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 304,850 < 340,095 < 340,500 < 345,000
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Misreading 340,095 as larger than 340,500 by overlooking the tens versus hundreds placeholder zero.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Check reverse descending: 345,000 > 340,500 > 340,095 > 304,850.
Question 4 (Level 2 Decimals & Micro-Measurements) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A biomedical laboratory analyses blood serum sample concentrations in mg/dL: 0.048, 0.408, 0.0408, 0.48, 0.084.
Arrange these concentrations in order of magnitude from largest to smallest.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Pad numbers to 4 decimal places for alignment: 0.0480, 0.4080, 0.0408, 0.4800, 0.0840 [M1]
2. Compare tenths and hundredths to find largest: 0.48, then 0.408 [P1]
3. Order remaining values: 0.084, 0.048, 0.0408 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 0.48, 0.408, 0.084, 0.048, 0.0408
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Assuming longer decimal strings are bigger (e.g. thinking 0.0408 is larger than 0.48 because it has 4 digits).
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Convert to fractions over 10,000: 4800/10000 > 4080/10000 > 840/10000 > 480/10000 > 408/10000.
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Problem Solving & Justification Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Budget Breakdown) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
An Integrated Care Board (ICB) allocates an annual mental health modernization grant of £3,850,000.
The financial director reports that 'Three hundred and eighty-five thousand pounds' will be spent on staff wellness, while 'Three million, four hundred and sixty thousand pounds' is allocated to clinical facility upgrades.

Calculate whether the sum of these two allocations matches the total grant, or if there is an unallocated contingency balance. State the exact difference if any.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Convert staff wellness to digits: £385,000 [P1]
2. Convert clinical facilities to digits: £3,460,000 [P1]
3. Sum allocations: £385,000 + £3,460,000 = £3,845,000 [M1]
4. Compare with total grant: £3,850,000 - £3,845,000 = £5,000 unallocated contingency balance [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £5,000 unallocated contingency balance
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Writing £385,000 as 38,500 or £3,460,000 as 3,046,000 when converting verbal financial statements.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: £3,460,000 + £385,000 + £5,000 = £3,850,000.
Question 6 (Level 2 Decision & Justification) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A procurement manager claims: 'Rounding our annual consumables expenditure of £1,496,250 to the nearest ten thousand gives £1,490,000.'

Is the manager's claim correct?
Show your rounding method clearly, explain why the claim is right or wrong, and state the correct rounded figure.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Identify the ten-thousands digit (9, representing 90,000) and the deciding digit immediately to its right in thousands column (6) [P1]
2. Apply rounding rule: since the deciding digit is 6 (≥ 5), round UP: 90,000 + 10,000 = 100,000 (carrying 1 to hundred-thousands) [M1]
3. Correct rounded value: £1,500,000 [A1]
4. Valid conclusion: No / Incorrect, the manager rounded down or truncated the number rather than rounding up to £1,500,000. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Incorrect; Correct rounded value is £1,500,000
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Learners often forget to carry over into the next place column when rounding a '9', resulting in £1,490,000 or £1,410,000.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: £1,496,250 is £6,250 away from £1,490,000, but only £3,750 away from £1,500,000. It is closer to £1,500,000.
