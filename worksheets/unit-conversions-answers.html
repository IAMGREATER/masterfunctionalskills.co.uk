Metric & Imperial Unit Conversions Pearson Edexcel: L1.18 • L2.20 • L2.21
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Core Skills (Warm-Up) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Level 1 Metric Length & Mass Conversion) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Complete both metric conversions below:
(a) Convert 4.08 kilometres into metres.
(b) Convert 650 grams into kilograms.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. (a) Multiply km by 1,000: 4.08 × 1,000 = 4,080 m [A1]
2. (b) Divide grams by 1,000: 650 ÷ 1,000 = 0.65 kg [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) 4,080 m | (b) 0.65 kg
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Multiplying or dividing by 100 instead of 1,000.
🔄 Quick Check: 4,080 ÷ 1,000 = 4.08 km; 0.65 × 1,000 = 650 g.
Question 2 (Level 2 Miles and Kilometres Conversion) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
An organ transplant courier drives 104 kilometres from an airport to a specialist hospital.
Using the conversion 5 miles = 8 kilometres, calculate this distance in miles.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Divide kilometres by 8 to find 1 unit: 104 ÷ 8 = 13 [M1]
2. Multiply by 5: 13 × 5 = 65 miles [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 65 miles
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Calculating (104 ÷ 5) × 8 = 166.4 miles (miles must be smaller than km!).
🔄 Quick Check: 65 ÷ 5 × 8 = 13 × 8 = 104 km.
Section B: Everyday & Workplace Maths Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 Liquid Capacity Unit Mixing) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A hospital catering team prepares a fruit punch by mixing:
• 3.5 litres of orange juice
• 75 centilitres (cl) of cranberry juice
• 450 millilitres (ml) of ginger ale

Calculate the total volume of fruit punch in millilitres (ml).
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Convert 3.5 L to ml: 3.5 × 1,000 = 3,500 ml [M1]
2. Convert 75 cl to ml: 75 × 10 = 750 ml [P1]
3. Sum all three volumes in ml: 3,500 + 750 + 450 = 4,700 ml [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 4,700 ml (or 4.7 litres)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Converting 75 cl as 75 ml instead of 750 ml (remember 1 cl = 10 ml).
🔄 Quick Check: 3.5 L + 0.75 L + 0.45 L = 4.7 L = 4,700 ml.
Question 4 (Level 2 Stone & Pounds to Kilograms Clinical Dosage) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A patient weighs 12 stone 10 lbs.
Use the conversions: 1 stone = 14 lbs and 1 kg = 2.2 lbs.

Calculate the patient's weight in kilograms (kg).
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Convert 12 stone to lbs: 12 × 14 = 168 lbs [M1]
2. Add remaining 10 lbs: 168 + 10 = 178 lbs total [P1]
3. Divide by 2.2 to convert to kg: 178 ÷ 2.2 = 80.91 kg (accept 80.9 kg or 81 kg) [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 80.91 kg (or 81 kg)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Treating 12 stone 10 lbs as 12.10 stone or assuming 10 lbs is 1 stone.
🔄 Quick Check: 80.909 × 2.2 = 178 lbs = (12 × 14) + 10.
Section C: Exam-Style Problem Solving Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Imported Medical Fridge Clearance Check) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A pharmacy orders an imported American vaccine refrigerator with dimensions:
• Height: 6 feet 4 inches
• Width: 30 inches
The pharmacy alcove where it will stand measures 198 cm high by 80 cm wide.
Use: 1 foot = 12 inches and 1 inch = 2.54 cm.

Will the refrigerator fit into the alcove? Check both height and width in centimetres.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Convert height to inches: (6 × 12) + 4 = 76 inches [P1]
2. Convert height to cm: 76 × 2.54 = 193.04 cm [M1]
3. Convert width to cm: 30 × 2.54 = 76.2 cm [P1]
4. Compare both with alcove (198 cm × 80 cm): Height 193.04 cm < 198 cm and Width 76.2 cm < 80 cm. Yes, the refrigerator will fit. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Yes, fits (Height = 193.04 cm < 198 cm; Width = 76.2 cm < 80 cm)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Converting 6 feet 4 inches as 6.4 feet × 12 = 76.8 inches.
🔄 Quick Check: 193.04 ÷ 2.54 = 76 inches = 6 ft 4 in; 76.2 ÷ 2.54 = 30 in.
Question 6 (Level 2 Fuel Tanker Gallons vs Litres Cost Comparison) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A backup hospital generator requires 250 gallons of red diesel.
• Supplier A quotes £5.22 per gallon.
• Supplier B quotes £1.12 per litre.
• Use the conversion: 1 gallon = 4.54 litres.

Which supplier is cheaper for the 250-gallon order, and by how much?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Supplier A total cost: 250 gallons × £5.22 = £1,305.00 [P1]
2. Convert 250 gallons into litres: 250 × 4.54 = 1,135 litres [M1]
3. Supplier B total cost: 1,135 litres × £1.12 = £1,271.20 [P1]
4. Compare and conclude: Supplier B (£1,271.20) is cheaper than Supplier A (£1,305.00) by £33.80. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Supplier B is cheaper by £33.80 (£1,271.20 vs £1,305.00)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Dividing 250 by 4.54 instead of multiplying gallons by 4.54 to get litres.
🔄 Quick Check: £1,305.00 - £1,271.20 = £33.80.
