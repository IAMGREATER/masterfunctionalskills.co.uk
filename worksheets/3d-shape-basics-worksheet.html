Examiner Rule for 3D Shape Basics: Cubes, Spheres & Cones: Golden Rule 1: Use 3D Names for Solids (Never Call a Cube a Square!): 2D shapes are flat (Square, Rectangle, Circle, Triangle). 3D shapes have depth and volume (Cube, Cuboid, Sphere/Cylinder, Pyramid/Cone). Calling a cuboid box a "rectangle" loses the mark!
