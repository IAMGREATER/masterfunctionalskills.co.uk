Word Processing: Entering Text and Fonts — Answer Key

2. Creating and Editing • Standard Code: E3.2.1
Style 1: Solutions (6 Drills)
Task 1 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What keyboard shortcut quickly turns selected text into BOLD?

A. Ctrl + B (CORRECT)
B. Ctrl + U
C. Ctrl + S
D. Ctrl + P
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Ctrl + B toggles Bold emphasis on and off for selected words.

Task 2 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

How can you select a single word quickly with the mouse without dragging?

A. Single-click on the word
B. Double-click on the word (CORRECT)
C. Right-click the mouse scroll wheel
D. Hold the spacebar
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Double-clicking any word immediately highlights that entire word.

Task 3 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

Why is it important to use clear sans-serif fonts (like Lexend, Arial, or Calibri) in workplace notices?

A. They use less printer ink than any other font
B. They are clean and much easier for people with dyslexia and visual impairments to read (CORRECT)
C. Computers cannot open documents with serif fonts
D. Word processors automatically delete fancy fonts
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Clean sans-serif fonts provide distinct letter shapes that enhance readability and accessibility.

Task 4 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

You accidentally highlight and delete two paragraphs of text while drafting a report. What is the quickest way to restore the deleted text?

A. Press Ctrl + Z (Undo) (CORRECT)
B. Press Ctrl + S (Save)
C. Close the program without saving and restart the computer
D. Press Ctrl + P (Print)
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

The keyboard shortcut Ctrl + Z (or clicking the curved Undo arrow on the toolbar) immediately reverses the last action and restores the accidentally deleted text.

Task 5 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

In a professional business letter, which font size is standard and recommended for the main body paragraphs?

A. 6 pt or 8 pt
B. 11 pt or 12 pt (CORRECT)
C. 24 pt or 28 pt
D. 48 pt
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Standard business typography recommendations use 11 pt or 12 pt for body text. Fonts smaller than 10 pt are difficult to read, while 24 pt and larger are reserved for headings and posters.

Task 6 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

You are editing an existing company policy document and want to keep the original copy unchanged while creating an updated version. What command should you use?

A. Save (Ctrl + S)
B. Save As (CORRECT)
C. Export to Audio
D. Print Preview
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

'Save As' prompts you to choose a new filename and storage location, creating an updated separate copy while leaving the original master document completely untouched.
