Part 1: Quick-Check Answer Key
Quick marking key for procedural drilling questions
Q1 Naming Polygons & Counting Sides
(a) Polygon with 5 straight sides → Pentagon (5 sides)
(b) Polygon with 6 straight sides → Hexagon (6 sides)
(c) Polygon with 8 straight sides → Octagon (8 sides)
(d) Polygon with 4 equal sides and 4 right angles → Square
(e) Polygon with 4 sides and only 1 pair of parallel sides → Trapezium
(f) Quadrilateral with 2 pairs of parallel sides and no right angles → Parallelogram / Rhombus
Q2 Symmetry & Shape Properties
(a) Equilateral triangle → 3 lines of symmetry
(b) Isosceles triangle → 1 line of symmetry
(c) Scalene triangle → 0 lines of symmetry
(d) Square → 4 lines of symmetry
(e) Rectangle (non-square) → 2 lines of symmetry
(f) Regular hexagon → 6 lines of symmetry
(g) Circle → Infinite lines of symmetry
(h) Parallelogram → 0 lines of symmetry (Rotational order 2)

Question 1 Worked Solution NHS Clinic Paving Design
[3 Marks Available]
An NHS healthcare facility installs safety signboards and paving.
An emergency stop sign is manufactured in the shape of a regular octagon.
Each side of the octagon measures 35 centimetres.
(a) Calculate the perimeter of the signboard in metres.
(b) How many lines of reflective symmetry does the sign have?
An octagon has 8 equal sides. Total perimeter in cm = 8 × 35 cm = 280 cm
[M1]
Convert to metres: 280 cm ÷ 100 = 2.8 metres
[A1]
A regular octagon has 8 lines of symmetry.
[A1]
Correct Final Answer: (a) 2.8 metres | (b) 8 lines of symmetry
Pearson Reverse Check Method: 2.8 m = 280 cm; 280 ÷ 8 = 35 cm.
Common Exam Trap / Pitfall: Confusing an octagon (8 sides) with a hexagon (6 sides).
