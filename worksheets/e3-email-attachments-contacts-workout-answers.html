Email Attachments and Contacts List — Answer Key

3. Communicating • Standard Code: E3.3.2
Style 1: Solutions (6 Drills)
Task 1 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

Which icon is universally used in email software to attach a file to a message?

A. A Paperclip icon (CORRECT)
B. A Magnifying glass
C. A Trash can
D. A Printer icon
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

The paperclip icon is the universal symbol for attaching a document or photo to an email.

Task 2 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What should you do if you receive an email with an unexpected .exe or .zip attachment from a stranger?

A. Do not open it; report and delete it immediately (CORRECT)
B. Open it immediately to see what it is
C. Forward it to all your friends
D. Save it to your desktop
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Unexpected attachments from unknown senders can contain viruses or malware; never open them.

Task 3 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What is the primary benefit of saving email addresses in your Contacts address book?

A. You can select contacts quickly without having to re-type long email addresses each time (CORRECT)
B. It automatically sends emails without you clicking send
C. It makes your computer run twice as fast
D. It prints emails automatically
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Address books prevent typos and save time by auto-filling verified email addresses.

Task 4 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

You attempt to attach a 65 MB raw video file to an email, but the email client displays an error. Why did this occur?

A. Most standard email servers enforce an attachment limit of 20 MB to 25 MB (CORRECT)
B. Videos are strictly illegal to send by email
C. The computer monitor is not set to high definition
D. The recipient's screen is turned off
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Standard email services (Outlook, Gmail) typically restrict attachment sizes to 20 MB or 25 MB. Larger files must be shared via cloud storage links or compressed into ZIP files.

Task 5 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What is the primary benefit of saving regular work contacts into your email address book?

A. It prevents typing errors in email addresses and allows quick auto-complete (CORRECT)
B. It gives you free unlimited internet bandwidth
C. It stops all spam emails from arriving
D. It automatically writes email replies for you
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Saving contacts prevents accidental typos in email addresses (which could send confidential data to the wrong person) and allows quick address auto-completion.

Task 6 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

You receive an unexpected email from an unknown sender with an attached file named 'Invoice_Overdue.exe'. What should you do?

A. Do not open the attachment; delete it or report it to IT security (CORRECT)
B. Double-click it immediately to see how much money is owed
C. Forward it to all your colleagues to see if it belongs to them
D. Save it to your desktop and run it as administrator
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

An '.exe' file is an executable application that can install dangerous malware or ransomware when opened. Never open unexpected executable files from unknown senders.
