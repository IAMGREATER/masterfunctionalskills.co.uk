Part 2: Real-Life Problem Solutions
Step-by-step methods and mark breakdown
Question 1 Worked Solution Commercial Sales Performance Audit
[3 Marks Available]
Three regional branch managers report their quarterly sales growth against target:
• Northern Branch achieved 3/8 above target
• Central Branch achieved 36% above target
• Southern Branch achieved 0.385 above target
Determine which branch achieved the greatest growth above target. You must show conversions to support your answer.
Convert Northern Branch to decimal: 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375 (or 37.5%)
[M1]
Convert Central Branch to decimal: 36% = 0.360 (or 36%)
[M1]
Compare all three decimals: 0.385 > 0.375 > 0.360 and state branch
[A1]
Correct Final Answer: Southern Branch (0.385 / 38.5% is the greatest)
Quick Check Method: 0.385 (38.5%) > 0.375 (37.5%) > 0.36 (36%).
Common Mistake to Avoid: Comparing numerators directly without converting to a common format.
