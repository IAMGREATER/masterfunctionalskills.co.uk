Planning Written Responses Pearson Edexcel: Pearson Edexcel Functional Skills English (WRITING)
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Core Skills (The Basics) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Building a 4-Paragraph Bullet Plan Before Drafting) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
An exam task asks you to write an email to staff explaining:
• Why the staff canteen is closing for 5 days
• Where temporary hot food vans will be parked
• How staff can claim a £5 daily meal voucher.
Outline a 4-step paragraph plan (including opening and sign-off) that guarantees full coverage.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Map Email Subject Line + Para 1 (Reason & dates for 5-day canteen closure) + Para 2 (Location & hours of temporary food vans) [A1]
2. Map Para 3 (Step-by-step instructions to claim £5 voucher) + Professional Sign-off ('Kind regards,') [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Subject Line → Para 1: 5-day refurbishment closure dates → Para 2: Courtyard location of hot food vans → Para 3: How to claim £5 voucher on intranet → Sign-off.
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Combining all three prompt bullets into a single unparagraphed block.
🔄 Quick Check: One paragraph per prompt bullet = guaranteed structure marks.
Question 2 (Core Skill Application — 3 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Read this exam prompt: 'Write a formal letter to your local council complaining about dangerous potholes on Highfield Road, explaining the risks to cyclists and suggesting repairs.'
Create a structured 4-paragraph plan showing the exact focus of each paragraph and the correct letter sign-off.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Identify PAF: Formal complaint letter to Local Council Highways Department (Dear Sir/Madam → Yours faithfully). [M1]
2. Map Paragraph 1 (Purpose & Location) and Paragraph 2 (Specific risks to cyclists/vehicles). [M1]
3. Map Paragraph 3 (Requested resurfacing action & timeline) and Paragraph 4 (Formal closing call to action). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Paragraph 1: Purpose of letter and exact location of potholes on Highfield Road. | Paragraph 2: Safety hazards to cyclists (swerving into traffic, punctured tyres) and recent near-misses. | Paragraph 3: Request urgent inspection and full resurfacing within 14 days. | Paragraph 4: Request written confirmation of repair schedule + 'Yours faithfully,' sign-off.
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Candidates who skip planning inevitably repeat themselves in Paragraphs 2 and 3, lose control of sentence structure, and run out of time before writing a proper conclusion.
🔄 Quick Check: Keep each paragraph focused on a single distinct stage: Situation → Impact/Risk → Solution → Formal Close.
Section B: Real-Life & Workplace Practice Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 Task Plan & Opening: Community Centre Hall Hire Email) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
You are organising a charity first-aid workshop and want to hire a room at Oakwood Community Centre on Saturday 14 November.
Write an email to the manager, Mr Samuels, to:
• Explain what the event is and how many people will attend
• Ask about room availability and projector equipment
• Enquire about weekend hire costs and parking.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Decode PAF & Email Headers: Format: Formal/Semi-formal Email. | To: m.samuels@oakwoodcentre.org.uk | Subject: Room Hire Enquiry – Charity First-Aid Workshop (14 November) | Salutation: Dear Mr Samuels, [A1]
2. Map 3 Short Body Paragraphs to the 3 Prompt Bullets: Para 1 (Event & Numbers): St John Ambulance community first-aid session, 25 local residents attending, 10:00 to 14:00. | Para 2 (Room & Equipment): Need ground-floor hall with a digital projector, screen, and 30 chairs. | Para 3 (Costs & Parking): Request charity discount rate and confirm if free parking is available for 15 cars. [A1]
3. Draft Complete Model Response: Write out the email cleanly using the 3-paragraph plan and sign off with Kind regards or Yours sincerely. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: To: m.samuels@oakwoodcentre.org.uk
Subject: Room Hire Enquiry – Charity First-Aid Workshop (14 November)

Dear Mr Samuels,

I am writing to enquire about hiring a training room at Oakwood Community Centre on Saturday 14 November from 10:00 to 14:00. We are holding a free community first-aid workshop for approximately 25 local residents.

Could you please confirm whether the Main Ground-Floor Hall is available on this date? For our practical demonstrations, we require a room equipped with a digital projector, a display screen, and seating for 30 people.

Finally, please could you send me your weekend hire rates, including any discounts for registered charities, and confirm whether delegates can use the centre car park free of charge?

Yours sincerely,
Alex Taylor
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Always spend 4 to 5 minutes sketching a 4-box paragraph outline in your planning space before typing your response.
🔄 Quick Check: Notice how each bullet point from the question becomes one clear, self-contained paragraph. The examiner can tick off all three content requirements in seconds.
Question 4 (Exam-Style Task — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A candidate wrote this disorganised draft paragraph containing three mixed-up topics:
'Our warehouse needs electric charging points in the car park. Also the canteen vending machine is broken and staff want more salad options. Seven delivery drivers have bought electric cars this year and have nowhere to charge them during shifts.'
Edit and restructure this draft so it maintains a single coherent paragraph topic suitable for an estates proposal.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Delete the irrelevant sentence about the canteen vending machine and salad options (off-topic drift). [M1]
2. Combine the remaining two points logically: state the proposal first, then follow with the supporting staff evidence. [M1]
3. Add a cohesive linking word ('Furthermore' or 'Consequently') and a concluding benefit. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'Our warehouse urgently requires the installation of electric vehicle (EV) charging points in the main staff car park. Seven delivery drivers have purchased electric cars this year; however, they currently have nowhere to charge their vehicles during shift hours. Installing four fast-charging bays will support staff retention and reduce our depot's carbon footprint.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Some candidates write full sentences in the planning box and then run out of time to copy them into the final answer box. Examiners primarily mark the final response area.
🔄 Quick Check: One paragraph = one core topic. If a sentence belongs to a different topic (like the canteen), delete it or move it to its own paragraph.
Section C: Exam-Style Tasks Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Extended Plan & Model: Healthcare Electronic Shift Handover Proposal) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
You are asked to write a 250-word proposal to your Clinical Lead recommending an electronic handover app.
Create a structured paragraph plan and draft the executive summary and recommendations addressing:
• Flaws in current paper notes
• Benefits of digital handover
• Proposed staff training timeline.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Plan Heading and Strategic Objective: Title: Proposal: Implementation of Electronic Ward Handover System | PAF: Formal Internal Proposal to Clinical Lead — analytical, persuasive, evidence-led. [A1]
2. Map Body Paragraphs 1 & 2 (Problem & Digital Solution): Section 1 (Paper Flaws): Illegible handwriting and misplaced paper sheets delay shift handovers by 25 minutes daily. | Section 2 (Digital Benefits): Real-time bedside tablet updates, automated vital-sign alerts, and full GDPR encryption. [A1]
3. Map Body Paragraph 3 (Phased Training Rollout): Section 3 (Timeline): Two-week pilot on Ward 4 starting next month; 30-minute lunchtime workshops led by two ward 'super-users'. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: PROPOSAL: IMPLEMENTATION OF ELECTRONIC SHIFT HANDOVER APP
To: Clinical Lead | From: Senior Ward Coordinator | Date: 14 October 2026

1. Current Paper Handover Deficiencies
Currently, ward handovers rely on handwritten paper sheets that are frequently illegible or incomplete; consequently, incoming nursing teams lose an average of 25 minutes per shift clarifying medication changes.

2. Operational & Safety Benefits of Digital Handover
Transitioning to a secure tablet-based handover app will synchronise patient observations in real time, flag overdue medications automatically, and ensure full GDPR compliance.

3. Proposed Rollout & Training Timeline
• Weeks 1–2: Train two senior nurses as digital super-users during 30-minute lunchtime modules.
• Weeks 3–4: Pilot the electronic app on Ward 4 alongside paper backups before trust-wide deployment.
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Keep your plan strictly to short 3-to-5 word bullet notes (e.g. 'Para 2: Cost savings — £400/month').
🔄 Quick Check: A tight 4-minute plan prevents mid-essay rambling and guarantees you include high-scoring Level 2 features such as numbered subheadings, bulleted timelines, and a semicolon.
Question 6 (Level 2 Mastery Challenge — 4 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
You have 30 minutes to complete a Level 2 report (250–300 words) for your Care Home Manager on improving visitor check-in security.
1. Break down how you should allocate your 30 minutes across planning, writing, and proofreading.
2. Write the exact Title and three numbered subheadings you would put in your plan.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Time allocation: 5 minutes planning, 20 minutes drafting the report, 5 minutes proofreading SPaG. [M1]
2. Title: Formal report heading stating the exact topic. [M1]
3. Subheadings: 1.0 Current Security Vulnerabilities, 2.0 Proposed Digital Sign-In System, 3.0 Cost & Implementation Recommendations. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1. Time Split: 5 mins Planning | 20 mins Writing | 5 mins Proofreading.
2. Report Structure:
Title: Report on Upgrading Visitor Check-In Security at Elmwood Care Home
• 1.0 Introduction & Current Sign-In Risks
• 2.0 Benefits of Electronic Visitor Badges & Lanyards
• 3.0 Recommendations & Rollout Costs
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Failing to proofread spelling, punctuation, and grammar after completing the draft.
🔄 Quick Check: Writing down your report subheadings during the 5-minute planning phase gives you an instant scaffold to fill in during your 20-minute writing phase.
