Part 1: Quick-Check Answer Key
Quick answers for practice questions
Q1 Question 1: Selecting Cohesive Connectives
(a) The budget was cut. [ ], we had to cancel the trip. → Consequently (cause & effect)
(b) She is a skilled surgeon. [ ], she trains junior doctors. → Furthermore (adding info)
(c) The weather was awful. [ ], the outdoor market stayed open. → However (contrast)
(d) Staff numbers increased. [ ], patient wait times dropped. → Consequently
(e) The course is free. [ ], travel expenses are covered. → In addition / Furthermore
(f) He applied late. [ ], he was granted an interview. → Nevertheless / However
Q2 Question 2: Transition Words for Adding Information & Cause and Effect
(a) The company expanded its delivery fleet. [ ], it hired six additional qualified drivers. → Furthermore / In addition (adding supporting action)
(b) The heavy snow blocked the main delivery route. [ ], morning deliveries were delayed by two hours. → Consequently / As a result (showing direct cause and effect)
(c) The candidate possesses extensive clinical experience. [ ], she holds a Master's degree in nursing. → In addition / Furthermore (adding positive credentials)
(d) The clinic refrigerator failed overnight; [ ], all temperature-sensitive vaccines had to be destroyed. → therefore / consequently (showing inevitable logical outcome)
(e) Staff training was completed ahead of schedule. [ ], productivity rose by 14% in the first quarter. → As a result / Consequently (linking training directly to performance improvement)
(f) The kitchen passed the environmental inspection. [ ], it was awarded a prestigious hygiene certificate. → Furthermore / In addition (adding complementary success)
(g) The customer failed to pay invoices despite three reminders; [ ], legal recovery proceedings began. → therefore / consequently (formal legal consequence)
(h) The community center installed solar panels. [ ], it insulated the roof space to minimize heat loss. → In addition / Furthermore (adding secondary environmental measure)
Q3 Question 3: Connectives for Contrast, Concession, and Comparison
(a) [ ] the initial purchase price of electric vehicles is high, long-term maintenance costs are low. → Although (signals concession at start of sentence)
(b) The day shift exceeded their packaging targets; [ ], the night team struggled with machine downtime. → however / in contrast (contrasting two shifts)
(c) Option A provides rapid short-term results, [ ] Option B ensures sustainable long-term stability. → whereas (contrasting parallel options)
(d) The hotel restaurant received outstanding food reviews. [ ], several diners complained about slow service. → However / On the other hand (contrasting positive food with negative service)
(e) [ ] the patient showed signs of dehydration, her blood pressure remained stable. → Although (conceding one symptom while noting normal vital sign)
(f) Full-time staff receive 28 days holiday, [ ] part-time employees receive pro-rata entitlements. → whereas (contrasting employment contractual terms)
(g) The new software is extremely powerful; [ ], it requires three days of intensive staff training. → however / on the other hand (introducing drawback to positive feature)
(h) Supermarket sales rose by 5% in the north; [ ], southern regional branches experienced a 2% decline. → in contrast / conversely (contrasting regional trends)
Q4 Question 4: Paragraph Linking & Cohesion Across Multi-Paragraph Essays
(a) From: Staff shortages caused delays. To: Recruitment plan. Best bridge? → 'To resolve these acute staffing shortages, management has devised a targeted three-point recruitment strategy.'
(b) From: High energy bills. To: Solar installation. Best bridge? → 'Having examined the unsustainable impact of rising utility bills, we must now consider renewable solar energy solutions.'
(c) From: Patient complaints about waiting. To: New triage system. Best bridge? → 'In light of these patient waiting time concerns, introducing an automated digital triage system offers an immediate remedy.'
(d) From: Poor communication between shifts. To: Daily digital handovers. Best bridge? → 'While communication breakdowns remain a challenge, implementing digital daily handovers directly bridges this information gap.'
(e) From: Low apprentice retention rates. To: Mentorship scheme. Best bridge? → 'Addressing the issue of apprentice retention requires establishing a structured, one-on-one workplace mentorship program.'
(f) From: Traffic congestion in town centre. To: Park-and-ride bus scheme. Best bridge? → 'Rather than accepting perpetual town centre gridlock, expanding the park-and-ride bus scheme provides a workable alternative.'
(g) From: Food waste in hospital kitchens. To: Composting and food donations. Best bridge? → 'Turning from the scale of kitchen food waste, practical donation partnerships offer an ethical and ecological route forward.'
(h) From: Rising tool theft from work vans. To: High-security deadlocks. Best bridge? → 'Faced with increasing tool theft statistics, equipping fleet vehicles with high-security deadlocks provides vital deterrence.'
Part 2: Real-Life Problem Solutions
Step-by-step methods and mark breakdown
Question 1 Worked Solution Formal Proposal to Hospital Board
[2 Marks Available]
A healthcare apprentice drafts a proposal for new recycling bins on hospital wards.
Link these two disjointed sentences using an appropriate contrastive transition and punctuation:
'Installing recycling bins requires an upfront cost of £1,200. Waste collection disposal fees will be reduced by £3,500 every year.'
Select contrastive connective: 'However,' or 'Nevertheless,' or 'Although...'
[M1]
Punctuate correctly: 'Installing recycling bins requires an upfront cost of £1,200; however, waste collection fees will be reduced by £3,500 every year.' (or '...£1,200. However, waste...')
[A1]
Correct Final Answer: 'Installing recycling bins requires an upfront cost of £1,200. However, waste collection fees will be reduced by £3,500 every year.'
Quick Check Method: Shows clear counter-argument resolution.
Common Mistake to Avoid: Using an additive connective like 'Furthermore' which contradicts the financial contrast.
