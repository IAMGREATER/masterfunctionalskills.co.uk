Complete Syllabus

The 3 Pillars of Level 2 English

Full preparation for all 3 components assessed on Ofqual-regulated Functional Skills English exams.

1. Reading Comprehension

Text Analysis & Evidence

Learn to analyse arguments, extract implicit meaning, and master comparative questions.

  • Comparing ideas, perspectives, and tones across Text A and Text B
  • Distinguishing hard facts, opinions, and biased statements
  • Identifying organisational markers (bullet points, captions, subheadings)
  • Decoding specialist vocabulary, nuances, and technical terms

2. Workplace Writing & SPaG

Form, Content & Technical Accuracy

Produce clear, persuasive, professional documents tailored to specific audiences and formats.

  • Formal letters, reports, emails, articles, and speeches
  • Sentence variety: Compound, complex, and rhetorical structures
  • SPaG rules: Accurate punctuation (commas, apostrophes, colons)
  • Organisational markers and logical paragraph transitions

3. Speaking, Listening & Communicating

Presentation & Discussion

Prepare for your 10-minute presentation and collaborative group discussion with full confidence.

  • Structuring a formal 5-minute presentation with Q&A
  • Active listening techniques and constructive counter-arguments
  • Adapting register and vocabulary for professional situations
  • Full presentation script templates and slide deck guidelines

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Functional Skills English Level 2 accepted for university or nursing?

Yes! Functional Skills English Level 2 is fully regulated by Ofqual and accepted by the Nursing and Midwifery Council (NMC), NHS Trusts, the majority of UK universities, and teaching/apprenticeship programmes as equivalent to a GCSE English Grade 4 / Grade C.

How is the Speaking, Listening and Communicating (SLC) exam conducted?

Your SLC assessment is carried out remotely over Zoom with an assessor and small group of fellow candidates. You deliver a short 5-minute presentation on a topic of your choice, answer questions, and participate in a guided discussion. Our course provides complete presentation templates and question practice.

Can I take the Reading and Writing exams on separate days?

Yes, our flexible online booking allows you to take your Reading, Writing, and SLC assessments on the same day or split across different days to reduce test fatigue.
