Searching, Buying and Booking Services Online — Answer Key

4. Transacting • Standard Code: E3.4.2
Style 1: Solutions
Task 1 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What does the 's' in 'https://' stand for in a website address?

A. Secure (CORRECT)
B. Standard
C. Speed
D. Software
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

The 's' stands for Secure, meaning the connection between your computer and the server is encrypted.

Task 2 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What should you check in your online shopping basket BEFORE clicking 'Pay Now'?

A. Check that the item quantities, delivery address, and total price are correct (CORRECT)
B. Check that your printer is turned on
C. Delete your browser history
D. Change your desktop background image
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Reviewing basket contents prevents accidentally ordering wrong quantities or paying incorrect totals.

Task 3 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What tool on a shopping website allows you to view only items under £20?

A. Price Filter (or Sort by Price) (CORRECT)
B. Spell Check
C. High Contrast Mode
D. Zoom Slider
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Filters allow shoppers to narrow search results by price, brand, rating, or delivery time.
