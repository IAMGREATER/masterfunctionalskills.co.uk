Video Calling and Digital Messaging

3. Communicating • Standard Code: E3.3.3
Style 1: Workout (6 Drills)
Core Rules & Methods to Remember:
  • Joining a Video Meeting: Online video calls are essential for team discussions, remote training, and client check-ins:Open your meeting invite (in your email or calendar).Clic
  • Tutor Abdul's Golden Etiquette Rule: The golden rule of video meetings: Stay on mute until you are invited to speak! Use the 'Raise Hand' button if you have a question so you don't interrupt the presenter.
  • Using the In-Meeting Chat: Click the Chat bubble icon to open the sidebar. You can type questions, share links, or post comments without interrupting the speaker. Keep all chat
