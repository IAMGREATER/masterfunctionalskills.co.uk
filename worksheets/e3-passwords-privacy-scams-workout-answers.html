Strong Passwords, Personal Privacy and Spotting Scams — Answer Key

5. Being Safe and Responsible Online • Standard Code: E3.5.1
Style 1: Solutions
Task 1 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

Which of the following passwords is the STRONGEST?

A. BlueSky!2026# (CORRECT)
B. 12345678
C. password
D. subi
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

'BlueSky!2026#' is long and contains uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols.

Task 2 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What is 'Phishing'?

A. A scam email or text that tricks you into revealing private passwords or bank details (CORRECT)
B. A computer game about catching fish
C. A program used to print photographs
D. A way of cleaning your computer keyboard
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Phishing is fraudulent communication designed to deceive victims into giving away sensitive information.

Task 3 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What should you do if your bank sends an email asking you to click a link and type your 4-digit card PIN?

A. Do not click the link and delete the email; banks never ask for your PIN by email (CORRECT)
B. Click the link and type your PIN immediately
C. Forward the email to your friends
D. Print the email and post it to the bank
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Real banks will NEVER ask for your full password or PIN via email or text message.
