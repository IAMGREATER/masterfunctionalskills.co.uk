Frequently Asked Questions About Our Pocket Revision Cards & Publications

Everything you need to know about our Pocket Revision Cards, Core Revision Guides, and Fast Tracked Delivery.

Why are Pocket Revision Cards the most effective way to revise?

Our Pocket Revision Cards utilize active recall and spaced repetition. By breaking down tricky topics (like trapezium area, percentage change, or formal letters) onto individual, double-sided cards with exam trap warnings and worked steps, you can quickly test yourself anywhere—on your commute, at lunch, or before exam day.

What is the difference between the Core Revision Guide and the Companion Workbook?

The Core Revision Guide is your complete teaching manual: it breaks down every single format or topic, teaches you the structural markers and examiners' expectations, and provides complete, unshortened Exemplar Model Answers.

The Companion Workbook is your hands-on exercise book: it provides scaffolded planning frames, sentence builders, error-correction tasks, and exam workspaces so you can practice independently without having to guess what to write.

Are these Pocket Revision Cards and books aligned with Pearson Edexcel?

Yes, 100%. All materials are meticulously mapped to the reformed Ofqual subject criteria. While our writing formats and exemplars are calibrated to Pearson Edexcel's rigorous standard, they are fully applicable to City & Guilds, Open Awards, NCFE, and AQA.

How fast is delivery and can I order for my college or training provider?

Individual orders are dispatched via Royal Mail Tracked 24/48 with free delivery across the UK. For bulk orders for colleges, NHS trusts, training providers, or apprenticeship cohorts, contact us via WhatsApp or phone (+44 (0)7564 525425) for institutional invoicing and bulk educational discounts.
