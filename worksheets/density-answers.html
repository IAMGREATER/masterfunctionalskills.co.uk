Density & Compound Units Pearson Edexcel: L2.19
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Procedural Fluency Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Level 2 Finding Density) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A solid brass medical valve has a volume of 45 cm³ and a mass of 382.5 grams.
Calculate the density of the brass in g/cm³.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Apply Density = Mass ÷ Volume: 382.5 ÷ 45 [M1]
2. Evaluate: 8.5 g/cm³ [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 8.5 g/cm³
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Multiplying 382.5 × 45 instead of dividing Mass by Volume.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 8.5 g/cm³ × 45 cm³ = 382.5 g.
Question 2 (Level 2 Finding Volume from Mass & Density) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A laboratory flask contains 1,030 grams of sterile glycerine solution.
The glycerine solution has a density of 1.25 g/cm³.
Calculate the volume of the glycerine solution in cm³.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Apply Volume = Mass ÷ Density: 1,030 ÷ 1.25 [M1]
2. Evaluate: 824 cm³ [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 824 cm³
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Multiplying 1,030 × 1.25 (= 1,287.5) instead of dividing by density.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 824 cm³ × 1.25 g/cm³ = 1,030 g.
Section B: Workplace & Functional Scenarios Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 2 Cuboid Volume & Mass in Kilograms) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A radiation-shielding lead brick is shaped as a cuboid measuring 20 cm long, 10 cm wide, and 5 cm high.
Lead has a density of 11.3 g/cm³.

Calculate the mass of the lead brick in kilograms (kg).
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate cuboid volume: 20 × 10 × 5 = 1,000 cm³ [M1]
2. Calculate mass in grams: 1,000 cm³ × 11.3 g/cm³ = 11,300 grams [P1]
3. Convert grams to kilograms: 11,300 ÷ 1,000 = 11.3 kg [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 11.3 kg
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Leaving the final answer as 11,300 when the question specifies kilograms.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 11.3 kg = 11,300 g; 11,300 ÷ 1,000 cm³ = 11.3 g/cm³.
Question 4 (Level 2 Floor Loading Pressure Compound Measure) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A heavy autoclave steriliser exerts a downward force of 14,400 Newtons (N) on a hospital plant-room floor.
The rectangular base of the autoclave measures 1.6 m long by 1.2 m wide.
Use the formula: Pressure (N/m²) = Force (N) ÷ Area (m²).

Calculate the pressure exerted on the floor in N/m².
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate contact base area: 1.6 m × 1.2 m = 1.92 m² [M1]
2. Substitute into Pressure = Force ÷ Area: 14,400 ÷ 1.92 [P1]
3. Evaluate: 7,500 N/m² [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 7,500 N/m²
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Dividing 14,400 by perimeter (5.6 m) instead of area (1.92 m²).
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 7,500 N/m² × 1.92 m² = 14,400 N.
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Problem Solving & Justification Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Concrete Driveway Ready-Mix Tonnage & Cost) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A civil engineer is pouring a concrete ambulance bay slab measuring 12 m long, 5 m wide, and 0.15 m deep.
• Wet concrete has a density of 2,400 kg/m³.
• Ready-mix concrete is sold at £85.00 per tonne (1 tonne = 1,000 kg).

Calculate the total cost of the concrete required for the ambulance bay slab.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate volume of concrete slab: 12 × 5 × 0.15 = 9 m³ [P1]
2. Calculate mass in kg: 9 m³ × 2,400 kg/m³ = 21,600 kg [M1]
3. Convert mass to tonnes: 21,600 ÷ 1,000 = 21.6 tonnes [P1]
4. Calculate total cost: 21.6 × £85.00 = £1,836.00 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £1,836.00 (21.6 tonnes)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Forgetting to convert 15 cm (0.15 m) or forgetting to divide kg by 1,000 to get tonnes.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: £1,836 ÷ £85 = 21.6 tonnes = 21,600 kg; 21,600 ÷ 9 m³ = 2,400 kg/m³.
Question 6 (Level 2 Cylindrical Liquid Nitrogen Tank Trailer Limit) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A cylindrical storage tank has an internal radius of 0.5 metres and a height of 2.0 metres.
• The tank is filled completely with liquid medical oxygen, which has a density of 1,140 kg/m³.
• The empty steel tank itself weighs 215 kg.
• Use π = 3.14.

A transport trailer has a maximum payload limit of 2,000 kg. Can the trailer legally carry the full tank? Show full working.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate cylinder volume (V = π × r² × h): 3.14 × 0.5² × 2.0 = 3.14 × 0.25 × 2.0 = 1.57 m³ [P1]
2. Calculate mass of liquid oxygen: 1.57 m³ × 1,140 kg/m³ = 1,789.8 kg [M1]
3. Add empty steel tank mass: 1,789.8 + 215 = 2,004.8 kg [P1]
4. Compare and conclude: 2,004.8 kg > 2,000 kg (exceeds limit by 4.8 kg). No, the trailer cannot legally carry the full tank. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: No (Total mass is 2,004.8 kg, exceeding the 2,000 kg limit by 4.8 kg)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Forgetting to add the 215 kg empty tank weight (which would give 1,789.8 kg and a false 'Yes').
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: (2,004.8 - 215) ÷ 1,140 = 1.57 m³.
