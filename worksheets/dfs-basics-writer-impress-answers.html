Word / LibreOffice Writer & PowerPoint / Impress Exercises

Practical Tasks Accompanying MFS Word/Writer (.docx) & PowerPoint/Impress (.pptx) Practice Packs
Task 1 (Community Notice Flyer .docx)
Open '01 Community Notice Flyer.docx' from the starter pack. Switch orientation to Landscape, add a box Page Border, make the headline 24pt Bold Centred, format the four activities as a clean bullet list, and export as PDF on ONE single page.
Worked Answer: Word: Layout > Orientation > Landscape; Design > Page Borders. LibreOffice Writer: Format > Page Style > Page (Landscape) + Borders tab. Keep margins/spacing tight so nothing spills onto Page 2, run F7 Spellcheck, and Export/Save as PDF.
Task 2 (Appointment Letter .docx)
Open '02 Appointment Confirmation Letter.docx'. Use Spellcheck (F7) to fix 'Pleese' and 'rearrnge', bold the appointment date and room, insert one relevant photo resized from a corner with Lock/Keep Aspect Ratio ticked.
Worked Answer: Fix 'Please' and 'rearrange'; highlight Thursday 26 November 2026 + Room 3 -> Bold; Right-click image > Size/Properties -> ensure Keep/Lock Aspect Ratio is ticked before resizing from a corner handle.
Task 3 (Weekly Shift Rota Table .docx)
Open '03 Weekly Shift Rota Table.docx'. Insert a 4-column, 6-row table, move the shift entries into the cells cleanly, shade the header row soft blue/slate, and bold the column titles.
Worked Answer: Word: Insert > Table; Writer: Table > Insert Table (Ctrl+F12). Shade row 1 light blue/slate and bold 'Day | Morning | Afternoon | Duty Lead'.
Task 4 (Welcome Induction Slides .pptx)
Open '01 Welcome Induction Slides.pptx'. Enlarge Slide 1 title to 36pt+ Bold, break Slide 2 bullets down to clear 22pt+ lines, add a 4th slide called 'Useful Contacts', drag it to the end, and launch full-screen Slideshow (F5).
Worked Answer: PowerPoint/Impress: Format Slide 1 title 36pt+ Bold; keep 3–5 concise bullets per slide; Ctrl+M / Slide > New Slide for 'Useful Contacts'; drag thumbnail in left strip; press F5 (Esc to exit).
Task 5 (Slide Contrast & Export Check)
Open '02 Everyday Digital Safety Slides.pptx'. Reorder 'Spotting Suspicious Links' ahead of 'Passphrases That Work', check text-to-background contrast, and export a handout PDF copy.
Worked Answer: Drag Slide 3 above Slide 2 in the left slide pane; ensure dark ink on light slide (or white ink on navy background); File > Export as PDF / Save As (.pdf).
