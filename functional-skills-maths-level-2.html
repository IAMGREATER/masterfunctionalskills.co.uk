Functional Skills Maths Level 2 — Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the exam, syllabus, preparation, and certificate.

Is Functional Skills Maths Level 2 easier than GCSE?

Many adult learners find Functional Skills Maths Level 2 more manageable than GCSE because questions focus directly on practical workplace and everyday life contexts. There is less abstract algebra and trigonometry, allowing focused revision in weeks rather than years.

Are all resources and past papers accessible online?

Yes. All 39 topic revision guides, downloadable PDF worksheets, model answers, and video walkthroughs are 100% accessible online from your desktop, tablet, or smartphone anytime.

Can I sit the Level 2 exam online from home?

Yes. We provide official Ofqual-regulated Pearson Edexcel and Open Awards online exams invigilated via secure remote proctoring from Monday through Saturday.

Is Level 2 accepted for university, nursing, and apprenticeships?

Yes. Functional Skills Level 2 is officially recognized by UCAS, the NHS, police forces, apprenticeship standards, and UK universities as a direct equivalent to GCSE grade 4 / C.
