Specification & Learning Outcomes

Candidates must demonstrate mastery of the following regulated Functional Skills Mathematics outcomes:

  • L1.16 / L2.18 – Calculate with time in 12-hour and 24-hour clocks, including time intervals across hours and days.
  • L1.17 – Read, interpret and use information from complex timetables and schedules.

Golden Rules for Time, 24-Hour Clocks & Timetables

Golden Rule 1: Time is Base-60, NOT Base-100

There are 60 minutes in an hour, not 100. 1 hour and 45 minutes is 1.75 hours (45/60 = 0.75), NOT 1.45 hours!

Golden Rule / Method: 15 mins = 0.25 hrs | 30 mins = 0.5 hrs | 45 mins = 0.75 hrs
Golden Rule 2: The Timeline Bridge for Elapsed Time

When calculating time elapsed (e.g. from 09:45 to 13:20), jump forward to the next whole hour first (09:45 to 10:00 = 15m), count whole hours (10:00 to 13:00 = 3h), then add remaining minutes (13:00 to 13:20 = 20m). Total = 3h 35m.

Golden Rule / Method: Jump to Next Hour → Count Whole Hours → Add Remaining Minutes

Core Skill 1: Skill 1: Converting Between 12-Hour (AM/PM) and 24-Hour Clock Formats

The +12 rule for afternoon/evening hours and handling 12:00 midnight/noon.

Core Skill 2: Skill 2: Converting Decimal Hours to Hours & Minutes (and Vice Versa)

Avoiding the #1 time calculation error in Functional Skills.

  • Decimal Hours to Minutes (× 60): Multiply the decimal part by 60: 2.4 hours = 2 hours + (0.4 × 60) = 2 hours 24 minutes.
  • Minutes to Decimal Hours (÷ 60): Divide the minutes by 60: 1 hour 36 minutes = 1 + (36 ÷ 60) = 1.6 hours.
  • Common Benchmarks: 15 mins = 0.25 h • 20 mins = 1/3 h • 30 mins = 0.5 h • 45 mins = 0.75 h.
Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 3: Skill 3: Working Backwards from Arrival Deadlines (Latest Departure Timetables)

How to select the right bus or train and work backwards to home departure time.

  1. Find the Latest Arrival at Station/Stop: Subtract the walking time from the final appointment deadline (e.g. Appointment 09:30 minus 12 mins walk = must arrive at station by 09:18).
  2. Select the Correct Train/Bus Column: Scan the destination row on the timetable and pick the latest service that arrives on or before 09:18 (e.g. arrives 09:12; an arrival at 09:22 is too late!).
  3. Read Up the Same Column: Trace up that exact column to find the departure time from the starting station.
Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Pearson Edexcel Step-by-Step Worked Models

Benchmark Exam Model 1 3 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 1/2)

Vocational Context: Community Health Nursing Travel Schedule

A community midwife departs the clinic at 08:35. She drives for 40 minutes to Patient A, spends 55 minutes conducting a post-natal examination, and drives for 25 minutes to Patient B.
At what time in 24-hour format does she arrive at Patient B's home?
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Sum total elapsed travel and appointment time
40 mins drive + 55 mins appointment + 25 mins drive = 120 minutes.
Criteria: M1: Total minutes summation
Step 2 Convert 120 minutes into hours
120 ÷ 60 = exactly 2 hours.
Criteria: M1: Conversion to hours
Step 3 Add 2 hours to departure time (08:35)
08:35 + 2 hours = 10:35.
Criteria: A1: Accurate 24-hour arrival time
Final Accredited Answer: Arrival Time = 10:35
Method Checklist & Marking Scheme Guidance
Notice how 40 + 55 + 25 = 120 minutes = exactly 2 hours. Adding whole hours to 08:35 is immediate.
Benchmark Exam Model 2 4 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 2 Problem Solving)

Vocational Context: Sterile Services Department Operations

A sterile services technician starts loading instrument trays at 13:45.
• Loading takes 25 minutes.
• The autoclave sterilisation cycle runs for 1.4 hours.
• Cooling and unloading takes 35 minutes.
• The technician must also take a mandatory 30-minute break after unloading before clocking off.

The technician's shift ends at 5:15 pm. Will she finish everything (including her break) before her shift ends?
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Convert 1.4 hours into hours and minutes
0.4 × 60 = 24 minutes → 1.4 hours = 1 hour 24 minutes (84 minutes).
Criteria: M1: Accurate decimal hour conversion (84 mins)
Step 2 Sum all task durations in minutes
25 mins (load) + 84 mins (cycle) + 35 mins (unload) + 30 mins (break) = 174 minutes.
Criteria: M1: Total minutes summation
Step 3 Convert 174 minutes to hours/minutes and add to 13:45 start time
174 mins = 2 hours 54 minutes.
13:45 + 2 hrs = 15:45; 15:45 + 54 mins = 16:39 (4:39 pm).
Criteria: M1: Accurate finish time calculation
Step 4 Compare against 5:15 pm (17:15) shift end
16:39 is 36 minutes before 17:15 (5:15 pm). Yes, she will finish with 36 minutes to spare.
Criteria: A1: Correct decision with 16:39 / 4:39 pm finish time
Final Accredited Answer: Yes (She finishes at 16:39 / 4:39 pm, which is 36 minutes before her 17:15 / 5:15 pm shift ends).
Method Checklist & Marking Scheme Guidance
Watch out for 1.4 hours! 1.4 hours is 1 hour 24 minutes (0.4 × 60), NOT 1 hour 40 minutes!

Critical Examiner Traps & Common Pitfalls

Examiner Pitfall: Entering 1.35 on a Calculator to Represent 1 Hour 35 Minutes
Calculators treat 1.35 as 1 and 35/100 (1 hr 21 mins), whereas 35 minutes is 35/60 = 0.583 hours.
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Always convert minutes to fractions of 60 before using a calculator.

Interactive Exam Practice Questions

Exam Practice Question 1 2 Marks
A staff training seminar starts at 14:45 and finishes at 17:20. How long did the seminar last in hours and minutes?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. 14:45 to 15:00 = 15 minutes.
2. 15:00 to 17:00 = 2 hours.
3. 17:00 to 17:20 = 20 minutes.
4. Total = 2 hours and 35 minutes.
Final Correct Answer: 2 hours and 35 minutes
Examiner Tip: Use the timeline bridge method to avoid subtraction errors across 60-minute thresholds.
Exam Practice Question 2 2 Marks
A night nurse clocks on at 21:30 on Tuesday and clocks off at 06:15 on Wednesday morning. She has an unpaid break of 45 minutes. For how many paid hours does she work?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. 21:30 to 24:00 (midnight) = 2 hours 30 minutes.
2. 00:00 to 06:15 = 6 hours 15 minutes → Total shift = 8 hours 45 minutes.
3. Subtract 45 mins unpaid break = exactly 8 paid hours.
Final Correct Answer: 8 hours
Examiner Tip: Bridge through midnight (24:00 / 00:00) in two steps when calculating overnight shifts.
Exam Practice Question 3 3 Marks
A train timetable shows departures from Oxford at 07:42, 08:12, and 08:42. The journey to London Paddington takes 56 minutes, followed by a 18-minute underground transfer to a hospital appointment at 09:45. Which is the latest train from Oxford the patient can catch?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Total travel time from Oxford departure to hospital = 56 + 18 = 74 minutes (1 hour 14 minutes).
2. Work backwards from 09:45: 09:45 - 1 hr 14 mins = 08:31 latest departure.
3. Compare trains: 08:42 is too late (arrives 09:56). The 08:12 train arrives at hospital at 09:26.
4. Latest train = 08:12.
Final Correct Answer: The 08:12 train (arrives at hospital at 09:26)
Examiner Tip: Always test your chosen train forward to confirm arrival is before 09:45 (08:12 + 1h 14m = 09:26).
