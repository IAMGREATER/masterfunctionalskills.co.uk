Direct & Inverse Proportion Pearson Edexcel: L1.11 • L2.11
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Procedural Fluency Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Level 1 Unitary Direct Proportion) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A clinic purchases 8 boxes of sterile dressings for £116.00.
Work out the cost of purchasing 15 boxes of the same dressings.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Cost of 1 box: £116.00 ÷ 8 = £14.50 [M1]
2. Cost of 15 boxes: 15 × £14.50 = £217.50 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £217.50
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Dividing 8 by 116 instead of dividing price by quantity.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: £217.50 ÷ 15 = £14.50; £14.50 × 8 = £116.00.
Question 2 (Level 2 Inverse Proportion Workforce Time) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
It takes 4 maintenance painters 9 hours to repaint a hospital corridor.
How long would it take 6 painters working at the same rate to repaint the corridor?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate total person-hours required: 4 painters × 9 hours = 36 person-hours [M1]
2. Divide by 6 painters: 36 ÷ 6 = 6 hours [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 6 hours
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Using direct proportion (9 ÷ 4 × 6 = 13.5 hours) — more painters must take LESS time!
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 6 painters × 6 hours = 36 person-hours = 4 × 9.
Section B: Workplace & Functional Scenarios Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 Catering Recipe Scaling) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A care home kitchen recipe for 12 portions of cottage pie requires 900 g of minced beef and 1.5 kg of potatoes.

Calculate the weight of minced beef (in grams) and potatoes (in kg) required to make 30 portions.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Scaling multiplier: 30 ÷ 12 = 2.5 (or find 1 portion: 75 g beef and 0.125 kg potatoes) [M1]
2. Minced beef for 30 portions: 900 g × 2.5 = 2,250 g [A1]
3. Potatoes for 30 portions: 1.5 kg × 2.5 = 3.75 kg [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Beef: 2,250 g | Potatoes: 3.75 kg
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Doubling for 24 portions and adding a fixed amount instead of scaling by 2.5.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 2,250 ÷ 30 = 75 g/portion; 75 × 12 = 900 g.
Question 4 (Level 2 Fleet Fuel Consumption Rate) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
An ambulance patient transport van uses 22.5 litres of diesel to travel 270 miles.
Diesel costs £1.48 per litre.

Calculate the fuel cost for the van to travel 420 miles at the same consumption rate.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate miles per litre: 270 ÷ 22.5 = 12 miles per litre [M1]
2. Calculate litres needed for 420 miles: 420 ÷ 12 = 35 litres [P1]
3. Multiply by diesel price: 35 × £1.48 = £51.80 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £51.80
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Rounding intermediate litre per mile figures (0.0833) instead of using exact 12 miles per litre.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: £51.80 ÷ £1.48 = 35 L; 35 × 12 = 420 miles.
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Problem Solving & Justification Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Limiting Ingredient Bakery Production) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A commercial bakery recipe for 20 protein flapjacks uses:
• 400 g rolled oats
• 250 g honey
• 160 g butter
The kitchen store has 3.6 kg of oats, 2.0 kg of honey, and 1.5 kg of butter.

What is the maximum number of flapjacks the bakery can make from this stock?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Convert stock to grams: 3,600 g oats, 2,000 g honey, 1,500 g butter [P1]
2. Find batches of 20 possible per ingredient: Oats = 3,600 ÷ 400 = 9 batches; Honey = 2,000 ÷ 250 = 8 batches; Butter = 1,500 ÷ 160 = 9.375 batches [M1]
3. Identify limiting ingredient: Honey limits production to 8 batches of 20 [P1]
4. Calculate max flapjacks: 8 × 20 = 160 flapjacks [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 160 flapjacks (limited by 2,000 g of honey)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Picking the highest batch count (9.375 × 20 = 187) instead of the lowest limiting ingredient (honey = 160).
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 160 flapjacks (8 batches) uses 3,200 g oats, 2,000 g honey (all used), and 1,280 g butter.
Question 6 (Level 2 Warehouse Unloading Deadline Decision) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
It takes 5 warehouse operatives 4 hours and 48 minutes to unpack and label a clinical equipment delivery.
Today, the delivery arrives at 09:30 and must be completely unpacked by 12:30.
The supervisor assigns 8 warehouse operatives (all working at the normal rate).

Will the 8 operatives finish unpacking the delivery by 12:30? Show your working clearly.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Convert 4 hours 48 minutes to decimal hours (4 + 48/60 = 4.8 hours) or minutes (288 mins) [P1]
2. Calculate total person-hours: 5 operatives × 4.8 hours = 24 person-hours (or 1,440 person-minutes) [M1]
3. Divide by 8 operatives: 24 ÷ 8 = 3.0 hours (180 minutes) [P1]
4. Conclude: 09:30 + 3 hours = 12:30. Yes, the 8 operatives will finish at exactly 12:30. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Yes (Takes 3 hours, finishing at exactly 12:30)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Writing 4 hours 48 minutes as 4.48 hours instead of 4.8 hours.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 8 operatives × 3 hours = 24 person-hours = 5 × 4.8 hours.
