Specification & Learning Outcomes

Candidates must demonstrate mastery of the following regulated Functional Skills Mathematics outcomes:

  • L1.11 / L2.11 – Understand and calculate ratio, direct proportion, and scaling.
  • L2.11 – Divide an amount into a given ratio of two or three parts.

Three Non-Negotiable Golden Rules for Ratio & Sharing

Golden Rule 1: The 3-Step Sharing Protocol (Add → Divide → Multiply)

Whenever you share a grand total into a given ratio, memorize this 3-step sequence:

The 3-Step Action Plan
  1. ADD: Add ratio parts together to find Total Parts.
  2. DIVIDE: Divide Grand Total by Total Parts = Value of 1 Part.
  3. MULTIPLY: Multiply 1-Part value by each individual ratio term.
Worked Example: Share £450 in Ratio 3 : 2
1. Parts = 3 + 2 = 5 parts
2. 1 Part = £450 ÷ 5 = £90
3. Share A: 3 × £90 = £270
    Share B: 2 × £90 = £180
Golden Rule 2: Dilutions & Mixtures: Concentrate vs. Total Solution

In a 1 : 4 dilution ratio of concentrate to water, there is 1 part concentrate and 4 parts water. The total volume is 1 + 4 = 5 parts:

1 Part Concentrate
4 Parts Water
Concentrate Fraction = 1⁄5 (20% of mixture) ✗ NEVER 1⁄4 (25%)! Water Fraction = 4⁄5 (80% of mixture)
Golden Rule 3: Match Units BEFORE Simplifying Any Ratio

Ratios compare quantities of identical dimension. Never simplify numbers with different units! Always convert to the smaller unit first:

✗ FATAL EXAM ERROR
50p : £2.50 → 50 : 2.5 (WRONG!)

Mixing pounds and pence leads to incorrect ratios.

✓ ACCREDITED METHOD
50p : 250p → 1 : 5 ✓

Convert £2.50 to 250p, then divide both by 50.

Core Skill 1: Simplifying Ratios & Converting to Unitary Form (1 : n) Level 1 & 2 Standard

In Functional Skills exams, ratios must often be simplified to their lowest whole terms or expressed in unitary form (1 : n or n : 1):

Simplifying to Lowest Whole Terms

Divide both sides by the Highest Common Factor (HCF):

Original Divide By HCF Simplest Form
24 : 36 ÷ 12 both sides 2 : 3
15 : 45 ÷ 15 both sides 1 : 3
18 : 24 : 30 ÷ 6 all sides 3 : 4 : 5
45 cm : 1.5 m 45 cm : 150 cm (÷ 15) 3 : 10
Unitary Form (1 : n and n : 1)

To make the leading term 1, divide both numbers by the first number:

Express 4 : 18 in the form 1 : n
(4 ÷ 4) : (18 ÷ 4) → 1 : 4.5 ✓
In unitary form, decimals are fully allowed and expected!
Express 35 : 7 in the form n : 1
(35 ÷ 7) : (7 ÷ 7) → 5 : 1 ✓
Divide both terms by 7 so the second number becomes 1.

Core Skill 3: Scaling from ONE Known Part or the Difference Between Parts High-Mark Exam Differentiator

In Level 2 problem-solving questions, the examiner rarely gives you the grand total. Instead, they tell you what ONE person receives or the DIFFERENCE between shares:

Given Scenario Golden Rule for 1 Part Worked Model (Ratio 3 : 7)
Case A: Known Single Share
e.g. "Person A gets £60" 		Divide value by its OWN parts only:
Do NOT add ratio terms together!
1 Part = £60 ÷ 3 = £20
Person B (7 parts) = 7 × £20 = £140
Grand Total = 10 × £20 = £200
Case B: Known Difference
e.g. "Person B gets £80 MORE than Person A" 		Divide difference by DIFFERENCE in parts:
Parts Difference = 7 − 3 = 4 parts
1 Part = £80 ÷ (7 − 3) = £80 ÷ 4 = £20
Person A = 3 × £20 = £60
Person B = 7 × £20 = £140
Diff check: £140 − £60 = £80 ✓
Lead Tutor Trap Alert: If the question says "Liam gets £60" in ratio 3 : 7, dividing £60 by 10 (= £6) is an instant fail! £60 is Liam's 3 parts, not the 10-part total. Always read whether you have a share, a difference, or a total.

Pearson Edexcel Step-by-Step Worked Models

Benchmark Exam Model 1 3 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 1/2 Non-Calculator)

Vocational Context: Clinical Healthcare Environmental Decontamination

A facility housekeeper must prepare 15 litres of surface sanitizing solution for an endoscopy suite.
Clinical guidelines mandate that disinfectant concentrate and clean water must be mixed in the ratio 1 : 4.
(a) How many litres of disinfectant concentrate are needed?
(b) How many litres of water are needed?
Step 1 Find Total Parts by Adding Ratio Terms [M1] Total Parts Calculation
ADD RATIO TERMS
1 + 4 = 5 parts
Add 1 part concentrate + 4 parts water = 5 total parts in the completed sanitizing solution.
Step 2 Calculate Volume of 1 Part [M1] Division by Total Parts
FIND 1 PART
15 ÷ 5 = 3 litres
Divide the total required solution (15 L) by 5 parts = 3 litres per part.
Step 3 Multiply 1-Part Volume by Each Ratio Term [A1] Both Quantities Accurate
FINAL QUANTITIES
Conc: 1 × 3 L = 3 L
Water: 4 × 3 L = 12 L
Concentrate (1 part) = 1 × 3 = 3 litres.
Water (4 parts) = 4 × 3 = 12 litres.
Sanity check: 3 L + 12 L = 15 litres total ✓.
FINAL ACCREDITED ANSWER: Disinfectant Concentrate = 3 litres | Water = 12 litres
Benchmark Exam Model 2 4 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 2 Multi-Stage Problem Solving)

Vocational Context: Healthcare Facility Workforce Compliance

Regulatory inspection guidelines for an acute healthcare unit require:
• A minimum ratio of senior nurses to healthcare assistants (HCAs) of 2 : 5.
• At least 1 total staff member for every 4 residents.
On Saturday, there are 84 residents on the unit. The roster currently has 6 senior nurses scheduled.
(a) Based on the 6 senior nurses and the 2 : 5 ratio, how many HCAs must be rostered?
(b) Will this combined roster meet the minimum 1 : 4 overall staff-to-resident requirement?
Step 1 Scale from Known Single Part: 6 Senior Nurses (2 Parts) [M1] Scaling from Known Term
FIND 1 PART
2 parts = 6 nurses
1 part = 6 ÷ 2 = 3
6 represents the senior nurse share (2 parts), NOT the total staff!
Calculate the value of 1 ratio part: 6 ÷ 2 = 3.
Step 2 Multiply to Find HCAs & Calculate Total Staff [M1] HCA Count & Staff Sum
HCAs & TOTAL STAFF
HCAs = 5 × 3 = 15
Total = 6 + 15 = 21 staff
HCAs (5 parts) = 5 × 3 = 15 HCAs.
Total team on duty = 6 senior nurses + 15 HCAs = 21 staff.
Step 3 Calculate Minimum Staff Required for 84 Residents [M1] Resident Ratio Division
RESIDENT RATIO
84 ÷ 4 = 21 staff
With 84 residents and a 1 : 4 staffing ratio requirement:
84 ÷ 4 = 21 staff members required.
Step 4 Compare Roster Total Against Requirement & Conclude [A1] Clear Justified Conclusion
COMPLIANCE VERDICT
21 = 21
✓ MEETS STANDARD
The roster provides exactly 21 staff members, which equals the minimum statutory requirement of 21 staff.
Conclusion: Yes, 15 HCAs are required and the combined team of 21 staff meets the compliance threshold.
FINAL ACCREDITED DECISION: (a) 15 HCAs | (b) Yes, total rostered staff is 21, which exactly meets the 84 ÷ 4 = 21 requirement.

Critical Examiner Traps & Common Pitfalls

Pitfall 1: Dividing by a Single Ratio Term Instead of Total Parts

When sharing 15 L of liquid in ratio 1 : 4, hurried candidates frequently compute 15 ÷ 4 = 3.75 L. This is 100% incorrect! You must divide by the sum of the parts (1 + 4 = 5).

✗ Candidate Mistake: 15 ÷ 4 = 3.75 L (0 MARKS) ✓ Accredited Rule: 15 ÷ 5 = 3 L per part (FULL MARKS)
How to Avoid: Always write down Total parts = [part 1] + [part 2] before doing any division.
Pitfall 2: Confusing a Single Share with the Grand Total

If Liam and Maya share tips in ratio 2 : 5, and Liam receives £40, candidates often mistakenly divide £40 by 7. £40 is Liam's 2 parts, NOT the total money!

✗ Candidate Mistake: £40 ÷ 7 = £5.71 (WRONG) ✓ Accredited Rule: 1 part = £40 ÷ 2 = £20 → Maya = 5 × £20 = £100
How to Avoid: Label each number in the scenario clearly: "Is this a total, a share, or a difference?"
Pitfall 3: Simplifying Ratios with Mixed Units Without Converting

Simplifying 50p : £2.50 as 50 : 2.5 or 1 : 0.05 is mathematically invalid.

✗ Candidate Mistake: 50p : £2.50 → 50 : 2.5 ✓ Accredited Rule: 50p : 250p → 1 : 5 (WHOLE NUMBERS)
How to Avoid: Always convert all terms to the smallest unit (pence, grams, millimetres) before simplifying.

Interactive Exam Practice Questions

Exam Practice Question 1 3 Marks (Level 1/2 Standard)
Share £540.00 between Ethan and Sophia in the ratio 4 : 5.
Calculate how much money each person receives. Show your full working.
Marking Scheme Step-Down Breakdown:
1. Total Parts [M1]: 4 + 5 = 9 parts
2. Value of 1 Part [M1]: £540 ÷ 9 = £60
3. Multiply Terms [A1]:
• Ethan (4 parts) = 4 × £60 = £240.00
• Sophia (5 parts) = 5 × £60 = £300.00
Check: £240 + £300 = £540.00 ✓
Final Accredited Answers: Ethan = £240.00 | Sophia = £300.00
Exam Practice Question 2 3 Marks (Level 2 Problem Solving)
In a clinic staffing department, the ratio of full-time employees to part-time employees is 3 : 8.
There are currently 24 full-time employees.
Work out the total number of employees working in the department.
Marking Scheme Step-Down Breakdown:
1. Scale from Full-Time Part [M1]: 3 parts = 24 staff → 1 part = 24 ÷ 3 = 8 staff
2. Calculate Part-Time Staff [M1]: 8 parts = 8 × 8 = 64 part-time staff
3. Sum Total Staff [A1]: 24 + 64 = 88 total employees
Alternative Method: Total parts = 3 + 8 = 11 parts → 11 × 8 = 88 employees.
Final Accredited Answer: Total Staff = 88 employees
Exam Practice Question 3 3 Marks (Difference in Parts)
A healthcare facility estate refurbishment company mixes blue paint and white paint in the ratio 2 : 5.
The decorator uses 12 litres more white paint than blue paint.
How many litres of white paint were used in total?
Marking Scheme Step-Down Breakdown:
1. Difference in Parts [M1]: 5 − 2 = 3 parts
2. Value of 1 Part [M1]: 12 litres ÷ 3 parts = 4 litres per part
3. Calculate White Paint [A1]: 5 parts × 4 litres = 20 litres of white paint
(Blue paint = 2 × 4 = 8 L; Check: 20 L − 8 L = 12 L more ✓)
Final Accredited Answer: 20 litres of white paint
Examiner Tip: Notice that the question specifically asked for white paint, not the total paint!
