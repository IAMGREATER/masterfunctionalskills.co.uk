Part 1: Quick-Check Answer Key
Quick answers for practice questions
Q1 Question 1: Completing Two-Way Table Cells
(a) Row total = 45. Column 1 = 28. What is Column 2? → 17 (45 - 28)
(b) Column total = 60. Row 1 = 34. What is Row 2? → 26 (60 - 34)
(c) Men: 18 tea, 22 coffee. Total Men? → 40 (18 + 22)
(d) Women: 15 tea, 25 coffee. Total coffee drinkers (Men + Women)? → 47 (22 + 25)
(e) Grand Total for table (c) and (d)? → 80 (40 men + 40 women)
Q2 Question 2: Reading Timetables and Elapsed Time
(a) Bus departs 08:35, arrives 09:18. Journey duration? → 43 minutes
(b) Train departs 14:45, journey takes 1 hour 25 minutes. Arrival? → 16:10
(c) Shift starts at 07:15 and finishes at 15:45 with 45 min unpaid lunch. Working hours? → 7 hours 45 mins
(d) Meeting from 10:20 to 11:55. Meeting length? → 1 hour 35 mins (95 mins)
Q3 Question 3: Multi-Column Workplace Spreadsheets & Invoices
(a) Row: Item A £45, Item B £65, Item C £120. Row Subtotal = ? → £230.00
(b) Row: 15 units at £8.00 per unit. Line Total = ? → £120.00
(c) Row: 24 units at £4.50 per unit. Line Total = ? → £108.00
(d) Row: 50 units at £1.20 per unit. Line Total = ? → £60.00
(e) Subtotal of rows: £120 + £108 + £60 = ? → £288.00
(f) Discount of 10% on £288.00 subtotal = ? → £28.80
(g) Net total after 10% discount (£288 - £28.80) = ? → £259.20
(h) Delivery surcharge £15.00 added to £259.20. Final Total Payable = ? → £274.20
(i) Column total check: Department A £1,450, Dept B £2,200, Dept C £850. Grand total? → £4,500.00
(j) Table sorting: What happens when a list of names is sorted alphabetically (A-Z)? → Sorted in ascending alphabetical order
Q4 Question 4: Timetables & Scheduled Handover Windows
(a) Shift schedule: Clinic handover 07:30-08:00 (30m), Ward rounds 08:00-11:30. Ward round duration? → 3 hours 30 minutes
(b) Break 11:30-12:15. Break duration? → 45 minutes
(c) Clinic appointments: 12:15-16:00. Clinic duration? → 3 hours 45 minutes
(d) Total working time in shift excluding 45m break (07:30 to 16:00 = 8h 30m total)? → 7 hours 45 minutes
(e) Bus schedule: Bus leaves town every 20 mins starting at 06:10. List next 3 buses → 06:30, 06:50, 07:10
(f) Arrive at bus stop at 07:02. How many minutes wait for 07:10 bus? → 8 minutes wait
(g) Flight timetable: Departs London 13:25, Arrives Rome 16:55 local time (+1h zone). Actual flight time? → 2 hours 30 minutes
(h) Cinema timetable: Film starts 19:40, runs for 115 minutes (1h 55m). Film ends at? → 21:35
Q5 Question 5: Timetable Interpretation & Train Schedules
(a) Train departs 08:14, arrives 09:02. How many minutes is the journey? → 48 minutes
(b) Bus runs every 20 minutes starting at 07:05. What is the time of the 4th bus? → 08:05 (07:05, 07:25, 07:45, 08:05)

(d) Appointment is at 10:15. Journey takes 35 minutes. Latest departure time from home? → 09:40
Part 2: Real-Life Problem Solutions
Step-by-step methods and mark breakdown
Question 1 Worked Solution Medical Diagnostics Phlebotomy Clinic
[3 Marks Available]
A diagnostic clinic tracks blood testing appointments in a two-way table across two days:
• Monday: 48 Fasting tests and 32 Routine tests
• Tuesday: 36 Fasting tests and 44 Routine tests
(a) Complete the grand total of all appointments across both days.
(b) What fraction of all appointments across both days were Fasting tests? Give your answer in simplest form.
Monday total: 48 + 32 = 80; Tuesday total: 36 + 44 = 80; Grand total: 80 + 80 = 160
[M1]
Total Fasting tests: 48 + 36 = 84
[M1]
Simplify fraction: 84 ÷ 160 = 42/80 = 21/40
[A1]
Correct Final Answer: (a) 160 total appointments | (b) 21/40 (52.5%)
Quick Check Method: 160 × 21/40 = 84 fasting tests.
Common Mistake to Avoid: Simplifying only to 42/80 without reducing to simplest form 21/40.
