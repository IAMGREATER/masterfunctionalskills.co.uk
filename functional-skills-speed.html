Golden Rules for Speed, Distance & Time

Golden Rule 1: Speed, Distance & Time Formula Triangle

Cover what you want to find with your thumb:
• Distance = Speed × Time
• Speed = Distance ÷ Time
• Time = Distance ÷ Speed

Golden Rule / Method: Distance = Speed × Time | Speed = Distance ÷ Time | Time = Distance ÷ Speed
Golden Rule 2: Divide Minutes by 60 to Get Decimal Hours

Never write 45 minutes as 0.45! To turn minutes into decimal hours, divide by 60 (15 mins = 0.25 hrs, 30 mins = 0.5 hrs, 45 mins = 0.75 hrs). To turn decimal hours back into minutes, multiply by 60 (0.4 hrs × 60 = 24 mins).

Golden Rule / Method: Decimal Hours = Minutes ÷ 60 | Minutes = Decimal Hours × 60

Core Skill 3: Skill 3: Two-Stage Journeys & Overall Average Speed

Why overall average speed is Total Distance ÷ Total Time (NEVER the average of two speeds).

To find the overall average speed of a multi-leg delivery journey:

  1. Total Distance: Add the distances of Leg 1 and Leg 2.
  2. Total Time: Add the driving times of Leg 1 and Leg 2 (in decimal hours).
  3. Overall Average Speed: Total Distance ÷ Total Time.

Sonya's Visual Quick-Check: The Fatal 1.45 Hours Trap!

"If you divide 84 miles by 1.45, you get 57.9 mph! That single mistake causes you to conclude 'NO', losing both the method mark and accuracy mark. Always convert minutes to hours by dividing by 60: 15 mins = 0.25 hrs, 30 mins = 0.5 hrs, 45 mins = 0.75 hrs!"

Benchmark Exam Model 2 4 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 2 Problem Solving)

Vocational Context: Urgent Medical Logistics

A blood bank courier leaves the regional hub at 10:15 am to deliver plasma 117 miles away.
• For the first 72 miles on the motorway, he drives at an average speed of 60 mph.
• For the remaining 45 miles on A-roads, he drives at an average speed of 36 mph.
• He also stops for a 15-minute roadworks delay.

The plasma must arrive before 1:00 pm (13:00). Does the courier arrive on time?
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Calculate Motorway driving time 72 miles ÷ 60 mph
72 ÷ 60 = 1.2 hours
1 hour + (0.2 × 60 mins) = 1 hr 12 mins = 72 minutes
[M1] Method Mark:
First leg time calculation (72 mins)
Step 2 Calculate A-road driving time 45 miles ÷ 36 mph
45 ÷ 36 = 1.25 hours
1 hour + (0.25 × 60 mins) = 1 hr 15 mins = 75 minutes
[M1] Method Mark:
Second leg time calculation (75 mins)
Step 3 Sum total journey time & add to 10:15 am Include 15 min roadworks delay
Timeline Step-Down:
Total Minutes 72 + 75 + 15 = 162 minutes
Hours & Mins 162 mins = 2 hours 42 minutes
Arrival Time 10:15 am + 2h 42m = 12:57 pm
[M1] Process Mark:
Total duration (162 mins) and arrival time (12:57 pm)
Step 4 Compare against 1:00 pm (13:00) deadline Final Justified Verdict
12:57 pm ≤ 1:00 pm → YES, ON TIME
The courier arrives 3 minutes before the 1:00 pm deadline.
[A1] Accuracy Mark:
Accurate arrival time (12:57 pm) and confirmed YES
Final Exam Answer: Arrival Time = 12:57 pm • Yes, courier arrives on time (3 minutes before 1:00 pm)

Subi's Assessor Marking Scheme Guidance

"Converting 1.2 hours to minutes: 0.2 × 60 = 12 minutes (NOT 20 minutes!). Working in total minutes (72 + 75 + 15 = 162 mins) is the safest method to prevent catastrophic errors when crossing 12 noon. Always finish by stating both the exact arrival time and YES or NO!"

Critical Examiner Traps & Common Pitfalls

Examiner Pitfall: Dividing by 1.45 Instead of 1.75
Treating 1 hour 45 minutes as 1.45 hours is the single most common error in Level 2 speed questions.
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Never use minutes as decimals directly: 45 mins = 45/60 = 0.75.

Interactive Exam Practice Questions

Exam Practice Question 1 2 Marks
A freight delivery lorry travels 165 miles at an average speed of 55 mph. How long does the journey take in hours?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Time = Distance ÷ Speed = 165 ÷ 55 = 3 hours.
2. Journey duration = 3 hours.
Final Correct Answer: 3 hours
Examiner Tip: Distance ÷ Speed = 165 ÷ 55 = 3.
Exam Practice Question 2 2 Marks
A district nurse drives at an average speed of 36 mph for 40 minutes. How many miles does she travel?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Convert 40 minutes into hours: 40/60 = 2/3 hour.
2. Distance = Speed × Time = 36 × (2/3) = (36 ÷ 3) × 2 = 24 miles.
Final Correct Answer: 24 miles
Examiner Tip: When minutes are 20 or 40, use fractions (1/3 or 2/3 of an hour) to avoid recurring decimals!
Exam Practice Question 3 3 Marks
A train travels 210 km between 09:20 and 11:05. Calculate the average speed of the train in km/h.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Elapsed time from 09:20 to 11:05 = 1 hour 45 minutes = 1.75 hours.
2. Speed = Distance ÷ Time = 210 ÷ 1.75 = 120 km/h.
Final Correct Answer: 120 km/h
Examiner Tip: 1 hour 45 minutes = 1.75 hours; 210 ÷ 1.75 = 120 km/h.
