Question 1 Worked Solution Healthcare Hospital Timesheet
[3 Marks Available]
A staff nurse works 37 1/2 hours per week. Over four weeks, she spends 2/5 of her total working hours in surgical triage.
How many hours does she spend in surgical triage over the four weeks?
Calculate total hours worked over 4 weeks: 37.5 × 4 = 150 hours
[M1]
Calculate 2/5 of 150 hours: (150 ÷ 5) × 2 = 30 × 2 = 60 hours
[M1]
State final triage hours: 60 hours
[A1]
Correct Final Answer: 60 hours
Quick Check Method: 60 ÷ 150 = 6/15 = 2/5.
Common Mistake to Avoid: Calculating 2/5 of one week (15 hrs) and forgetting to multiply by 4 weeks.
Question 2 Worked Solution Construction Timber Cutting
[2 Marks Available]
A joiner has an oak timber beam measuring 4 1/2 metres long. He cuts off a piece measuring 1 3/4 metres for a door lintel.
What length of timber remains?
Set up common denominator (quarters): 4 2/4 - 1 3/4 = 3 6/4 - 1 3/4 = 2 3/4 metres (or 4.5 - 1.75 = 2.75)
[M1]
Give exact fraction answer: 2 3/4 metres (or 2.75 m)
[A1]
Correct Final Answer: 2 3/4 metres
Quick Check Method: 2 3/4 + 1 3/4 = 3 + 6/4 = 4 2/4 = 4 1/2 metres.
Common Mistake to Avoid: Subtracting whole numbers and numerators directly (4 - 1 = 3, 3 - 2 = 1) without borrowing.
Question 3 Worked Solution Commercial Recipe Scaling
[2 Marks Available]
A catering recipe for artisan bread requires 3/8 kg of rye flour per batch. A baker plans to bake 16 batches today.
How many kilograms of rye flour does the baker need?
Multiply batch amount by 16: (3/8) × 16 = (3 × 16) ÷ 8 = 48 ÷ 8 = 6 kg
[M1]
State required rye flour: 6 kg
[A1]
Correct Final Answer: 6 kg
Quick Check Method: 6 ÷ 16 = 6/16 = 3/8 kg per batch.
Common Mistake to Avoid: Inverting the fraction or dividing 16 by 3/8.
Question 4 Worked Solution Retail Stock Inventory
[3 Marks Available]
A warehouse holds 480 electrical appliances.
• 1/3 are microwave ovens.
• 3/8 are air fryers.
• The remainder are kettle and toaster sets.
How many kettle and toaster sets are in stock?
Microwave count: 480 ÷ 3 = 160
[M1]
Air fryer count: (480 ÷ 8) × 3 = 60 × 3 = 180
[M1]
Remaining sets: 480 - (160 + 180) = 480 - 340 = 140
[A1]
Correct Final Answer: 140 appliances
Quick Check Method: 160 + 180 + 140 = 480 appliances.
Common Mistake to Avoid: Calculating 3/8 of the remaining stock rather than the total original stock.
Question 5 Worked Solution Charity Fundraising Allocation
[3 Marks Available]
A community fundraising event raises £2,400. The committee allocates 5/12 to a local foodbank and 3/8 to a youth centre.
How much money remains for administrative costs?
Foodbank allocation: (2,400 ÷ 12) × 5 = 200 × 5 = £1,000
[M1]
Youth centre allocation: (2,400 ÷ 8) × 3 = 300 × 3 = £900
[M1]
Remaining balance: £2,400 - (£1,000 + £900) = £2,400 - £1,900 = £500
[A1]
Correct Final Answer: £500.00
Quick Check Method: 1,000 + 900 + 500 = £2,400.
Common Mistake to Avoid: Adding numerators without finding common denominators.
Question 6 Worked Solution Commercial Flooring Waste
[2 Marks Available]
An office refurbishment project requires 180 m² of carpet tiles. Due to layout cuts and wastage, the contractor must purchase 1 1/10 times the net floor area.
How many square metres of carpet tiles must be purchased?
Calculate 1 1/10 of 180: 180 × 1.1 = 198 m² (or 180 + 180/10 = 180 + 18 = 198)
[M1]
State total carpet tiles to order: 198 m²
[A1]
Correct Final Answer: 198 m²
Quick Check Method: 198 ÷ 1.1 = 180 m².
Common Mistake to Avoid: Multiplying by 1.01 instead of 1.1 (1/10 = 0.1).
