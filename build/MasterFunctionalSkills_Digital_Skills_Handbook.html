Unit 1 • Operating Systems, File Hierarchy & Cloud Storage

Workplace Application: Operating Systems, File Hierarchy & Cloud Storage
▶Core Knowledge & Statutory Standards

Operating Systems & File Systems: Modern workplace devices operate on Windows, macOS, ChromeOS, or mobile OS (iOS/Android). A structured hierarchical file architecture organises data into logical nested folders (e.g. /Documents/Clients/2025/Invoices/). Standard file naming conventions use ISO dates and clear descriptors (e.g. 2025-05-14_Invoice_SmithLtd_v2.pdf) to avoid version confusion.

Cloud Storage & Synchronisation: Cloud platforms (OneDrive, Google Drive, Nextcloud) synchronize local files to secure remote servers. This enables multi-device access, automated version history, and real-time multi-author collaboration with granular viewing/editing permissions.

★Workplace Context & Employer Expectations

In modern enterprises, competence in Operating Systems, File Hierarchy & Cloud Storage is essential for data security, administrative velocity, and regulatory compliance.

Workplace Application: Advanced Search Syntax & Information Verification
▶Core Knowledge & Statutory Standards

Search Syntax & Precision Filtering: Professional workplace research requires targeted Boolean operators and search filters: quotes "exact phrase" for verbatim matching, site:.gov.uk to restrict results to verified government domains, filetype:pdf for official reports, and the minus operator -keyword to exclude irrelevant commercial results.

CRAAP Evaluation Framework: Not all online data is reliable. Candidates must evaluate information using the CRAAP test: Currency (publication date), Relevance (matches research scope), Authority (author credentials and domain reputation), Accuracy (backed by cited evidence), and Purpose (identifying commercial bias vs objective research).

Unit 3 • Professional Word Processing & Structured Documents

Workplace Application: Professional Word Processing & Structured Documents
▶Core Knowledge & Statutory Standards

Document Hierarchy & Styles: Professional word processing (MS Word, Google Docs) relies on semantic paragraph styles (Heading 1, Heading 2, Body Text) rather than manual font resizing. Styles guarantee consistent typography, automatically generate clickable Tables of Contents, and maintain screen reader accessibility.

Tables, Headers & Page Architecture: Long documents require structured headers and footers with automated page numbering (Page X of Y), balanced margins (minimum 20 mm), accessible data tables with dedicated header rows, and embedded high-resolution graphics with descriptive Alt-Text.

Unit 4 • Spreadsheet Architecture, Formulas & Data Visualisation

Workplace Application: Spreadsheet Architecture, Formulas & Data Visualisation
▶Core Knowledge & Statutory Standards

Spreadsheet Principles: Spreadsheets (MS Excel, Google Sheets) manage numerical and tabular records across columns and rows. Formulas always begin with an equals sign (=) and use relative cell referencing (=B2*C2) or absolute cell referencing (=$B$1*C2) to automate calculations without manual data re-entry.

Essential Formulas & Functions: Statutory Level 1 & Level 2 standards require mastery of: =SUM(range) for totals, =AVERAGE(range) for arithmetic means, =COUNT(range) / =COUNTA(range), =MIN(range) and =MAX(range), and logical conditional expressions =IF(logical_test, value_if_true, value_if_false).

Unit 5 • Presentation Design, Multimedia & Digital Accessibility

Workplace Application: Presentation Design, Multimedia & Digital Accessibility
▶Core Knowledge & Statutory Standards

Visual Storytelling & Cognitive Load: Effective slide presentations (PowerPoint, Google Slides) communicate complex ideas succinctly. Avoid wall-of-text slides; adhere to the 6x6 guideline (maximum 6 bullet points, maximum 6 words per bullet). Use high-contrast color palettes (dark text on white/cream backgrounds) and legible sans-serif typography (Arial, Calibri, Montserrat) at 24pt+ for readability.

Multimedia Integration & Accessibility: Audio and video clips must be compressed and formatted with closed captions. Ensure all visual graphics have Alt-Text descriptions. Never use colour as the sole visual indicator of status (e.g. combine green/red icons with text labels 'Pass' / 'Fail').

Unit 6 • Professional Email Etiquette, Attachments & Scheduling

Workplace Application: Professional Email Etiquette, Attachments & Scheduling
▶Core Knowledge & Statutory Standards

Workplace Email Protocols: Business email requires precise communication conventions: a concise, informative subject line (e.g. Action Required: Q3 Budget Review Meeting – 14 June), professional salutations (Dear Mr Davies, / Good morning Sarah,), and appropriate closing sign-offs. Understand recipient fields: To for primary action owners, Cc (Carbon Copy) for informational awareness, and Bcc (Blind Carbon Copy) for mass distributions to preserve recipient data privacy under UK GDPR.

Calendar Scheduling & Secure Attachments: Scheduling meetings via digital calendars (Outlook, Google Calendar) requires checking attendee availability via scheduling assistants, including agenda points, and attaching meeting links. Before attaching files, compress large folders into .zip archives and verify that sensitive documents are password-encrypted.

Unit 7 • Video Conferencing, Collaboration Tools & Netiquette

Workplace Application: Video Conferencing, Collaboration Tools & Netiquette
▶Core Knowledge & Statutory Standards

Virtual Meeting Platforms: Platforms like MS Teams, Zoom, and Google Meet are integral to remote teamwork. Digital Skills & Cybersecurity Glossary

Absolute Cell Reference ($A$1)
A locked cell coordinate in a spreadsheet formula that does not change when autofilled into adjacent cells.
Bandwidth
The maximum rate of data transfer across a given network path, measured in Megabits per second (Mbps).
Cloud Synchronization
The automated process of mirroring local files to a secure remote cloud server (e.g. OneDrive, Nextcloud).
Cybersecurity Hygiene
Routine practices (e.g. unique passphrases, regular patching, MFA) that maintain security and protect data.
Data Subject Access Request (DSAR)
A formal request under GDPR allowing individuals to obtain a copy of personal data held by an organization.
DSE Assessment
Display Screen Equipment risk assessment mandated by Health and Safety regulations to prevent ergonomic strain.
Encryption (End-to-End)
A communication system where only the communicating users can read the messages, preventing eavesdropping.
Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
An authentication method requiring two or more verification factors (password + authenticator app code).
Netiquette
The conventional rules of polite, professional, and respectful behavior in online and email communications.
Phishing
A fraudulent attempt to obtain sensitive information by disguising as a trustworthy entity in electronic communications.
Ransomware
Malicious software designed to block access to a computer system or encrypt files until a sum of money is paid.
URL (Uniform Resource Locator)
The unique address used to identify and locate a specific webpage or file on the World Wide Web.
VPN (Virtual Private Network)
An encrypted connection over the internet from a device to a network, shielding online activity.
