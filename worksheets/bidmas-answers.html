BIDMAS / Order of Operations Pearson Edexcel: L1.6 • L2.5
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Procedural Fluency Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Level 1 Arithmetic Order) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Work out: 15 + 8 × 4 - 6.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Multiplication first: 8 × 4 = 32 [M1]
2. Addition and subtraction left to right: 15 + 32 = 47; 47 - 6 = 41 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 41
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Calculating strictly from left to right: (15 + 8) = 23; 23 × 4 = 92; 92 - 6 = 86 (incorrect).
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 41 + 6 - (8 × 4) = 47 - 32 = 15.
Question 2 (Level 2 Brackets & Indices) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Evaluate: (14 - 5)² ÷ 3 + 7.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Brackets first: 14 - 5 = 9; Indices: 9² = 81 [M1]
2. Division: 81 ÷ 3 = 27; Addition: 27 + 7 = 34 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 34
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Multiplying 9 by 2 instead of squaring (9² = 81), or adding before dividing (81 ÷ 10).
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: (34 - 7) × 3 = 81; √81 = 9; 9 + 5 = 14.
Section B: Workplace & Functional Scenarios Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 Shift Wage Calculation) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A delivery driver's daily pay is calculated using the formula:
Pay = £45 + (£12 × Hours) - £8 (Vehicle charge)

Calculate the driver's pay for a day where they work 7.5 hours.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate hourly earnings: £12 × 7.5 = £90.00 [M1]
2. Add base pay: £45 + £90 = £135.00 [P1]
3. Deduct vehicle charge: £135.00 - £8.00 = £127.00 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £127.00
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Adding £45 + £12 first to get £57, then multiplying by 7.5 (£427.50).
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: £127 + £8 - £45 = £90; £90 ÷ £12 = 7.5 hours.
Question 4 (Level 2 Warehouse Pallet Volumetric Formula) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
An export shipping container charges storage fees based on the formula:
Fee = £35 + 4 × (Length + Width) × Height
For a pallet measuring: Length = 1.2 m, Width = 0.8 m, Height = 1.5 m.

Calculate the storage fee in pounds (£).
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Evaluate brackets: Length + Width = 1.2 + 0.8 = 2.0 m [M1]
2. Multiply factors: 4 × 2.0 × 1.5 = 12.0 [P1]
3. Add base charge: £35 + £12 = £47.00 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £47.00
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Adding £35 + 4 first (getting 39) before multiplying by the bracketed terms.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: £47 - £35 = £12; £12 ÷ (4 × 1.5) = 2.0; 2.0 - 0.8 = 1.2 m.
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Problem Solving & Justification Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Heating Energy Cost Audit) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A community leisure centre heating cost is determined by:
Cost = Daily Standing Charge + (Day Units × Day Rate) + (Night Units × Night Rate)

For one billing period:
• Daily standing charge: 30 days at £0.85 per day
• Day units: 4,200 kWh at £0.24 per kWh
• Night units: 1,800 kWh at £0.15 per kWh

Work out the total heating cost for the billing period.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate standing charge: 30 × £0.85 = £25.50 [P1]
2. Calculate day units cost: 4,200 × £0.24 = £1,008.00 [P1]
3. Calculate night units cost: 1,800 × £0.15 = £270.00 [M1]
4. Sum all three components: £25.50 + £1,008.00 + £270.00 = £1,303.50 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £1,303.50
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Adding day and night units together (6,000 kWh) and multiplying by an average rate.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: £1,303.50 - £25.50 = £1,278.00; £1,278 - £1,008 = £270.00.
Question 6 (Level 2 Calculator Input Error Evaluation) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
An administrative apprentice calculates the average material cost across three work packages.
The invoices are £450, £620, and £580.
The apprentice enters into their calculator: 450 + 620 + 580 ÷ 3 and records an average of £1,263.33.

Explain the mathematical error the apprentice made using BIDMAS.
Show the correct formula that should have been used, and calculate the true average cost.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Explain error: Due to BIDMAS, the calculator divides only the 580 by 3 (193.33) and then adds 450 + 620, instead of dividing the entire sum. [P1]
2. State required formula with brackets: (450 + 620 + 580) ÷ 3 [M1]
3. Evaluate correct sum: 450 + 620 + 580 = £1,650 [P1]
4. Calculate accurate average: £1,650 ÷ 3 = £550.00 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Error: Omitted brackets around the sum. True average is £550.00
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Not recognizing that £1,263.33 is impossible as an average of three numbers between £450 and £620.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: £550 × 3 = £1,650; £1,650 - £450 - £620 = £580.
