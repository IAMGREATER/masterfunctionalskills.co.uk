Question 1 Worked Solution Healthcare Procurement Order Estimation
[3 Marks Available]
A procurement manager orders 38 digital blood pressure monitors at £48.75 each.
(a) Use estimation (by rounding each number to 1 significant figure) to calculate an approximate total cost.
(b) The supplier sends an invoice for £2,450.00. Is this invoice reasonable based on your estimate?
Round 38 to 1 s.f. = 40; Round £48.75 to 1 s.f. = £50
[M1]
Estimate calculation: 40 × £50 = £2,000
[M1]
Conclusion: No, the invoice for £2,450 is significantly too high (overcharged by ~£600; actual cost is 38 × £48.75 = £1,852.50).
[A1]
Correct Final Answer: (a) Estimate = £2,000 | (b) No, £2,450 is unreasonable (much higher than estimate)
Quick Check Method: Exact = £1,852.50, which is close to the £2,000 estimate, confirming £2,450 is incorrect.
Common Mistake to Avoid: Calculating the exact value (£1,852.50) instead of showing the 1 s.f. rounded values (40 × £50).
