Step-by-Step Teaching & Key Concepts

Core Knowledge
1
Inserting a Picture

Adding photos or company logos enhances visual interest and communication:

  1. Click on the page where you want the picture to appear.
  2. Go to the Insert tab on the top ribbon.
  3. Click Pictures > This Device.
  4. Browse to your folder (e.g. Pictures), click your file, and click Insert.
Copy Picture: Ctrl + C • Paste Picture: Ctrl + V
2
Resizing Images Proportionally (No Squashing!)

When you click on an inserted picture, a border with eight small circular dots (called sizing handles) appears around the edges.

  • CORRECT: Drag from a CORNER handle: Both the width and height change together equally. The image stays proportional and natural.
  • INCORRECT: Dragging from side or top handles: This squashes or stretches the photo, making people look abnormally wide or squashed.
Examiner Marking Trap: Examiners will deduct marks if an image appears visibly stretched or distorted. Always drag from the corner circles!
3
Cropping and Text Wrapping

To trim away unwanted background clutter:

  1. Click the picture to open the Picture Format tab.
  2. Click the Crop tool ().
  3. Drag the black crop bars inward to cut away unwanted parts, then press Enter.
  4. Click Wrap Text > Square so your text flows smoothly around the image rather than pushing it onto a new page.

Authentic Pearson Edexcel Exam Model

Accredited Model

Here is how this skill is tested in the real Pearson Edexcel onscreen digital assessment, broken down with official examiner mark codes ([B1] knowledge, [M1] process, [A1] accuracy).

Exam Scenario Prompt: A health worker inserts a photo of an ambulance into a safety poster. The photo is too large and spills onto page 2. Explain how to reduce the size of the photo without distorting its shape.
Step 1: Select the picture [B1]
Click on the ambulance photo to display the sizing handles.
Tutor Marking Rationale: Sizing handles must be active to resize.
Step 2: Resize proportionally [M1]
Click and drag inward from one of the four CORNER handles until the photo fits neatly on page 1.
Tutor Marking Rationale: Corner handles scale width and height simultaneously to maintain aspect ratio.
Tutor Abdul's Exam Golden Rule: Always use the word 'corner' when answering image resizing questions in the exam.

Pearson Edexcel Benchmark Solution

Step 1: Click the Insert tab, select Pictures > This Device, browse to the Pictures folder, choose the logo file and click Insert. Step 2: Select the image, click Wrap Text > Square, and drag the logo to the top right corner of the page.
Official Marking Criteria:
  • [1 Mark]: Correct pathway: Insert > Pictures > Select file.
  • [1 Mark]: Using Wrap Text and positioning the image at the top right.
