Section A: Core Skills (Warm-Up) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Multi-Condition Lookup in a Tariff Table) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A courier parcel tariff table charges:
• Up to 2 kg: Standard £4.50 | Express £7.20
• 2.01 kg to 5 kg: Standard £6.80 | Express £10.50
• Over 5 kg: Standard £9.40 | Express £14.00

Calculate the total cost of sending two 3.5 kg parcels by Express and one 1.8 kg parcel by Standard.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Lookup rates: 3.5 kg Express = £10.50 each (× 2 = £21.00); 1.8 kg Standard = £4.50 [M1]
2. Total postage: £21.00 + £4.50 = £25.50 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £25.50
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Summing the weights first (3.5 + 3.5 + 1.8 = 8.8 kg) instead of pricing each parcel individually.
🔄 Quick Check: £25.50 - £4.50 = £21.00; £21.00 ÷ 2 = £10.50.
Question 2 (Level 1 Foundation — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Using the Parcel Weight table in Core Skill 1 above, calculate the total postage cost to send one 1.5 kg parcel by Standard (48h) and one 6.8 kg parcel by Saturday Special.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. 1.5 kg (Up to 2 kg) Standard = £3.95. [M1]
2. 6.8 kg (5.01 to 10 kg) Saturday Special = £13.40. [M1]
3. Total = £3.95 + £13.40 = £17.35. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £17.35
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Missing a line below the table stating that delivery or VAT must be added.
🔄 Quick Check: Match each parcel weight to its correct row before reading across to the service column.
Section B: Everyday & Workplace Maths Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Hospital Clinic Shift Cover Schedule Lookup (Entry 3 / Level 1 Foundation)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A table shows nurse staffing across 3 hospital wards:
• Ward A: Morning 6, Afternoon 5, Night 3
• Ward B: Morning 8, Afternoon 6, Night 4
• Ward C: Morning 5, Afternoon 4, Night 2
(a) Which ward has the highest Afternoon staffing?
(b) What is the total number of Night nurses across all three wards?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Compare the Afternoon numbers (5, 6, 4): Highest is Ward B with 6 nurses. [A1]
2. Sum the Night numbers (3 + 4 + 2): 3 + 4 + 2 = 9 night nurses. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) Ward B (6 nurses) | (b) 9 night nurses
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Always read the small text directly underneath a table before starting your working.
🔄 Quick Check: Circle the specific shift name (Afternoon / Night) before adding.
Question 4 (Workplace Application — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A hospital shuttle bus timetable shows:
• Bus 1: Departs Station 07:45 → Arrives Hospital 08:18
• Bus 2: Departs Station 08:15 → Arrives Hospital 08:48
• Bus 3: Departs Station 08:40 → Arrives Hospital 09:13
A nurse starts her shift at 08:45 and needs 10 minutes to walk from the bus stop to her ward. Which is the latest bus she can catch, and what time does it depart the Station?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Latest arrival at Hospital bus stop = 08:45 − 10 mins = 08:35. [M1]
2. Bus 2 arrives at 08:48 (too late). She must catch Bus 1 (arrives 08:18). [P1]
3. Bus 1 departs Station at 07:45. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Bus 1 (departs Station at 07:45)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: If an appointment is at 09:15 and buses arrive at 09:02 and 09:20, picking the 09:20 bus makes the passenger 5 minutes late!
🔄 Quick Check: Subtract the 10-minute walk from 08:45 first: she must arrive at the bus stop by 08:35.
Section C: Exam-Style Problem Solving Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Medical Equipment Supplier Table & Delivery Fee Comparison (Level 1/2 Multi-Step)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A practice manager needs to order 8 digital blood pressure monitors. Three suppliers quote in a table:
• MedEquip: £24.00 per monitor | Delivery: £12.00
• HealthDirect: £25.50 per monitor | Delivery: Free on orders over £150 (otherwise £15.00)
• CarePlus: £23.00 per monitor | Delivery: £22.00
Calculate the total cost for each supplier and identify the cheapest option.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate MedEquip total for 8 monitors: (8 × £24.00) + £12.00 = £192.00 + £12.00 = £204.00. [M1]
2. Calculate HealthDirect total (checking free delivery threshold): 8 × £25.50 = £204.00 (Over £150, so delivery is £0) → Total = £204.00. [M1]
3. Calculate CarePlus total and compare all three: (8 × £23.00) + £22.00 = £184.00 + £22.00 = £206.00. | MedEquip and HealthDirect are tied as the cheapest at £204.00 each (£2.00 cheaper than CarePlus). [M1 / A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: MedEquip and HealthDirect are joint cheapest at £204.00 (CarePlus costs £206.00)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: You must arrive ON or BEFORE the appointment time (pick the 09:02 arrival).
🔄 Quick Check: Notice that CarePlus had the cheapest monitor (£23), but its £22 delivery fee made it the most expensive overall!
Question 6 (Level 2 Multi-Step Problem Solving — 2 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
In Question 2, how many minutes does the Bus 1 journey take from Station (07:45) to Hospital (08:18)?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. From 07:45 to 08:00 = 15 minutes. [M1]
2. From 08:00 to 08:18 = 18 minutes. [P1]
3. Total = 15 + 18 = 33 minutes. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 33 minutes
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Stating a conclusion without showing the full numerical comparison values.
🔄 Quick Check: Bridge across the hour (08:00): 15 mins + 18 mins = 33 mins.
