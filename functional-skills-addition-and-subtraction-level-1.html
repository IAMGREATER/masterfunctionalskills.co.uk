Official Level 1 Specification & Scope (L1.N.1 / L1.N.11)

Regulated Ofqual Functional Skills Mathematics Level 1 learning outcomes (Open Awards, Pearson Edexcel, City & Guilds, NCFE & AQA):

  • Add and subtract multi-digit whole numbers and decimals up to two decimal places without a calculator.
  • Align decimal points vertically and pad whole numbers with .00 when calculating money.
  • Write a valid reverse-operation check (using subtraction to check addition, or addition to check subtraction).
Included at Level 1
Whole numbers up to 1,000,000 and decimals up to 2 decimal places (e.g. £45.60 - £18.75) + reverse checks.

Core Level 1 Golden Rules: Addition and Subtraction (Level 1)

Golden Rule 1: Line Up Units & Decimal Points

Stack Hundreds, Tens, Units, and Decimal Points in strict vertical columns. Turn whole pounds like £50 into £50.00 before subtracting.

Level 1 Key Rule: £50.00 − £16.45 = £33.55
Golden Rule 2: Borrow Cleanly Across Zeros

When subtracting from 50.00, exchange from the 5 first (making 4 | 9 | 9 | 10) rather than writing '0 − 5 = 5'!

Level 1 Key Rule: Never flip bottom minus top!
Golden Rule 3: Show the Inverse Check Explicitly

When an exam question says 'Show a check of your answer', reverse your exact calculation on a separate line.

Level 1 Key Rule: If 340 − 185 = 155, Check: 155 + 185 = 340

Step 1 Pad Whole Numbers with Trailing Zeros
Write £8 as £08.00. This prevents accidentally adding 8 into the tens or tenths column!
Step 2 Add Hundredths Column (h)
0 + 5 + 0 = 5
Write 5 in the hundredths answer space. No digit to carry.
Step 3 Add Tenths Column (t) & Carry
8 + 4 + 0 = 12 tenths
Write down 2 in the tenths space. Carry 1 across the decimal line into the Units column.
Step 4 Add Units Column (U) & Carry
5 + 9 + 8 + 1 (carried) = 23
Write down 3 in the units space. Carry 2 into the Tens column.
Step 5 Add Tens (T) & Hundreds (H)
Tens: 4 + 3 + 2 = 9  |  Hundreds: 1 + 0 = 1
Final answer: £193.25. Fully aligned, clearly carried, zero guesswork.
Sonya's Visual Quick-Check

"In Pearson Edexcel Section A (Non-Calculator), examiners test decimal addition every exam. The number one mistake learners make is placing £8 under the tens column instead of the units, giving £265.25 instead of £193.25! Always write 08.00 to lock every single digit into its true column."

Step 1 Trigger the Cascading Borrow (Th → H)
You cannot compute 0 - 8. The units, tens, and hundreds all have zeros! Go all the way to Thousands (5). Cross out 5 → write 4. The Hundreds column now has 10.
Step 2 Borrow from Hundreds into Tens
Borrow 1 hundred from the 10 hundreds. Cross out 10 → write 9. The Tens column now has 10.
Step 3 Borrow from Tens into Units
Borrow 1 ten from the 10 tens. Cross out 10 → write 9. The Units column now has 10!
Step 4 Subtract Column-by-Column (Right to Left)
U: 10 − 8 = 2  |  T: 9 − 6 = 3  |  H: 9 − 4 = 5  |  Th: 4 − 1 = 3
Every single column works out smoothly without negative numbers! Result: 3,532.
Subi's Shortcut: The "Nines Domino Rule"

"Notice the golden pattern: whenever you borrow across several zeros from a lead digit, the first digit goes down by 1 (5 → 4), all middle zeros turn directly into 9, and only the very last zero becomes 10! Remembering this will save you 2 minutes of messy scribbles in your exam."

Core Skill 3: Writing a Valid Pearson Edexcel Reverse Operation Check

Securing the guaranteed [B1] independent check mark in Non-Calculator Section A and Section B.

In almost every Pearson Edexcel Functional Skills exam paper, 1 mark is explicitly allocated for a check. Even if your initial answer is wrong, writing a mathematically valid reverse check awards you 1 free method mark!

What Gets 1 Full Mark [B1]
  • Full equation with answer: e.g. 551 + 299 = 850.
  • Using the opposite operation: (+ reversed into −, or − reversed into +).
  • Exact numerical agreement: The check confirms the starting figure.
What Gets ZERO Marks [0 / 1]
  • Incomplete calculation: Writing 551 + 299 without = 850!
  • Writing words instead of math: "I checked my work", "I did it again on paper".
  • Repeating the original calculation: Writing 850 - 299 = 551 again.
Sonya's Guaranteed Mark Secret

"Every Pearson Edexcel paper has at least one question stating: 'Show a check of your answer'. Even if you made a slip in the main question and got 555 instead of 551, writing 555 + 299 = 854 still gets you the [B1] check mark because the method is valid! Never leave this box blank!"

Common Level 1 Examiner Traps to Avoid

Level 1 Examiner Trap: Subtracting Upwards (0 − 5 = 5)
Writing £90.00 − £43.55 = £47.55 by doing 5 − 0 in the pence columns. Always exchange from the left!
Level 1 Examiner Trap: Repeating the Same Sum as a 'Check'
Re-writing 90 − 43.55 = 46.45 gains zero check credit—you must use the opposite operation (+) or rounded estimation.
