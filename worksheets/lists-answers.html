Section A: Core Skills (Warm-Up) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Filtering & Ordering Multi-Criteria Lists) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Four agency nurses submit their hourly rates and weekend availability:
• Nurse A: £24.50/hr (Weekdays only)
• Nurse B: £26.00/hr (Weekends available)
• Nurse C: £23.75/hr (Weekends available)
• Nurse D: £25.20/hr (Weekends available)

Select the two lowest-cost nurses who can work a 10-hour Sunday shift, and calculate their combined wages for that Sunday.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Exclude Nurse A (weekdays only); select cheapest two weekend nurses: Nurse C (£23.75) and Nurse D (£25.20) [M1]
2. Combined 10-hour shift cost: (£23.75 + £25.20) × 10 = £48.95 × 10 = £489.50 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Nurse C and Nurse D (£489.50)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Including Nurse A (£24.50) despite being unavailable on weekends.
🔄 Quick Check: £237.50 + £252.00 = £489.50.
Question 2 (Level 1 Foundation — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Arrange these five delivery towns in alphabetical order: Bolton, Bradford, Bristol, Bath, Barnsley.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Ba comes before Bo and Br: Barnsley (Bar) vs Bath (Bat) → r before t → Barnsley, Bath. [M1]
2. Bo: Bolton. [M1]
3. Br: Bradford (Bra) vs Bristol (Bri) → Bradford, Bristol. [M1]
4. Full order: Barnsley, Bath, Bolton, Bradford, Bristol. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Barnsley, Bath, Bolton, Bradford, Bristol
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Putting 02/11/2026 before 18/04/2026 because 02 is smaller than 18!
🔄 Quick Check: Compare the 3rd letters for Barnsley (r) and Bath (t).
Section B: Everyday & Workplace Maths Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Patient Medical Records Alphabetical Filing (Entry 3 / Level 1 Foundation)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Arrange these five patient surnames in alphabetical order (A to Z):
Patel, Parker, Phillips, Price, Pearce
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Group by 2nd letter (Pa, Pe, Ph, Pr): Pa: Parker, Patel | Pe: Pearce | Ph: Phillips | Pr: Price. [M1]
2. Compare 3rd letter of Parker (r) vs Patel (t): r comes before t → Parker, Patel, Pearce, Phillips, Price. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Parker, Patel, Pearce, Phillips, Price
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Always compare Year first, then Month (April = 04 comes before November = 11), then Day.
🔄 Quick Check: Write down the 2nd and 3rd letters above each name to avoid slips.
Question 4 (Workplace Application — 2 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A pharmacy technician checks four medicine batch expiry dates so the earliest expiry is used first (FEFO): 14/09/2026, 28/03/2026, 05/12/2025, 19/03/2026. List them from earliest to latest.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. 2025 comes first: 05/12/2025. [M1]
2. March 2026 (03/2026): 19/03/2026 then 28/03/2026. [P1]
3. September 2026: 14/09/2026. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 05/12/2025, 19/03/2026, 28/03/2026, 14/09/2026
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Picking Hall B because it is the cheapest without noticing its capacity is only 40 (< 45 needed).
🔄 Quick Check: Sort by Year (2025 before 2026), then Month (03 before 09), then Day (19 before 28).
Section C: Exam-Style Problem Solving Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Conference Venue Multi-Criteria Filtering & Costing (Level 1/2 Multi-Step)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A training coordinator needs a venue for 45 delegates that has Wheelchair Access, includes Free Parking, and costs under £900 in total (Room Hire + Catering at £12 per delegate):
• Hall A: Capacity 60 | Wheelchair: Yes | Parking: Free | Room Hire: £320
• Hall B: Capacity 40 | Wheelchair: Yes | Parking: Free | Room Hire: £250
• Hall C: Capacity 50 | Wheelchair: Yes | Parking: Paid (£5) | Room Hire: £280
• Hall D: Capacity 55 | Wheelchair: Yes | Parking: Free | Room Hire: £390
Identify which hall meets all criteria and state its total cost.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Eliminate venues failing capacity or parking criteria: Hall B fails capacity (40 < 45 delegates). | Hall C fails Free Parking. [M1]
2. Calculate catering cost for 45 delegates at £12 each: 45 × £12 = £540 catering cost. [M1]
3. Check total cost for Hall A and Hall D against £900 limit: Hall A Total = £320 + £540 = £860 ( £900 — Fails). | Select Hall A at £860. [M1 / A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Hall A (Total cost = £860, meeting all criteria)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Underline every single requirement in the question stem before checking the list.
🔄 Quick Check: State clearly why each rejected option failed so you earn full process marks.
Question 6 (Level 2 Multi-Step Problem Solving — 2 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
From this list of staff: Ali (Band 5, Night, First Aid), Ben (Band 6, Day, First Aid), Cara (Band 6, Night, No First Aid), Dan (Band 6, Night, First Aid) — who meets ALL three rules: Band 6, Night Shift, and First Aid qualified?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Ali is Band 5 (Eliminated). [M1]
2. Ben is Day Shift (Eliminated). [P1]
3. Cara has No First Aid (Eliminated). [P1]
4. Dan meets all three criteria. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Dan
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Stating a conclusion without showing the full numerical comparison values.
🔄 Quick Check: Cross off candidates who fail any one of the three rules.
