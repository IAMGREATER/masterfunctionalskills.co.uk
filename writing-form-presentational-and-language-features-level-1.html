Core Level 1 Golden Rules: Mastering All Level 1 Writing Formats: Letters, Emails, Articles, Reports & Leaflets

Golden Rule 1: Lock In Format Marks Before Writing Word One!

Every Functional Skills Writing mark scheme awards dedicated marks for correct layout conventions—set up your headers/addresses in the first 60 seconds.

Level 1 Key Rule: Missing postal addresses on a formal letter throws away instant layout marks!
Golden Rule 2: Know Your Core Level 1 Format Blueprints Cold

Formal Letter: 2 addresses + Date + Greeting + Sign-off | Email: To + Subject + Greeting + Sign-off | Article: Headline + Subheadings | Report/Leaflet: Title + Subheadings + Short Bullet List.

Level 1 Key Rule: Match layout strictly to the task prompt!
Golden Rule 3: Use Bullet Points Sparingly (Only in Leaflets/Reports/Instructions)

A 3-item bullet list looks great inside a leaflet or report recommendations section, whereas writing an entire formal letter in bullets loses paragraphing marks.

Level 1 Key Rule: Always write full paragraphs around any short bullet list.

Core Skill 3: Language Features Matched to Format Types

Choosing the right rhetorical and grammatical tools for articles vs reports.

  • Articles & Blogs: Use direct address ("Have you ever wondered...?"), inclusive pronouns ("our neighbourhood"), short punchy subheadings, and a lively, motivating tone.
  • Formal Reports: Avoid personal emotion or rhetorical questions. Use objective third-person phrasing ("The audit revealed that 68% of respondents...") and numbered recommendations.
Level 1 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Common Level 1 Examiner Traps to Avoid

Level 1 Examiner Trap: Putting Postal Addresses on an Email!
Emails ONLY use `To:` and `Subject:`—never put street addresses at the top of an email.
Level 1 Examiner Trap: Writing an Article Without a Headline
Always give an article or leaflet a clear, engaging main heading at the very top.

Interactive Level 1 Exam Practice Questions

Level 1 Practice 1: Format Feature Quick-Check 3 Marks
List the key layout features required for:
1. A workplace Report
2. A newsletter Article
3. A formal Email
Level 1 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
Report (1 Mark): Main Title + Subheadings (Introduction, Findings, Recommendations) + Author/Date
Article (1 Mark): Catchy Headline + Byline ('By...') + Subheadings/Paragraphs
Email (1 Mark): To: + Subject: line + Polite greeting & sign-off
Final Answer: 1. Title & Subheadings | 2. Headline & Byline | 3. To: & Subject: lines + Greeting/Sign-off
Level 1 Practice 2: Choosing Language to Suit Format 2 Marks
Write a catchy article headline and opening rhetorical question encouraging neighbours to join a Saturday street litter-pick.
Level 1 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
Headline (1 Mark): e.g. 'Let's Make Oakfield Sparkle This Saturday!'
Opening question (1 Mark): e.g. 'Have you noticed litter building up along our pavements lately?'
Final Answer: Headline: 'Pride in Our Street: Join Saturday's Clean-Up!' + Opening rhetorical question.
