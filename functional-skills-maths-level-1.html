Functional Skills Maths Level 1 — Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the exam, syllabus, preparation, and certificate.

What is Functional Skills Maths Level 1 equivalent to?

Functional Skills Maths Level 1 is equivalent to GCSE grades 1 to 3 (grades D-G in the old grading scale). It serves as the primary stepping stone to Level 2 and is recognized for many entry-level apprenticeships and workplace training programs.

Are all resources and past papers accessible online?

Yes. All 38 topic revision guides, downloadable PDF worksheets, model answers, and video walkthroughs are 100% accessible online from your desktop, tablet, or smartphone anytime.

Can I sit the exam online from home?

Yes. We provide official Ofqual-regulated Pearson Edexcel and Open Awards online exams invigilated via secure remote proctoring from Monday through Saturday.

How quickly are results and certificates issued?

Exam results are typically released within 6 to 14 working days, and official Ofqual-certified e-certificates and paper certificates are delivered shortly after.
