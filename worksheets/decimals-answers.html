Decimals Pearson Edexcel: E3.5 • L1.10 • L2.10
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Core Skills (Warm-Up) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Level 1 Decimal Addition) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Work out: 48.65 + 7.9 + 134.08 without a calculator.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Align decimal points vertically with trailing placeholder zero: 48.65 + 07.90 + 134.08 [M1]
2. Accurate column addition: 190.63 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 190.63
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Misaligning 7.9 into the hundredths column as 0.79 or 7.09.
🔄 Quick Check: 190.63 - 134.08 - 7.90 = 48.65.
Question 2 (Level 2 Decimal Division) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Calculate: 14.7 ÷ 0.03 without a calculator.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Multiply dividend and divisor by 100 to clear the decimal divisor: 1,470 ÷ 3 [M1]
2. Compute 1,470 ÷ 3 = 490 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 490
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Dividing 14.7 by 3 to get 4.9 without shifting the decimal point two places.
🔄 Quick Check: 490 × 0.03 = 14.7.
Section B: Everyday & Workplace Maths Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 Medical Dosage Administration) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A nurse administers liquid analgesic at a dosage of 3.75 ml per patient.
The medication bottle contains 90 ml.

How many full patient doses are contained in the bottle?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Set up division: 90 ÷ 3.75 [M1]
2. Multiply both by 100 to divide whole numbers: 9,000 ÷ 375 [P1]
3. Evaluate: 9,000 ÷ 375 = 24 doses [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 24 doses
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Multiplying 90 × 3.75 (= 337.5) instead of dividing total bottle volume by single dose volume.
🔄 Quick Check: 24 × 3.75 ml = 90 ml.
Question 4 (Level 2 Fuel Consumption & Cost) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A community health visitor travels 348.5 miles across a rural district.
Her hybrid car consumes 0.082 litres of petrol per mile, and petrol costs £1.45 per litre.

Work out the total fuel cost for the trip. Give your answer to the nearest penny.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate total fuel used: 348.5 × 0.082 = 28.577 litres [M1]
2. Multiply by fuel price: 28.577 × £1.45 = £41.43665 [P1]
3. Round to 2 decimal places (nearest penny): £41.44 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £41.44
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Prematurely rounding 28.577 litres to 28.6 litres before multiplying by £1.45 (which gives £41.47).
🔄 Quick Check: £41.44 ÷ £1.45 ≈ 28.58 L; 28.58 ÷ 348.5 ≈ 0.082 L/mile.
Section C: Exam-Style Problem Solving Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Chemical Water Purification) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A hydrotherapy pool contains 45,000 litres of water.
Safety regulations require adding 0.0035 litres of sanitising concentrate for every 100 litres of pool water.
Sanitising concentrate is sold in 0.5-litre bottles at £18.20 per bottle.

How much does it cost to purchase enough whole bottles for one complete pool treatment?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Find number of 100-litre units: 45,000 ÷ 100 = 450 units [P1]
2. Calculate required concentrate: 450 × 0.0035 = 1.575 litres [M1]
3. Find bottles needed: 1.575 ÷ 0.5 = 3.15 bottles → round UP to 4 whole bottles [P1]
4. Total cost: 4 × £18.20 = £72.80 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £72.80
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Multiplying 3.15 × £18.20 = £57.33, forgetting that bottles can only be bought as whole units.
🔄 Quick Check: 3 bottles = 1.5 L (short by 0.075 L). 4 bottles = 2.0 L at 4 × £18.20 = £72.80.
Question 6 (Level 2 Precision Tolerance Evaluation) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
An orthopaedic implant pin must have a target diameter of 4.50 mm with an allowable tolerance of ±0.04 mm (acceptable between 4.46 mm and 4.54 mm inclusive).
A batch inspector tests 4 sample pins: 4.45 mm, 4.48 mm, 4.53 mm, 4.55 mm.
The quality controller claims: 'At least 75% of the inspected pins are within the safety tolerance.'

Is the quality controller's claim correct? Show your evaluation of each pin and state a clear conclusion.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Establish tolerance bounds: 4.50 - 0.04 = 4.46 mm to 4.50 + 0.04 = 4.54 mm [P1]
2. Test pins: 4.45 mm (FAIL, < 4.46); 4.48 mm (PASS); 4.53 mm (PASS); 4.55 mm (FAIL, > 4.54) [M1]
3. Calculate pass percentage: 2 out of 4 pass = 2/4 = 50% [P1]
4. Conclude: Incorrect; only 50% (2 out of 4) of the pins pass, which is below the 75% claim. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Incorrect (Only 50% / 2 of 4 pins pass, which is less than 75%)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Assuming 4.45 mm passes because it rounds to 4.5 mm.
🔄 Quick Check: 2 ÷ 4 × 100 = 50% < 75%.
