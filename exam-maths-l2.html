3. Sit your exam online, from home

You can sit your Functional Skills Maths Level 2 exam online from home. Exams for TQUK and Open Awards last two hours, and the pass mark varies depending on the exam board and the difficulty of the paper.

Sections of the exam:

Non-calculator section

Total time: 30 minutes (15 marks)

Calculator section

Total time: 90 minutes (45 marks)

Areas of Study

Using Numbers

Operations, fractions, percentages, decimals

Measure, Shape & Space

Topics such as money, area, volume, geometry

Handling Data

Topics such as mean, probability, graphs

Technical Requirements

A suitable computer

A PC, Laptop, Mac or Chromebook for human invigilation. A PC, Laptop or Chromebook for remote invigilation. Phones and tablets are not allowed.

Webcam & Microphone

This equipment is usually built into your laptop but be purchased cheaply if required.

Zoom Desktop App (Human Invigilation Only)

Ensure this application is downloaded to your computer, installed and you are signed in. (This is for human invigilation only)

Wi-Fi connection (10Mbit+)

Minimum: 1 Mbit Upload, 10Mbit Download. (Hotspots, Mobile or otherwise are not allowed)

Entry Requirements

No entry requirements!

This qualification has no entry requirements. You will just need a computer and a reliable internet connection. If you need reasonable exam adjustments please allow a minimum of 5 working days for Online Remote Invigilation, or a minimum of 10 working days for Online Human Invigilation, for these adjustments to be reviewed.

What equipment do I need for the maths level 2 exam? + −
The equipment needed for your online level 2 Functional Skills maths exam includes:
  • A laptop or desktop (Windows or Mac)
  • A Working webcam and microphone
  • Zoom (if using online human invigilation)
  • Stable internet: 1 Mbps upload / 10 Mbps download
  • Quiet room where no one can enter during the exam
  • Valid photo ID
  • Non-scientific calculator
  • Let us know 20 working days in advance if you need reasonable adjustments
You will be provided with a full set of the exam instructions via your account dashboard after you sign up
Is this Functional Skills qualification accepted with it being online? + −

The Functional Skills maths Level 2 qualification you receive after passing our online exam is Ofqual-approved. This means it is an official UK government-regulated qualification and is recognised as equivalent to a GCSE at Level 2. Universities, colleges and employers across the UK accept this qualification as a GCSE equivalent, allowing your Functional Skills maths to support your next steps in education or employment.

How quickly can I take the exam after booking? + −

After booking your Level 2 maths exam online, you can choose any available date in 2026 or beyond, and sit the Functional Skills maths Level 2 online test with as little as one day’s notice. You will usually find discounted exam prices when booking further in advance, similar to how holiday prices work.

Do I have to pay again if I want to resit my level 2 maths online exam? + −

If you choose the ‘exam & course’ Premium Bundle option, your resit is included at no extra cost. If you select the ‘exam only’ option or Basic Bundle option, our resit support team will contact you to help arrange a resit. However, there will be a fee to retake the Functional Skills maths Level 2 online exam unless the Premium Bundle has been purchased.

How long does it take to get my results and certificate? + −

We offer fast track Functional Skills level 2 results so from the day you complete your exam, results are usually delivered within 2-6 working days for TQUK, or 6-16 working days for Open Awards. Your results will arrive via email; if successful, a Functional Skills digital certificate will follow shortly after. 

Is Functional Skills Maths level 2 equivalent to a GCSE? + −

The Functional Skills maths level 2 qualification is recognised by all training providers as a GCSE equivalent, and is widely accepted as such by colleges and universities as well as employers. 

What percentage do I need to pass the Functional Skills level 2 maths online exam? + −

The pass mark for each paper can vary, as some papers can be slightly more difficult than others. In general, the harder the paper, the lower the pass mark, in order to compensate for the difficulty.

What can I expect from the paper when I have to take it? + −

The Functional Skills Maths exam is split up into two key sections. The first of these two, the non-calculator section, lasts 30 minutes and makes up 15 marks in total. The second section, the calculator section, lasts 90 minutes and makes up 45 marks in total. The total duration of the exam is 2 hours.

Can you process a reasonable adjustment for my exam? + −

Yes, we do everything we can to ensure learners have the reasonable adjustments they need. Please inform us a minimum of 10 working days in advance. Contact us now to let us know.

What careers can I pursue with a Functional Skills qualification in Maths? + −

The Functional Skills Level 2 qualification in Maths demonstrates your ability to apply mathematical skills in practical, real-life situations, making you a valuable asset to employers.

The qualification could open doors to jobs in sectors such as Health and Social Care, Construction and Trades, Education, Business and Finance, and roles in the public sector.

This qualification offers you the flexibility to pursue a wide range of careers, depending on your interests and goals.

What education can I pursue with a Functional Skills qualification in Maths? + −

A Functional Skills Maths Level 2 qualification can open up a range of educational pathways. This includes progressing to A Levels, as some colleges now accept Functional Skills maths as an entry requirement for certain subjects.

Many apprenticeship programmes also accept Functional Skills maths as part of their entry requirements, including opportunities in engineering, health and social care, IT and business administration.

You could also progress to higher education diplomas, which are designed for adults returning to education and can provide a route into university courses.
