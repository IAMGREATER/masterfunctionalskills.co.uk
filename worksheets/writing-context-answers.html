Vocational Context & Purpose Pearson Edexcel: Pearson Edexcel Functional Skills English (WRITING)
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Core Skills (The Basics) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Decoding PAF: Purpose, Audience, and Format from an Exam Prompt) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Read this Pearson Edexcel Writing prompt:
'Your local council plans to close the town library to save money. Write a letter to the Editor of the local newspaper arguing why the library must remain open.'
Identify the exact Purpose, Audience, and Format.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Purpose: To persuade / argue that the town library should remain open [A1]
2. Audience & Format: Newspaper Editor & local residents | Formal Letter (sender address, recipient address, date, salutation, sign-off) [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Purpose: Persuade/Argue | Audience: Newspaper Editor & local readers | Format: Formal Letter.
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Writing an article with a headline when the prompt explicitly asks for a 'letter to the Editor'.
🔄 Quick Check: Matches all three PAF pillars.
Question 2 (Core Skill Application — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
You are a warehouse supervisor writing to senior management requesting anti-fatigue rubber floor mats for staff working on the packing line. Write two sentences that justify this request using workplace health and productivity priorities.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Sentence 1 (Health & Safety): Link standing on concrete floors for 8 hours to joint strain and sickness absence. [M1]
2. Sentence 2 (Productivity & Cost): Explain how anti-fatigue mats improve packing speed and pay for themselves through reduced agency cover costs. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'Installing ergonomic anti-fatigue floor mats along Packing Lines 1 to 4 will significantly reduce lower-back and joint strain for operatives standing on concrete floors throughout eight-hour shifts. Furthermore, reducing musculoskeletal sickness absence by just three shifts per quarter will fully offset the £450 equipment cost while maintaining peak dispatch throughput.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: If asked to write an internal workplace memo or incident report, writing a detached academic essay ('In this essay I will discuss...') loses format and context marks.
🔄 Quick Check: Always connect employee wellbeing directly to reduced absenteeism and operational performance.
Section B: Real-Life & Workplace Practice Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 Model: Warehouse PPE Replacement Request Email) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
You are a warehouse team leader. Write a short email to the Depot Manager, Mr Khan, requesting 15 new high-visibility thermal jackets for the loading bay team ahead of winter.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Set up email headers and state the specific request: To: r.khan@metrologistics.co.uk | Subject: Urgent Order Request – 15 Thermal High-Visibility Jackets [A1]
2. Connect request to workplace safety and winter weather conditions: Explain that loading bay doors remain open during early morning dispatches and current jackets have faded reflective strips. [A1]
3. Provide supplier cost detail and call to action: State unit cost (£22 per jacket, total £330) and ask for purchase approval by Friday. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: To: r.khan@metrologistics.co.uk
Subject: Urgent Order Request – 15 Thermal High-Visibility Jackets

Dear Mr Khan,

I am writing to request authorisation to order 15 replacement thermal high-visibility jackets for our Loading Bay 2 team before winter shift schedules begin next month.

Several existing jackets have worn reflective strips that no longer meet site safety standards around moving forklifts. Furthermore, because the loading bay shutters remain open during 05:00 dispatches, thermal insulation is essential to prevent cold-related illness.

Our approved PPE supplier can deliver 15 jackets for £330 in total (£22 each). Please could you approve this purchase order by Friday?

Kind regards,
Jordan Smith, Loading Bay Team Leader
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Always adopt the exact document format (Memo, Email, Letter, Report) and speak directly from your assigned workplace role.
🔄 Quick Check: Notice how linking the jackets to forklift visibility and 05:00 winter temperatures makes the request realistic and compelling.
Question 4 (Exam-Style Task — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Rewrite this weak student draft so it fits the vocational context of a Dental Practice Manager writing to patients about a new SMS appointment reminder system:
'We are starting text reminders next month because loads of patients keep forgetting to turn up and it wastes the dentist's time and costs us money.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Remove blaming/hostile tone ('loads of patients keep forgetting', 'wastes time'). [M1]
2. Frame the SMS system positively around patient convenience and making urgent emergency slots available for others. [M1]
3. Explain clearly how patients can reply to confirm or reschedule. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'From 1 November, Oakridge Dental Practice is introducing a free automated SMS reminder service to help you manage your routine check-ups conveniently. You will receive a text message 48 hours before your appointment, allowing you to confirm your attendance or reschedule with a single reply so that unused clinical slots can be offered to patients requiring urgent dental care.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Proposing that a small local charity should 'build a brand-new £5 million swimming pool next week' shows poor vocational realism.
🔄 Quick Check: When writing to customers or patients, frame policy changes around improved service and convenience rather than scolding them.
Section C: Exam-Style Tasks Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Model: Care Home Sensory Garden Capital Funding Proposal) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Write the opening two paragraphs of an internal funding request to the Care Home Finance Director for £800 to create a sensory garden for residents living with dementia.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. State the capital expenditure proposal clearly: Formal request for £800 capital funding to convert the unused South Lawn courtyard into a secure sensory garden. [A1]
2. Anchor proposal to clinical outcomes and CQC inspection criteria: Link sensory horticulture to reduced resident agitation, non-pharmacological therapy, and the upcoming CQC 'Responsive' and 'Well-Led' inspection domains. [A1]
3. Emphasise cost-effectiveness and community co-funding: Highlight that local garden centres have donated plants and staff volunteers will complete the planting over one weekend. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'I am writing to submit a capital expenditure proposal for £800 to construct a secure therapeutic sensory garden on the South Lawn courtyard. Clinical research demonstrates that outdoor sensory stimulation significantly reduces sundowning agitation among residents living with moderate-to-severe dementia, while directly strengthening our evidence portfolio for our upcoming CQC inspection.

To minimise costs to the Trust, local suppliers have already donated raised timber planters and sensory herbs free of charge; consequently, the £800 budget is required solely for non-slip pathway surfacing and a wheelchair-accessible bench.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Keep your recommendations practical, phased, and cost-conscious (e.g. a 4-week pilot, staff rota adjustments, or applying for a local council grant).
🔄 Quick Check: Citing clinical benefits, CQC inspection standards, and cost mitigation proves to the examiner that you understand how real workplace proposals succeed.
Question 6 (Level 2 Mastery Challenge — 4 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
You are an Apprentice Coordinator at an engineering firm. Draft a 3-paragraph email to Department Heads proposing a 'Buddy Mentorship Scheme' where experienced technicians mentor first-year apprentices for one hour a week.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Paragraph 1: State purpose of email and current challenge (first-year apprentice workshop errors and confidence). [M1]
2. Paragraph 2: Explain how the 1-hour weekly buddy check-in improves safety compliance and speeds up skill acquisition. [M1]
3. Paragraph 3: Request each Department Head to nominate two senior technicians by Friday. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: To: Department Heads
Subject: Proposal – First-Year Apprentice Buddy Mentorship Scheme

Dear Colleagues,

I am writing to propose launching a structured Buddy Mentorship Scheme pairing each of our eight first-year engineering apprentices with an experienced workshop technician for one hour per week.

Providing dedicated on-the-job guidance during the first six months will reinforce machinery safety protocols, reduce component wastage, and accelerate apprentice progression toward independent work.

Please could each Department Head nominate two experienced technicians from your team by Friday 23 October so we can schedule a brief 20-minute mentor briefing next week?

Kind regards,
Samira Patel, Apprentice Coordinator
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Failing to proofread spelling, punctuation, and grammar after completing the draft.
🔄 Quick Check: Notice how the email addresses what Department Heads care about: workshop safety, reduced material wastage, and minimal time disruption.
