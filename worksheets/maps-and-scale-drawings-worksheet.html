Examiner Rule for Maps, Scale Ratios & Floor Plans: Golden Rule 1: Drawing to Real Life → MULTIPLY by Scale Factor: A ratio scale of 1 : 50 means 1 cm on the plan represents 50 cm (0.5 m) in reality. To find the real-world size: multiply the drawing measurement by the scale number, then convert cm to metres (÷ 100) or kilometres (÷ 100,000).
