Official Level 1 Specification & Scope (L1.N.14)

Regulated Ofqual Functional Skills Mathematics Level 1 learning outcomes (Open Awards, Pearson Edexcel, City & Guilds, NCFE & AQA):

  • Find percentages of quantities in multiples of 5% (e.g. 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, 50%, 75%).
  • Calculate percentage increases and decreases (discounts, VAT at 20%, pay rises) in multiples of 5%.
  • Use the 10% and 5% building-block method on non-calculator papers and (Percentage ÷ 100) × Amount on calculator papers.

Core Level 1 Golden Rules: Percentages and Multiples of 5% (Level 1)

Golden Rule 1: Master the 10% and 5% Building Blocks

Without a calculator: find 10% by dividing by 10, then halve 10% to get 5%. Add them together for 15%, or double 10% for 20%!

Level 1 Key Rule: 15% of £80: 10% = £8, 5% = £4 → £12
Golden Rule 2: Increase = ADD, Discount/Decrease = SUBTRACT

Read carefully! A 20% discount means subtract 20% from the price; 20% VAT means add 20% onto the price.

Level 1 Key Rule: £150 minus 20% (£30) = £120
Golden Rule 3: Calculator Formula: (Percentage ÷ 100) × Total

With a calculator, find 35% of £260 cleanly as (35 ÷ 100) × 260 = £91.

Level 1 Key Rule: 35 ÷ 100 × 260 = £91

Core Skill 1: The Non-Calculator 10% & 5% Building-Block Method

Build any multiple of 5% rapidly using 10% (÷10) and 5% (half of 10%).

Find 10% of £140
140 ÷ 10 = £14.00
Find 5% of £140
Half of £14.00 = £7.00
Combine for 15% of £140
£14.00 + £7.00 = £21.00

Vocational Context: Electrical Wholesaler Trade Discount vs Voucher

A toolkit normally costs £160. Supplier A offers 15% off the normal price. Supplier B offers £25 off the normal price. Which supplier is cheaper, and by how much?

Common Level 1 Examiner Traps to Avoid

Level 1 Examiner Trap: Stopping at the Discount Amount
Writing '£24' as the final price instead of subtracting £160 − £24 = £136.
Level 1 Examiner Trap: Dividing by 15 to Find 15%
15% does NOT mean ÷15! Always divide by 100 (or build from 10% + 5%).

Interactive Level 1 Exam Practice Questions

Level 1 Practice 1: Laptop 20% VAT Calculation 2 Marks
A refurbished office monitor costs £120 before VAT. Add 20% VAT to find the total price.
Level 1 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
Find 20% of £120 (10% = £12, 20% = £24) (M1): 20% = £24
Add VAT to £120 (A1): £120 + £24 = £144
Final Answer: £144
Level 1 Practice 2: Staff Travel Pass 35% Saving 2 Marks
An annual bus pass costs £480. Staff receive a 35% discount. How much do staff pay?
Level 1 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
Find 35% of £480: (35 ÷ 100) × 480 = £168 (M1): Discount = £168
Subtract from £480 (A1): £480 − £168 = £312
Final Answer: £312
