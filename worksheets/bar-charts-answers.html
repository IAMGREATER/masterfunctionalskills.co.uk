Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Procedural Fluency Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Reading & Comparing Dual Bar Chart Frequencies) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A dual bar chart records urgent blood test requests across three days:
• Monday: Morning = 45, Afternoon = 30
• Tuesday: Morning = 52, Afternoon = 38
• Wednesday: Morning = 48, Afternoon = 42

Calculate the difference between total Morning requests and total Afternoon requests across the three days.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Total Morning = 45 + 52 + 48 = 145; Total Afternoon = 30 + 38 + 42 = 110 [M1]
2. Difference: 145 - 110 = 35 requests [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 35 requests
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Comparing only one day instead of summing all three days first.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: (45-30) + (52-38) + (48-42) = 15 + 14 + 6 = 35.
Question 2 (Level 1 Foundation — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
On a bar chart vertical axis, major grid lines are labelled 0, 20, 40, 60, 80, with 4 equal subdivisions between each major label. A bar reaches 3 subdivisions above 40. What value does this bar represent?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Value of 1 subdivision = (20 − 0) ÷ 4 = 5 units. [M1]
2. Bar height = 40 + (3 × 5) = 40 + 15 = 55. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 55
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Reading 27 tonnes instead of 27 − 12 = 15 tonnes for the middle segment.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Divide the jump (20) by the number of spaces (4) to get 5 per line.
Section B: Workplace & Functional Scenarios Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Hospital Surgical Department Dual Bar Chart Audit (Level 1/2 Foundation)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A dual bar chart compares Scheduled vs Emergency operations. In October, Scheduled is 85 and Emergency is 40. In November, Scheduled is 70 and Emergency is 55.
(a) Compare the total number of operations in October and November.
(b) Calculate the percentage increase in Emergency operations from October to November.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Sum Scheduled + Emergency for each month: October Total = 85 + 40 = 125 operations. | November Total = 70 + 55 = 125 operations. | Both months had the exact same total (125 operations). [M1]
2. Find the increase in Emergency operations: Increase = 55 − 40 = 15 operations. [M1]
3. Divide by October original (40) and multiply by 100: (15 ÷ 40) × 100 = 37.5%. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) Equal total of 125 operations in both months | (b) 37.5% increase
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Always do: Top of Coloured Block − Bottom of Coloured Block.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Always divide the change (15) by the ORIGINAL starting month (40), never the new month (55).
Question 4 (Workplace Application — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A bar chart shows clinic attendances over 4 days: Monday: 42, Tuesday: 56, Wednesday: 38, Thursday: 60. Calculate the mean daily attendance across the 4 days.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Total = 42 + 56 + 38 + 60 = 196 patients. [M1]
2. Mean = 196 ÷ 4 = 49 patients per day. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 49 patients per day
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Drawing two sets of bars without a key showing which shading represents which group loses 1 mark automatically.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Extract all four bar heights first, sum them, and divide by 4.
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Problem Solving & Justification Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Stacked Recycling Waste Bar Chart Extraction & Target Check (Level 2 Multi-Step)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A stacked (component) bar chart shows weekly hospital waste in tonnes across General Waste (bottom section), Recycling (middle section), and Clinical Waste (top section):
• For Week 1, the General Waste section goes from 0 to 12 tonnes, the Recycling section goes from 12 to 27 tonnes, and the Clinical Waste section goes from 27 to 30 tonnes.
(a) How many tonnes of Recycling waste were collected in Week 1?
(b) Did Recycling achieve the sustainability target of at least 45% of total Week 1 waste?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Subtract bottom of Recycling segment from top of Recycling segment: Recycling = 27 − 12 = 15 tonnes. [M1 / A1]
2. Identify total Week 1 waste from the top of the stacked bar: Total waste = 30 tonnes. [B1]
3. Calculate Recycling percentage and compare with 45% target: (15 ÷ 30) × 100 = 50%. | Yes, 50% exceeds the 45% sustainability target. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) 15 tonnes of Recycling | (b) Yes, Recycling was 50% of total waste (> 45% target)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Always draw a small box labelled "Key" in the corner of your chart.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: In a stacked bar chart, NEVER read the top of a middle segment (27) as its value—always subtract the base of that segment (27 − 12 = 15)!
Question 6 (Level 2 Multi-Step Problem Solving — 3 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
In a component (stacked) bar chart for staff transport, a bar reaches a total height of 80 staff. Car drivers occupy 0 to 44, Bus users occupy 44 to 68, and Cyclists occupy 68 to 80. How many more staff travel by Bus than by Bicycle?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Bus users = 68 − 44 = 24 staff. [M1]
2. Cyclists = 80 − 68 = 12 staff. [P1]
3. Difference = 24 − 12 = 12 more staff. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 12 more staff
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Stating a conclusion without showing the full numerical comparison values.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Find the size of each stacked segment by subtracting its bottom reading from its top reading.
