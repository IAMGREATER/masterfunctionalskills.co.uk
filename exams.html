REMOTE PROCTORING

How Your At-Home Online Exam Works

We offer two accredited online examination formats depending on your technical setup and preferences.

1-to-1 Live Human Invigilation

Open Awards Certified

A friendly human invigilator connects with you via Zoom to verify your ID, check your room environment, and guide you through the initial setup before your exam begins.

  • Compatible with PC, Mac, and Chromebooks.
  • Ideal for candidates who appreciate human reassurance.
  • Results delivered within 6 to 16 working days.

Fast-Track Remote Proctoring

TQUK / Saras System

Asynchronous secure browser testing. Your webcam, audio, and screen are recorded and reviewed by compliance examiners, enabling 24/7 on-demand scheduling.

  • Fastest results in the UK: Verified results in 2 to 6 working days.
  • Guaranteed 2-day turnaround add-on available.
  • Requires Windows PC or Laptop with webcam.
