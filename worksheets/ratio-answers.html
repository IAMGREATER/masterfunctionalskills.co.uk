Ratio Pearson Edexcel: L1.11 • L2.11
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Core Skills (Warm-Up) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Level 1 Simplifying Ratios) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Simplify the ratio 24 : 36 to its simplest form.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Identify HCF (12) or divide by 2, 3, 4: 24 ÷ 12 = 2, 36 ÷ 12 = 3 [M1]
2. State in simplest form: 2 : 3 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 2 : 3
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Reversing the terms (3 : 2) or stopping at 12 : 18.
🔄 Quick Check: 2 × 12 = 24; 3 × 12 = 36.
Question 2 (Level 1 Unitary Form 1 : n) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Express the ratio 5 : 18 in the form 1 : n.
Give n as a decimal.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Divide both terms by 5: 5 ÷ 5 = 1; 18 ÷ 5 = 3.6 [M1]
2. State ratio: 1 : 3.6 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1 : 3.6
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Dividing 5 by 18 to get 1 : 0.27.
🔄 Quick Check: 3.6 × 5 = 18.
Section B: Everyday & Workplace Maths Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 Chemical Disinfectant Mixing) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Hospital infection control guidelines state that surgical surface disinfectant must be mixed with water in the ratio 1 : 7 (disinfectant concentrate to water).
A domestic services assistant prepares 32 litres of ready-to-use cleaning solution.

How many litres of disinfectant concentrate and how many litres of water are needed?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Total parts: 1 + 7 = 8 parts [M1]
2. Value of 1 part: 32 L ÷ 8 = 4 litres [P1]
3. Water: 7 × 4 L = 28 litres. Concentrate: 1 × 4 L = 4 litres [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 4 litres concentrate, 28 litres water
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Dividing 32 by 7 instead of the total 8 parts.
🔄 Quick Check: 4 L + 28 L = 32 L; 4 : 28 = 1 : 7.
Question 4 (Level 2 Three-Part Capital Allocation) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A community diagnostic hub divides an annual medical equipment grant of £84,000 between Radiology, Cardiology, and Pathology in the ratio 5 : 4 : 3.

How much money is allocated to Cardiology?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Total parts: 5 + 4 + 3 = 12 parts [M1]
2. Value of 1 part: £84,000 ÷ 12 = £7,000 [P1]
3. Cardiology (4 parts): 4 × £7,000 = £28,000 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £28,000
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Calculating Radiology (5 parts = £35,000) or Pathology (3 parts = £21,000) instead of Cardiology.
🔄 Quick Check: £35,000 + £28,000 + £21,000 = £84,000.
Section C: Exam-Style Problem Solving Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Apprentice Mortar Mixing Ratio) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A bricklayer mixes mortar using cement, lime, and building sand in the ratio 1 : 2 : 9 by weight.
The bricklayer has 25 kg of cement, 45 kg of lime, and 240 kg of building sand in their workshop.

Calculate the maximum total weight of mortar the bricklayer can mix, and identify which ingredient runs out first.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Check maximum batches per ingredient: Cement: 25 ÷ 1 = 25 batches; Lime: 45 ÷ 2 = 22.5 batches; Sand: 240 ÷ 9 = 26.66 batches [M1]
2. Identify limiting ingredient: Lime allows only 22.5 batches (runs out first) [P1]
3. Total parts in formula: 1 + 2 + 9 = 12 parts [P1]
4. Calculate total mortar weight: 22.5 batches × 12 = 270 kg [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 270 kg of mortar; Lime runs out first
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Using cement (25 batches × 12 = 300 kg), which would require 50 kg of lime (exceeding the available 45 kg).
🔄 Quick Check: 22.5 kg cement + 45 kg lime + 202.5 kg sand = 270 kg. All within available stock.
Question 6 (Level 2 Staff Ratio Compliance Justification) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A specialist dementia care wing has a statutory safety policy stating that the ratio of qualified nurses to healthcare care assistants must be at least 1 : 4 (i.e. at least 1 nurse for every 4 care assistants).
On a weekend night shift, the ward is staffed with 3 nurses and 14 care assistants.
The shift coordinator states: 'We are in full compliance with the statutory staffing ratio.'

Is the shift coordinator correct?
Show your calculations clearly and state a definitive conclusion.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate maximum care assistants allowed for 3 nurses: 3 × 4 = 12 care assistants [M1]
2. Or calculate required nurses for 14 care assistants: 14 ÷ 4 = 3.5 nurses (requires 4 nurses) [P1]
3. Compare actual staffing: 14 care assistants exceeds the maximum allowed (12) for 3 nurses (actual ratio is 3 : 14 = 1 : 4.67) [M1]
4. Conclude: No / Incorrect; the ward is NOT compliant. 3 nurses can safely oversee a maximum of 12 assistants, so having 14 assistants breaches the safety ratio (short by 1 nurse or has 2 assistants too many). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Incorrect (Breaches ratio; 3 nurses can only oversee 12 assistants; requires 4 nurses for 14 assistants)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Assuming 3 : 14 is compliant because 3 is a large number of nurses.
🔄 Quick Check: 3 : 12 = 1 : 4 (compliant); 3 : 14 = 1 : 4.67 (non-compliant).
