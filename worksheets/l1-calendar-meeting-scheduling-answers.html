Calendar Management and Meeting Scheduling — Mark Scheme

3. Communicating • Standard Code: L1.3.2
Style 2: Solutions
Benchmark Model Solution:
1. Title: Emergency Team Briefing. 2. Date and Time: Tomorrow's date, 11:30 AM to 12:00 PM. 3. Location / Channel: Online Microsoft Teams Meeting (link generated). 4. Attendees: Email addresses of all required team members, with an alert reminder set for 15 minutes prior.
Task Assessment Criteria & Expected Candidate Performance Marks
Task 1 Clear title, correct date, and start/end time. [1 Mark]
Task 2 Virtual location (Teams) and attendee email addresses with reminder. [1 Mark]
Tutor Abdul's Marking Tip: In Level 1 exams, calendar questions check: Date/Time, Location, Recurrence, and Reminders.
