Identifying Phishing and Social Engineering — Answer Key

5. Being Safe and Responsible Online • Standard Code: L1.5.2
Style 1: Solutions
Task 1 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

How can you check where a link in an email really leads BEFORE clicking on it?

A. Hover your mouse cursor over the link to preview the destination URL (CORRECT)
B. Click on it as fast as possible
C. Print the email onto paper
D. Turn off your computer monitor
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Hovering reveals the target destination address without executing code or visiting the site.

Task 2 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What is 'Smishing'?

A. A phishing attack delivered via SMS text message to a mobile phone (CORRECT)
B. A physical robbery at an office
C. A tool for compressing video files
D. A type of printer ink
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Smishing combines SMS and Phishing to trick mobile phone users via text message.

Task 3 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What should you do if you receive a suspicious email in your workplace inbox?

A. Do not click links or open attachments; report it to your IT department and delete it (CORRECT)
B. Reply with your password to verify your identity
C. Forward it to all your personal social media accounts
D. Download all attachments to your desktop
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Never engage with phishing emails; reporting them allows IT teams to block the sender across the network.
