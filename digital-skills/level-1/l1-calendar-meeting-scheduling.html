Step-by-Step Teaching & Key Concepts

Core Knowledge
1
Creating a Calendar Event / Meeting

A digital calendar replaces wall planners and paper diaries, synchronising across your computer, phone, and tablet:

  1. Open your Calendar app and click New Event (or double-click the required time slot).
  2. Title: Give it a specific, clear name (e.g. 'Quarterly Health & Safety Review').
  3. Date & Time: Set the exact date, start time, and duration (e.g. 10:00 - 11:00 AM).
  4. Location: Enter the physical room (e.g. 'Room 2B') or toggle Teams / Online Meeting to generate a virtual video link automatically.
New Appointment: Ctrl + Shift + A • New Meeting Request: Ctrl + Shift + Q
2
Inviting Attendees and Tracking Responses

Type colleagues' names or email addresses into the Required Attendees box:

  • When you click Send, an invitation is delivered to their inbox.
  • Attendees can click Accept (adds event to their calendar), Tentative, or Decline.
  • The meeting organiser can open the event anytime to view the Tracking tab to see who is attending.
Calendar Meeting Invite Window
Title: Clinical Governance Meeting
Attendees: dr.patel@clinic.org, nurse.sarah@clinic.org
24 Nov 2026 14:00 - 15:30
Reminder: 15 minutes before
3
Recurring Events and Reminders

For ongoing tasks:

  • Recurrence: Set events to repeat Weekly (e.g. team catch-up every Monday), Monthly, or Annually.
  • Reminders: Set an alert pop-up (e.g. 15 minutes or 1 day before) so you and your attendees never miss a deadline.

Accredited Model

Exam Scenario Prompt: A clinic manager needs to arrange a monthly staff training session that occurs on the first Monday of every month from 09:00 to 10:30 in Conference Room A. Explain three settings the manager must configure when creating this calendar appointment.
Step 1: Set Recurrence [B1]
Set the recurrence pattern to 'Monthly on the first Monday'.
Tutor Marking Rationale: Automates repeat scheduling across future months.
Step 2: Set Duration & Location [B1]
Set start time to 09:00, end time to 10:30, and location to 'Conference Room A'.
Tutor Marking Rationale: Specifies time boundaries and venue.
Step 3: Add Attendees & Reminder [B1]
Add staff email addresses to Required Attendees and set a 15-minute advance reminder alert.
Tutor Marking Rationale: Dispatches invitations and ensures automated notification.
Tutor Abdul's Exam Golden Rule: In Level 1 exams, calendar questions check: Date/Time, Location, Recurrence, and Reminders.
