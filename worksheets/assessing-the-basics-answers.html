Section A: Core Skills (The Basics) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Correcting Homophones, Apostrophes & Subject-Verb Agreement) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Read this flawed sentence from a clinic reception notice:
'All patients must bring there appointment letter to the desk, as the list of afternoon clinics are already full.'
Rewrite the sentence correcting both errors.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Correct homophone error: 'there' → 'their' (possessive) [A1]
2. Correct singular collective subject-verb agreement: 'the list... are' → 'the list... is' [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: All patients must bring their appointment letter to the desk, as the list of afternoon clinics is already full.
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Changing 'clinics' instead of changing 'are' to 'is' to agree with singular 'the list'.
🔄 Quick Check: Test: 'their appointment letter' (belonging to them) and 'the list is full'.
Question 2 (Core Skill Application — 3 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Insert the missing punctuation and correct the homophone in this sentence:
'The nurses station requires three items sanitiser gloves and aprons however its currently out of stock.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Possessive apostrophe: 'nurses' station' (or 'nurse's station') [1 Mark]. [M1]
2. Colon and list commas: '...requires three items: sanitiser, gloves, and aprons' [1 Mark]. [M1]
3. Sentence boundary & contraction: '; however, it's (or it is) currently out of stock.' [1 Mark]. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'The nurses' station requires three items: sanitiser, gloves, and aprons; however, it is currently out of stock.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Writing 'we should of ordered more PPE' is a spoken phonetic habit that heavily damages SPaG marks. The correct modal verb phrase is always 'should have'.
🔄 Quick Check: Here 'its currently out of stock' means 'it is currently out of stock', so expand it to 'it is' (or 'it's') and precede 'however' with a semicolon or full stop.
Section B: Real-Life & Workplace Practice Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Homophone and Apostrophe Proofreading Drill (Level 1)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Read this handover note and correct three errors in homophones or apostrophes:
'Please tell the visitors that there permits are at reception. The childrens play area is closed because it's floor is being polished.' [3 Marks]
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Fix Homophone Error (there vs their): 'there permits' refers to permits belonging to the visitors, so it must be spelled 'their permits'. [A1]
2. Fix Irregular Plural Possessive Apostrophe: 'childrens play area' requires an apostrophe before the s because 'children' is already plural: 'children's play area'. [A1]
3. Fix Possessive Pronoun 'its': 'it's floor' means 'it is floor', which is incorrect. Possessive 'its' has no apostrophe: 'its floor'. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'Please tell the visitors that their permits are at reception. The children's play area is closed because its floor is being polished.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Always write 'should have', 'could have', and 'would have' in full.
🔄 Quick Check: Because 'children', 'men', and 'women' are already plural without an 's', the apostrophe always goes BEFORE the 's' ('children's', 'women's').
Question 4 (Exam-Style Task — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Correct the three spellingerrors in this workplace sentence:
'We definately recomend booking overnight accomodation before the conference.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. 'definately' -> 'definitely' (spelled with an 'i', from finite) [1 Mark]. [M1]
2. 'recomend' -> 'recommend' (one c, double m) [1 Mark]. [M1]
3. 'accomodation' -> 'accommodation' (double c, double m) [1 Mark]. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'We definitely recommend booking overnight accommodation before the conference.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Writing 'Two lorry's arrived with ten box's of apples' is incorrect because nobody owns anything — they are simple plurals.
🔄 Quick Check: Definitely, recommend, and accommodation are three of the most frequently tested spellings across all awarding bodies.
Section C: Exam-Style Tasks Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Proofreading and Editing an Incident Log Entry (Level 2)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Identify and correct four SPaG errors in this incident report entry:
'On friday evening, the night shift nurses was delayed because there minibus broke down. The care assistants done their best, but it's battery was completely flat.' [4 Marks]
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Capitalization & Subject-Verb Agreement: 'friday' must be capitalized as 'Friday', and plural 'nurses was delayed' must be 'nurses were delayed'. [A1]
2. Homophone Correction (There vs Their): 'there minibus' must be corrected to possessive 'their minibus'. [A1]
3. Non-Standard Past Tense Verb (Done vs Did): 'The care assistants done their best' is non-standard; correct to 'did their best'. [A1]
4. Possessive Pronoun Apostrophe (It's vs Its): 'it's battery' must be corrected to possessive 'its battery' (no apostrophe). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 'On Friday evening, the night shift nurses were delayed because their minibus broke down. The care assistants did their best, but its battery was completely flat.'
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Never use an apostrophe just to make a word plural: write 'lorries' and 'boxes'.
🔄 Quick Check: Notice 'it's' with an apostrophe means 'it is'. A minibus's battery cannot be 'it is battery', so 'its' without an apostrophe is always right for possession.
Question 6 (Level 2 Mastery Challenge — 2 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Choose the correct verb form in brackets for each sentence:
(a) 'The box of sterile syringes (is / are) stored in the locked cabinet.'
(b) 'Neither the supervisor nor the operatives (was / were) informed of the delay.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Sentence (a): The head noun of the subject is singular ('The box'), not 'syringes', so the verb must be singular: 'is' [1 Mark]. [M1]
2. Sentence (b): With 'neither... nor', the verb agrees with the closer plural noun ('operatives'), so the verb is plural: 'were' [1 Mark]. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) is ('The box... is stored'). | (b) were ('...the operatives were informed').
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Failing to proofread spelling, punctuation, and grammar after completing the draft.
🔄 Quick Check: In sentence (a), cross out the prepositional phrase 'of sterile syringes' — you are left with 'The box is stored', which makes the singular subject obvious!
