Golden Rules for Tally Charts & Frequency Tables

Golden Rule 1: The Gate of 5 Tally System (4 Vertical + 1 Diagonal Slash)

Draw four upright lines |||| and cross the 5th line diagonally through them. Never draw 5 upright lines together—grouping in fives lets you count 5, 10, 15, 20 at a glance.

Golden Rule / Method: 1 Gate (||||) = 5 • Count in 5s, Then Add Loose Strokes
Golden Rule 2: Sum of Frequencies MUST Equal the Total Raw Data Count

Cross off each item in the raw list as you tally it. When finished, add up the Frequency column—if there were 30 items in the list, your frequencies must add up to 30!

Golden Rule / Method: Sum of Frequency Column = Total Items in Raw List

Core Skill 1: Converting Raw Data Lists into Tallies Without Missing Items

The cross-off-and-tally method for 100% accuracy.

  1. Read the raw list from left to right, putting a neat slash through each word as you mark its tally stroke.
  2. On every 5th mark in a row, draw the diagonal gate line.
  3. Write the ordinary number (e.g. 14) in the Frequency column.
Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 2: Finding Missing Tallies & Frequencies from a Known Total

Working backwards when one row in a tally chart is torn or blank.

If a survey of 50 staff has four rows and the first three frequencies are 16, 12, and 9:

  • Add known frequencies: 16 + 12 + 9 = 37.
  • Subtract from 50: Missing frequency = 50 − 37 = 13 (which is drawn as 2 gates of 5 + 3 vertical lines: |||| |||| |||).
Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 3: Identifying the Mode & Proportions from a Tally Table

Answering follow-up analysis questions on tally charts.

  • Most Popular (Mode): The category name with the highest frequency.
  • Fraction / Percentage of Total: Divide the category’s frequency by the Total Frequency.

Vocational Context: Hospital Travel Plan Audit

An outpatient receptionist tallies how 60 patients travelled to the clinic:
• Car: |||| |||| |||| |||| |||| ||
• Bus: |||| |||| |||| |
• Walk/Cycle: |||| |||| ||
• Taxi: ||||
(a) Find the frequency for each mode of transport and verify the total is 60.
(b) The hospital awards a green travel grant if at least 45% of patients travel by Bus or Walk/Cycle combined. Does the clinic qualify?
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Convert all four tallies to numbers and check sum
Car = 27 | Bus = 16 | Walk/Cycle = 12 | Taxi = 5.
Total = 27 + 16 + 12 + 5 = 60.
Criteria: M1 / A1: All 4 frequencies accurate
Step 2 Combine Bus + Walk/Cycle frequencies
16 + 12 = 28 patients.
Criteria: M1: Combined green travel count (28)
Step 3 Calculate percentage of 60 and compare with 45%
(28 ÷ 60) × 100 = 46.7%.
Yes, 46.7% is above the 45% threshold, so the clinic qualifies.
Criteria: A1: 46.7% and valid Yes conclusion
Final Accredited Answer: (a) Car: 27, Bus: 16, Walk/Cycle: 12, Taxi: 5 | (b) Yes, 46.7% used Bus or Walk/Cycle (> 45%)
Method Checklist & Marking Scheme Guidance
Always convert every tally row into digits before doing percentage calculations.

Critical Examiner Traps & Common Pitfalls

Examiner Pitfall: Drawing 5 Vertical Lines + 1 Slash (= 6) Instead of 4 Vertical Lines + 1 Slash (= 5)
Drawing ||||| and then crossing it makes 6 strokes instead of 5!
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Chant: "1, 2, 3, 4, shut the gate on 5."
Examiner Pitfall: Leaving the Frequency Column Blank
Completing the tally column but forgetting to write the numerical digits in the Frequency column loses 1 mark.
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Check that both the Tally column and the Frequency column are filled in.

Interactive Exam Practice Questions

Exam Practice Question 1 2 Marks
A tally chart shows |||| |||| |||| |||| |||| for PPE orders on Tuesday. What is the numerical frequency, and how many more orders are needed to reach 30?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. 4 gates of 5 + 4 strokes = 20 + 4 = 24.
2. Needed to reach 30: 30 − 24 = 6 more orders.
Final Correct Answer: Frequency = 24; 6 more orders needed
Examiner Tip: 4 × 5 = 20, plus 4 = 24.
Exam Practice Question 2 2 Marks
How should a frequency of 17 be drawn as a tally?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. 17 ÷ 5 = 3 full gates of 5 (15) with 2 remainder.
2. Tally: |||| |||| |||| ||.
Final Correct Answer: 3 gates of 5 and 2 vertical strokes (|||| |||| |||| ||)
Examiner Tip: 3 × 5 = 15, plus 2 = 17.
Exam Practice Question 3 3 Marks
A staff satisfaction survey of 50 employees records: Very Satisfied: 22, Satisfied: 19, Neutral: 6, Dissatisfied: ?
Find the frequency for Dissatisfied and calculate what percentage of staff were either Very Satisfied or Satisfied.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Known sum = 22 + 19 + 6 = 47.
2. Dissatisfied = 50 − 47 = 3.
3. Very Satisfied + Satisfied = 22 + 19 = 41 out of 50 = 82%.
Final Correct Answer: Dissatisfied = 3; 82% were Very Satisfied or Satisfied
Examiner Tip: Out of 50, multiply by 2 to get a percentage out of 100 (41 × 2 = 82%).
