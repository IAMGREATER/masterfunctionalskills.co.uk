Fractions, Decimals & Percentages (FDP) Conversions Pearson Edexcel: L1.10 • L1.14 • L2.10 • L2.11
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Procedural Fluency Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Non-Calculator FDP Benchmark Conversions) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Complete the following equivalences without a calculator:
(a) Write 3/8 as a decimal.
(b) Write 35% as a fraction in its simplest form.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. (a) Divide 3 by 8: 3.000 ÷ 8 = 0.375 [M1]
2. (b) Write 35/100 and divide top and bottom by 5: 7/20 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) 0.375, (b) 7/20
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Writing 3/8 as 0.38 instead of dividing 3 by 8 (= 0.375).
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 0.375 × 8 = 3; 7 ÷ 20 = 0.35 = 35%.
Question 2 (Ordering Mixed Fractions, Decimals & Percentages) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Arrange these four clinical audit scores in ascending order (from smallest to largest):
68% , 7/10 , 0.66 , 13/20
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Convert all four to decimals: 68% = 0.68; 7/10 = 0.70; 0.66 = 0.66; 13/20 = 0.65 [M1]
2. Order smallest to largest using original values: 13/20, 0.66, 68%, 7/10 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 13/20 (0.65), 0.66, 68% (0.68), 7/10 (0.70)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Listing the converted decimals without writing the original numbers given in the question.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 0.65 < 0.66 < 0.68 < 0.70.
Section B: Workplace & Functional Scenarios Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Hospital Staff Vaccination Uptake Comparison) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Three hospital wards report their seasonal flu vaccination uptake:
• Ward A: 19 out of 25 staff vaccinated
• Ward B: 74% of staff vaccinated
• Ward C: 0.78 of staff vaccinated

Which ward achieved the highest vaccination uptake? Show all three values as percentages.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Convert Ward A to percentage: (19 ÷ 25) × 100 = 76% [M1]
2. Convert Ward C to percentage: 0.78 × 100 = 78% [M1]
3. Compare (Ward A = 76%, Ward B = 74%, Ward C = 78%) → Highest is Ward C [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Ward C (78% vs Ward A 76% and Ward B 74%)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Assuming 19/25 is 19% or 81% without multiplying numerator and denominator by 4.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 78% of 100 > 76% of 100 > 74% of 100.
Question 4 (Retail Supplier Discount Comparison) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A dental practice needs to order £600 worth of nitrile gloves. Three suppliers offer different promotional discounts off the £600 list price:
• Supplier X: 1/6 off the list price
• Supplier Y: 18% discount
• Supplier Z: 0.15 off the list price plus a £15 loyalty voucher

Calculate the cash discount for each supplier and state which supplier gives the biggest saving.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Supplier X saving: £600 ÷ 6 = £100.00 [M1]
2. Supplier Y saving: 0.18 × £600 = £108.00; Supplier Z saving: (0.15 × £600) + £15 = £90 + £15 = £105.00 [P1]
3. Compare savings (£108 > £105 > £100) → Supplier Y gives the biggest saving (£108) [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Supplier Y (£108 saving)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Forgetting to add the £15 loyalty voucher to Supplier Z's 0.15 (£90) discount.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 108 / 600 = 18%; 105 / 600 = 17.5%; 100 / 600 = 16.67%.
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Problem Solving & Justification Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Multi-Stage Budget Allocation & Remaining Fraction) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A community health project receives a grant of £12,000:
• 35% is allocated to mobile screening equipment.
• 3/8 is allocated to specialist nursing hours.
• 0.15 is allocated to printed patient leaflets.
• The remainder is kept as an emergency contingency reserve.

Calculate the exact cash value of the contingency reserve and express it as a fraction of the total grant in its simplest form.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Convert all allocations to decimals/percentages: 35% = 0.35; 3/8 = 0.375; leaflets = 0.15 [P1]
2. Total proportion spent: 0.35 + 0.375 + 0.15 = 0.875 (87.5%); Remaining proportion = 1 - 0.875 = 0.125 [M1]
3. Cash value of contingency reserve: 0.125 × £12,000 = £1,500 [A1]
4. Express 0.125 (or 1,500/12,000) as simplest fraction: 1/8 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £1,500 and 1/8
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Rounding 3/8 (0.375) to 0.38, which causes an error of £60 in the final cash total.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: £4,200 (35%) + £4,500 (3/8) + £1,800 (0.15) + £1,500 (1/8) = £12,000.
Question 6 (Compliance Audit Decision & Justification) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
An apprenticeship provider passes its annual quality audit only if at least 17/20 of learners pass their first exam sitting.
This year, out of 320 learners:
• 0.65 passed in the Autumn window.
• A further 68 learners passed their first sitting in the Spring window.

Did the provider meet the 17/20 benchmark? Show full FDP comparisons to justify your decision.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Convert target 17/20 to percentage: (17 ÷ 20) × 100 = 85% (or 272 learners) [P1]
2. Calculate Autumn passes: 0.65 × 320 = 208 learners [M1]
3. Total passes = 208 + 68 = 276 learners; Achieved percentage = (276 ÷ 320) × 100 = 86.25% [P1]
4. Conclusion: Yes, 86.25% > 85% (or 276 > 272 required passes, exceeding target by 4 learners) [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Yes — achieved 86.25% (276/320), beating the 85% (17/20) benchmark by 1.25% (4 learners).
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Adding 68% instead of 68 learners to 65%.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 208 + 68 = 276; 276 ÷ 320 = 0.8625 > 0.85.
