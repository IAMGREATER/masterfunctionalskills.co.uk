Specification & Learning Outcomes

Candidates must demonstrate mastery of the following regulated Functional Skills Mathematics outcomes:

  • L1.12 / L2.11 – Recognise and use equivalences between common fractions, decimals, and percentages.
  • L2.12 – Order and compare fractions, decimals, and percentages across mixed representations.

Golden Rules for Fractions, Decimals & Percentages (FDP Conversions)

Golden Rule 1: The FDP Conversion Triangle

Percentage to Decimal: Divide by 100. Decimal to Percentage: Multiply by 100. Fraction to Decimal: Top divided by Bottom.

Golden Rule / Method: Fraction → (Top ÷ Bottom) → Decimal → (× 100) → Percentage
Golden Rule 2: Convert All to Decimals or Percentages Before Ordering

When asked to compare mixed values (e.g. 3/8, 0.35, 36%), convert EVERY value into a percentage or 3-digit decimal before comparing.

Golden Rule / Method: 3/8 = 37.5% | 0.35 = 35% | 36% = 36% → 0.35 < 36% < 3/8

Core Skill 2: Skill 2: Converting Any Fraction to a Percentage (Scaling vs Bus-Stop)

Two reliable methods depending on whether the denominator divides into 100.

  • Method A (Scale Denominator to 100): If the denominator is a factor of 100 (2, 4, 5, 10, 20, 25, 50), multiply top and bottom to make the denominator 100: 17/25 = (17 × 4)/(25 × 4) = 68/100 = 68%.
  • Method B (Numerator ÷ Denominator × 100): For denominators like 8 or 16, divide top by bottom using short division: 5/8 = 5.000 ÷ 8 = 0.625 = 62.5%.
Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 3: Skill 3: Converting Decimals & Percentages Back to Simplest Fractions

Writing over 10, 100, or 1,000 and cancelling common factors.

  1. 2-Digit Decimal / Percentage: Write over 100 and divide by HCF: 0.36 = 36% = 36/100 → (÷ 4) = 9/25.
  2. 3-Digit Decimal: Write over 1,000 and simplify: 0.375 = 375/1000 → (÷ 125) = 3/8.
Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Pearson Edexcel Step-by-Step Worked Models

Benchmark Exam Model 1 3 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 1/2)

Vocational Context: Infection Prevention Compliance

Three hospital wards reported their staff hand hygiene compliance rates:
• Ward A: 4/5
• Ward B: 78%
• Ward C: 0.82
(a) Convert all three scores to percentages.
(b) Which ward achieved the highest compliance score?
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Convert Ward A (4/5) to a percentage
4/5 = 8/10 = 80/100 = 80% (or 4 ÷ 5 × 100 = 80%)
Criteria: M1: Fraction to percentage conversion
Step 2 Convert Ward C (0.82) to a percentage
0.82 × 100 = 82%
Criteria: M1: Decimal to percentage conversion
Step 3 Compare all three percentage scores
Ward A = 80%, Ward B = 78%, Ward C = 82%. Since 82% > 80% > 78%, Ward C achieved the highest compliance.
Criteria: A1: Correct comparison and conclusion
Final Accredited Answer: (a) Ward A: 80%, Ward B: 78%, Ward C: 82% | (b) Ward C achieved the highest compliance.
Method Checklist & Marking Scheme Guidance
Converting to percentages makes comparison immediate and avoids fractional common denominator complications.
Benchmark Exam Model 2 4 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 2 Problem Solving)

Vocational Context: Commercial Purchasing Comparison

A dental practice needs to order £600 worth of examination gloves. Three suppliers have identical list prices (£600) but offer different promotional reductions:
• Supplier X: 3/8 off the list price.
• Supplier Y: 36% discount off the list price.
• Supplier Z: Pay only 0.62 of the list price.

Which supplier offers the largest saving, and how much will the practice pay with that supplier?
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Convert all three offers into comparable discount percentages
Supplier X discount: 3/8 = 0.375 = 37.5% off.
Supplier Y discount: 36% off.
Supplier Z: Paying 0.62 (62%) means a discount of 100% - 62% = 38% off.
Criteria: M1: Converting all three offers to a common discount format
Step 2 Identify the supplier with the largest discount
Compare discounts: 38% (Supplier Z) > 37.5% (Supplier X) > 36% (Supplier Y).
Supplier Z gives the largest saving.
Criteria: M1: Correct comparison identifying Supplier Z
Step 3 Calculate final amount payable with Supplier Z
£600 × 0.62 = £372.00 (a saving of £228.00).
Criteria: M1: Calculating exact cost with winning supplier
Step 4 State final conclusion clearly
Supplier Z offers the largest saving (38% off), and the practice will pay £372.00.
Criteria: A1: Correct supplier and £372.00 payable
Final Accredited Answer: Supplier Z offers the largest saving (38% discount vs 37.5% and 36%), with a final price payable of £372.00.
Method Checklist & Marking Scheme Guidance
Read wording traps carefully! Supplier Z says "Pay 0.62 of the price" (meaning 62% paid = 38% discount), whereas X and Y quote the discount directly.

Critical Examiner Traps & Common Pitfalls

Examiner Pitfall: Confusing 0.05 with 50%
0.05 = 5%, whereas 0.5 = 50%. A common error is assuming 0.05 is 50% because of the digit 5.
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Always multiply the decimal by 100: 0.05 × 100 = 5%.

Interactive Exam Practice Questions

Exam Practice Question 1 2 Marks
Arrange in ascending order (smallest to largest): 0.62, 3/5, 65%, 5/8.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Convert all to 3-digit decimals: 0.620; 3/5 = 0.600; 65% = 0.650; 5/8 = 0.625.
2. Sequence: 0.600 (3/5), 0.620 (0.62), 0.625 (5/8), 0.650 (65%).
Final Correct Answer: 3/5, 0.62, 5/8, 65%
Examiner Tip: Write the final answer using the original representations given in the question.
Exam Practice Question 2 2 Marks
Convert 44% into a fraction in its simplest form, and convert 13/20 into a percentage.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. 44% = 44/100. Divide top and bottom by 4 = 11/25.
2. 13/20 = (13 × 5)/(20 × 5) = 65/100 = 65%.
Final Correct Answer: 44% = 11/25 | 13/20 = 65%
Examiner Tip: Always check if your fraction can be divided further by 2, 4, or 5.
Exam Practice Question 3 3 Marks
In a staff survey of 160 employees, 35% travel by train, 3/8 travel by car, and the rest cycle. What decimal proportion of the staff cycle, and how many employees is this?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Train = 35% = 0.35; Car = 3/8 = 0.375.
2. Combined train + car = 0.35 + 0.375 = 0.725.
3. Cycle decimal = 1 - 0.725 = 0.275 (27.5%).
4. Number of cyclists = 0.275 × 160 = 44 employees.
Final Correct Answer: 0.275 (44 employees)
Examiner Tip: All proportions of a whole group must add up to 1 (or 100%).
