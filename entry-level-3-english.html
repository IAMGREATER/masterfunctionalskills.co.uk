Functional Skills English Entry Level 3 — Frequently Asked Questions

What is tested in Functional Skills English Entry Level 3?

Entry Level 3 English assesses three practical components across Open Awards, Pearson Edexcel, City & Guilds, and NCFE: Reading (short everyday emails, notices, instructions, and dictionary/alphabetical skills), Writing & SPaG (short emails, letters, forms, core punctuation, grammar, and Entry 3 spellings), and Speaking, Listening & Communicating (two supportive small-group discussions).

Can I use a dictionary in Entry Level 3 English?

Yes in Reading (in fact, Entry 3 Reading explicitly tests looking up words and alphabetical sequencing under E3.R.11!), whereas dictionaries and spellcheck are not allowed in the Writing component.

What do I move onto after passing Entry Level 3 English?

Entry Level 3 builds the exact foundation needed to progress smoothly onto Functional Skills English Level 1 and then Level 2 (GCSE Equivalent) for apprenticeships, healthcare roles, and university entry.
