Recognizing Tone & Perspective
  • Tone reflects the writer's attitude: Critical, enthusiastic, authoritative, sarcastic, empathetic, cautionary.
  • Formality spectrum: Highly formal (academic/legal) to highly informal (chatty/colloquial).
  • First-person voice ('I', 'we'): Subjective, personal, reflective, direct.
  • Third-person voice ('he', 'she', 'they', 'the organisation'): Objective, professional, detached.
  • Humorous or satirical tone: Uses irony, exaggeration (hyperbole), and wit.
Calculator Strategy: When asked to identify tone, always provide a specific adjective and quote 2 words that create that tone.

Question 3 Question 3: Identifying Tone and Register
Classify the register and tone of each communication (e.g. Formal, Informal, Urgent, Sarcastic, Empathetic):
(a)
'We deeply regret the disruption caused to your morning travel plans.'
(b)
'Hey folks, don't forget it's pizza Friday in the canteen! See ya there!'
(c)
'All personnel must evacuate the premises immediately via marked fire doors.'
(d)
'Oh brilliant, another software update right in the middle of our inventory count.'
(e)
'We appreciate how challenging this bereavement period is for you and your family.'
(f)
'Please find attached the quarterly financial balance sheet for your perusal.'
(g)
'Drop whatever you are doing right now and phone head office immediately.'
(h)
'Can you believe how lucky we got with that last delivery slot? Total lifesaver!'
Question 4 Question 4: Active vs Passive Voice & Professional Distance
Identify whether the sentence is written in the Active or Passive voice:
(a)
The nurse administered the medication at 08:00 sharp.
(b)
The medication was administered at 08:00 sharp by the nurse.
(c)
Management investigated the breach of safety protocol.
(d)
The safety breach was investigated by senior management.
(e)
Ten pallets of medical PPE were delivered to the loading bay.
(f)
The driver delivered ten pallets of medical PPE to the loading bay.
(g)
Mistakes were made during the initial shift handover.
(h)
The night team made mistakes during the shift handover.
Question 5 Question 5: Analyzing Writer's Voice & Personality
Describe the persona or voice projected by the writer in each extract:
(a)
'As a veteran paramedic of twenty years, I urge parents to secure car seats properly.'
(b)
'I was completely terrified when the computer screen went black, but let me tell you what happened next!'
(c)
'Our corporate mission is to pioneer synergy across multifaceted stakeholder matrices.'
(d)
'Take a deep breath. You're not alone, and together we will take this recovery one day at a time.'
(e)
'No excuses, no shortcuts: wear your hard hat on site or leave immediately.'
(f)
'Ever wondered what goes on behind the scenes at a busy Michelin-starred restaurant? Let's take a peek.'
(g)

(h)
'We messed up our delivery schedule yesterday, and we are working flat out to make things right.'
