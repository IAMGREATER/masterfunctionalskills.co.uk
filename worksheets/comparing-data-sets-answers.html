Section A: Core Skills (Warm-Up) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Comparing Consistency Using the Range) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Two pathology couriers record their daily delivery times (in minutes):
• Courier A: Mean = 34 mins, Range = 8 mins
• Courier B: Mean = 31 mins, Range = 22 mins

State which courier is faster on average and which courier is more consistent, giving a numerical reason for each.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Faster on average: Courier B because their mean delivery time is lower (31 mins < 34 mins) [M1]
2. More consistent: Courier A because their range is smaller (8 mins < 22 mins) [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Faster: Courier B (mean 31 mins). More consistent: Courier A (smaller range of 8 mins).
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Claiming a higher range means higher consistency (a smaller range always means more consistent).
🔄 Quick Check: Lower mean time = faster speed; smaller range = tighter consistency.
Question 2 (Level 1 Foundation — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Depot A: Mean daily parcels = 420, Range = 45. Depot B: Mean daily parcels = 465, Range = 130. Write two sentences comparing the daily parcel deliveries of Depot A and Depot B.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Average: Depot B delivers more parcels per day on average (mean 465 vs 420). [M1]
2. Consistency: Depot A has more consistent daily delivery numbers because its range is smaller (45 vs 130). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Depot B has a higher average (465 vs 420), but Depot A is more consistent (smaller range of 45 vs 130).
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Writing "Day Shift has a smaller range of 4%" without interpreting what a smaller range means in real life loses the interpretation mark.
🔄 Quick Check: Always write one sentence for the mean and one sentence for the range.
Section B: Everyday & Workplace Maths Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Hospital Shift Medication Dispensing Accuracy Comparison (Level 2 Core)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A clinical audit compares dispensing accuracy rates across Day and Night shifts:
• Day Shift: Mean = 96.5%, Range = 4.0%
• Night Shift: Mean = 94.0%, Range = 9.0%
Compare the dispensing accuracy of the two shifts using both the mean and the range.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Compare the Mean in context: Day Shift had a higher average dispensing accuracy than Night Shift (mean 96.5% vs 94.0%). [A1]
2. Compare the Range in terms of consistency: Day Shift was more consistent than Night Shift because its range was smaller (4.0% vs 9.0%). [A1]
3. State overall recommendation: Day Shift performed better both on average accuracy and consistency. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Day Shift had higher average accuracy (mean 96.5% vs 94.0%) and was more consistent (smaller range of 4.0% vs 9.0%).
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Always add the words "...which means their performance is more consistent."
🔄 Quick Check: Always include the actual numbers (96.5% vs 94.0% and 4.0% vs 9.0%) inside your comparison sentences.
Question 4 (Workplace Application — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Nurse A records 5 blood pressure check times (in mins): 6, 9, 5, 12, 8. Nurse B has a median of 7 mins and a range of 4 mins. Calculate the median and range for Nurse A and state which nurse is more consistent.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Nurse A ordered: 5, 6, 8, 9, 12 → Median = 8 mins. [M1]
2. Nurse A Range = 12 − 5 = 7 mins. [P1]
3. Nurse B is more consistent because Nurse B has a smaller range (4 mins vs 7 mins). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Nurse A Median = 8 mins, Range = 7 mins; Nurse B is more consistent (range 4 vs 7 mins)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: You must compare like with like (Mean vs Mean, or Median vs Median).
🔄 Quick Check: Since Nurse B’s summary uses the median, calculate the median for Nurse A.
Section C: Exam-Style Problem Solving Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Two Courier Companies Delivery Time Audit (Raw Data vs Summary) (Level 2 Multi-Step)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A pathology lab tests two courier firms over 6 urgent sample deliveries (time in minutes):
• SwiftMed (Raw times): 28, 35, 24, 31, 42, 26
• BioExpress (Summary): Mean = 34 minutes, Range = 8 minutes.
Calculate the mean and range for SwiftMed, and write two comparisons between the two couriers.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate SwiftMed Mean (Sum ÷ 6): Sum = 28 + 35 + 24 + 31 + 42 + 26 = 186 minutes. | Mean = 186 ÷ 6 = 31 minutes. [M1]
2. Calculate SwiftMed Range (Highest − Lowest): Range = 42 − 24 = 18 minutes. [A1]
3. Compare Averages in context: SwiftMed was faster on average, with a lower mean delivery time (31 mins vs 34 mins). [A1]
4. Compare Consistency using Range: However, BioExpress had more consistent delivery times because its range was smaller (8 mins vs 18 mins). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: SwiftMed Mean = 31 mins, Range = 18 mins. SwiftMed is faster on average (31 vs 34 mins), but BioExpress is more consistent (range 8 vs 18 mins).
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Calculate the same type of average that is given for the second group.
🔄 Quick Check: Notice the trade-off: SwiftMed has a faster average, whereas BioExpress is more consistent!
Question 6 (Level 2 Multi-Step Problem Solving — 3 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Machine X produces bolts with a mean length of 50.0 mm (Range = 0.4 mm). Machine Y produces bolts with a mean length of 50.0 mm (Range = 1.8 mm). If the engineering specification requires bolts to be as close to 50.0 mm as possible, which machine should the factory keep and why?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Both machines have the exact same mean length (50.0 mm). [M1]
2. Machine X has a much smaller range (0.4 mm vs 1.8 mm), meaning its bolt lengths are much more consistent and closer to 50.0 mm. [P1]
3. Keep Machine X. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Keep Machine X because it has a much smaller range (0.4 mm vs 1.8 mm), making bolt lengths far more consistent.
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Stating a conclusion without showing the full numerical comparison values.
🔄 Quick Check: When averages are equal, the range (consistency) decides the winner!
