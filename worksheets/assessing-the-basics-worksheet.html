Examiner Rule for Core Literacy Baseline: Grammar, Punctuation & Spelling: Golden Rule 1: Master the Non-Negotiable Homophone & Apostrophe Trio: Examiners immediately spot errors in: (1) They're / Their / There, (2) You're / Your, and (3) It's / Its. Remember: 'It's' ALWAYS means 'It is' or 'It has'. Possessive 'its' (belonging to it) NEVER takes an apostrophe.
