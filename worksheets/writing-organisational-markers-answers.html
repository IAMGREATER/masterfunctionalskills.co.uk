Organisational Markers & Headings Pearson Edexcel: Pearson Edexcel Functional Skills English (WRITING)
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Foundational Concept Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Writing Informative Subheadings for a Workplace Report) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
You are writing a formal report investigating Staff Car Park Congestion.
Write three professional subheadings to organise the middle and final sections of your report.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Diagnostic/Findings subheadings: e.g. '1. Current Peak-Hour Congestion Data' and '2. Safety & Late-Arrival Impacts' [A1]
2. Action-oriented closing subheading: e.g. '3. Costed Recommendations & Permit Controls' [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1. Current Peak-Hour Congestion Findings | 2. Impact on Clinical Shift Handovers | 3. Proposed Permit & Park-and-Ride Recommendations
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Using vague one-word headings like 'Middle' or 'End' instead of topic-specific subheadings.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Each subheading clearly signposts the exact content of its section.
Question 2 (Core Skill Application — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Rewrite these unformatted notes into a structured workplace safety section with a bold heading and two grammatically complete bullet points:
'Workers need steel toe cap boots on the warehouse floor and also high-vis jackets must be worn at all times near forklift trucks.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Create a clear heading (e.g. Mandatory Warehouse PPE Regulations). [M1]
2. Write an introductory lead-in clause followed by two parallel, complete-sentence bullet points. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Mandatory Warehouse PPE Regulations
All operatives entering the main distribution floor must comply with the following personal protective equipment rules:
• Safety Footwear: Steel toe-cap boots must be worn at all times across all warehouse storage and packing zones.
• High-Visibility Clothing: Fastened high-visibility vests or jackets are mandatory whenever working near active forklift transit lanes.
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: If 80% of your response consists of 3-word bullet points without full sentences or paragraphs, examiners cannot award Level 2 grammar or cohesion marks.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Adding bold lead-ins ('Safety Footwear:', 'High-Visibility Clothing:') makes bullet points look like an authentic workplace policy document.
Section B: Functional Workplace Application Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 Model: Sequenced Fire Evacuation Instruction Notice) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Convert these disorganised notes into a clearly structured staff instruction notice using a bold title, a numbered sequence for the evacuation steps, and a final warning note:
'When you hear the continuous siren leave the building right away by the green fire exits. Don't stop to pick up bags or coats and never use the lift. Walk to Car Park A and wait for the Fire Warden to call your name.'
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Create a clear, authoritative main heading: EMERGENCY FIRE EVACUATION PROCEDURE [A1]
2. Use a numbered list (1 to 3) for the chronological evacuation steps: Because the actions must happen in strict order (Hear alarm → Exit via stairs → Register at assembly point), use numbers 1, 2, 3. [A1]
3. Place the prohibitions in a bold warning callout at the bottom: Highlight the 'Do not use lifts or collect personal belongings' rule clearly. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: EMERGENCY FIRE EVACUATION PROCEDURE
If the continuous fire siren sounds, all staff and visitors must follow these three steps immediately:
1. Stop work at once and leave the building calmly via the nearest illuminated green fire exit.
2. Walk directly to Assembly Point A in the main front car park.
3. Remain in your department group until the Fire Warden has completed the roll call.

IMPORTANT SAFETY WARNING: Never use the passenger lifts during an alarm, and do not return to your desk to collect coats or personal bags.
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Limit bullet points to one specific section (such as Recommendations), and always write full, grammatically complete sentences inside each bullet.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Using numbered steps (1, 2, 3) for chronological actions and a bold warning box for prohibitions makes safety instructions instant to follow.
Question 4 (Exam-Style Task — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Explain whether you should use a numbered list (1, 2, 3) or unordered bullet points (•) for each workplace document below, and give the reason why:
Document A: The four stages of sterilising surgical instruments in an autoclave.
Document B: The four employee benefits offered in a company recruitment leaflet.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Analyse Document A: Sterilising instruments must follow a strict chronological sequence to be safe → Numbered list. [M1]
2. Analyse Document B: Employee benefits are a collection of features with no required time order → Bullet points. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Document A: Use a numbered list (1, 2, 3, 4) because sterilisation steps must be performed in a strict chronological sequence.
Document B: Use unordered bullet points (•) because employee benefits are separate features that do not happen in a sequence.
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Writing the subheading 'Cost Savings' and then spending the paragraph talking about staff parking permits confuses the reader and loses organisation marks.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Numbers = strict sequence where order matters; Bullets = non-sequential collection.
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Exam-Style Analysis & Evaluation Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Model: Hospital Clinical Waste Segregation SOP) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Format a guidance section for hospital domestic and auxiliary staff detailing the 3-tier waste bag colour-coding system (Yellow, Orange, Black) and sharps disposal rule using professional organisational markers.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Create an authoritative section heading and introductory colon clause: Section 2.0: Clinical Waste Segregation Protocols followed by a complete lead-in sentence ending with a colon. [A1]
2. Use bulleted bold lead-ins with complete sentences for the 3 bag colours: Detail Yellow (infectious incineration), Orange (clinical autoclaving), and Black (domestic general waste). [A1]
3. Insert a high-visibility mandatory compliance callout: Add a boxed or bold directive regarding rigid yellow sharps bins. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 2.0 Clinical Waste Segregation Protocols
To maintain strict CQC infection-control compliance, all ward staff must segregate waste according to the three colour-coded categories below:
• Yellow Bags (Highly Infectious Waste): Use exclusively for dressings, swabs, and PPE contaminated with infectious bodily fluids requiring high-temperature incineration.
• Orange Bags (Standard Clinical Waste): Use for non-infectious clinical items such as disposable aprons, wipes, and incontinence pads treated via autoclaving.
• Black Bags (Domestic General Refuse): Use strictly for non-clinical waste including paper towels, food packaging, and clean cardboard.

[CRITICAL SAFETY DIRECTIVE]: Needles, lancets, and scalpel blades must be deposited immediately into rigid yellow BS-approved sharps bins; never place sharps inside plastic waste bags.
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Ensure every sentence beneath a subheading directly addresses the topic promised in that heading.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Notice how every bullet point starts with a bold category label followed by a colon and a complete grammatical sentence. This earns top marks for both layout and SPaG.
Question 6 (Level 2 Mastery Challenge — 4 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
You are compiling the final section of a Level 2 Report titled 'Reducing Patient Waiting Times at Oakfield GP Surgery'. Draft Section 3.0: Recommendations containing a brief lead-in sentence and three bulleted, actionable recommendations with bold lead-ins.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Write heading: '3.0 Strategic Recommendations'. [M1]
2. Write a formal introductory sentence ending with a colon. [M1]
3. Draft three full-sentence bullet points covering online triage, evening nurse clinics, and SMS cancellations. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 3.0 Strategic Recommendations
To reduce average appointment waiting times by 30% over the next quarter, the Practice Partnership should implement the following three measures:
• Digital Triage Portal: Launch an online symptom-checker form from 1 November so routine prescription renewals do not block morning telephone lines.
• Extended Nurse Practitioner Hours: Introduce two evening minor-illness clinics on Tuesdays and Thursdays until 19:30 for working patients.
• Automated SMS Cancellation Links: Send automated text reminders 24 hours prior to appointments, allowing patients to cancel unused slots with a single reply.
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Failing to proofread spelling, punctuation, and grammar after completing the draft.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Notice how each recommendation states the specific initiative, how it works, and the operational benefit.
