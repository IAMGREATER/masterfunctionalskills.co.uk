Document Uploads and Digital Support Portals — Answer Key

4. Transacting • Standard Code: L1.4.3
Style 1: Solutions (6 Drills)
Task 1 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

Why might an online government portal refuse to accept an uploaded photo of your passport?

A. The file size exceeds the maximum limit or the file format is not accepted (e.g. docx instead of pdf/jpg) (CORRECT)
B. The government website is sleeping
C. Passports cannot be stored on computers
D. The internet is only for text, not photos
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Portals reject files that violate strict format (.pdf, .jpg) or file size (MB) constraints.

Task 2 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What should you always retain as evidence after submitting an official document or cancelling a subscription online?

A. The confirmation reference number or confirmation email (CORRECT)
B. A photograph of your computer keyboard
C. Your computer power cable
D. The cardboard box your laptop arrived in
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Confirmation emails and reference numbers act as legal proof of transaction.

Task 3 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

How can you prevent a 30-day free software trial from charging your bank card automatically?

A. Set a calendar reminder and cancel the subscription in account settings before the trial period expires (CORRECT)
B. Throw away your computer monitor
C. Uninstall your web browser
D. Change your desktop colour to black
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Cancelling prior to the renewal date terminates the recurring billing instruction.

Task 4 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

A digital portal for submitting accreditation evidence specifies: 'Upload PDF or JPG files only, maximum 5 MB per file'. You have an 8 MB Word (.docx) document. How must you prepare the file?

A. Save/export the Word file as a compressed PDF ensuring the final size is under 5 MB (CORRECT)
B. Simply rename the file extension from .docx to .pdf in File Explorer
C. Upload the 8 MB file anyway and ignore the portal warning
D. Take a video of the document and upload that instead
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Manually changing the file extension does not convert the file format and will cause an upload failure. You must export/save as PDF using compression to stay below the 5 MB limit.

Task 5 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

After submitting an urgent service request ticket on an IT portal, what reference item should you record to follow up on the status?

A. The unique Ticket Reference Number (e.g. #INC-89421) (CORRECT)
B. The color of the submit button on the website
C. The time your computer monitor was manufactured
D. The office postcode
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

The system-generated ticket reference number is the unique key used by support agents to track, update, and resolve service issues.

Task 6 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

When communicating with an automated customer support AI chatbot, what should you do if the bot fails to resolve your complex issue?

A. Request to be transferred or escalated to a human customer service agent (CORRECT)
B. Keep typing the same sentence 50 times in a row
C. Unplug your office broadband router
D. Leave the chat open forever
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Chatbots are designed to handle standard FAQs. For non-standard or complex issues, modern support portals offer an option to escalate to a live human representative.
