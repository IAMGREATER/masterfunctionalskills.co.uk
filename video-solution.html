Assessment Criteria & Method Marks

[M1] Method Marks
Awarded for correct mathematical process, place value partitioning, column alignment, and clear multi-step reasoning—even if an arithmetic slip occurs.
[A1] Accuracy Marks
Awarded for precisely evaluated answers with correct units (e.g. £, cm², kg, hours), commas, or correct placeholder zeros.

Common Pitfalls Highlighted by Examiners

  • Placeholder Zeros: Missing zero in intermediate columns (e.g. writing 2,45,608 instead of 2,045,608).
  • Unit Omission: Forgetting money symbols (£ or p) or square units (cm², m²).
  • Reverse Sanity Checks: Skipping the reverse operation to verify answers (e.g. subtraction check for addition).
