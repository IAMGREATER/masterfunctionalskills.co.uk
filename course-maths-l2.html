Curriculum Overview

39 Topics Across 3 Core Standards

Full coverage of the Department for Education and Ofqual Subject Content for Functional Skills Maths Level 2.

1. Using Numbers & The Number System

14 Core Topics • Non-Calc & Calc

Master the foundational operations that represent over 40% of your exam marks.

  • Ordering positive and negative numbers
  • Fractions of amounts, addition & mixed numbers
  • Percentages, percentage increase/decrease & reverse percentages
  • Ratio sharing and direct proportion problems
  • Compound interest and AER loan comparisons

2. Measures, Shape & Space

15 Core Topics • Real-World Problem Solving

Navigate workplace scenarios involving dimensions, floorplans, turfing, and packaging.

  • Perimeter, circumference, and compound shape area
  • Volume and surface area of prisms and cylinders
  • Scale drawings, plans, elevations & bearings
  • Metric and imperial unit conversions
  • Compound measures: Speed, density & pressure

3. Handling Information & Data

10 Core Topics • Statistical Analysis

Interpret workplace tables, charts, probability outcomes, and statistical comparisons.

  • Mean, median, mode, and range comparisons
  • Calculating estimated mean from grouped frequency tables
  • Scatter graphs, correlation & lines of best fit
  • Single and combined probability events & sample space
  • Pie charts, bar charts, and frequency polygons

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Functional Skills Maths Level 2 equivalent to a GCSE?

Yes. Functional Skills Maths Level 2 is officially regulated by Ofqual and recognized nationally by universities, the NHS, the College of Policing, and apprenticeship standards as equivalent to a GCSE Grade 4 (Grade C).

How does the Pass Guarantee work?

We are so confident in our structured curriculum that if you complete 100% of the course modules and score at least 80% on our practice mock tests, in the unlikely event you don't pass your official exam, we will cover your exam resit free of charge.

How long do I need to complete the course?

The course is completely self-paced. Many students who need urgent certification for a job or university offer finish the course in 2 to 3 weeks studying 5-10 hours a week. Others spread their revision over 2 to 3 months. You retain 12 full months of course access.
