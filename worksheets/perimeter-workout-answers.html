Question 1 Worked Solution Joinery Skirting Board Installation
[4 Marks Available]
A carpenter is fitting skirting boards around a bedroom measuring 5.4 metres long by 3.8 metres wide. There is one doorway measuring 0.9 metres wide where no skirting board is needed.
Skirting board is sold in 2.4-metre lengths at £14.50 per length.
How many lengths must the carpenter purchase, and what is the total cost?
Calculate total room perimeter: 2 × (5.4 + 3.8) = 2 × 9.2 = 18.4 m
[M1]
Deduct doorway width: 18.4 - 0.9 = 17.5 m of skirting needed
[M1]
Calculate lengths required: 17.5 ÷ 2.4 = 7.29 → rounds UP to 8 lengths
[M1]
Calculate total cost: 8 lengths × £14.50 = £116.00
[A1]
Correct Final Answer: 8 lengths costing £116.00
Quick Check Method: 8 × 2.4 m = 19.2 m (covers 17.5 m with 1.7 m offcut).
Common Mistake to Avoid: Rounding down to 7 lengths, which leaves the room 0.7 metres short.
