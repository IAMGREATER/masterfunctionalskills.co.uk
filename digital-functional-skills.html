Choose Your Digital Qualification Level

Digital Functional Skills Qualifications are available at two official Ofqual levels designed for different career and educational stages.

DFSQ Entry 3

Foundation Level / Essential User

Ideal for beginners seeking to build solid confidence using smartphones, tablets, laptops, safe web browsing, online health services and public portals, and introductory email.

  • Basic device navigation, touchscreen, mouse & keyboard
  • Simple word processing, text styling & saving files
  • Creating and sending basic emails with attachments
  • Simple digital transactions & filling in web forms
  • Recognizing basic scams & protecting personal passwords

National Curriculum Framework

The 5 Core Digital Functional Skills Standards

Both DFSQ Entry 3 and DFS Level 1 are structured around the 5 government national standards for digital skills:

1. Using Devices & Handling Information

Learn to navigate operating systems, manage system settings, organize folders hierarchically, understand data storage metrics (KB, MB, GB, TB), perform effective internet queries, and evaluate online source reliability.

2. Creating & Editing

Produce workplace-ready documents, crop and insert digital images, master spreadsheet formulas (SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT, sorting), and format charts to present commercial information clearly.

3. Communicating

Master professional email etiquette, manage attachments and subject lines, organize video conference invitations (Teams / Zoom), control group messaging, and understand personal digital footprints.

4. Transacting

Confidently complete complex online application forms, upload verification documents, manage e-commerce checkouts safely, utilize public health apps, and verify secure SSL padlock indicators.

5. Being Safe & Responsible Online

Implement strong password strategies and two-factor authentication (2FA), spot suspicious phishing communications, understand GDPR rights, protect privacy, and practice physical ergonomics and screen wellbeing.

Digital Functional Skills — Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the reformed qualification, funding, exam format, and certificate.

What happened to Functional Skills ICT?

Functional Skills ICT was officially retired by Ofqual and the Department for Education in 2023. It was replaced by Digital Functional Skills Qualifications (DFSQ), which reflect modern cloud computing, smartphones, online privacy, and workplace digital tools.

Is the Digital Functional Skills exam taken online?

Yes. The entire assessment is completed onscreen. You can sit your exam securely from home using our remote invigilation system, provided you have a computer with a webcam and microphone.

Do employers and universities accept DFSQ?

Yes. Digital Functional Skills qualifications are regulated by Ofqual and listed on the Register of Regulated Qualifications. They are widely recognized by employers, apprenticeship providers, healthcare clinics, and colleges across the UK.

Which awarding bodies offer DFSQ?

Major UK awarding bodies including Pearson Edexcel, City & Guilds, NCFE, and Open Awards offer accredited Digital Functional Skills Qualifications. Master Functional Skills prepares you for and delivers Pearson Edexcel and City & Guilds assessments.
