Examiner Rule for Tone, Style, Register & Writer's Voice: Tone is the writer's emotional attitude (e.g. reassuring, urgent, apologetic, critical, sarcastic, authoritative). Style and Register refer to the level of formality, sentence length, and active vs passive voice. Never describe tone simply as 'good' or 'bad' — always use a precise adjective backed by a quote.
