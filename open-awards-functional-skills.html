Open Awards Functional Skills: Accepted Nationwide by Universities, NHS & Employers

Whether you are applying for nursing or midwifery university degrees, completing an apprenticeship gateway, or progressing at work, Open Awards Functional Skills Level 2 carries the exact same legal GCSE Grade 4 (C) recognition as Pearson Edexcel, City & Guilds, and AQA.

Open Awards Maths L2 Papers Open Awards English L2 Papers Open Awards Digital Skills Online Exam Options

100% UCAS & University Accepted

Regulated directly by Ofqual on the Regulated Qualifications Framework (RQF). Accepted across UK universities for Nursing, Midwifery, Teaching (PGCE/QTS), Paramedic Science, and Access to HE progression.

NHS, Public Sector & Apprenticeships

Recognised by the Education and Skills Funding Agency (ESFA), NHS employers, police & public services, and End-Point Assessment Organisations (EPAOs) as the official Level 2 literacy, numeracy, and digital standard.

Built for Smooth Online Exams (XAMS)

Open Awards' modern onscreen testing platform (XAMS) features clear layout navigation, built-in onscreen tools, dyslexia-friendly spacing, and flexible remote-invigilated sittings from home.
