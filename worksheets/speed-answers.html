Speed, Distance & Time Pearson Edexcel: L2.19
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Core Skills (Warm-Up) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Level 2 Average Speed with Minutes) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A medical courier motorcycle travels 63 miles in 1 hour and 15 minutes.
Calculate the average speed of the motorcycle in miles per hour (mph).
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Convert 1 hour 15 minutes to decimal hours: 1 + (15 ÷ 60) = 1.25 hours [M1]
2. Speed = Distance ÷ Time = 63 ÷ 1.25 = 50.4 mph [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 50.4 mph
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Dividing 63 by 1.15 (= 54.78 mph) instead of 1.25 hours.
🔄 Quick Check: 50.4 mph × 1.25 hours = 63 miles.
Question 2 (Level 2 Journey Time in Hours & Minutes) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A delivery van travels 154 miles along a motorway at an average speed of 56 mph.
How long does the journey take? Give your answer in hours and minutes.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Time = Distance ÷ Speed = 154 ÷ 56 = 2.75 hours [M1]
2. Convert 0.75 hours to minutes (0.75 × 60 = 45 mins): 2 hours 45 minutes [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 2 hours 45 minutes
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Writing 2.75 hours as 2 hours 75 minutes (or 3 hours 15 minutes).
🔄 Quick Check: 56 mph × 2.75 hours = 154 miles.
Section B: Everyday & Workplace Maths Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 2 Distance Travelled in Minutes) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
An air ambulance helicopter flies from its base to an incident scene at an average speed of 135 mph.
The flight takes 24 minutes.

Calculate the distance flown by the air ambulance in miles.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Convert 24 minutes into decimal hours: 24 ÷ 60 = 0.4 hours [M1]
2. Apply Distance = Speed × Time: 135 mph × 0.4 hours [P1]
3. Evaluate: 54 miles [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 54 miles
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Multiplying 135 × 24 = 3,240 miles without converting minutes into hours.
🔄 Quick Check: 54 miles ÷ 135 mph = 0.4 hours = 24 minutes.
Question 4 (Level 2 Metric-Imperial Speed Conversion) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A European freight driver is driving on a UK dual carriageway where the speed limit for his vehicle is 60 mph.
His digital speedometer is set to kilometres per hour and reads 92 km/h.
Use the conversion factor: 5 miles = 8 km.

By how many mph is the driver below the 60 mph speed limit?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Convert 92 km/h to mph: (92 ÷ 8) × 5 [M1]
2. Evaluate speed in mph: 11.5 × 5 = 57.5 mph [P1]
3. Subtract from 60 mph limit: 60 - 57.5 = 2.5 mph below the limit [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 2.5 mph (travelling at 57.5 mph)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Multiplying 92 by 8 and dividing by 5 (= 147.2 mph).
🔄 Quick Check: 57.5 mph ÷ 5 × 8 = 92 km/h.
Section C: Exam-Style Problem Solving Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Two-Stage Journey Overall Average Speed) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A mobile screening unit drives from Leeds to Birmingham in two stages:
• Stage 1: Drives 75 miles in 1 hour 30 minutes.
• Stage 2: Drives the remaining 45 miles at an average speed of 30 mph.

Calculate the unit's overall average speed for the entire 120-mile journey in mph.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Stage 1 time = 1.5 hours; Stage 2 time = Distance ÷ Speed = 45 ÷ 30 = 1.5 hours [P1]
2. Total distance = 75 + 45 = 120 miles; Total time = 1.5 + 1.5 = 3.0 hours [P1]
3. Overall Average Speed = Total Distance ÷ Total Time = 120 ÷ 3.0 [M1]
4. Evaluate: 40 mph [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 40 mph
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Wait — here Stage 1 speed is 50 mph and Stage 2 is 30 mph, and since times are equal (1.5h each) the average is 40 mph, but students must show Total Distance (120) ÷ Total Time (3) to earn method marks!
🔄 Quick Check: 40 mph × 3 hours = 120 miles (75 + 45).
Question 6 (Level 2 Cold-Chain Vaccine Delivery ETA Verification) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A refrigerated vaccine van leaves the depot at 07:50 to make a delivery 132 miles away.
• The driver must take a mandatory 30-minute rest break en route.
• Due to heavy traffic, the van achieves an average driving speed of 48 mph.
• The hospital pharmacy closes its loading bay at 11:00.

Will the vaccine van arrive before the loading bay closes at 11:00? Show full working.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate driving time: Time = 132 ÷ 48 = 2.75 hours [M1]
2. Convert 2.75 hours to hours and minutes: 2 hours + (0.75 × 60) = 2 hours 45 minutes [P1]
3. Add 30-minute rest break: 2 hrs 45 mins + 30 mins = 3 hours 15 minutes total journey time [P1]
4. Add to 07:50 departure: 07:50 + 3 hrs 15 mins = 11:05. Conclude: No, the van arrives at 11:05, which is 5 minutes after the 11:00 closure. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: No (Arrives at 11:05, which is 5 minutes late)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Forgetting to add the 30-minute mandatory rest break (which gives 10:35).
🔄 Quick Check: 11:05 - 30m break = 10:35 driving finish; 07:50 to 10:35 = 2h 45m = 2.75h; 2.75 × 48 = 132 miles.
