Entry Level 3 Specification & Learning Outcomes (E3.W.16 / E3.W.17)

Regulated Ofqual Entry Level 3 English outcomes (Open Awards, Pearson Edexcel, City & Guilds & NCFE):

  • E3.W.16 – Spell correctly words designated for Entry Level 3, including common homophones, plurals, silent-letter words, and prefixes/suffixes.
  • E3.W.17 – Produce legible text (or accurate typed text on screen).
Entry Level 3 Focus: Spelling statutory Entry Level 3 everyday words (because, friend, Wednesday, February, receive, address, people, money), regular/irregular plurals, '-ing/-ed' endings, and core homophones (there/their, to/too/two, your/you're).

Core Entry Level 3 Golden Rules: Entry Level 3 Spelling: Everyday Words, Plurals & Homophones

Golden Rule 1: Learn the 4 Easy Plural Rules (`-s`, `-es`, `-ies`, `-ves`)

Most nouns add -s (cups). Words ending in `ch, sh, s, x, z` add -es (boxes, buses, lunches). Consonant + `y` changes to -ies (baby → babies). Some `f/fe` words change to -ves (knife → knives, shelf → shelves).

Entry Level 3 Key Rule: Irregular plurals change vowel: child → children, woman → women, tooth → teeth!
Golden Rule 2: Drop Silent '-e' Before Adding '-ing'

make → making, come → coming, have → having, write → writing, take → taking.

Entry Level 3 Key Rule: Short 1-vowel + 1-consonant words double the consonant: stop → stopped, shop → shopping!
Golden Rule 3: Use Memory Hooks for Tricky Entry 3 Words

Because = Big Elephants Can Always Understand Small Elephants | Wednesday = Wed-nes-day | Friend = Fri-end.

Entry Level 3 Key Rule: Copy words printed in the question box carefully!

Core Skill 1: Plurals & Verb Endings (`-es`, `-ies`, `-ing`, `-ed`)

High-frequency spelling patterns tested across every Entry 3 board.

Vowel + Y vs Consonant + Y
Vowel + y JUST adds s: day → days, key → keys, boy → boys. Consonant + y → ies: city → cities, party → parties.
Silent Letters at Entry 3
Spot silent letters in everyday words: know, write, wrong, answer, listen, half.

Common Entry Level 3 Examiner Traps to Avoid

Entry Level 3 Examiner Trap: Adding Apostrophes to Plurals ('Three Box's')
Plurals NEVER use an apostrophe—write `boxes, buses, Mondays`.
Entry Level 3 Examiner Trap: Writing 'Becuse' or 'Diffrent'
Sound out hidden syllables clearly: `be-cause` and `dif-fer-ent`.

Interactive Entry Level 3 Exam Practice Questions

Entry Level 3 Practice 1: Plurals Quick-Fire 3 Marks
Write the plural of:
1. sandwich | 2. lorry | 3. child
Entry Level 3 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
Word 1 (1 Mark): sandwiches (-ch adds -es)
Word 2 (1 Mark): lorries (consonant r + y → ies)
Word 3 (1 Mark): children (irregular plural)
Final Answer: 1. sandwiches | 2. lorries | 3. children
Entry Level 3 Practice 2: Homophone Choice 2 Marks
Choose the right spelling:
A) Please wait right (hear / here) at reception.
B) The suitcase was (to / too / two) heavy to lift.
Entry Level 3 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
Sentence A (1 Mark): here (place)
Sentence B (1 Mark): too (excessively)
Final Answer: A: here | B: too
