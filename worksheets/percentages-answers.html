Percentages Pearson Edexcel: L1.12 • L2.11 • L2.16
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Procedural Fluency Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Level 1 Percentage of an Amount) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Calculate 15% of £3,600 without a calculator.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Find 10% (£3,600 ÷ 10 = £360) and 5% (half of £360 = £180) [M1]
2. Add 10% + 5%: £360 + £180 = £540.00 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £540.00
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Dividing £3,600 by 15 instead of finding 15 hundredths.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: £540 ÷ £3,600 = 0.15 = 15%.
Question 2 (Level 2 Reverse Percentage / Pre-VAT Price) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
An electronic medical monitor costs £756.00 including 20% VAT.
Work out the original price of the monitor before VAT was added.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Recognise £756 represents 120% of the pre-VAT price: divide £756 by 1.20 (or ÷ 120 × 100) [M1]
2. Evaluate: £756 ÷ 1.20 = £630.00 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £630.00
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Finding 20% of £756 (£151.20) and subtracting it to get £604.80 — the classic reverse percentage trap!
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: £630.00 + 20% (£126.00) = £756.00.
Section B: Workplace & Functional Scenarios Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 NHS Pay Award Increase) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A healthcare support worker earns a gross annual salary of £24,500.
The annual pay settlement awards a 5.5% pay increase.

Work out the worker's new gross annual salary.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate 5.5% increase: £24,500 × 0.055 = £1,347.50 [M1]
2. Add increase to original salary: £24,500 + £1,347.50 [P1]
3. State new salary: £25,847.50 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £25,847.50
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Multiplying by 0.55 (55%) instead of 0.055 (5.5%).
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: £24,500 × 1.055 = £25,847.50.
Question 4 (Level 2 Percentage Decrease in Waiting Times) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Following a clinical pathway review, an outpatient clinic reduces its average patient waiting time from 45 minutes down to 36 minutes.

Calculate the percentage decrease in waiting time.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate time reduction: 45 - 36 = 9 minutes [M1]
2. Divide reduction by ORIGINAL time: 9 ÷ 45 = 0.20 [P1]
3. Convert to percentage: 0.20 × 100 = 20% [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 20%
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Dividing by the new time (9 ÷ 36 = 25%) instead of the original 45 minutes.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 45 mins - 20% (9 mins) = 36 mins.
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Problem Solving & Justification Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Medical Equipment Lease vs Buy) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A diagnostic ultrasound machine has a list price of £32,000.
The supplier offers two purchase options:
• Option A (Outright Buy): 15% promotional discount off list price, plus £450 delivery.
• Option B (2-Year Lease): Deposit of £4,000, followed by 24 monthly payments of £1,050.

Which option is cheaper, and by how much?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Option A: £32,000 × 0.85 = £27,200; plus £450 delivery = £27,650 [P1]
2. Option B: £4,000 + (24 × £1,050) = £4,000 + £25,200 = £29,200 [P1]
3. Calculate difference: £29,200 - £27,650 = £1,550 [M1]
4. Conclude: Option A is cheaper by £1,550.00 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Option A is cheaper by £1,550.00
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Omitting the £4,000 deposit in Option B.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: £27,650 + £1,550 = £29,200.
Question 6 (Level 2 Audit Target Justification) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
An NHS Trust targets reducing patient complaints by at least 12% over one year.
• In 2025, the trust recorded 850 complaints.
• In 2026, the trust recorded 740 complaints.
The customer service lead reports: 'We achieved our target reduction of at least 12%.'

Is the report correct? Show your calculations clearly and state a definitive conclusion.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Actual reduction in complaints: 850 - 740 = 110 complaints [P1]
2. Required 12% reduction: 850 × 0.12 = 102 complaints (or max 748 complaints) [M1]
3. Actual percentage reduction: (110 ÷ 850) × 100 = 12.94% [P1]
4. Conclude: Correct; the trust achieved a 12.94% reduction (110 complaints), exceeding the 12% (102 complaints) target. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Correct (Achieved 12.94% / 110 reduction vs 12% / 102 target)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Dividing 110 by 740 (14.86%) instead of dividing by the original 2025 figure of 850.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: 850 - 102 = 748; actual 740 is 8 complaints better than target.
