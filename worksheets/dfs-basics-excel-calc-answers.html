Excel & LibreOffice Calc Practical Mastery Exercises (All 6 Workbooks)

Exercise 1 (01 Corner Shop Sales.xlsx)
Open workbook 01. Write the multiplication formula in D5, drag down to D9 using the fill handle, calculate Grand Total in D10 with SUM, and format money columns as Currency (£) to 2 decimal places.
Worked Answer: D5 formula: =B5*C5 (fill down D5:D9). D10 formula: =SUM(D5:D9). Format B5:B9 and D5:D10 as Currency £ 2 d.p. (Expected Total: £125.00).
Exercise 2 (02 Community Cafe Orders.xlsx)
Compute Weekly Total (Row 11) and Daily Average (Row 12) for both Orders Count (Col B) and Takings (Col C). Widen any column showing ###.
Worked Answer: B11: =SUM(B5:B10) -> 314; C11: =SUM(C5:C10) -> £2,400.95. B12: =AVERAGE(B5:B10) -> 52.3; C12: =AVERAGE(C5:C10) -> £400.16. Double-click column header dividers to clear ###.
Exercise 3 (03 Monthly Household Budget.xlsx)
Compute Remaining (£) in Column D (Planned minus Actual), plus Lowest Spend in D11 and Highest Spend in D12 using functions.
Worked Answer: D5: =B5-C5 (drag down to D10). D11: =MIN(C5:C10) -> £44.75. D12: =MAX(C5:C10) -> £680.00.
Exercise 4 (04 Staff Hours and Pay.xlsx)
Fix capitalisation on 'benjamin hughes', compute Gross Pay (=B5*C5), add a SUM total in Row 10, and sort ONLY rows A4:D9 cleanly by Gross Pay Descending (Highest first).
Worked Answer: Capitalise 'Benjamin Hughes'. D5: =B5*C5 (D10 =SUM(D5:D9)). Select A4:D9 (NOT row 10!) -> Data > Sort (tick Column labels/Headers) -> Gross Pay Descending (Chloe £486.40 first, David £225.00 last).
Exercise 5 (05 Stock Check with IF.xlsx)
Write an =IF formula in Column D returning 'Reorder' if stock C5 is less than Minimum B5, otherwise 'OK'. Count total stock items in C11.
Worked Answer: D5 formula: =IF(C5<B5,"Reorder","OK") dragged to D10 (Paper, Clipboards, Laminating show Reorder). C11: =COUNT(C5:C10) -> 6.
Exercise 6 (06 Full Exam Practice Sales and Chart.xlsx)
Merge A1:D1 Bold 14pt, calculate Revenue & Totals, format £ 2 d.p., and insert a Column Chart of Product Group vs Revenue on a NEW worksheet tab named 'Sales Chart' (excluding Totals!).
Worked Answer: D5: =B5*C5; B10: =SUM(B5:B9) (574); D10: =SUM(D5:D9) (£1,639.15). Select A4:A9 + Ctrl + D4:D9 (skip Row 10!) -> Insert > Column Chart -> add Title + X/Y Axis labels -> move/paste onto new sheet tab named 'Sales Chart'.
