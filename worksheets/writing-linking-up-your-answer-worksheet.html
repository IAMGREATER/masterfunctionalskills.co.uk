Examiner Rule for Paragraphing, Transitions & Cohesion: Golden Rule 1: Build Every Body Paragraph Using the PEEL Formula: Every body paragraph should contain 3 to 4 sentences following PEEL: Point (clear topic sentence) → Evidence (realistic statistic, cost, or example) → Explain (impact on the workplace/community) → Link (bridge to the overall goal).
