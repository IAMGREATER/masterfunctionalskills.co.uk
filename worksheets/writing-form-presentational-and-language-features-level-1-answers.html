Format & Conventions: Letters, Emails, Articles & Reports Pearson Edexcel: Pearson Edexcel Functional Skills English (WRITING)
Total Marks Available: 10 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~6/10)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Core Skills (The Basics) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Mandatory Layout Features of a Formal Workplace Report) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
List four mandatory layout and structural features required to earn full Format marks in a Level 1 Formal Report.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Report Title / Header block (To / From / Date / Subject) and an Introduction section [A1]
2. Thematic Subheadings (e.g. Findings) + Numbered/Bulleted Recommendations conclusion [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1. Main Report Title (plus To/From/Date); 2. Introduction; 3. Subheadings for Findings; 4. Bulleted Recommendations.
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Starting a Formal Report with 'Dear Sir/Madam' and ending with 'Yours sincerely' (those belong to letters!).
🔄 Quick Check: Verify Title + Subheadings + Bullet points are present.
Question 2 (Core Skill Application — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
State the exact salutation and complimentary close (sign-off) required for each formal letter below:
1. A letter addressed to Ms Samantha Davies, Operations Director.
2. A letter addressed to The Customer Services Manager (name unknown).
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Named recipient (Ms Davies) takes 'Dear Ms Davies,' and 'Yours sincerely,'. [M1]
2. Unnamed recipient takes 'Dear Sir or Madam,' and 'Yours faithfully,'. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 1. Salutation: Dear Ms Davies, | Sign-off: Yours sincerely,
2. Salutation: Dear Sir or Madam, | Sign-off: Yours faithfully,
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Writing an email response without 'To:' and 'Subject:' lines at the top automatically forfeits format marks in Pearson Edexcel and City & Guilds exams.
🔄 Quick Check: Memory rule: Never put two S's together (Sir/Madam never pairs with Sincerely!).
Section B: Real-Life & Workplace Practice Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 Model: Complete Formal Letter Layout & Opening) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
You bought a laptop from TechMart Store (45 High Street, Leeds, LS1 4BR) last Saturday, but the battery does not hold a charge. Set out the full formal letter header (using your address: 12 Park Lane, Leeds, LS2 9AA), date, salutation to the Store Manager (unnamed), opening paragraph, and sign-off.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Position sender address and date on the top right: Write 12 Park Lane, Leeds, LS2 9AA followed by today's date (14 October 2026). [A1]
2. Position recipient address on the left and select salutation: Write The Store Manager, TechMart Store, 45 High Street, Leeds, LS1 4BR, followed by 'Dear Sir or Madam,'. [A1]
3. Draft opening paragraph and match sign-off: State purchase details and fault, then close with 'Yours faithfully,' because the manager's name is unknown. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark:
12 Park Lane
Leeds
LS2 9AA

14 October 2026

The Store Manager
TechMart Store
45 High Street
Leeds
LS1 4BR

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to formally complain about a faulty Lenovo IdeaPad laptop (Receipt No. TM-8821) that I purchased from your Leeds High Street branch on Saturday 10 October for £429. Despite charging the device overnight, the battery drains completely within fifteen minutes of unplugging the power cable. Under the Consumer Rights Act, I would like to request a full refund or an immediate replacement laptop.

Yours faithfully,
Jamie Clarke
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Even if the onscreen exam box doesn't have pre-printed To/Subject boxes, always type 'To:' and 'Subject:' on the first two lines of your email.
🔄 Quick Check: Never skip addresses in a formal letter! Even in an onscreen exam where alignment is tricky, typing your address, a blank line, the date, a blank line, and the recipient's address earns automatic format marks.
Question 4 (Exam-Style Task — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
You are asked to write an email to all warehouse staff reminding them that the annual fire drill takes place tomorrow at 10:30 am and that everyone must assemble at Point B. Write the exact email header block (To, Subject, Salutation) and the opening sentence.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Write 'To: All Warehouse Staff (staff@warehouselogistics.co.uk)'. [M1]
2. Write a specific, informative 'Subject:' line including the time. [M1]
3. Include a professional greeting and clear opening sentence. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: To: allstaff@warehouselogistics.co.uk
Subject: Mandatory Annual Fire Drill – Tomorrow at 10:30 am (Assembly Point B)

Dear Colleagues,

Please be reminded that our mandatory annual warehouse fire evacuation drill will take place tomorrow morning at 10:30 am prompt, and all staff must evacuate immediately to Assembly Point B in the north car park.
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Ending a magazine article or a workplace report with 'Yours sincerely, John' shows the examiner you have confused letter conventions with article/report conventions.
🔄 Quick Check: A strong Subject line summarizes the entire purpose of the email in 6 to 10 words.
