Key vs Detailed Points in Reading & Writing Pearson Edexcel: Pearson Edexcel Functional Skills English (READING)
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Foundational Concept Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Distinguishing Overarching Key Points from Supporting Detail) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Read this workplace paragraph:
'To pass our annual fire safety inspection, all fire exit corridors must be kept completely clear at all times. Yesterday, inspectors found three linen cages blocking Stairwell B and two waste bins wedged against Door 4.'
Identify the main overarching key point and one specific supporting detail.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Overarching Key Point: All fire exit corridors must be kept completely clear at all times to pass inspection [A1]
2. Supporting Detail: Three linen cages blocked Stairwell B (or two waste bins wedged against Door 4) [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Key Point: Fire exit corridors must be kept clear at all times. Supporting Detail: Three linen cages were found blocking Stairwell B.
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Confusing the specific example (three linen cages) with the main rule (keeping fire exits clear).
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Does the supporting detail prove why the main rule is needed?
Question 2 (Core Skill Application — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Read this extract from a logistics company newsletter:
'Fleet electrification has achieved substantial operational savings across our Midland depots. By replacing 25 diesel vans with Nissan Townstar EVs, depot fuel costs fell by 42% over six months, while tailpipe emissions decreased by 18 tonnes.'
Identify one main point and one detailed supporting point.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Main Point: Fleet electrification has generated major operational savings across the Midland depots [1 Mark]. [M1]
2. Detailed Point: Replacing 25 diesel vans cut depot fuel costs by 42% over six months (or reduced emissions by 18 tonnes) [1 Mark]. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Main Point: Electrifying the vehicle fleet has delivered significant operational savings. | Detailed Point: Fuel costs fell by 42% (or 25 diesel vans were replaced / emissions dropped by 18 tonnes).
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: If a question asks 'What is the main message of Text A?', quoting a narrow figure like '£180 per tonne' or 'Bay 4' scores zero because it represents only one supporting branch, not the trunk.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Extract the broad statement of success in the opening sentence as the main point, and cite the exact percentage or tonnage from the second sentence as your supporting detail.
Section B: Functional Workplace Application Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Extracting Key and Detailed Points from a Staff Notice (Level 1)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Read the notice from the Warehouse Manager:
'All loading bay operatives must wear high-visibility vests and steel-toe boots at all times to prevent serious crush injuries. Last quarter, two agency workers suffered foot fractures near Bay 4 after entering the forklift zone in standard trainers.'
(a) Identify the main point of the notice. [1 Mark]
(b) Identify one detailed point that supports the manager's rule. [1 Mark]
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Locate the overarching rule or message (Main Point): The first sentence sets out the core policy rule that applies to everyone: all loading bay staff must wear high-visibility vests and steel-toe boots to prevent crush injuries. [A1]
2. Extract the specific incident evidence (Detailed Point): The second sentence gives the specific factual proof: two agency workers suffered foot fractures near Bay 4 last quarter while wearing standard trainers. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) Main Point: Operatives must wear high-visibility vests and steel-toe boots at all times for safety. | (b) Detailed Point: Two agency staff broke bones in their feet near Bay 4 last quarter while wearing trainers.
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Ask yourself: 'What is the headline takeaway that covers the whole paragraph?'
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Never swap the two around! If you write 'Two agency workers broke their feet near Bay 4' as the main point, you score 0 for part (a) because that is a past incident example, not the overarching workplace instruction.
Question 4 (Exam-Style Task — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Read this paragraph from a dental practice hygiene memo:
'Cross-contamination in treatment rooms remains our highest clinical risk during busy afternoon clinics. Yesterday's spot audit revealed that autoclave sterilisation logs were left unsigned in Surgery 2, and three ultrasonic bath filters had not been flushed since Tuesday.'
State the main point [1 Mark] and extract two detailed points [2 Marks].
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Main Point: Cross-contamination in treatment rooms is the clinic's highest clinical risk during busy afternoon shifts [1 Mark]. [M1]
2. Detailed Point 1: Autoclave sterilisation logs were left unsigned in Surgery 2 during yesterday's audit [1 Mark]. [M1]
3. Detailed Point 2: Three ultrasonic bath filters had not been flushed since Tuesday [1 Mark]. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Main Point: Cross-contamination in treatment rooms is the highest clinical risk. | Detail 1: Autoclave logs were unsigned in Surgery 2. | Detail 2: Three ultrasonic bath filters were unushed since Tuesday.
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Some candidates copy out three full sentences hoping the examiner will pick out the main point for them. Examiners mark this down because it shows you cannot distinguish the main point from the details.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Notice how the two audit findings in the second sentence provide concrete proof of the overarching risk stated in the first sentence.
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Exam-Style Analysis & Evaluation Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (NHS Trust Waste Audit Multi-Point Synthesis (Level 2)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Read the extract from the hospital facilities report:
'Clinical waste disposal costs reached £420,000 last year due to widespread misclassification across surgical wards. Staff frequently discard non-contaminated cardboard packaging into orange clinical waste sacks rather than domestic clear bags, inflating incineration tariffs by £180 per tonne.'
(a) Identify the overarching main point of this extract. [1 Mark]
(b) Identify two distinct supporting detailed points used as evidence. [2 Marks]
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Identify the central overarching point: The main point is that hospital waste disposal costs have risen heavily (£420,000) because staff are misclassifying waste on surgical wards. [A1]
2. Identify Supporting Detail 1 (Specific Action): Staff put non-contaminated cardboard packaging into orange clinical waste sacks instead of clear domestic bags. [A1]
3. Identify Supporting Detail 2 (Specific Financial Metric): This incorrect sorting inflates incineration tariffs by £180 per tonne. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) Main Point: Misclassification of waste across wards has driven clinical disposal costs up to £420,000. | (b) Supporting Details: 1. Staff place uncontaminated cardboard in orange clinical sacks instead of clear bags. 2. This error increases tariffs by £180 per tonne.
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Isolate only the specific clause or sentence that answers the prompt cleanly.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Do not confuse the specific cost penalty (£180 per tonne) with the overall message. The £180 per tonne figure is a granular detail proving why misclassification is so expensive.
Question 6 (Level 2 Mastery Challenge — 3 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
Read this extract from a college Principal's report:
'Although installing swipe-card barriers at the main campus entrance cost £18,500 in July and caused brief queues during enrolment week, the new security gates have dramatically improved student safety and eliminated unauthorised trespassers.'
(a) What is the writer's main point? [1 Mark]
(b) Give two detailed points mentioned about the installation. [2 Marks]
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Main Point: Located in the main clause after the introductory concession — the new swipe-card barriers have dramatically improved student safety and stopped unauthorised trespassers [1 Mark]. [M1]
2. Detailed Point 1: Installing the barriers cost £18,500 in July [1 Mark]. [M1]
3. Detailed Point 2: The barriers caused brief queues during enrolment week [1 Mark]. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) Main Point: The new security barriers have significantly improved student safety and stopped trespassers. | (b) Details: 1. They cost £18,500 in July. 2. They caused brief queues during enrolment week.
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Failing to proofread spelling, punctuation, and grammar after completing the draft.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: In Level 2 texts starting with 'Although' or 'While', the main point is always in the second half of the sentence after the comma!
