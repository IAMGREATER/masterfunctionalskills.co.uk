Step-by-Step Teaching & Key Concepts

Core Knowledge
1
The Dangers of Open Public Wi-Fi

Public Wi-Fi networks in coffee shops, hotels, and airports are convenient, but they carry severe risks:

  • Unencrypted Traffic: Open networks without passwords transmit data in plain text. Criminals on the same network can 'eavesdrop' on your web traffic (called packet sniffing).
  • Fake Hotspots (Evil Twins): Criminals set up rogue Wi-Fi networks with names like 'Free_Airport_WiFi' to trick people into connecting so they can intercept usernames and credit cards.
  • Malware Distribution: Unsecured networks can be used to inject malicious pop-ups and fake software updates into your browser.
Workplace Data Security Rule: Never access confidential customer records, financial accounts, or internal hospital databases on an open public Wi-Fi network without a corporate VPN active!
2
Virtual Private Networks (VPNs)

A VPN creates an encrypted digital 'tunnel' between your device and your organisation's network:

  1. Even if you are connected to an untrusted public Wi-Fi, the VPN encrypts all data leaving your laptop.
  2. Any hacker snooping on the network only sees scrambled, unreadable code.
  3. It allows remote staff to access internal workplace servers securely from home.

3
Using Mobile Tethering Safely

If trusted Wi-Fi is unavailable, you can turn your smartphone into a Personal Mobile Hotspot:

  • Shares your phone's 4G/5G mobile data with your laptop.
  • Always set a strong password (WPA2/WPA3 encryption). Never leave your hotspot open!
  • Be mindful of data limits — video streaming consumes mobile allowances rapidly.

Authentic Pearson Edexcel Exam Model

Accredited Model

Here is how this skill is tested in the real Pearson Edexcel onscreen digital assessment, broken down with official examiner mark codes ([B1] knowledge, [M1] process, [A1] accuracy).

Exam Scenario Prompt: A community healthcare worker is travelling on a train and needs to access confidential patient records on their laptop. The train offers free public Wi-Fi with no password required. Explain two precautions the worker must take to keep patient data secure.
Step 1: State first security precaution [B1]
Connect through their organisation's secure VPN (Virtual Private Network) before logging into patient databases.
Tutor Marking Rationale: VPN encrypts the transmission over unsecure networks.
Step 2: State second alternative precaution [B1]
Use their own encrypted mobile phone hotspot instead of the open public train Wi-Fi.
Tutor Marking Rationale: Cellular tethering bypasses shared public network risks.
Tutor Abdul's Exam Golden Rule: In Level 1 exams, whenever public Wi-Fi is mentioned in a scenario, the accredited answers are: 1. Use a VPN; 2. Use a secure personal mobile hotspot.

Pearson Edexcel Benchmark Solution

Rule 1: Staff must never conduct confidential work on open, password-free public Wi-Fi networks without an active corporate VPN. Rule 2: Staff should use password-protected personal mobile hotspots (tethering) or an approved VPN connection at all times when working remotely.
Official Marking Criteria:
  • [1 Mark]: Mandating VPN usage when connecting over public networks.
  • [1 Mark]: Requiring secure/encrypted connections or personal mobile hotspots.
