Movement, Direction & Compass Bearings Pearson Edexcel: Pearson Edexcel L1 & L2 Mathematics
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Core Skills (Warm-Up) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Compass Turns & Grid Navigation) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A Automated Guided Vehicle (AGV) in a pharmacy warehouse is facing North-East (045°).
It makes a three-quarter turn (270°) clockwise.
Which compass direction and three-figure bearing is the AGV now facing?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Add 270° clockwise to 045°: 45° + 270° = 315° [M1]
2. Identify compass direction for 315°: North-West (NW) [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: North-West (315°)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Turning anticlockwise instead of clockwise.
🔄 Quick Check: 270° clockwise is equivalent to 90° anticlockwise: 360° + 45° - 90° = 315°.
Question 2 (Level 1 Foundation — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
You are facing South-East (SE). You make a half turn (180°). Which compass direction are you now facing?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. A half turn (180°) points in the exact opposite direction. [M1]
2. The opposite of South-East (SE) is North-West (NW). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: North-West (NW)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: If a person is walking South down a map and turns Right, they turn towards the West (left side of the page), NOT East!
🔄 Quick Check: Swap both words: South becomes North, East becomes West → North-West!
Section B: Everyday & Workplace Maths Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Hospital Emergency Evacuation Route Direction (Entry 3 / Level 1 Foundation)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A hospital porter is pushing a wheelchair facing North down Main Corridor. At the junction, she turns a quarter turn (90°) clockwise, walks 30 metres, then turns a three-quarter turn (270°) clockwise.
(a) In which compass direction is she facing after the first turn?
(b) In which compass direction is she facing after the second turn?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Quarter turn (90°) clockwise from North: North + 90° clockwise = EAST. [A1]
2. Three-quarter turn (270°) clockwise from East: From East: 1/4 = South, 2/4 = West, 3/4 = NORTH. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) East | (b) North
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Imagine yourself standing in the person’s shoes facing their direction before turning left or right.
🔄 Quick Check: A 3/4 turn clockwise is the exact same final position as a 1/4 turn anti-clockwise!
Question 4 (Workplace Application — 2 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A security guard is facing West. Through how many degrees clockwise must he turn to face South?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Clockwise from West (270°): West → North (90°) → East (180°) → South (270°). [M1]
2. Angle = 270° clockwise (a three-quarter turn). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 270°
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Turning West from North when asked to turn clockwise.
🔄 Quick Check: Going clockwise from West to South passes through North and East (3 quarter turns = 270°).
Section C: Exam-Style Problem Solving Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Warehouse Forklift Grid Navigation & Net Displacement (Level 1/2 Multi-Step)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
On a warehouse floor grid where 1 square = 5 metres, a forklift starts at Loading Bay S and follows these instructions:
1. Drive 6 squares North.
2. Turn 90° clockwise and drive 4 squares.
3. Turn 90° clockwise and drive 2 squares.
(a) How many metres in total has the forklift driven?
(b) How many squares North and how many squares East is the forklift from its starting point S?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Sum total squares driven and multiply by 5 metres: Total squares = 6 + 4 + 2 = 12 squares. | Total distance = 12 × 5 m = 60 metres. [M1 / A1]
2. Track net North/South and East/West position: Step 1 goes 6 North; Step 2 goes 4 East; Step 3 turns clockwise from East to go 2 South. | Net North = 6 − 2 = 4 squares North; Net East = 4 squares East. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) 60 metres total driven | (b) 4 squares North and 4 squares East of S
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Draw a small clock face: 12 → 3 → 6 → 9 is Clockwise (N → E → S → W).
🔄 Quick Check: Sketch the path on scrap grid paper with arrows so you can see that the third leg goes South.
Question 6 (Level 2 Multi-Step Problem Solving — 3 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A delivery driver leaves the depot, drives 3.5 km East, turns left (90° anti-clockwise) and drives 2.0 km, then turns left again and drives 3.5 km. In which compass direction is she now facing, and how far is she directly from the depot?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Starts East → turns left to face North (2.0 km) → turns left to face West (3.5 km). [M1]
2. The 3.5 km West cancels out the 3.5 km East, leaving her exactly 2.0 km North of the depot. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Facing West; 2.0 km North of the depot
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Stating a conclusion without showing the full numerical comparison values.
🔄 Quick Check: Draw the 3 sides of the rectangle: 3.5 km East, 2.0 km North, 3.5 km West.
