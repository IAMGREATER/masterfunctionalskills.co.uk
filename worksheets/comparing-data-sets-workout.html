Practice & Repeat
Pearson Edexcel 2-Statement Comparison Rule
  • In Functional Skills exams, comparing two data sets ALWAYS requires TWO statements:
  • Statement 1 (Average): Compare ONE average (Mean or Median) to state which group is higher on average, quoting both values.
  • Statement 2 (Spread/Consistency): Compare the Range (or IQR) to state which group is more consistent / less varied, quoting both values.
  • Golden rule: A smaller range means MORE consistent (less spread out). A larger range means LESS consistent (more spread out).
  • Always include context words (e.g. 'Patients waited less on average in Ward B...').
Calculator Strategy: Never write 'The range is better'. Write 'The range is smaller (4 mins vs 12 mins), so times were more consistent.'
Part 1: Quick Practice
Quick calculation drills to build confidence
Question 1 Interpreting Averages and Ranges
Compare the two sets and state the valid mathematical deduction:
(a)
Team A Mean = £420, Team B Mean = £480. Which team earned more on average?
(b)
Depot 1 Range = 14 kg, Depot 2 Range = 6 kg. Which depot had more consistent parcel weights?
(c)
Clinic X Median = 24 mins, Clinic Y Median = 18 mins. Which clinic had shorter typical wait?
(d)
Shift 1 Range = 8 hours, Shift 2 Range = 15 hours. Which shift was more variable?
Question 2 Formulating Comparative Sentences
Fill in the missing words (higher/lower, more/less consistent):
(a)
Mean A is 25, Mean B is 20. On average, A is ___ than B.
(b)
Range A is 12, Range B is 5. Group B's results are ___ than Group A.
(c)
Can the range ever be negative?
