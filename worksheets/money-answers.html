Money, Invoicing & Payroll Pearson Edexcel: L1.14 • L2.15 • L2.16
Total Marks Available: 18 Marks (Pass Threshold: ~11/18)
Examiner Standard: Full Method (M) and Process (P) marks awarded even if final arithmetic errs.
Section A: Procedural Fluency Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Level 1 Payslip Net Pay Reconciliation) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A dental receptionist earns a monthly gross pay of £1,945.00.
Her monthly deductions are: Income Tax £158.40, National Insurance £96.80, and Pension £97.25.
Calculate her monthly net take-home pay.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Sum total deductions: £158.40 + £96.80 + £97.25 = £352.45 [M1]
2. Subtract from gross pay: £1,945.00 - £352.45 = £1,592.55 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £1,592.55
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Adding deductions onto gross pay instead of subtracting.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: £1,592.55 + £352.45 = £1,945.00.
Question 2 (Level 2 Weekly Wage with Overtime) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
A medical laboratory technician works 37.5 basic hours at £14.00 per hour, plus 8 overtime hours paid at time-and-a-half (1.5×).
Calculate the technician's total gross weekly pay.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Basic pay = 37.5 × £14.00 = £525.00; Overtime rate = £14 × 1.5 = £21.00/hr → Overtime pay = 8 × £21 = £168.00 [M1]
2. Total gross pay = £525.00 + £168.00 = £693.00 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £693.00
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Paying overtime at standard £14.00/hr instead of £21.00/hr.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: (37.5 + 12 equivalent hrs) × £14 = 49.5 × £14 = £693.00.
Section B: Workplace & Functional Scenarios Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 Commercial Equipment Invoice with VAT) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A care home orders 6 adjustable profiling beds at £420.00 each and 6 pressure-relief mattresses at £130.00 each.
Standard UK VAT at 20% is added to the subtotal.

Calculate the total invoice amount payable.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate net subtotal: (6 × £420) + (6 × £130) = £2,520 + £780 = £3,300.00 [M1]
2. Calculate 20% VAT: £3,300.00 × 0.20 = £660.00 [P1]
3. Add VAT to subtotal: £3,300.00 + £660.00 = £3,960.00 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £3,960.00
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Calculating VAT on only 1 bed and 1 mattress instead of all 6.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: £3,960.00 ÷ 1.20 = £3,300.00; £3,300 ÷ 6 = £550 per set (£420 + £130).
Question 4 (Level 2 Trade Discount Followed by VAT) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
An electrical wholesaler quotes a list price of £1,250.00 (excluding VAT) for LED hospital lighting panels.
The contractor receives a 12% trade discount off the list price, and then 20% VAT is added to the discounted subtotal.

Calculate the final total payable including VAT.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Deduct 12% trade discount: £1,250 × 0.12 = £150 discount → Net price = £1,250 - £150 = £1,100.00 [M1]
2. Calculate 20% VAT on discounted net price: £1,100.00 × 0.20 = £220.00 [P1]
3. Final total payable: £1,100.00 + £220.00 = £1,320.00 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £1,320.00
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Adding 8% (+20% - 12% = +8%) to £1,250 to get £1,350 — percentages apply sequentially, not additively!
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: £1,250 × 0.88 × 1.20 = £1,320.00.
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Problem Solving & Justification Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Annual Tax Threshold & Take-Home Pay) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A senior physiotherapist earns a gross annual salary of £38,570.
• The first £12,570 of income is a tax-free Personal Allowance (0% tax).
• Income Tax is charged at 20% on all earnings above £12,570.
• In addition, she pays £1,980 per year in National Insurance and 6% of her total gross salary into her workplace pension.

Calculate her annual net take-home pay.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Taxable income: £38,570 - £12,570 = £26,000 → Income Tax (20%) = £26,000 × 0.20 = £5,200.00 [P1]
2. Pension contribution (6% of total £38,570): £38,570 × 0.06 = £2,314.20 [P1]
3. Total annual deductions: £5,200.00 (Tax) + £1,980.00 (NI) + £2,314.20 (Pension) = £9,494.20 [M1]
4. Annual Net Pay: £38,570.00 - £9,494.20 = £29,075.80 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £29,075.80
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Charging 20% Income Tax on the entire £38,570 without subtracting the £12,570 tax-free allowance first.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: £29,075.80 + £9,494.20 = £38,570.00.
Question 6 (Level 2 Clinic Refurbishment Quote Comparison) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A GP practice manager receives two quotes to repaint the reception area:
• Contractor A: 32 hours of labour at £28.00/hour, plus £245.00 for paint. (Contractor A is not VAT-registered, so no VAT is charged).
• Contractor B: Fixed labour & materials package of £1,050.00 with a 10% promotional discount, plus 20% VAT on the discounted price.

Which contractor is cheaper, and by how much?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Contractor A total: (32 × £28.00) + £245.00 = £896.00 + £245.00 = £1,141.00 [P1]
2. Contractor B discounted net: £1,050.00 × 0.90 = £945.00 [P1]
3. Contractor B with 20% VAT: £945.00 × 1.20 = £1,134.00 [M1]
4. Compare and conclude: Contractor B (£1,134.00) is cheaper than Contractor A (£1,141.00) by £7.00. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Contractor B is cheaper by £7.00 (£1,134.00 vs £1,141.00)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Forgetting to add 20% VAT to Contractor B after taking off the 10% discount.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: £1,141.00 - £1,134.00 = £7.00.
