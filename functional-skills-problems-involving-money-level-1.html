Golden Rules for Problems Involving Money & Multi-Tier Pricing

Golden Rule 1: Deconstruct Multi-Tier Pricing into Line Items

Break pricing into: 1. Base fee / Standing charge. 2. Per-unit or hourly charge. 3. Promotional discounts. 4. VAT or taxes. Sum systematically.

Golden Rule / Method: Standing Charge + (Rate × Units) - Discounts + Taxes
Golden Rule 2: Mind the Pence-to-Pounds Conversion

Never mix pounds and pence without converting. 45p + £3.20 is NOT 48.20! Convert 45p to £0.45 before adding (£0.45 + £3.20 = £3.65).

Golden Rule / Method: 14.5p per kWh = £0.145 per kWh (Divide pence by 100)

Core Skill 1: Skill 1: Foreign Currency Exchange Calculations (Buying & Selling)

Converting between GBP (£) and foreign currencies (€ / $) with commission.

  • GBP (£) to Foreign Currency: MULTIPLY by the exchange rate: £500 × 1.16 = €580.00.
  • Foreign Currency back to GBP (£): DIVIDE by the exchange rate: €464 ÷ 1.16 = £400.00.
  • Handling Commission: If a travel bureau charges 2% commission, deduct 2% before converting (or multiply by 0.98).
Level 1 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 2: Skill 2: Household & Commercial Utility Bills (Electricity & Gas Meters)

Calculating unit consumption from meter readings, standing charges, and 5% energy VAT.

  1. Units Used (kWh): Present Meter Reading − Previous Meter Reading.
  2. Usage Charge: Units Used × Cost per kWh (convert pence to pounds by dividing by 100!).
  3. Standing Charge: Number of Days × Daily Standing Charge.
  4. Total Bill: Sum Usage + Standing Charge, then add 5% domestic energy VAT (× 1.05).
Level 1 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 3: Skill 3: Group Booking Tariffs & Family/Multi-Ticket Combinations

Checking whether a family/group pass is cheaper than individual tickets.

When booking for a mixed group (e.g. 2 Adults and 3 Children where Adult = £18, Child = £11, and a Family Ticket for 2 Adults + 2 Children = £48):

  • Option 1 (All Individual): (2 × £18) + (3 × £11) = £36 + £33 = £69.00.
  • Option 2 (1 Family Ticket + 1 Extra Child): £48 + £11 = £59.00 (Saves £10.00!).
Level 1 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Sonya's Visual Quick-Check: Multiplier Shortcut on Group Discounts

"If you have a 15% discount, you pay 85% of the price (100% − 15% = 85%). On a calculator, simply punch: £249.00 × 0.85 = £211.65 in one single step! It is faster, cleaner, and completely eliminates subtraction rounding slips."

Critical Examiner Traps & Common Pitfalls

Examiner Pitfall: Forgetting Staff in Group Counts or Mixing Pence and Pounds
Adding 2,400 × 24 = 57,600 onto £40.50 without dividing pence by 100 is a classic exam error.
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Convert every pence figure into pounds (£0.24) before starting your calculations.

Interactive Exam Practice Questions

Exam Practice Question 1 2 Marks
A staff canteen offers a 10% discount on orders over £25. A department orders 6 sandwiches at £3.50 each and 4 coffees at £2.25 each. What is the total discounted cost?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. 6 × £3.50 = £21.00; 4 × £2.25 = £9.00.
2. Subtotal = £21 + £9 = £30.00 (qualifies for 10% discount).
3. 10% discount = £3.00. Total = £30.00 - £3.00 = £27.00.
Final Correct Answer: £27.00
Examiner Tip: Check that the subtotal exceeds £25 before applying the discount.
Exam Practice Question 2 2 Marks
A nurse travels to a medical conference in Dublin. She exchanges £450 into Euros (€) at an exchange rate of £1 = €1.16. During the trip she spends €382.80 and converts the remaining Euros back into pounds at the same rate. How many pounds (£) does she get back?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. GBP to Euros: £450 × 1.16 = €522.00.
2. Remaining Euros: €522.00 - €382.80 = €139.20.
3. Euros back to GBP: €139.20 ÷ 1.16 = £120.00.
Final Correct Answer: £120.00
Examiner Tip: Multiply to go from £ to €, and divide by the rate to convert € back to £.
Exam Practice Question 3 3 Marks
A car hire firm charges £38.00 per day plus 15p per mile for every mile driven over a free allowance of 100 miles per day. Calculate the total cost to hire a car for 3 days and drive a total of 460 miles.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. 3-day hire fee = 3 × £38.00 = £114.00.
2. Free mileage allowance for 3 days = 3 × 100 = 300 miles.
3. Excess miles = 460 - 300 = 160 miles @ £0.15/mile = £24.00.
4. Total cost = £114.00 + £24.00 = £138.00.
Final Correct Answer: £138.00
Examiner Tip: Remember the free mileage allowance is 100 miles PER DAY (300 miles over 3 days), not 100 miles total!
