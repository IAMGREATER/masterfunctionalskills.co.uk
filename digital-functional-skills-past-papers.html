Tutor Advice: How to Pass Digital Functional Skills First Time

Section A: Quick Wins (10 Marks)

Master data storage units (1 KB = 1000 Bytes, 1 MB = 1000 KB). Remember GDPR individual rights (Erasure, Rectification, Access). Know how private browsing protects history and recognise the HTTPS padlock symbol.

Section B: Spreadsheet Precision (15 Marks)

Pearson strictly awards marks for =SUM() and =AVERAGE(). Never type manual cell additions like =C4+D4+E4. Always format money columns as British Pounds (£) with 2 decimal places.

Document Design & White Space (10+ Marks)

Check document orientation (Landscape vs Portrait). Always lock aspect ratio when resizing photos, apply tight text wrapping so images never obscure sentences, and check for balanced white space.
