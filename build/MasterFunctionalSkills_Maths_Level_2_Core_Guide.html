Place Value, Ordering & Large Numbers

Workplace application of Place Value, Ordering & Large Numbers.
★Core Formula & Statutory Method
In large numbers up to 1,000,000, each digit column represents a power of ten. Use commas or spaces between thousands.

Separate digits into groups of three from the right: Millions, Thousands, Units.
✎Official Worked Solution
A warehouse records 425,800 units in Q1 and 389,950 units in Q2. How many more in Q1?
  1. Align columns: 425,800 - 389,950
  2. Perform column subtraction with borrowing
  3. Difference = 35,850 units
✓Examiner Golden Rule
Always align digits by their place value, never by the leftmost digit.
!Watch Out!
Don't confuse hundred thousands with millions.

Negative Numbers & Financial Balances

Workplace application of Negative Numbers & Financial Balances.
★Core Formula & Statutory Method
Negative numbers represent values below zero (bank overdrafts, temperatures, sea level). Adding a negative is subtraction.

Positive + Positive = Positive. Positive + Negative = Subtract smaller from larger.
✎Official Worked Solution
A business account is at -£1,450. A client pays £3,200, then bills of £850 and £1,120 are debited.
  1. Start: -£1,450 + £3,200 = +£1,750
  2. Deduct bills: £1,750 - £850 = £900
  3. Deduct staff: £900 - £1,120 = -£220 (Overdraft)
✓Examiner Golden Rule
Moving right on the number line increases value; moving left decreases value.
!Watch Out!
Subtracting a negative becomes an addition: 5 - (-3) = 8.

Fraction Operations & Mixed Numbers

Workplace application of Fraction Operations & Mixed Numbers.
★Core Formula & Statutory Method
Fractions represent equal parts of a whole. To add or subtract, you MUST find a common denominator.

Multiply fractions: multiply numerators together, then denominators together. Divide: flip second fraction and multiply.
✎Official Worked Solution
Calculate 2 1/4 + 1 2/3 for a catering recipe calculation.
  1. Convert to improper: 9/4 + 5/3
  2. Common denominator 12: 27/12 + 20/12 = 47/12
  3. Convert to mixed: 3 11/12
✓Examiner Golden Rule
Always convert mixed numbers to improper fractions before multiplying or dividing.
!Watch Out!
Never add denominators together: 1/2 + 1/2 is not 2/4!

Percentages of Amounts & Multipliers

Workplace application of Percentages of Amounts & Multipliers.
★Core Formula & Statutory Method
Percent means 'out of 100'. Use single-step decimal multipliers to calculate percentage changes efficiently.

Percentage increase: Multiplier = 1 + (percentage / 100). Decrease: Multiplier = 1 - (percentage / 100).
✎Official Worked Solution
An operative earns £24,500. They receive a 4.5% cost of living pay rise. What is the new salary?
  1. Multiplier for 4.5% increase = 1.045
  2. Calculation: £24,500 x 1.045
  3. New salary = £25,602.50
✓Examiner Golden Rule
Use decimal multipliers for calculator questions to save time and reduce errors.
!Watch Out!
Check if question asks for the INCREASE or the FINAL TOTAL.

Reverse Percentages & Original Value

Workplace application of Reverse Percentages & Original Value.
★Core Formula & Statutory Method
Reverse percentage problems give you the value AFTER an increase or decrease and ask for the ORIGINAL value.

Original Value = Final Value / Multiplier. NEVER just calculate the percentage of the final value!
✎Official Worked Solution
A laptop is on sale for £468 after a 20% discount. What was the original price before the sale?
  1. After 20% off, £468 represents 80% of original price (0.80 multiplier)
  2. Calculation: £468 / 0.80
  3. Original price = £585.00
✓Examiner Golden Rule
Always divide by the percentage multiplier to work backwards to the original 100%.
!Watch Out!
Do NOT add 20% of £468 (£93.60) — that gives the wrong answer (£561.60).

Compound Interest & Depreciation

Workplace application of Compound Interest & Depreciation.
★Core Formula & Statutory Method
Compound interest applies interest to both the initial principal and the accumulated interest from previous periods.

Final Amount = Principal x (1 + r)^n, where r is decimal interest rate and n is number of years.
✎Official Worked Solution
An NHS worker invests £5,000 in a savings bond paying 3.2% compound interest per year for 3 years.
  1. Multiplier = 1.032
  2. Formula: £5,000 x (1.032)^3
  3. £5,000 x 1.09911 = £5,495.55
✓Examiner Golden Rule
Apply the multiplier repeatedly or use the power key (x^y) on your scientific calculator.
!Watch Out!
Simple interest gives less than compound interest over multiple years.

Ratio Division in 2 & 3 Parts

Workplace application of Ratio Division in 2 & 3 Parts.
★Core Formula & Statutory Method
Ratios show relative sizes of quantities. To divide into a ratio, find the value of one part.

Step 1: Add ratio parts. Step 2: Total amount / Total parts = 1 part. Step 3: Multiply by each ratio part.
✎Official Worked Solution
Concrete is mixed in the ratio cement : sand : gravel = 1 : 2 : 4. How much sand is needed for 840 kg of mix?
  1. Total parts: 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 parts
  2. Value of 1 part: 840 kg / 7 = 120 kg
  3. Sand (2 parts): 2 x 120 kg = 240 kg
✓Examiner Golden Rule
Always check your answers sum back to the original total (120 + 240 + 480 = 840).
!Watch Out!
Read the order of terms carefully: cement : sand : gravel.

Direct & Inverse Proportion

Workplace application of Direct & Inverse Proportion.
★Core Formula & Statutory Method
Direct proportion: as one increases, the other increases at the same rate. Inverse: as one increases, the other decreases.

Direct: find unitary value (value for 1) then multiply. Inverse: Total work = workers x days = constant.
✎Official Worked Solution
It takes 4 decorators 6 days to paint an office block. How long would it take 3 decorators?
  1. Total work required = 4 decorators x 6 days = 24 worker-days
  2. Time for 3 decorators = 24 worker-days / 3 decorators
  3. Total time = 8 days
✓Examiner Golden Rule
More workers means FEWER days: inverse proportion always multiplies to a constant product.
!Watch Out!
Direct proportion problems maintain a constant ratio (division).

Decimals & Money Calculations

Workplace application of Decimals & Money Calculations.
★Core Formula & Statutory Method
Decimals represent tenths, hundredths, and thousandths. Money is always rounded to 2 decimal places (£0.00).

Align decimal points vertically for addition and subtraction. For multiplication, count total decimal places.
✎Official Worked Solution
An operative buys 14.5 metres of cabling at £2.64 per metre plus VAT at 20%.
  1. Cable cost: 14.5 x £2.64 = £38.28
  2. Add 20% VAT: £38.28 x 1.20 = £45.936
  3. Rounded to 2 decimal places = £45.94
✓Examiner Golden Rule
Always include two decimal places for currency answers (write £45.90, not £45.9).
!Watch Out!
Round down only at the very final step of your multi-step calculation.

Order of Operations (BIDMAS)

Workplace application of Order of Operations (BIDMAS).
★Core Formula & Statutory Method
Calculations must follow the statutory mathematical order: Brackets, Indices, Division/Multiplication, Addition/Subtraction.

Work inside brackets first, then evaluate powers/roots, then left-to-right for division/multiplication.
✎Official Worked Solution
Evaluate the formula for monthly mortgage repayment factor: (180 - 4 x 5^2) / 2.
  1. Indices first: 5^2 = 25
  2. Multiplication: 4 x 25 = 100
  3. Brackets: 180 - 100 = 80
  4. Division: 80 / 2 = 40
✓Examiner Golden Rule
Calculators without bracket keys do operations from left to right; enter BIDMAS steps manually.
!Watch Out!
Division and multiplication have equal priority — execute left to right.

Estimation & Approximation

Workplace application of Estimation & Approximation.
★Core Formula & Statutory Method
Estimation involves rounding every number in a calculation to 1 significant figure to check plausibility.

Round each number to 1 significant figure BEFORE performing any calculation.
✎Official Worked Solution
Estimate the cost of 48.7 hours of plant machinery hire at £31.25 per hour.
  1. Round 48.7 to 1 sig fig = 50
  2. Round £31.25 to 1 sig fig = £30
  3. Estimated calculation = 50 x £30 = £1,500
✓Examiner Golden Rule
Do not calculate the exact answer and then round it — examiners award marks for pre-rounding.
!Watch Out!
Estimation is an essential checking method required in Section A exam tasks.

Compound Units: Speed, Density & Pressure

Workplace application of Compound Units: Speed, Density & Pressure.
★Core Formula & Statutory Method
Speed is how fast something travels: Speed = Distance ÷ Time. Density = Mass ÷ Volume. Pressure = Force ÷ Area.

Speed formula triangle: Put Distance (D) at the top, Speed (S) and Time (T) at the bottom. Cover S to get D ÷ T. Cover D to get S × T. Cover T to get D ÷ S.
✎Official Worked Solution
A delivery van travels 145 miles from Birmingham to London in 2 hours 45 minutes. Find average speed.
  1. Convert 45 mins to decimal hours: 45 ÷ 60 = 0.75 hours
  2. Total time = 2.75 hours
  3. Speed = 145 ÷ 2.75 = 52.7 mph
✓Examiner Golden Rule
Always convert minutes to decimal hours by dividing by 60 (2 hours 45 mins is 2.75h, NOT 2.45h!).
!Watch Out!
Density is measured in g/cm³ or kg/m³.

Perimeter of Composite 2D Shapes

Workplace application of Perimeter of Composite 2D Shapes.
★Core Formula & Statutory Method
Perimeter is the total boundary distance around the outside edge of a 2D shape.

Find all missing horizontal and vertical side lengths first, then sum all outer edges.
✎Official Worked Solution
An L-shaped industrial yard has outer dimensions 18 m by 14 m with an inset section 7 m by 6 m.
  1. Missing horizontal = 18 - 7 = 11 m
  2. Missing vertical = 14 - 6 = 8 m
  3. Perimeter = 18 + 14 + 7 + 6 + 11 + 8 = 64 m
✓Examiner Golden Rule
Do not include interior dashed boundary lines in perimeter totals.
!Watch Out!
Tick off each side as you sum it to avoid missing any edge.

Workplace application of Circumference of Circles.
★Core Formula & Statutory Method
Circumference is the distance around the outside edge of a circle: Circumference = 3.142 × diameter.

If you are given the radius, double it first to get the diameter: diameter = 2 × radius.
✎Official Worked Solution
A circular roundabout has a diameter of 24 metres. Calculate its outer circumference.
  1. Formula: Circumference = 3.142 × diameter
  2. Calculation: 3.142 × 24 m
  3. Circumference = 75.41 metres
✓Examiner Golden Rule
For a semi-circle perimeter: calculate (3.142 × diameter ÷ 2) + diameter (don't forget to add the flat straight edge!).
!Watch Out!
Remember: Circumference = 3.142 × diameter.

Area of Rectangles, Triangles & Parallelograms

Workplace application of Area of Rectangles, Triangles & Parallelograms.
★Core Formula & Statutory Method
Area is the flat surface space inside a 2D shape, measured in square units (cm², m²).

Rectangle = length × width. Triangle = (base × height) ÷ 2. Parallelogram = base × height.
✎Official Worked Solution
A triangular warehouse roof gable has a base of 8.4 m and perpendicular vertical height of 3.5 m.
  1. Formula: Triangle Area = (base × height) ÷ 2
  2. Calculation: (8.4 × 3.5) ÷ 2 = 29.4 ÷ 2
  3. Area = 14.7 m²
✓Examiner Golden Rule
Always use the upright vertical height at 90°, NEVER the slanted sloping side!
!Watch Out!
For triangles, always divide by 2 at the end.

Area of Circles & Semicircles

Workplace application of Area of Circles & Semicircles.
★Core Formula & Statutory Method
Area of a circle: Circle Area = 3.142 × radius × radius. Multiply the radius by itself first, then multiply by 3.142.

If you are given the diameter, halve it first to get the radius: radius = diameter ÷ 2. Semicircle area = circle area ÷ 2.
✎Official Worked Solution
A circular patio has a diameter of 7.2 metres. Calculate its total paved surface area.
  1. Halve diameter to get radius: 7.2 ÷ 2 = 3.6 m
  2. Multiply radius by itself: 3.6 × 3.6 = 12.96
  3. Area: 3.142 × 12.96 = 40.72 m²
✓Examiner Golden Rule
Always square the radius (radius × radius) before multiplying by 3.142.
!Watch Out!
Do NOT use diameter in the area calculation — always halve to radius first!

★Core Formula & Statutory Method
Total surface area is the sum of the areas of all external faces of a 3D solid.

Mean, Median, Mode & Range

Workplace application of Mean, Median, Mode & Range.
★Core Formula & Statutory Method
Averages summarise a data set: Mean = Total / Count; Median = middle number; Mode = most frequent; Range = spread.

Order data from smallest to largest BEFORE finding the median. If even number of values, average the middle two.
✎Official Worked Solution
Seven delivery drivers log their daily deliveries: 18, 24, 21, 19, 32, 21, 26. Find mean and median.
  1. Mean: (18+24+21+19+32+21+26) / 7 = 161 / 7 = 23 deliveries
  2. Ordered: 18, 19, 21, 21, 24, 26, 32
  3. Median = 21, Mode = 21, Range = 32 - 18 = 14
✓Examiner Golden Rule
Range is a measure of SPREAD/CONSISTENCY, NOT an average. A smaller range means more consistent.
!Watch Out!
Outliers (extreme high/low values) distort the mean, but not the median.

Grouped Frequency Tables & Estimated Mean

Workplace application of Grouped Frequency Tables & Estimated Mean.
★Core Formula & Statutory Method
When continuous data is grouped into classes, calculate the midpoints to estimate the mean value.

Step 1: Find midpoint of each class. Step 2: Multiply midpoint x frequency (f x x). Step 3: Sum(f x x) / Sum(f).
✎Official Worked Solution
Hospital waiting times: 0-10 mins (f=12), 10-20 mins (f=24), 20-30 mins (f=14). Estimate the mean wait time.
  1. Midpoints: 5, 15, 25
  2. f x x: (12 x 5) + (24 x 15) + (14 x 25) = 60 + 360 + 350 = 770
  3. Sum of frequencies: 12 + 24 + 14 = 50
  4. Estimated mean: 770 / 50 = 15.4 minutes
✓Examiner Golden Rule
Always divide by total frequency (number of patients/items), NEVER by the number of rows!
!Watch Out!
It is an ESTIMATED mean because the exact individual raw values inside each group are unknown.

Single & Combined Event Probability

Workplace application of Single & Combined Event Probability.
★Core Formula & Statutory Method
Probability measures how likely an event is to occur, on a scale from 0 (impossible) to 1 (certain).

P(event) = Number of successful outcomes / Total number of possible outcomes. P(not event) = 1 - P(event).
✎Official Worked Solution
A quality control batch has 450 items: 18 are defective. Find the probability of choosing a NON-defective item.

  2. Probability = 432 / 450 = 48 / 50 = 0.96 (or 96% / 24/25)
✓Examiner Golden Rule
Probabilities can be written as fractions, decimals, or percentages, but NEVER as ratios!
!Watch Out!
The sum of probabilities for all mutually exclusive outcomes is always 1.
