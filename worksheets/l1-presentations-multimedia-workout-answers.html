Designing Digital Presentations and Slide Design — Answer Key

2. Creating and Editing • Standard Code: L1.2.5
Style 1: Solutions (6 Drills)
Task 1 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What keyboard key starts a slide show from the very first slide in Microsoft PowerPoint?

A. F5 (CORRECT)
B. F1
C. Ctrl + P
D. Esc
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

F5 launches the slide show from slide 1; Shift + F5 starts from the current active slide.

Task 2 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

Where should a presenter type personal reminders and talking prompts that the audience cannot see on the main screen?

A. In the Speaker Notes pane below the slide (CORRECT)
B. Directly into the slide title box in tiny 4pt font
C. In the Recycle Bin
D. On the slide master background
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Speaker notes appear on the presenter's private display monitor without showing on the projector.

Task 3 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

Why should flashy, spinning slide transitions be avoided in professional workplace presentations?

A. They distract the audience from the content and look unprofessional (CORRECT)
B. They cause computer monitors to overheat
C. They erase slide text automatically
D. They prevent PowerPoint from saving
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Subtle transitions maintain professional focus, whereas excessive animations distract.

Task 4 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

How can a presenter ensure that the organisation logo, corporate color theme, and footer disclaimer appear consistently across all 30 slides in a slide deck?

A. Add them to the Slide Master (Master Slide template) (CORRECT)
B. Manually paste them onto each individual slide one by one
C. Tell the audience to imagine the logo
D. Print them onto stickers and place them on the projector screen
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

The Slide Master controls the default design, formatting, and background elements for the entire presentation. Any element added to the Slide Master appears on all dependent layout slides.

Task 5 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

What is the recommended rule regarding animations and transitions in a formal workplace business presentation?

A. Use simple, subtle transitions (such as Fade) and avoid loud sound effects or excessive flying animations (CORRECT)
B. Apply a different flashy 3D transition with explosion sounds to every single bullet point
C. Make all text bounce across the screen continuously for 10 minutes
D. Never change slides during a meeting
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Excessive animations and sound effects distract audiences and appear unprofessional. Subtle transitions maintain focus on the core information.

Task 6 — Model Solution [2 Marks]

While delivering a presentation on a connected digital projector, which PowerPoint/Keynote feature allows the speaker to see private speaking notes and upcoming slides while the audience only sees the active slide?

A. Presenter View (CORRECT)
B. Slide Sorter View
C. Reading View
D. Outline View
Tutor Diagnostic Explanation:

Presenter View leverages dual monitors: the audience sees the full-screen slide on the projector, while the speaker sees private prompts, a timer, and slide previews on their laptop.
