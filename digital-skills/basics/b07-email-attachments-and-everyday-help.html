Step-by-Step Practical Guide

1
1. Sending Clear Emails & Adding Attachments

Click New Mail / Compose and work cleanly from top to bottom:

  • To / CC / BCC: Use To for the main recipient; CC to keep someone in the loop; BCC when emailing a group of external people who must NOT see each other's private email addresses!
  • Subject Line: Write a crisp topic summary (e.g. Maths Homework — Jordan) — never write your whole message in the Subject box or leave it blank.
  • Paperclip Icon (Attach): Click Paperclip → Browse this computer → select the file (wait until the file bar finishes uploading!).
Tutor Tip: Reply answers only the sender; Reply All answers every single person copied on the email thread — check before clicking!
2
2. Joining Online Video Lessons (Teams / Zoom) Without Stress

Five minutes before an online class or exam slot:

  • Open your calendar invite or email and single-click the blue Join Meeting link.
  • When asked "Allow browser/app to use Camera and Microphone?", always click Allow.
  • Know the two main call buttons: the Microphone icon mutes/unmutes your voice (keep muted in big groups when not speaking); the Camera icon toggles video; Share Screen lets your tutor help you look at your spreadsheet.
Tutor Tip: Test audio using the small arrow beside Microphone ('Test speaker & microphone') before important meetings.
3
3. Calm First-Aid Troubleshooting Checklist

Before calling for IT support or giving up on a task, run these 5 quick checks in order:

  1. No Internet? Check the bottom-right Wi-Fi icon is connected (not Airplane Mode) and your router lights are on.
  2. No Sound? Check the bottom-right Speaker icon isn't muted (`×`) and plug-in headphones aren't stealing the audio.
  3. Frozen App? Wait 20 seconds, or press Ctrl + Shift + Esc (Task Manager) to close just the stuck app.
  4. Screen Tiny or Hard to Read? Hold Ctrl + Plus (+) to zoom in smoothly inside any browser or document.
  5. Still Glitched? Save open files (`Ctrl + S`) and Restart the laptop cleanly.
Tutor Tip: Never give remote access or card payment details to random phone callers claiming your computer has a virus!

Three Common Beginner Traps

Avoid Trap: Hitting Send before the attachment bar finishes loading

Wait until you see the file size or solid attachment chip — and open Sent folder if unsure.

Avoid Trap: Putting 15 learner or customer emails in To instead of BCC

Exposes private personal email addresses under UK GDPR rules — always use BCC for groups.

Avoid Trap: Clicking Reply All on a sensitive one-to-one message

Check the To/CC line before replying so private details stay private.
