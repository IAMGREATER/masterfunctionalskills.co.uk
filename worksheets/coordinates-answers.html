Section A: Procedural Fluency Check Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Four-Quadrant Coordinate Midpoints) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
On a site grid plan, Emergency Generator A is located at coordinates (-4, 6) and Transformer B is located at (8, -2).
Calculate the coordinates of the exact midpoint between Generator A and Transformer B.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Average x-coordinates: (-4 + 8) ÷ 2 = 4 ÷ 2 = 2 [M1]
2. Average y-coordinates: (6 + -2) ÷ 2 = 4 ÷ 2 = 2 → Midpoint (2, 2) [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (2, 2)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Subtracting coordinates instead of adding them before dividing by 2.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: From (-4, 6) to (2, 2) is (+6, -4); from (2, 2) to (8, -2) is also (+6, -4).
Question 2 (Level 1 Foundation — 2 Marks) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Calculate the coordinates of the midpoint between point P(−4, 6) and point Q(8, −2).
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Midpoint x = (−4 + 8) ÷ 2 = 4 ÷ 2 = 2. [M1]
2. Midpoint y = (6 + −2) ÷ 2 = 4 ÷ 2 = 2. [M1]
3. Midpoint = (2, 2). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (2, 2)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Writing (2, −3) instead of (−3, 2) plots the point in Quadrant 4 instead of Quadrant 2.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Add the x-coordinates and divide by 2; add the y-coordinates and divide by 2.
Section B: Workplace & Functional Scenarios Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Logistics Depot Automated Guided Vehicle (AGV) Grid Plotting (Level 1/2 Foundation)) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
An automated warehouse dispatch floor is mapped on a Cartesian coordinate grid. Three charging bays form three corners of a rectangle:
• Bay A: (−3, 2)
• Bay B: (5, 2)
• Bay C: (5, −4)
(a) State the coordinates of the fourth corner, Bay D.
(b) Calculate the area of the rectangle in square grid units.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Align x and y coordinate pairs for the 4th corner D: X-coordinates pair as −3 and 5; Y-coordinates pair as 2 and −4. Bay D = (−3, −4). [A1]
2. Calculate horizontal length and vertical width across axes: Length (from −3 to 5) = 3 + 5 = 8 units. | Width (from −4 to 2) = 4 + 2 = 6 units. [M1]
3. Multiply length by width for area: Area = 8 × 6 = 48 square units. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) Bay D = (−3, −4) | (b) Area = 48 square units
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Always write (x, y) above your answer box and check Horizontal first, Vertical second.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: Notice how the x-coordinates pair up (−3, −3 and 5, 5) and the y-coordinates pair up (2, 2 and −4, −4).
Question 4 (Workplace Application — 3 Marks) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
Three vertices of a square on a coordinate grid are (−1, −1), (4, −1), and (4, 4).
(a) State the coordinates of the fourth vertex.
(b) Calculate the perimeter of the square in grid units.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Missing fourth vertex aligns with x = −1 and y = 4 → (−1, 4). [M1]
2. Side length = 4 − (−1) = 5 units. [P1]
3. Perimeter = 4 × 5 = 20 grid units. [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) (−1, 4) | (b) 20 grid units
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: (4, 0) lies on the horizontal X-axis; (0, 4) lies on the vertical Y-axis.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: From −1 to 4 is 5 units (1 unit to zero plus 4 units past zero).
Section C: Pearson Edexcel Problem Solving & Justification Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Hospital Wi-Fi Router Central Midpoint & Cabling Scale (Level 2 Multi-Step)) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
On a hospital floor plan grid where 1 grid unit = 2.5 metres, Nursing Station P is at (−6, 4) and Pharmacy Terminal Q is at (2, −2).
(a) A relay router is installed at the exact midpoint M between P and Q. Find the coordinates of M.
(b) The straight-line distance from P to Q is 10 grid units. Calculate the real-world cabling length if an extra 15% is added for vertical drops.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate midpoint M by averaging x and y coordinates: x = (−6 + 2) ÷ 2 = −4 ÷ 2 = −2. | y = (4 + −2) ÷ 2 = 2 ÷ 2 = 1. | Midpoint M = (−2, 1). [M1 / A1]
2. Convert 10 grid units to real metres and add 15%: Real distance = 10 × 2.5 m = 25 metres. | With 15% extra = 25 m × 1.15 = 28.75 metres. [M1 / A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (a) Midpoint M = (−2, 1) | (b) Cable length = 28.75 metres
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: For (0, 4): move 0 along the corridor, then go 4 straight up the stairs.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: When averaging a negative and positive number, be careful with signs: −6 + 2 = −4, and −4 ÷ 2 = −2.
Question 6 (Level 2 Multi-Step Problem Solving — 2 Marks) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A drone starts at coordinates (−2, 3). It flies 7 units East (right) and 5 units South (down). What are its new coordinates?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. New x-coordinate = −2 + 7 = 5. [M1]
2. New y-coordinate = 3 − 5 = −2. [P1]
3. New coordinates = (5, −2). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: (5, −2)
⚠️ Examiner Trap Alert: Stating a conclusion without showing the full numerical comparison values.
🔄 Reverse Sanity Check: East increases X (+7); South decreases Y (−5).
