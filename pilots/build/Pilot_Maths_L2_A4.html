4
Number • Standard N7

Percentages of Amounts

A 30% off sale: how much do you really save?
★The 10% method
  • 10%: divide by 10
  • 5%: halve the 10%
  • 1%: divide by 100
  • Add the parts you need
✎Worked Example
A £240 coat is 30% off. Find the sale price.
  1. 10% of £240 = £24
  2. 30% = 3 × £24 = £72
  3. £240 − £72 = £168
✓Golden Rule
Always find 10% first. Build everything else from it.
!Watch Out!
Is the question asking for the saving or the new price?

Maths Level 2 Core Study GuideUnit 15 • Shape
15
Measure & Shape • Standard M12

Area of Compound Shapes

Flooring is sold by the m².
An L-shaped room
★Split it into rectangles
Area of a rectangle = length × width. Split the shape into rectangles A and B. Find each area, then add.
✎Worked Example
Find the area of the L-shaped room.
  1. Missing sides: 10 − 4 = 6 m and 8 − 3 = 5 m
  2. A: 6 × 8 = 48 m²    B: 4 × 5 = 20 m²
  3. Total: 48 + 20 = 68 m²
✓Golden Rule
Area is always in square units: m², cm².
!Watch Out!
Don't add the side lengths – that gives the perimeter, not the area.

Scales, Time & Temperature

Reads 18°C
2:45 – quarter to three
★Read any scale
  • Find two labelled marks
  • Count the gaps
  • Work out what one gap is worth
✎Worked Example
At night it was −6°C. At midday it was 4°C. How much did it rise?
  1. −6 up to 0 = 6 degrees, then 0 up to 4 = 4 degrees
  2. 6 + 4 = 10°C

Percentages (p3)

Practice 1

  1. £650 − £130 = £520
  2. 12 × 7.5 = 90 m²; 90 × £4.20 = £378
  3. −4 to 9 = 13°C
  4. 35% of £1,850 = £647.50; left = £1,202.50
  5. 300 ÷ 4 = 75 g per person; 75 × 10 = 750 g

Practice 2

  1. 120+95+140+160+185 = 700 bikes
  2. 700 ÷ 5 = 140
  3. 185 × 1.15 = 212.75 → 213 bikes

Mock Q1

Area = (3.5 × 5) + (2.5 × 3) = 17.5 + 7.5 = 25 m². Add 10%: 27.5 m². 27.5 ÷ 2.5 = 11 packs. Cost = 11 × £32.99 = £362.89.

Mock Q2

(a) 45% of 480 = 216 coffees. (b) 20% of 480 = 96 juices. 96 < 100, so she is wrong.
