Official Level 1 Specification & Scope (L1.M.22)

Regulated Ofqual Functional Skills Mathematics Level 1 learning outcomes (Open Awards, Pearson Edexcel, City & Guilds, NCFE & AQA):

  • Calculate the area of rectangles and squares using Area = Length × Width in square units (cm², m²).
  • Calculate the area of compound L-shapes and T-shapes by splitting into two non-overlapping rectangles.
  • Solve practical flooring, turfing, and wall-painting problems (including subtracting un-painted windows/doors).
Included at Level 1
Area of rectangles, squares, and compound rectilinear shapes (L/T-shapes) + paint/tile pack coverage.
Saved for Level 2 (Not Tested Here)
Area of circles (πr²), triangles/trapeziums in composite blueprints, and 3D curved surface area (Level 2 only). View Level 2 Guide →

Core Level 1 Golden Rules: Area of Rectangles and Compound L-Shapes (Level 1)

Golden Rule 1: Area = Length × Width (Always State Square Units!)

Flat 2D surface coverage is measured in m² or cm². Always check both measurements share the same unit before multiplying!

Level 1 Key Rule: 6 m × 4.5 m = 27 m²
Golden Rule 2: Split L-Shapes Cleanly Into Rectangle A + Rectangle B

Draw one clear dotted line across the L-shape to split it into two simple rectangles. Check you use the split length (not the full outer length!) on the shorter piece.

Level 1 Key Rule: Total Area = Area A + Area B
Golden Rule 3: Cutouts (Windows, Doors, Ponds) = SUBTRACT Areas

When painting a wall with a window, work out Wall Area − Window Area before dividing by tin coverage.

Level 1 Key Rule: Don't paint over the glass!

Core Skill 1: Splitting an L-Shaped Room Into Two Rectangles

Draw a single horizontal or vertical split line and label each sub-rectangle's L × W.

Rectangle A (Main Section)
6 m × 4 m = 24 m²
Rectangle B (Side Alcove)
3 m × 2 m = 6 m²
Total Floor Area (A + B)
24 m² + 6 m² = 30 m²

Core Skill 2: Tile Boxes & Paint Tins (Two Coats!)

Remember: '2 coats of paint' doubles the area you need to cover before dividing by tin coverage!

Wall Area Needed = 18 m² × 2 coats
36 m² total paint coverage
Each Tin Covers 10 m²
36 ÷ 10 = 3.6 → buy 4 tins

Vocational Context: Office Carpet Tile Refurbishment (L-Shape)

An L-shaped reception floor is split into Rectangle A (5 m by 4 m) and Rectangle B (3 m by 2.5 m). Carpet packs each cover 4 m² and cost £32 per pack. Work out the total cost of the carpet packs needed.
Step
Level 1 Method & Working
Marking Focus
Step 1 Calculate Area A and Area B
A = 5 × 4 = 20 m² | B = 3 × 2.5 = 7.5 m²
→ 20 m² and 7.5 m²
[M1] Correct rectangle multiplication
Step 2 Add for total L-shape floor area
20 + 7.5 = 27.5 m²
→ 27.5 m²
[A1] Accurate compound area
Step 3 Divide by 4 m² per pack and round UP
27.5 ÷ 4 = 6.875 → 7 packs
→ 7 packs
[M1] Rounded up to 7 whole packs
Step 4 Multiply 7 packs by £32
7 × £32 = £224
→ £224
[A1] Correct final cost
Final Exam Answer: £224 (7 packs covering 28 m²)

Common Level 1 Examiner Traps to Avoid

Level 1 Examiner Trap: Multiplying the Two Longest Outer Edges of an L-Shape
Doing 8 × 6 calculates the whole bounding box including the missing corner! Always split or subtract the cutout.
Level 1 Examiner Trap: Forgetting to Double Area When Applying 2 Coats of Paint
If a wall is 22 m² and requires 2 coats, you need 44 m² worth of paint.

Interactive Level 1 Exam Practice Questions

Level 1 Practice 1: Wall Painting With Window Cutout 3 Marks
A rectangular wall measures 6 m by 2.5 m. It contains a window measuring 2 m by 1.5 m. Find the painted wall area.
Level 1 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
Full wall = 6 × 2.5 = 15 m² (M1): 15 m²
Window = 2 × 1.5 = 3 m²; subtract 15 - 3 (M1): 15 − 3
Painted area with square units (A1): 12 m²
Final Answer: 12 m²
Level 1 Practice 2: Lawn Turf Rolls 2 Marks
A rectangular lawn measures 9 m by 6 m. Each roll of turf covers 1.5 m². How many rolls are needed?
Level 1 Worked Solution & Mark Breakdown:
Area = 9 × 6 = 54 m² (M1): 54 ÷ 1.5
Exact number of rolls (A1): 36 rolls
Final Answer: 36 rolls
