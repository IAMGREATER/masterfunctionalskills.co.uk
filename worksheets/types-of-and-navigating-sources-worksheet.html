Top Tip for Types of Sources, Layout & Organizational Devices: Golden Rule 1: Distinguish Primary from Secondary Sources: Primary sources are raw, firsthand records created at the time of an event (witness logs, clinical blood pressure charts, original survey responses). Secondary sources analyse, summarise, or comment on primary data (newspaper articles, textbook chapters, review reports).
