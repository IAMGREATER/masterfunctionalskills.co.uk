Specification & Learning Outcomes

Candidates must demonstrate mastery of the following regulated Functional Skills Mathematics outcomes:

  • L1.25 / L2.31 – Read, construct, and use linear conversion graphs to convert between units, currencies, and tariffs, including scaling for large values.

Golden Rules for Conversion Graphs & Currency Exchange

Golden Rule 1: Scale Up Multiples for Values Beyond the Printed Graph

Conversion graphs for units and currencies pass through (0, 0) in direct proportion. If a graph only goes up to £50 (€60) and you need to convert £250: read the value at £50 (€60) and multiply by 5 (5 × €60 = €300).

Golden Rule / Method: For Large Values: Read a Clean Factor (e.g. £50 or £10) and Multiply
Golden Rule 2: Check the Small Grid Square Scale on BOTH Axes Separately

The horizontal X-axis and vertical Y-axis almost always have different scales! Calculate what 1 small grid square represents on the X-axis and what 1 small square represents on the Y-axis before reading.

Golden Rule / Method: Check X-Axis Small Square Value AND Y-Axis Small Square Value

Core Skill 1: Direct Proportion Scaling Using a Conversion Graph

Converting large amounts accurately by reading at 10, 20, or 50.

Pick a point where the conversion line crosses an exact grid intersection (a "lattice point"):

  • Miles ↔ Kilometres: Line passes through (5 miles, 8 km) and (50 miles, 80 km).
  • Kilograms ↔ Pounds (lbs): Line passes through (10 kg, 22 lbs).
  • Litres ↔ Gallons: Line passes through (9 litres, 2 gallons) (1 gallon = 4.5 litres).
Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 2: Fixed-Charge + Per-Unit Tariff Graphs (Non-Zero Y-Intercept)

Why taxi and utility graphs start above (0, 0) on the vertical axis.

Warning: If a graph shows a courier or taxi fare starting at (0 miles, £4.00), the £4.00 is a fixed call-out charge:

  • Because it does NOT start at (0, 0), you cannot simply double the 10-mile total fare to find a 20-mile fare!
  • Instead, find the per-mile rate (slope) by subtracting the £4.00 standing charge first.
Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Core Skill 3: Bidirectional Currency Exchange & Commission Deductions

Converting Pounds to foreign currency and converting leftover currency back.

  1. Pounds (£) → Euros (€): Start on the Pounds axis, go to the line, read across to the Euros axis.
  2. Euros (€) → Pounds (£): Start on the Euros axis, go to the line, read across to the Pounds axis.
Level 1 Level 2 Pearson Edexcel City & Guilds NCFE

Pearson Edexcel Step-by-Step Worked Models

Benchmark Exam Model 1 3 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 1/2)

Vocational Context: International Medical Equipment Purchasing

A conversion graph between Pounds (£) and Euros (€) passes through (0, 0) and the point (£40, €48).
(a) Use the relationship to convert £25 into Euros.
(b) A specialist diagnostic monitor costs €360 from a German supplier. Calculate its cost in Pounds (£).
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Find the unit exchange rate (1 Pound in Euros)
€48 ÷ £40 = €1.20 per £1.
Criteria: M1: Unit exchange rate (€1.20 per £1)
Step 2 Convert £25 into Euros
25 × €1.20 = €30.00.
Criteria: A1: Accurate conversion (€30)
Step 3 Convert €360 into Pounds (÷ 1.20 or scale from €48)
€360 ÷ 1.20 = £300.00 (or 360 ÷ 48 = 7.5; 7.5 × £40 = £300.00).
Criteria: A1: Accurate cost in Pounds (£300.00)
Final Accredited Answer: (a) €30 | (b) £300.00
Method Checklist & Marking Scheme Guidance
Finding the exact lattice point (£40, €48) lets you scale any value with 100% accuracy.
Benchmark Exam Model 2 4 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 2)

Vocational Context: Anaesthetic Drug Dosage Calculation

An anaesthetist calculates a patient’s medication dose at 1.5 mg per kg of body weight.
• The patient weighs 11 stone 6 lbs (where 1 stone = 14 lbs).
• A conversion graph between kilograms (kg) and pounds (lbs) passes through (0, 0) and (20 kg, 44 lbs).
Calculate the patient’s weight in kg and the required medication dose in mg.
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Convert 11 stone 6 lbs entirely into pounds (lbs)
(11 × 14 lbs) + 6 lbs = 154 + 6 = 160 lbs.
Criteria: M1: Total weight in lbs (160 lbs)
Step 2 Use the conversion graph point (20 kg = 44 lbs → 1 kg = 2.2 lbs) to convert to kg
160 lbs ÷ 2.2 = 72.73 kg (or 160 ÷ 44 × 20 = 72.73 kg).
Criteria: M1: Conversion to kg (72.73 kg)
Step 3 Multiply weight in kg by 1.5 mg/kg
72.727... × 1.5 = 109.09 mg (or 109.1 mg).
Criteria: M1 / A1: Accurate dose (109.1 mg)
Final Accredited Answer: Weight = 72.73 kg | Required Dose = 109.1 mg
Method Checklist & Marking Scheme Guidance
Convert stones and pounds into total pounds FIRST (11 × 14 + 6 = 160 lbs) before using the lbs-to-kg conversion graph.

Critical Examiner Traps & Common Pitfalls

Examiner Pitfall: Scaling a Non-Zero Intercept Tariff Graph by Multiplication
If a taxi graph starts at £3.50 for 0 miles and costs £13.50 for 5 miles, doubling £13.50 to £27.00 for 10 miles double-counts the £3.50 flag fall!
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Only multiply directly when the conversion line passes through the origin (0, 0).
Examiner Pitfall: Reading from the Wrong Axis (e.g. Reading 50 on X instead of 50 on Y)
Looking up 50 km on the miles axis instead of the kilometres axis.
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Circle the axis label that matches the unit given in the question before drawing your reading line.

Interactive Exam Practice Questions

Exam Practice Question 1 2 Marks
A conversion graph between gallons and litres passes through (0, 0) and (10 gallons, 45.5 litres). How many litres are in 35 gallons?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. 1 gallon = 45.5 ÷ 10 = 4.55 litres.
2. 35 gallons = 35 × 4.55 = 159.25 litres.
Final Correct Answer: 159.25 litres
Examiner Tip: Multiply the 10-gallon reading (45.5 L) by 3.5 to get 35 gallons.
Exam Practice Question 2 3 Marks
A miles-to-kilometres conversion graph passes through (0, 0) and (50 miles, 80 km). A courier drives 280 km in France. Convert this distance into miles.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Scale factor: 8 km = 5 miles (or 1.6 km = 1 mile).
2. Miles = 280 ÷ 1.6 = 175 miles (or (280 ÷ 80) × 50 = 3.5 × 50 = 175 miles).
Final Correct Answer: 175 miles
Examiner Tip: Divide km by 8 and multiply by 5 (280 ÷ 8 = 35; 35 × 5 = 175 miles).
Exam Practice Question 3 3 Marks
A conversion graph between Pounds (£) and US Dollars ($) passes through (£50, $64). A nurse returning from New York exchanges $192 back into Pounds, and the bureau deducts a £3.50 handling fee. How many Pounds does the nurse receive?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Scale factor from $64 to $192: 192 ÷ 64 = 3.
2. Gross Pounds = 3 × £50 = £150.00.
3. Subtract £3.50 fee: £150.00 − £3.50 = £146.50.
Final Correct Answer: £146.50
Examiner Tip: $192 is exactly 3 times $64, so it converts to 3 × £50 = £150 before the fee.
