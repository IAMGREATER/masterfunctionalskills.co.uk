3 Simple Entry 3 Golden Rules: Fraction Basics

Golden Rule 1: Bottom Number = Total Equal Parts • Top Number = Shaded Parts

Every fraction MUST be split into equal-sized parts. In 3/4, the bar has 4 equal parts in total and 3 parts are shaded.

Entry 3 Example: If parts are uneven sizes, it is NOT a true fraction!
Golden Rule 2: To Find 1 Part (Unit Fraction), Divide by the Bottom Number

To find 1/4 of 20, divide 20 by 4 → 5. To find 1/5 of 35, divide 35 by 5 → 7.

Entry 3 Example: 1/2 = divide by 2 | 1/3 = divide by 3 | 1/10 = divide by 10.
Golden Rule 3: For 3/4, Find 1/4 First — Then Multiply by 3!

Break two-step fractions into two easy bites: divide by the bottom number first, then multiply by the top number.

Entry 3 Example: 3/4 of 20: 20 ÷ 4 = 5 (one quarter), then 5 × 3 = 15!

Visual Unit Bar Model: Finding 3/4 of £24

Whole Bar = £24 split into 4 equal quarters (£24 ÷ 4 = £6 each):
1/4 = £6
1/4 = £6
1/4 = £6
£6
3 shaded quarters = £6 × 3 = £18!

Core Skill 1: Simple Equivalent Fractions to Know by Sight

Two fractions are equivalent when they cover the exact same amount of a bar.

2/4 is the same as 1/2
5/10 is also the same as 1/2!
2/10 is the same as 1/5
Bigger bottom number = smaller slices (1/10 is smaller than 1/2!).

Core Skill 2: Finding Fractions of Everyday Amounts

Always divide the total by the bottom number first.

1/3 of 30 minutes → 30 ÷ 3 = 10 minutes
1/10 of £80 → £80 ÷ 10 = £8.
Emma’s Entry 3 Exam Tip
Don't let bigger bottom numbers trick you! Imagine sharing one pizza: sharing between 2 people (1/2) gives you a much bigger slice than sharing between 10 people (1/10)!

Entry Level 3 Exam Model 3 Marks (Non-Calculator)

Everyday Scenario: Staff Discount on Work Boots

A pair of safety boots normally costs £40. Apprentices get 1/4 off the normal price.
(a) How much money is taken off?
(b) How much does an apprentice pay?
Step
Clear Working Out
Mark Focus
Step 1 Find 1/4 of £40 by dividing by 4
£40 ÷ 4 = £10 off
[M1] £10 discount
Step 2 Subtract the discount from £40
£40 − £10 = £30
[M1] Subtract from original price
Step 3 State final price clearly
£30
[A1] £30
Final Exam Answer: (a) £10 off | (b) £30 to pay
Emma’s Check Reminder: Always include units (£, p, cm, g, ml, or °C) and double-check you answered the exact question asked!

Common Entry 3 Mistakes to Avoid

Common Entry 3 Mistake: Thinking 1/10 Is Bigger Than 1/2
10 is bigger than 2, so each tenth slice is much SMALLER than a half!
Common Entry 3 Mistake: Stopping After Finding 1/4 When Asked for the Sale Price
If a £40 coat has 1/4 off, £10 is the saving — remember to do £40 − £10 = £30 if asked what you pay!

Interactive Entry Level 3 Practice Questions

Entry 3 Practice 1: Shaded Car Park Spaces 2 Marks
A small car park has 30 spaces. 1/3 of the spaces are reserved for staff. How many spaces are reserved for staff?
Entry Level 3 Worked Solution:
Divide by 3 (1 Mark): 30 ÷ 3
Answer (1 Mark): 10 spaces
Final Answer: 10 spaces
Entry 3 Practice 2: Three Quarters of a Shift 2 Marks
Work out 3/4 of 28 training packs.
Entry Level 3 Worked Solution:
One quarter (1 Mark): 28 ÷ 4 = 7
Three quarters (1 Mark): 7 × 3 = 21 packs
Final Answer: 21 packs
