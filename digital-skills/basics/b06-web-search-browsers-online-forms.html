Web Browsers, Safe Searching, Bookmarks & Online Forms

Search online efficiently without falling for top-of-page adverts, manage browser tabs and bookmarks, and submit online forms accurately.

Step-by-Step Practical Guide

1
1. Browser Parts: Tabs, Address Bar, Back & Bookmarks

In Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Firefox, everything sits in a neat top strip:

  • Tabs (Top Strip): Each open website sits in its own tab. Click the small + button to open a brand-new tab without closing your exam or email tab; click × on one tab to close only that page.
  • Address Bar (Long White Box): Type a full website link like gov.uk OR search keywords directly, then press Enter.
  • Back (←) & Refresh (↻): Goes back one page or reloads a stuck webpage.
  • Star Icon (Right of Address Bar): Saves a Bookmark so you can return to a favourite revision site or portal in one click.
Tutor Tip: Look for the padlock symbol (HTTPS) beside the website name before entering personal details or card payments.
2
2. Doing a Smart Web Search (Skipping Paid Adverts)

Keep web searches short and specific using 3–5 clear keywords (no need to type full polite sentences):

  1. Example: instead of typing "Please tell me how much minimum apprentice pay is per hour in England this year", type minimum apprentice wage UK gov.
  2. Look closely at the top results: skip links labelled Sponsored or Ad — private companies pay to appear first and may charge fees for free public services.
  3. Prefer trusted domain endings: .gov.uk (UK Government/Councils), .ac.uk (Colleges/Universities), .org.uk (Registered charities/advice).
Tutor Tip: Use inverted commas around an exact phrase ("functional skills digital sample") to narrow huge search results cleanly.
3
3. Filling Online Forms & Attaching Files Safely

When completing booking, job, or utility forms online:

  • Red Asterisk (*): Marks mandatory boxes you cannot leave empty.
  • Dates & Postcodes: Follow exact prompt formats like DD/MM/YYYY (e.g. 05/03/2026) and keep postcodes clean with uppercase letters and a space (e.g. OX1 4AA).
  • File Upload ('Choose File'): Click Browse/Choose File, click the exact folder (Documents or Downloads), click the right file once, and wait until its filename appears next to the button before clicking Submit.
Tutor Tip: Never click Back or close the tab while a form shows 'Submitting...'; wait for the Reference Number confirmation page.

Three Common Beginner Traps

Avoid Trap: Typing passwords or bank details into a Sponsored lookalike site

Check the exact web address in the Address Bar BEFORE pressing Submit.

Avoid Trap: Using the Browser Search box for a website URL you already know

If you type www.pearson.com in a search bar instead of the top Address Bar, you may click an unrelated link.

Avoid Trap: Closing the browser too fast after clicking Submit

Wait until the confirmation receipt appears and check your email for the automated confirmation.
