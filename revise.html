Functional Skills Revision — Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about preparing for your Functional Skills exams.

How do I know which level to start with?

We recommend taking our Free Diagnostic Assessment. It takes 15–20 minutes and pinpoint your exact current skill level (Entry 3, Level 1, or Level 2) with a tailored revision study plan.

Are all resources free on Master Functional Skills?

Yes. All of our topic revision overviews, downloadable question worksheets, model answer sheets, video solutions, and official awarding body past papers are 100% free to view and download with 1-click access and no email paywalls.

Which exam board does Master Functional Skills follow?

The Functional Skills subject content is established by the Department for Education (DfE) and regulated by Ofqual. Our curriculum directly covers Pearson Edexcel, City & Guilds, AQA, NCFE, Open Awards, TQUK, and NOCN specifications.
