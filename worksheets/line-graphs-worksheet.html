Examiner Rule for Line Graphs & Trend Analysis: Golden Rule 1: Time Belongs on the Horizontal X-Axis; Join Points with Ruled Segments: When plotting time-series data (hours, days, months, years), always place Time along the bottom horizontal X-axis and the measured variable up the vertical Y-axis. Plot points as neat crosses (×) and join consecutive points with straight ruler lines.
