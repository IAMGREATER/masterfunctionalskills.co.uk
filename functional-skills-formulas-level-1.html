Golden Rules for Algebra, Formulas & Substitution

Golden Rule 1: Letters Next to Letters Mean Multiplication

In algebra, 4h means 4 × h; 2lw means 2 × l × w. Always write in the invisible multiplication signs before substituting values.

Golden Rule / Method: C = 50 + 25h → C = 50 + (25 × h)
Golden Rule 2: Wrap Negative Substitutions in Brackets

When substituting a negative number into a squared variable, (-3)² = +9, NOT -9. Always enclose negative values in brackets.

Golden Rule / Method: (-4)² = (-4) × (-4) = +16

Converting algebraic and word formulas into solvable numerical equations.

Equal Priority Rule: Division and Multiplication have equal standing (work left to right). Addition and Subtraction also have equal standing.

  1. Write out the formula with multiplication signs visible: e.g. P = 2L + 2W → P = (2 × L) + (2 × W).
  2. Replace each letter with its given number in brackets: Make sure units match the formula specification (e.g. convert minutes to hours if the formula requires hours).
  3. Apply BIDMAS strictly: Resolve brackets and powers first, then multiplications/divisions, then additions/subtractions.

To find Speed

To find Time

Core Skill 3: Skill 3: Rearranging Two-Step Formulas (Working Backwards)

Finding an unknown input when you are given the final output.

Inverse Method: Working Backwards
Formula: Cost = 40 + 25h | Given Total Cost = £190
Forward Order (BIDMAS)
  1. Start with unknown hours (h)
  2. Multiply by £25 → 25h
  3. Add fixed callout fee £40 → Total = £190
Rule: In reverse, do inverse operations in reverse order!
Backward (Inverse) Steps
Step 1: Undo +40 by subtracting £40
£190 − £40 = £150
Step 2: Undo ×25 by dividing by 25
£150 ÷ 25 = 6 hours ✓
Substituted correctly into the formula to find the missing input!
VERIFICATION: 40 + (25 × 6) = 40 + 150 = £190 ✓ FULL MARKS [M1][A1]

Suppose a hire formula is Cost = 40 + 25h and a customer is billed £190. To find the hours (h):

  1. Undo the addition first (subtract fixed callout fee): £190 - £40 = £150.
  2. Undo the multiplication second (divide by hourly rate): £150 ÷ £25 = 6 hours.

Pearson Edexcel Step-by-Step Worked Models

Benchmark Exam Model 1 3 Marks (Pearson Edexcel Level 1/2)

Vocational Context: Healthcare Clinical Calculations

The formula for a child's drug dose in milligrams is:
Dose = (Age in years ÷ [Age in years + 12]) × Adult Dose
A 6-year-old child requires medicine where the adult dose is 240 mg. Calculate the child's dose.
Step
Description / Working
Criteria
Step 1 Substitute Age = 6 and Adult Dose = 240 into formula
Dose = (6 ÷ [6 + 12]) × 240
Criteria: M1: Correct numerical substitution
Step 2 Resolve the bracketed denominator: 6 + 12 = 18
Dose = (6 ÷ 18) × 240
Criteria: M1: Order of operations
Step 3 Simplify fraction and calculate final dose
Fraction simplification: 6 ÷ 18 = 1⁄3
Child Dose = 240 ÷ 3 = 80 mg
Criteria: A1: Accurate medication dosage
Final Accredited Answer: Child Dose = 80 mg
Sonya’s Examiner Tip Examiner Method
Notice how 6/18 simplifies to 1/3, making 240 ÷ 3 = 80 straightforward without a calculator.

Critical Examiner Traps & Common Pitfalls

Examiner Pitfall: Adding Before Multiplying in Pricing Formulas
In Cost = 40 + 20h with h = 3, adding 40 + 20 = 60 then 60 × 3 = 180 is wrong. The multiplication must be done first: 20 × 3 = 60, then 40 + 60 = 100.
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Always underline the multiplication term first: Cost = 40 + [20 × h].

Interactive Exam Practice Questions

Exam Practice Question 1 2 Marks
A plumber uses the formula: Total Charge = 45 + 32h, where h is the number of hours worked. Calculate the total charge for a job lasting 4.5 hours.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Substitute h = 4.5: Total = 45 + (32 × 4.5).
2. 32 × 4.5 = 144.
3. Total = 45 + 144 = £189.00.
Final Correct Answer: £189.00
Examiner Tip: Remember to write the answer as currency with a pound sign (£189.00).
Exam Practice Question 2 2 Marks
The formula to convert Celsius (C) to Fahrenheit (F) is F = 1.8C + 32. Calculate the temperature in Fahrenheit when a clinical incubator is set to 35°C.
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Substitute C = 35: F = (1.8 × 35) + 32.
2. 1.8 × 35 = 63.
3. F = 63 + 32 = 95°F.
Final Correct Answer: 95°F
Examiner Tip: Multiply 1.8 by 35 first (63), then add 32.
Exam Practice Question 3 3 Marks
An equipment hire company charges using the formula C = 65 + 18d, where C is the total cost in pounds and d is the number of days hired. A building contractor receives an invoice for £299. For how many days did the contractor hire the equipment?
Marking Scheme Steps:
1. Substitute C = 299: 299 = 65 + 18d.
2. Subtract fixed fee: 299 - 65 = 234.
3. Divide by daily rate: d = 234 ÷ 18 = 13 days.
Final Correct Answer: 13 days
Examiner Tip: Work backwards using inverse operations: subtract 65 first, then divide by 18.
