Question 1 Worked Solution NHS Emergency Blood Courier
[3 Marks Available]
An emergency medical motorcycle courier carries urgent blood supplies between two hospital trauma centres 84 miles apart.
The courier leaves at 14:15 and must arrive before 15:45.
(a) What is the maximum journey time allowed in hours and minutes, and in decimal hours?
(b) Calculate the minimum average speed the courier must maintain to arrive on time.
Time available: 14:15 to 15:45 = 1 hour 30 minutes = 1.5 hours
[M1]
Calculate Speed: Speed = Distance ÷ Time = 84 miles ÷ 1.5 hours
[M1]
Speed = 56 mph
[A1]
Correct Final Answer: (a) 1 hour 30 mins (1.5 hrs) | (b) 56 mph
Quick Check Method: 56 mph × 1.5 hours = 84 miles.
Common Mistake to Avoid: Dividing 84 by 1.3 instead of 1.5.
