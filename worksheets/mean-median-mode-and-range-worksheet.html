Examiner Rule for Averages (Mean, Median, Mode) & Range: Golden Rule 1: The Four Statistical Measures Defined: Mean = Total Sum ÷ Number of Values. Median = Exact middle value AFTER putting numbers in ascending order. Mode = Most frequent value. Range = Highest − Lowest (a measure of spread/consistency, NOT an average!).
