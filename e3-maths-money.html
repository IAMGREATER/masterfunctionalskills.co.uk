3 Simple Entry 3 Golden Rules: Money

Golden Rule 1: Never Write £ and p Together in the Same Price

Write EITHER £2.45 OR 245p — never write ‘£2.45p’ or ‘£0.65p’!

Entry 3 Example: 65 pence = 65p or £0.65.
Golden Rule 2: Always Show Two Digits After the Decimal Point in £

A calculator shows three pounds fifty as 3.5 — in your final money answer, always write the trailing zero: £3.50! And 5 pence is £0.05 (not £0.5!).

Entry 3 Example: £1 = 100p | £0.50 = 50p | £0.05 = 5p.
Golden Rule 3: Convert All Prices into the Same Unit Before Adding

If one item is in pence (85p) and another is in pounds (£2.40), change 85p into £0.85 first so the decimal points line up!

Entry 3 Example: £2.40 + £0.85 = £3.25.

Visual Money Notation & Shopkeeper Change Board

Correct Money Writing
• Three pounds fifty = £3.50
• Eight pence = 8p or £0.08
• Seventy-five pence = 75p or £0.75
Counting Up Change: £3.65 from £10.00
• £3.65 + 35p → £4.00
• £4.00 + £6.00 → £10.00
• Total Change = £6.35!

Core Skill 1: Converting Between Pence and Pounds (100p = £1.00)

Every 100 pence makes £1.00.

340p → £3.40 | 95p → £0.95 | 7p → £0.07
Always check there are 2 digits after the dot!
Rounding Money
£4.78 rounded to the nearest £1 is £5.00; rounded to the nearest 10p is £4.80.

Core Skill 2: Counting Up to Find Change Without Borrowing Mistakes

Instead of subtracting across lots of zeros in £10.00 − £4.35, count UP from £4.35 to £10.00!

£4.35 + 65p makes £5.00
Then £5.00 + £5.00 makes £10.00 → Change = £5.65!
Subi’s Entry 3 Exam Tip
Watch out for the calculator zero trap! When you type 2.40 + 1.10 on a calculator, the screen says 3.5. Always add the extra zero on paper so your answer reads £3.50!

Entry Level 3 Step-by-Step Worked Exam Question

Entry Level 3 Exam Model 3 Marks (Non-Calculator)

Everyday Scenario: Café Lunch Order & Change

Tariq buys a panini for £3.45 and a cup of tea for 85p. He pays with a £10 note.
How much change should Tariq get?
Step
Clear Working Out
Mark Focus
Step 1 Write both prices in pounds: 85p = £0.85
£3.45 + £0.85 = £4.30 total spent
[M1] Convert & add to £4.30
Step 2 Subtract £4.30 from £10.00 (or count up)
£4.30 + £0.70 = £5.00, plus £5.00 = £10.00
[M1] £10.00 − £4.30
Step 3 Write change in correct money notation
£5.70
[A1] £5.70 (with trailing 0)
Final Exam Answer: £5.70
Subi’s Check Reminder: Always include units (£, p, cm, g, ml, or °C) and double-check you answered the exact question asked!

Common Entry 3 Mistakes to Avoid

Common Entry 3 Mistake: Adding £3.45 + 85 as 88.45
Never add whole pence straight onto pounds! Turn 85p into £0.85 first.
Common Entry 3 Mistake: Writing £5.7 Instead of £5.70
Money in pounds must ALWAYS have 2 digits after the decimal point (except exact whole pounds like £5).

Interactive Entry Level 3 Practice Questions

Entry 3 Practice 1: Bus Ticket Change 2 Marks
A return bus ticket costs £4.25. Sara pays with a £5 note. How much change does she receive?
Entry Level 3 Worked Solution:
Subtract / Count up (1 Mark): £5.00 − £4.25
Change (1 Mark): 75p or £0.75
Final Answer: £0.75 (or 75p)
Entry 3 Practice 2: Stationery Basket Total 2 Marks
A notebook costs £1.80, a pen costs 65p, and a ruler costs 50p. Work out the total cost in pounds (£).
Entry Level 3 Worked Solution:
Convert to £ (1 Mark): £1.80 + £0.65 + £0.50
Total (1 Mark): £2.95
Final Answer: £2.95
