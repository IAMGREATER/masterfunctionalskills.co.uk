Spreadsheet Layout, Sorting and Formatting

Design clear, readable spreadsheets for workplace use: format numbers as currency and percentages, adjust column widths to prevent ### overflow errors, merge and centre title cells, and sort data alphabetically and numerically.

Step-by-Step Teaching & Key Concepts

Core Knowledge
1
Fixing the '###' Display Error (Column Widths)

When a number or date is too wide to fit inside its column, Excel does not cut off the digits — it displays ### across the cell to warn you that data is hidden.

To fix this: Double-click the divider line between the column letters at the top (e.g. between column B and C). The column will automatically expand to fit the widest entry (AutoFit)!

Select Entire Column: Ctrl + Space • Select Entire Row: Shift + Space
2
Formatting Numbers: Currency, Decimals, and Percentages

Raw numbers look unprofessional. Format them on the Number group of the ribbon:

  • Currency (£): Formats figures with a pound sign and 2 decimal places (e.g. £45.50).
  • Increase / Decrease Decimals: Controls decimal precision (e.g. rounding 14.2857 to 14.3).
  • Percentage (%): Multiplies by 100 and displays the percent sign (e.g. 0.15 becomes 15%).
  • Merge & Centre: Combines multiple cells across the top of a table into one wide header banner.
3
Sorting Data Records (Ascending vs Descending)

Sorting organises records logically without re-typing:

  • Ascending (A to Z / Smallest to Largest): Alphabetical order from A to Z, or numbers from lowest to highest.
  • Descending (Z to A / Largest to Smallest): Reverse alphabetical, or numbers from highest to lowest.

Always highlight the entire table before sorting to ensure related columns stay together!

Exam Scenario Prompt: A medical supplies spreadsheet shows cell C4 displaying '#####'. The instructions state: 'Make all data in column C fully visible, format cells C2:C10 as Currency to two decimal places, and sort the table by Cost in descending order.' Describe the actions required.
Step 1: Fix column width [B1]
Double-click the boundary between column C and column D headers to AutoFit column width.
Tutor Marking Rationale: Resolves number clipping / ### display issue.
Step 2: Format currency [B1]
Select C2:C10 and apply Currency format (£) with 2 decimal places.
Tutor Marking Rationale: Standard financial formatting.
Step 3: Sort descending [M1]
Select the table and sort by Column C (Cost) from Largest to Smallest (Descending).
Tutor Marking Rationale: Applies descending numerical sort.
Tutor Abdul's Exam Golden Rule: In Level 1 exams, marks are always allocated for: 1. Making all data visible (no clipped text or ###); 2. Correct currency formatting; 3. Proper sort direction.

Pearson Edexcel Benchmark Solution

Formatting pay: Highlight the overtime pay column and choose 'Currency' from the Number Format dropdown, ensuring the '£' symbol and two decimal places are set. Sorting surnames: Select the entire table data, click Sort & Filter > Custom Sort, choose 'Surname' column, and sort in Ascending order (A to Z).
