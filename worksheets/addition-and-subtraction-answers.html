Section A: Core Skills (Warm-Up) Solutions Questions 1 & 2 • 4 Marks
Question 1 (Level 1 Multi-Digit Arithmetic) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Calculate: £48,675 + £19,488.
Set out your column addition clearly.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Correct column alignment and carry-overs shown [M1]
2. Final accurate sum: £68,163 [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £68,163
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Forgetting to add carried digits from tens and hundreds columns into the thousands column.
🔄 Quick Check: £68,163 - £19,488 = £48,675.
Question 2 (Level 1 Decimal Subtraction) Model Solution [2 Marks Total]
Work out: 524.3 kg - 187.65 kg.
Show your decomposition / borrowing clearly.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Pad 524.3 with placeholder zero (524.30) and align decimal points [M1]
2. Complete subtraction: 336.65 kg [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 336.65 kg
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Subtracting '0 minus 5 = 5' without borrowing from the tenths column, giving 336.75 kg.
🔄 Quick Check: 336.65 + 187.65 = 524.30 kg.
Section B: Everyday & Workplace Maths Solutions Questions 3 & 4 • 6 Marks
Question 3 (Level 1 Medical Stock Reconciliation) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A surgical pharmacy begins the week with 12,450 ampoules of saline solution.
During the week:
• Monday to Wednesday deliveries dispatch 3,820 ampoules to Ward 4 and 4,165 ampoules to Intensive Care.
• On Thursday, a restock shipment of 5,500 ampoules arrives.
• On Friday, Emergency Care uses 2,980 ampoules.

How many saline ampoules remain in the pharmacy at the end of Friday?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate total initial stock plus delivery: 12,450 + 5,500 = 17,950 [P1]
2. Calculate total dispatches: 3,820 + 4,165 + 2,980 = 10,965 ampoules [M1]
3. Subtract total dispatches: 17,950 - 10,965 = 6,985 ampoules [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 6,985 ampoules
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Subtracting the 5,500 restock delivery instead of adding it to the available inventory.
🔄 Quick Check: 6,985 + 2,980 - 5,500 + 4,165 + 3,820 = 12,450 ampoules.
Question 4 (Level 2 Ward Operating Budget Tracking) Model Solution [3 Marks Total]
A healthcare ward has a quarterly operational budget of £95,000.
The ward clerk logs the following actual expenditures:
• Nurse agency staffing: £54,320.50
• Medical consumables & bandages: £18,745.25
• Equipment maintenance: £9,860.00
• Patient dietary supplies: £7,412.80

Calculate the exact surplus or deficit balance remaining on the budget at quarter end.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Add all four expenditures: £54,320.50 + £18,745.25 + £9,860.00 + £7,412.80 = £90,338.55 [M1]
2. Subtract total expenditure from budget: £95,000.00 - £90,338.55 = £4,661.45 [P1]
3. Identify as a surplus (underspend): £4,661.45 surplus [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: £4,661.45 Surplus
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Arithmetic slip when aligning the decimal hundredths (.50 + .25 + .00 + .80 = 1.55).
🔄 Quick Check: £90,338.55 + £4,661.45 = £95,000.00.
Section C: Exam-Style Problem Solving Solutions Questions 5 & 6 • 8 Marks
Question 5 (Level 2 Multi-Step Solar Panel Cost Comparison) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A community clinic evaluates installing solar panels to reduce electricity bills.
• Current annual electricity bill: £16,420.
• The solar system will save £4,850 each year on electricity.
• Total installation cost: £18,500.
• The clinic secures an environmental grant of £6,200 towards installation.

How many whole years of electricity savings will it take to recover the clinic's out-of-pocket installation cost?
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Calculate net out-of-pocket installation cost: £18,500 - £6,200 = £12,300 [P1]
2. Divide net cost by annual saving: £12,300 ÷ £4,850 = 2.536... years [M1]
3. Interpret in context: 2 years only saves £9,700 (still £2,600 short); requires 3 years to fully recover out-of-pocket cost [P1]
4. Award mark for stating 3 whole years [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: 3 whole years
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Using the gross £18,500 without deducting the £6,200 grant, or rounding down 2.536 years to 2 years.
🔄 Quick Check: Year 2 savings: 2 × £4,850 = £9,700 (< £12,300). Year 3 savings: 3 × £4,850 = £14,550 (> £12,300).
Question 6 (Level 2 Dispute & Justification) Model Solution [4 Marks Total]
A transport manager reviews quarterly diesel fuel expenditure across three depot delivery fleets:
• Fleet North: £34,820
• Fleet South: £42,150
• Fleet East: £29,980

The finance controller states: 'The combined expenditure for Fleet North and Fleet East was more than £3,000 greater than Fleet South alone.'

Is the finance controller correct?
Show your calculations clearly and state a definitive conclusion.
Step-by-Step Marking Criteria:
1. Sum Fleet North and Fleet East: £34,820 + £29,980 = £64,800 [M1]
2. Compare against Fleet South: £64,800 - £42,150 = £22,650 [P1]
3. Evaluate against claim of 'more than £3,000': £22,650 is substantially greater than £3,000 [M1]
4. Conclude: Yes, the controller is correct. Fleet North and Fleet East combined exceed Fleet South by £22,650 (which is well over £3,000). [A1]
Award Final Answer Mark: Yes, correct (combined spend exceeds South by £22,650)
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid: Comparing North alone against South (£34,820 - £42,150) rather than North and East combined.
🔄 Quick Check: £42,150 + £22,650 = £64,800 = £34,820 + £29,980.
