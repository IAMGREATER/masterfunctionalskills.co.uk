Three Non-Negotiable Exam Golden Rules

Golden Rule 1: Read Portal Upload Spec Strictly (Format, Max MB Size, Page Limits)

Online application portals enforce strict filters: e.g. 'PDF or DOCX only, under 5 MB, no special symbols in filename'. Rename cleanly before browsing.

✗ Trying to upload a 14 MB `.pages` or `.jpg` phone photo into a `.pdf`-only slot
✓ Export cleanly as `.pdf` (< 5 MB) named `Jordan_Patel_CV.pdf` and wait for confirmation
Golden Rule 2: Always Capture Reference Number / Support Ticket Transcript

When contacting customer live chat/helpdesk, have Order/Account Ref ready, write polite numbered steps summarizing the bug, and save the transcript / ticket ID.

✗ Closing a live support chat window without copying your Case Reference
✓ Click 'Email Transcript' or Save PDF confirmation receipt cleanly
Golden Rule 3: Manage Online Subscriptions Directly — Keep Cancellation Proof

Simply uninstalling a phone app or blocking card charges does NOT cancel subscription contracts! Cancel officially inside Account > Subscriptions BEFORE renewal dates.

✗ Deleting the Spotify/streaming app icon and assuming billing stops
✓ Cancel inside Account Billing Portal + keep cancellation confirmation email

Step-by-Step Teaching & Key Concepts

1
Uploading Documents to Web Portals

Workplaces, universities, and government bodies (e.g. DBS checks, HMRC, healthcare clinic portals) require you to upload proof of identity and certificates. Check portal rules before uploading:

  • File Format: Many portals only accept .PDF or .JPG. If you try to upload a .docx or .pages file, the system will reject it.
  • File Size Limit: Portals often enforce limits (e.g. 'Max file size 4 MB'). If your scan is 12 MB, you must compress or resize it before uploading.
  • Confirmation Message: Always wait for the progress bar to reach 100% and look for a green checkmark or confirmation reference number (e.g. REF-98432).

2
Using Online Customer Support & Live Chat

When technical or billing problems arise:

  • Live Chat / Chatbots: Provide your account reference and state the issue clearly in your first message.
  • Ticketing Systems: Receive a support ticket number. Keep this reference for following up.
3
Managing & Cancelling Online Subscriptions

Many digital services offer 'free 30-day trials' that automatically convert into paid monthly direct debits:

  • Set a calendar reminder 3 days before the trial ends.
  • To cancel: Navigate to Account Settings > Subscriptions / Billing > Cancel Subscription.
  • Always save the cancellation confirmation email as proof of termination.

Critical Examiner Traps & Common Pitfalls

Trap 1: Deleting an App Does Not Cancel a Direct Debit or Trial
App stores and SaaS portals keep billing monthly regardless of whether an icon sits on your home screen!
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Manage cancellations strictly via official `Subscriptions` settings (`Apple ID / Play Store / Website Portal`).
Trap 2: Manually Retyping `.pdf` Extension Onto a `.docx` File!
Renaming `CV.docx` to `CV.pdf` in File Explorer leaves corrupted binary Word data that crashes portal viewers!
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Always convert properly using `File > Save As / Export > PDF`.
Trap 3: Sharing Full Card PIN/Password With Fake Support Popups
Legitimate IT helpdesks NEVER ask for your password or demand remote takeover from random browser alert popups.
How to Avoid This Pitfall: Initiate contact only via verified company portal contact pages.
